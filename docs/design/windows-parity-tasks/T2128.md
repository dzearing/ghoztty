# T2128 - Stacked panes: a strip you drag gets its own drop targets - slots between cards, the window edges, a screen edge for a new window, and the sidebar - each with a preview

## In plain terms

On a Mac, a folded pane (a thin strip at the side of a row) can be picked up
and dropped somewhere sensible: between two other panes of its row, along an
edge of the window, against the edge of the screen to make a new half-screen
window, or onto the sidebar. Windows needs the same rules for where such a
drop lands and what the drop preview shows. This task is that rulebook and its
previews; making a strip actually draggable is T2120.

## Goals

- [x] the rules for where a dragged strip may land: slots between its row's
      cards and strips, the window's edges, a screen's edge, the sidebar - and
      nothing over the middle of a pane
- [x] what each of those previews as (a caret in the gutter; the half of the
      screen a new window will cover)
- [x] the Mac tests for those rules ported and passing
- [ ] wiring a press-and-drag on a strip to these rules - moved to T2120

## Summary

Split from T2084 (Mac 2811a3043 PaneDropResolver/PaneDropFeedbackView). A dragged strip never splits or swaps over a pane; it offers slots between the cards and strips of its row, the window's edges, a screen's edge (tiles a new window) and the sidebar, each previewed through DropHighlight.zig. Pure resolver in pane_drop.zig with a port of PaneDropResolverTests; acceptance through the rearrange/drag harnesses.

## Validation criteria

- [x] Pure resolver: `pane_drop.bladeSlots`, `resolveBlade`, `tileFrame`
      answer every case of Mac's PaneDropResolverTests strip section
      (2811a3043) - slots skip the strip's own place, a column's slots run
      top to bottom, a strip of a column names what is left of it, a gutter
      reorders, a card's middle is nothing, the window edge is top-level, the
      screen edge tiles each half, another window offers only its edges, the
      sidebar stashes. Plus two Windows-only cases: a window lying over the
      row hides its slots, and the screen band scales with DPI with a pixel of
      slack. Verified: `zig test src/apprt/win32/pane_drop.zig` (55/55) and
      the none + win32 lanes.
- [x] Previews: `drop_highlight.forTarget` maps `.reorder` to its caret
      (only in its own window) and `.tile` to a new-window footprint over the
      tiled half; DropHighlight paints `.reorder` as a caret. Verified:
      `zig test src/apprt/win32/drop_highlight.zig` (81/81).
- [ ] On-screen strip drag + commit + acceptance: T2120 (deps on this task).
