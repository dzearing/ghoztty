# T2531 - The viewer nav bar is still ~1.3 ms of every window-resize pass after T2521: two child-window SetWindowPos moves (bar ~0.36 ms, address EDIT ~0.19 ms) plus the paints they force (bar paint + GDI flush ~0.5 ms, EDIT ~0.2 ms)

## Summary

T2521 cut the viewer nav bar's share of a window-edge resize pass from ~1.8 ms to ~1.3 ms median (ReleaseSafe, `scripts/resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Exe zig-out-rsafe` with `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`) by making its paint cheaper. What is left is structural. The trace measured it on 2026-10-30 (a temporary split of `chrome_reposition.place`):

- the bar's `SetWindowPos` alone: ~0.36-0.45 ms median
- the address EDIT's `SetWindowPos`: ~0.19-0.25 ms
- the bar's forced paint: glass 0.11 + buttons 0.13 ms in our code, plus ~0.3 ms inside `UpdateWindow` outside `paint` (BeginPaint/EndPaint and the GDI batch flush)
- the EDIT's own forced paint: ~0.2 ms

Two child HWNDs moved and repainted synchronously cannot get under 0.3 ms. Directions to try, measured with the same trace line (`nav_bar_us`, `nav_edit_us`): find what the child `SetWindowPos` spends 0.4 ms on (the tooltip subclass? clip-region work against the WebView2 sibling? `CS_HREDRAW|CS_VREDRAW` invalidation?); skip the bar's move when only the host width changed by letting it be sized wider than the host and clipped; or paint the band as part of the host's own surface so a resize pays one paint instead of two windows.

## In plain terms

Dragging a window edge with a web/markdown viewer open, the viewer's address bar still costs over a millisecond of every frame, which is part of why the panes fall out of step with each other during the drag.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] `nav_us` under 0.3 ms median on `resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Exe zig-out-rsafe` with `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`
- [ ] the bar still repaints in the same frame as the move (T1392's `test/win32/banner-resize-repaint.ps1`-style evidence, or the film's `top` check)
