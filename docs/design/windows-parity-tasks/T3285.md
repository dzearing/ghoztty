# T3285 - A pane released over a monitor at a DIFFERENT DPI should open at the size it left, scaled for that monitor

## Summary

T1547 proved a pane released over a second monitor opens its window on that
monitor (claim H of `test\win32\rearrange-desktop-drop.ps1`), but this box's
two monitors share one DPI. `Window.newWindowFrameAt` sizes the new window
from the SOURCE window's pixel frame and passes a scale to
`pane_relocate.newWindowFrame`; when the target monitor's DPI differs, the
window gets a WM_DPICHANGED as it lands there and resizes, so the frame the
preview promised and the frame that arrives can disagree, and the pane may
not keep the size it had. Nothing measures that.

## In plain terms

On a laptop plus an external monitor at different scaling, dragging a pane
onto the other screen should open a window that looks the same size as the one
it left and sits where the preview showed. Nobody has checked.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Claim H (or a sibling) runs when the box has two monitors at different
      DPI and compares the preview against the arrived frame; named SKIP
      otherwise.
- [ ] Verified on mixed-DPI hardware, or the task records that it could not be.
