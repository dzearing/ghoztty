# T1997 - The pane sidebar's hover card shows the panes behind it as flat colour, not a live blur: put a real DWM blur under the card once it can be measured on screen

## In plain terms

The pane sidebar's hover card is now solid enough that nothing behind it
can be read. On Mac the same card is frosted glass: a bright or colourful
thing behind it (an image, a coloured diff) tints the card softly. Ours
shows the window's background gradient and the panes' tint, but not those
live colours.

## Summary

T1939 fixed the readability defect by backing the card's sheet with the
panes as a blur would leave them (`pane_sidebar_layer.frostPanes`: the desk
under `GLASS_TINT` of the terminal background), making it opaque. That is
a static approximation of a blur: content-specific colour behind the card
no longer reaches it.

The live version: a blur material behind the card. The card is a layered
(`UpdateLayeredWindow`, per-pixel alpha) popup, so the options are an
accent blur-behind (`SetWindowCompositionAttribute`) or a system backdrop
(`DWMSBT_TRANSIENTWINDOW`, T1788) on the card or on a card-shaped window
under it, clipped to the card's rounded shape so the shadow margin is not
blurred. Then the frost under the sheet can go back to translucent.

Blocked on measurement, like T1808: neither blur composites on the test
desktop, and acceptance may not take the user's desktop while they are
present. Do not ship a blur that has never been seen.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] On screen (user away), over a pane of high-contrast text: no glyph
      edges legible through the card (contrast < 1.2:1 against the card's
      own colour), and a strongly coloured region behind it visibly tints
      the card.
- [ ] The card's corners and shadow show no blurred rectangle beyond the
      card's shape.
- [ ] Falls back to T1939's frosted sheet wherever the blur is unavailable
      (transparency effects off, below 22H2 without the accent).
