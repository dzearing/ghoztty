# T2277 - Dragging the gutter beside the Windows pane sidebar should tuck it to its rail or bring it out, and a pane row's gutter pushed past every tucked pane should tuck the expanded sidebar (Mac 543489bca, 075d787d1)

## Summary

**Re-scoped 2026-10-26 at claim:** the 543489bca half (edge grip in the gutter beside the sidebar in both modes; `edgeDrag` pulling the rail out past the panel minimum and tucking the panel under 120) is T2299's, filed separately with the same content - this task is the 075d787d1 half only.

075d787d1: in a row spanning the grid, a gutter pushed left past every pane it has already tucked into strips tucks the expanded sidebar into its rail (once past the panel's give, `dividerTuckGive` = clamp(width - minimum, 24, 80)), and dragged back in the same drag past that point plus a 24 hysteresis brings it out; tucked, the row measures the room it gained. Every collapse/expand is logged with its cause (already true on Windows: `setPaneSidebarExpanded` logs `pane sidebar expanded=<b> cause=<cause>` since T2289).

## In plain terms

When you drag the line between two panes all the way to the left, the panes fold into thin strips - but the pane list beside them used to stay wide, so the drag just stopped. Now, as on the Mac, pushing on past the strips folds the pane list down to its narrow rail too and gives that room to the panes; drag back the other way without letting go and the list comes back out.
