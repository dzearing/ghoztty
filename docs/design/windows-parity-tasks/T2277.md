# T2277 - Dragging the gutter beside the Windows pane sidebar should tuck it to its rail or bring it out, and a pane row's gutter pushed past every tucked pane should tuck the expanded sidebar (Mac 543489bca, 075d787d1)

## Summary

Mac 543489bca: the edge grip lies in the gutter beside the sidebar in both modes, never over the rail tiles. Pulling the rail's gutter past the panel's minimum brings the panel out; pushing the panel well under it tucks it back (PaneSidebarState.edgeDrag), both ways in one drag. 075d787d1: in a row spanning the grid, a gutter pushed left past every pane it has already tucked into strips tucks the expanded sidebar into its rail, and dragged back in the same drag brings it out; the row then measures the room it gained. Every collapse/expand is logged with its cause. Depends on T2265 (sidebar resizes from its gutter with the accent bar).
