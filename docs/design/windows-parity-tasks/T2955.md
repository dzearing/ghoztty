# T2955 - A 4K glide still spends ~110 us per moved terminal copying its glass slice (a quarter of the pane's size, ~1.3 MB for the widest pane, every tick it moves); send slices at the desk's own 1/8 resolution, or let every pane sample one shared desk texture at an offset

## Summary

Found by T2947. After T2947 a glass slice is cropped from a quarter-size
RGBA copy of the desk with one row memcpy per slice row, and the 4K
drag-glide's glass step (`stack trace vacated tail`: glass - under) fell from
~500 to ~170 us median. What is left is the slices themselves: the new
`terms=`/`term_ticks=` fields on that line put one terminal's send at ~111 us
median (2: ~148, 3: ~179, 4: ~323) - writing a quarter-of-the-pane RGBA slice
(~650x500 texels, ~1.3 MB for the widest pane) into a pooled buffer, plus the
renderer mailbox push, on every tick the pane moves. The renderer then
uploads the same bytes.

Two ways to cut it:

1. **Slices at 1/8 of the pane** for panes at least 400 px on their short
   side - the desk itself is rendered at 1/8 there (`deskDownscale`), so a
   1/8 slice carries all the gradient has; a quarter of the bytes. Check the
   seam against the desk around the card (T2712) stays within a level or two,
   with a unit test in the shape of T2947's.
2. **One shared desk texture**: hand every pane's renderer the same quarter
   desk once per desk and send only the pane's rect per tick, so a glide sends
   a few numbers instead of a slice. Bigger change (renderer side, the
   texture's lifetime across panes), and it removes both the copy and the
   upload.

Measure with T2946/T2947's A/B runner (`temp/t2946-ab.ps1`, ring trace; not
committed) on the 4K drag-glide arm.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Release 4K drag-glide ring trace: the `vacated tail` glass step median <= 0.1 ms (T2947 left it at ~0.17 ms)
- [ ] The glass sheet still meets the desk around the card without a visible seam (unit test within a level or two; T2712's tests green)
