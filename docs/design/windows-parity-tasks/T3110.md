# T3110 - Each pane's OpenGL allocations cost about 5.5 MB of video memory, which the driver then keeps at the peak pane count; measure what they are (per-pane font atlas copies, cell buffers, framebuffers) and shrink them

## Summary

T2976 found the 50 MB left after closing 10 panes is the NVIDIA OpenGL driver's reuse pool, not ours: it survives GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT=0, every knob and a D3D flush+trim, scales with the PEAK pane count (2 panes +5.5 MB, 10 panes +50 MB, tabs or splits alike) and never grows on a second cycle. No GL/WGL call returns that pool, so the lever left is the per-pane GL cost itself (~5.5 MB each, also what the open-10-panes peak pays). Step 1: account for it - log the size of every GL texture/buffer/renderbuffer a pane allocates (grayscale + color atlas, cell/instance buffers, the interop FBO) and compare with 5.5 MB. Step 2: shrink the biggest (e.g. a pane-shared atlas would need a share group; a smaller initial atlas is cheap). Measure with scripts\perf-baseline.ps1 -Phase cycle -CycleShape one|mixed.
