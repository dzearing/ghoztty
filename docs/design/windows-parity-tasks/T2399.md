# T2399 - update-progress.ps1 red twice on 2026-10-28 (alone too): the cut-off download's panel never logs 'update progress: The download failed', and the second download's panel logs no new 'update progress:' lines - the log says Truncated correctly, so the panel's sentence is not reaching the log; suspect T2365's refresh-clock move (f240f86ce, after the last green stamp at da3798a68) or a locked box with no vblank

## In plain terms

The test that watches the update download panel went red twice, which looked
like the panel had stopped telling the user a download failed. It had not: the
app said the right thing every time, just a fraction of a second later than
the test looked. The test now waits for the panel's words instead of glancing
once, so it goes red only when the panel is actually wrong.

## Summary

Not a product defect, and not T2365's refresh-clock move. Both red runs of
2026-10-28 kept their app logs (`%TEMP%\ghoztty-t1195-9808`, `-38524`):

- **Section 4 (cut-off download).** The panel DID log `update progress: The
  download failed. Ghoztty will keep the build you have.` - but on its next
  150 ms tick, after the GUI thread had raised the "The update could not be
  downloaded." answer dialog (T2322, 2026-10-27), a few lines after the
  worker's `the transfer was cut short`. The assertion read the log once, right
  after `Wait-Log 'cut short'` returned, and lost that race.
- **Section 6 (second download after a cancel).** `Wait-Log 'update progress:
  0 bytes downloaded'` matched the FIRST panel's line still in the log and
  returned at once, so `the second panel is reporting` became a one-shot count
  taken before the new panel's first tick.

Fix (test only): section 4 waits up to 10 s for the failed sentence and also
asserts the panel never said "Download complete."; section 6 uses a new
`Wait-CountAbove` helper that waits for the count of panel lines to grow, then
checks that the `0 bytes downloaded` count grew too.
