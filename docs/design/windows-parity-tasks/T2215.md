# T2215 - A maximized window's synchronous capture still shows the pane outline at its pre-maximize size in the top-left corner

## Summary

caption-bar.ps1 section 6 saved %TEMP%\caption-bar-maximized.png (2026-10-24, T1286): the window is maximized to 3858x2118 but a ~1100x700 rounded pane border with a second offset outline sits in the top-left, i.e. the size the window had before the maximize click. Either the pane layout/redraw has not followed the resize when -Sync (WM_PRINTCLIENT) captures it, or the app really paints a stale frame. Determine which: re-capture after a settle delay and with PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT; if the ghost survives, it is a product repaint defect a user would see on maximize.

## In plain terms

A test screenshot of a maximized window showed a leftover, smaller copy of the
window in its top-left corner, which looked like Ghoztty forgetting to redraw
after maximize. It is not: the leftover sits only where the terminal text area
is, a region the test's screenshot method cannot photograph, so it shows old
screen memory there. The app redraws correctly; the test now blanks that region
so its picture and its score show only what the app painted.

## Goals

- [x] decide whether the ghost is a product repaint defect or a capture artifact
- [x] stop caption-bar's maximized capture (PNG and content score) from carrying the artifact

## Findings (2026-11-25)

Re-captured with a scratch probe (maximize via SC_MAXIMIZE, -Sync capture at 0s
and 3s, child rects listed each time). Result:

- The children follow the maximize at once: GhozttyTerminal 976x623 -> 3734x2032
  at (92,54); GhozttyPaneSidebar 78x651 -> 78x2060. Same at 0s and 3s.
- The ghost survives the 3s settle, but EVERY stale pixel lies inside the
  GhozttyTerminal child's rect (the ghost starts exactly at the child origin,
  window-relative 101,63), and its content is the whole window at its earlier
  sizes/positions (launch size at the origin, the 1100x700 window at its old
  screen position) - i.e. stale screen memory, not stale app layout.
- The caption band, sidebar, frame and pane border around it are all repainted
  at the maximized size.

So this is the T214 capture limit (PrintWindow cannot reproduce the GPU-presented
terminal surface), not a repaint defect. Not reproducible as a user-visible
symptom from the capture; no product change.

Harness fix: caption-bar.ps1 section 6 now fills the GhozttyTerminal child
rects with the launch --background (#000000) before saving
`caption-bar-maximized.png` and scoring it, so the "sees the caption band"
Distinct score can no longer be satisfied by the stale garbage.
