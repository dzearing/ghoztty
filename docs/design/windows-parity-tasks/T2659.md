# T2659 - On a window-edge drag the terminal and the viewer's bar reach the screen one compose after the frame change, while the WebView2 page lands with it: find what holds child-window content a compose behind (independent of present interval and of when in the pass it presents)

## In plain terms

While you drag the window's right edge narrower with a terminal beside a web
page, the page moves in step with the window, but the terminal and the page's
address bar arrive one screen refresh late. For that instant the page overlaps
the terminal by a few pixels and sits out of line with its own address bar.

## Summary

T2580 filmed `scripts/resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Drags right-shrink` (ReleaseSafe
`zig-out-rsafe`, `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`, films `%TEMP%	2580-*`). Read with the
**bar** as the reference (`right=` measures the bar, `gap=` is measured off the
PAGE): on the first frame that shows each new window width, the page is at the
new geometry, and the bar and terminal are at the previous one (`gap=9 right=2`,
28/49 frames). That held under every A/B:

- **page bounds set last** (after the terminal present and the bar paint, at
  the end of `layoutSplits`): unchanged - the page still lands with the frame.
- **terminal `Present` at sync interval 0** instead of 1: unchanged.
- **shrink pre-layout** (`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_PRELAYOUT_SHRINK=1`, kept as a knob):
  the 12 ms pass presents before the frame change, so content lands a compose
  EARLY (`right=26` frames, desk strip) and the page earlier still
  (`gap=9` frames where the terminal and bar are correct and the page is a
  step ahead). 31-36 bad frames.

So the terminal (DComp swap-chain visual on its child HWND) and the bar (a
child HWND in the viewer) share a fixed one-compose lag behind the top-level
frame change and the WebView2 visual, and the lag does not depend on when in
the pass they present. Common factor to test first: both are geometry carried
by child-HWND moves (`DeferWindowPos` / `SetWindowPos`) - DWM may apply child
window position/size one compose after the top-level size it ships in
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED`. Experiments: move the terminal visual by a DComp offset
on the window's tree (no child move) for one step; or place the children with
`SWP_NOSENDCHANGING|SWP_DEFERERASE` before the frame change; time each with the
trace and frames.txt.
