# T2459 - Blades: under a divider or grip drag the cards that stay open hold their content size and are clipped by the card, resizing once when the motion settles (or after a short pause), not on every pointer step (T2457 drag debounce)

## Summary

User follow-up on T2457, 2026-10-29: under a drag every pointer step moves the target width, and a `resize` slide (pane_stack_slide.rectOf with resize=true) resizes each neighbour terminal - a ConPTY reflow - on every step. Mac holds content at the larger of its current target and its size when the glide began (aFastPullKeepsTheGlideOnTheHand) and resizes once at settle. Do the same: hold, clip to the card, resize on settle or after a quiet period. Close with a count of terminal resizes per drag: about 1 per pane per settle.

## In plain terms

Dragging the line between two panes used to make every terminal next to it
re-wrap its text and redraw on almost every mouse movement - a flickering
storm in Claude Code or an editor, and wasted work. Now each terminal keeps
its text laid out as it was while your hand moves, and re-wraps once: when
you let go, or when you pause for a moment mid-drag. The same holds while a
pane folds into its blade under the drag.

## Goals

- [x] Under a divider or grip drag, and the fold/unfold glide it starts, terminals on show keep their grid; their windows still follow the hand
- [x] One resize per pane when the motion settles; a 150 ms rest mid-drag applies the size too
- [x] Nothing can stay held: the drag's end, the glide's end and a sweep on the rest clock all release
- [ ] Growing side: wait (built) vs fill as you drag (Mac) - decision D105

## Validation criteria

- [x] A 40-move divider drag over a prompt is 80 resizes, every one decided `held`, 2 applied (one per pane), never a blank prompt - prompt-reflow.ps1, all four sections (cmd/pwsh x local/agent), ALL PASS 80; the same drag with a program running: 2 applied. -NegativeControl goes red (12 failures). Window-edge resizes still reflow a prompt live (section 4 unchanged, green).
- [x] The program in the pane sees a 3.1 s drag as 1 resize, at the true final size; a rest mid-drag with the button still down delivers one resize DURING the rest, and the drag as a whole 2 - resize-debounce.ps1 B/B2/D, ALL PASS 12; -NegativeControl red on B and B2.
- [x] Blades: a fold the drag starts (glide stretched to 2 s) widens P through 28 window widths while its terminal sees 100 resizes held and 2 applied - stack-fold-slide.ps1 F18, flat ALL PASS 41, elevated ALL PASS 37 (106 held, 2 applied).
- [x] Pure rule: drag_hold.zig (wanted/holds/quietReleases, plus a 100-step model applying exactly once per settle) - none lane.
- [x] Floor: lib, none, win32, agent lanes PASS (floor-lane -Lane all).
