# T2648 - Dragging a divider between two bannered terminals tears the pink bar for a frame: on the banner rows the bar is still at its old x while the rows below have moved (4 frames per drag, NoViewer scene, after T2642's banner ordering)

## Summary

Found working T2642 (2026-11-02, Debug, idle box). With T2642's banner ordering in, T2414's own scene (`test\win32\divider-flicker.ps1 -Live -NoViewer -Panes 1 -Orientation vertical`: two terminals, both bannered) still reads 4 BITTEN frames per drag, and none of them is a banner over the bar: on the banner rows the accent bar is drawn whole at its OLD x while every row below has it at the new one, for one composed frame (frame 530 of `temp\divider-flicker\20261102-093351-none`: mid 1053, banner rows 1061; 189, 261, 321 the same shape on the outbound half). So the bar's top segment, between the two banner strips, reaches the screen a frame after the rest of it.

Suspects: the band rows beside a banner are painted by a different paint than the rest of the bar (the strip faces / the desk's composed band above the grid, `paintMovedBands` clipping to the children as they stood), or a popup region (the strips' card corners, T1851) holds the old pixels there. Next: film one frame pair with the resize trace on and find which paint owns the bar's banner-row segment.

### 2026-11-03: static reading, not yet filmed

The banner strips are separate `WS_POPUP` layered windows, not children of
the desk, so they reach the screen in a different composition from the
band paint. `Window.placeOverlaysBeforeBands` (T2642) places a SHRINKING
strip before the band paint (plus one `DwmFlush`) and holds a GROWING strip
for `placeHeldBanners` after it. Shape 2 (banner rows a whole step behind
the mid rows, at the old x) is what both strips arriving one composition
late would show: the shrinking strip still covering the new x, the growing
strip not yet over the old one, with the desk's old bar segment between
them. If the film confirms it, the candidates are (a) a `DwmFlush` after the
held strips too, so a pass never ends with strips uncomposed, or (b)
making the strips draw from the same composition as the bands (desk-child
strips, or the bar's banner-row segment painted INTO the strip faces so it
moves with them). Unconfirmed: the live film is an
`@input-desktop-exception` the loop may not run while the user is present,
and the pass criterion needs T2647's tear count first (dep added).

**Re-scored offline with T2647's classifier** (`docs\design\t2647-rescore.ps1`
on `temp\divider-flicker\20261102-093351-none\1-vertical`, banner rows 113 and
128): of the four frames filed above, only **#321 and #530 are torn** (banner
rows a whole 5 px run 8 px from the mid row's). **#189 and #261 are banner
bites**: on the banner rows the old bar's columns are already desk colour and
the RIGHT strip still covers the new bar's columns. That is the shrinking strip
lagging, which T2642's later `DwmFlush` targets, and this run predates that fix
(09:33 vs b8c26c4f3). So the tear is 2 frames per drag on the outbound and
inbound halves, not 4. The baseline on the current build comes from T2672's
live run.

## In plain terms

While you drag the line between two panes that both have a banner, the top part of the pink line, beside the banners, jumps a moment after the rest of it, so the line looks broken for a split second.
