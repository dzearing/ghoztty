# T2648 - Dragging a divider between two bannered terminals tears the pink bar for a frame: on the banner rows the bar is still at its old x while the rows below have moved (4 frames per drag, NoViewer scene, after T2642's banner ordering)

## Summary

Found working T2642 (2026-11-02, Debug, idle box). With T2642's banner ordering in, T2414's own scene (`test\win32\divider-flicker.ps1 -Live -NoViewer -Panes 1 -Orientation vertical`: two terminals, both bannered) still reads 4 BITTEN frames per drag, and none of them is a banner over the bar: on the banner rows the accent bar is drawn whole at its OLD x while every row below has it at the new one, for one composed frame (frame 530 of `temp\divider-flicker\20261102-093351-none`: mid 1053, banner rows 1061; 189, 261, 321 the same shape on the outbound half). So the bar's top segment, between the two banner strips, reaches the screen a frame after the rest of it.

Suspects: the band rows beside a banner are painted by a different paint than the rest of the bar (the strip faces / the desk's composed band above the grid, `paintMovedBands` clipping to the children as they stood), or a popup region (the strips' card corners, T1851) holds the old pixels there. Next: film one frame pair with the resize trace on and find which paint owns the bar's banner-row segment.

## In plain terms

While you drag the line between two panes that both have a banner, the top part of the pink line, beside the banners, jumps a moment after the rest of it, so the line looks broken for a split second.
