# T3624 - Linux L11d-4: the flat pane style's 6px gutter with a 1px split-divider-color line down its middle

## Summary

Follow-up of T3566. Mac's flat style (pane-style.md) is not the stock edge-to-edge look: a real 6pt gutter (PaneLayout.Metrics.flatGap) with a 1px split-divider-color line down its middle, because the gutter is where the resize grip lives. GTK's flat style is still upstream's 1px GtkPaned separator in a fixed grey (style.css / style-dark.css) and ignores split-divider-color. Acceptance: pane-style.sh S12 measures a ~6px gap under flat and the line's colour follows split-divider-color.
