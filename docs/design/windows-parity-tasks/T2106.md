# T2106 - On glass, an unfocused pane's fade eases in and out over 250ms with focus, like Mac's FocusFade, instead of switching

## Summary

Mac 89848fd59 FocusFade: a dimmed glass card's content and sheet ease to dimmedOpacity over .easeOut(0.25s) when focus moves (opacity only, never the frame). Windows (T2086) switches: the desk wash's layered alpha and the card's faded sheet change in one step, while the focus glow beside it fades on fadeLevel. Owed: drive the wash alpha and the card's Sheet.fade off the same glow_t0_ms clock (fadeLevel) so the dim, the thin sheet's fade and the glow move together; the renderer's sheet tint stays a step change, as on Mac (tintStrength is not animated there).
