# T2457 - Blades: a pane folding into its blade clips and fades its content out without resizing it during the tween, and a blade expanding presizes its content while invisible, then glides open and fades it in - no content flash and no reflow in either direction

## Summary

User spec 2026-10-29 morning, on the installed 1.50.3, which has T2442's fade cap and the T2448/T2450 frame-cost fixes: "blades when minimized still flash their content on the blade while they animate." The user's design, which this task builds exactly:
COLLAPSE (pane -> blade): the terminal content is NOT resized during the tween. A resize mid-tween means a reflow, its overhead and a flash. The content keeps its pre-collapse size and pixels, is CLIPPED by the narrowing card (pinned to its far edge, per Mac), and its opacity FADES smoothly to 0 over the tween while the blade's title fades in. No frame shows the content at full strength on a narrow card, and no frame flashes. The terminal takes its blade-state size once, after the motion, while invisible (or never, per Mac's "a minimized pane is never resized").
EXPAND (blade -> pane): the reverse. The terminal is resized to its final size FIRST, while still invisible (opacity 0), and has presented a frame at that size before anything shows. Then the card animates out to its new width with the presized content clipped by the card, and the content fades in during the tween. No reflow and no resize after the motion starts.
This matches Mac (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md, Glides: "one resize per terminal, after the motion"; "A minimized pane is never resized"; "content fades out and title fades in as it narrows"). Today's Windows code fades the strip face, and T2442 capped the fade per frame. But the terminal's own GL surface is evidently still drawn at full opacity, or resized, during the tween. Find out which for both directions and replace the mechanism, don't patch it. The likely shape: during the tween the live child HWND is hidden and a snapshot of its last frame (collapse) or its presized first frame (expand) is composited by the card painter with clip + alpha. The live child is shown only once the tween ends and it is fully opaque at its final size. Or use per-surface alpha via DirectComposition if T2360's path is ready. It must hold at 60 fps on a 4K window (T2454's budget).
CLOSE CONDITION: a frame-by-frame capture on the RELEASE build (portable zip in temp, never the installed copy) of a collapse and an expand, at 1x and 4K. Content opacity is monotonic across the tween, with no frame where it jumps up (the flash). The terminal resize count during the tween is 0 (log or counter). Expand shows no content until the presized frame exists. No frame is over 16.7 ms. Attach the numbers.
