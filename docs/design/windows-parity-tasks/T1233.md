# T1233 - Zooming an image pane by hand is never exercised by a real gesture

## Summary

T1183 renders images in a viewer pane and decides every zoom in viewer_image.zig, which the none lane asserts exhaustively. What no harness on this box touches is the GESTURE path: a double-click, a ctrl+wheel notch and a touchpad pinch all die on the background test desktop (T233), so viewer-image.ps1 asserts the load-time fit and the reload and stops there. A regression that broke the wheel listener, the anchor arithmetic or the imageZoom bridge call would be invisible to every lane. Wanted: an interactive-desktop arm (the kb-actions.ps1 GrabForeground pattern) that double-clicks an image pane and reads the pane's own 'viewer image ... event=toggle' line back, plus a ctrl+wheel arm, with the usual positive control.

## In plain terms

Nothing tested what happens when you actually double-click or ctrl+scroll a
picture in a side pane. Once something did, it found three things wrong: the
first double-click on a big screenshot only nudged it instead of jumping to
actual size, a zoomed-in picture's left edge could not be scrolled into view,
and zooming from the fitted view drifted away from the spot under the mouse.
All three are fixed, and the test now runs on every change to that code.

## Goals

- [x] A double-click, a ctrl+wheel notch and the ctrl+= / ctrl+0 chords are
      driven into a real image pane by the acceptance suite, off the user's
      desktop.
- [x] The first double-click on a large picture goes to 100%, every time.
- [x] Every part of a zoomed picture can be scrolled to.
- [x] A zoom keeps the point under the pointer under the pointer, from the
      fitted view too.

## Decision (mechanism, this seat's call)

The task asked for an interactive-desktop arm (kb-actions GrabForeground).
That would take the user's focus, which go.md forbids. Instead section L of
`test\win32\viewer-image.ps1` drives the page over the DevTools protocol
(`lib\WebViewCdp.ps1`, T1702): `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` enters the
renderer's input pipeline, so the page's listeners see a real `dblclick` and a
real ctrl `wheel`. Everything from the listener on is product code. Only the
OS half (a touchpad driver turning a pinch into ctrl+wheel) is out of reach,
and the header says so. The chords go through `Send-TestViewerChord`, the same
posted-chord route section K's ctrl+C uses, so they exercise native
`imageZoomChord` -> `window.__viewer.imageZoom`.

## What the gestures found (fixed here)

1. **Stale best-fit.** The `<img>` sat at its intrinsic 4000x3000 behind
   `visibility:hidden` at `load`, so scrollbars were up and the viewport
   reported 694x488 instead of 710x503. Fit 0.2033 was computed for room the
   pane did not have once it fitted, and the first double-click read
   zoom != fit and toggled "to fit" (0.2096): a 3% nudge. Fix: `viewport()`
   reports the stage's whole box rather than `clientWidth`, and the stage
   scrolls nothing at fit (`overflow:hidden`, also before the first transform).
2. **Unreachable strip.** `.viewer-image-stage` had `justify-content` /
   `align-items: center`, which centers an OVERFLOWING picture too and puts
   half its overflow past the scroll origin (image left at -144 with
   scrollLeft 72). Fix: centering is the image's `margin:auto` alone, which
   collapses to zero on overflow.
3. **Anchor drift.** The anchor was stage-relative and ignored the fitted
   picture's centering margin. Fix: the anchor is the fraction of the picture
   under the pointer, and the scroll is corrected from the picture's
   re-measured box.

## Validation criteria

- [x] A double-click from fit goes to 100% with the pointed pixel kept, and a
      second one returns to fit. Verified: `viewer-image.ps1` L1/L6 (red on
      the old code: L1 read zoom=0.2096 fitting=True, the anchor drifted
      0.2821 -> 0.2886).
- [x] The toggle still toggles after a round trip, and best-fit is the same
      number at load and after a zoom. Verified: L7 (0.2097 both).
- [x] A ctrl+wheel notch zooms in and out, anchored. Verified: L2/L4 (L2 red
      on the old code: u 0.6062 -> 0.6799).
- [x] Controls: a plain wheel pans and decides no zoom; a sub-step ctrl+wheel
      decides none. Verified: L3.
- [x] ctrl+= and ctrl+0 zoom the PICTURE. Verified: L5.
- [x] Nothing else moved. Verified: viewer-image.ps1 ALL PASS (71),
      `-NegativeControl` exactly 2 failures, `floor-lane.ps1 -Lane all`.
