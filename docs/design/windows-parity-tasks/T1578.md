# T1578 - Count-bounded cross-thread waits outside src/remote and src/apprt/win32 were never swept

## In plain terms

Some tests wait for another part of the program to finish by trying a fixed
number of times instead of waiting for a fixed amount of time. On a busy
machine those tests fail for no reason. Two rounds of cleanup fixed every one
of them in the two areas we looked at - but nobody has looked anywhere else.

## Summary

T472 fixed `connection.zig`'s 100k-spin drain; T738 swept `src/remote/` and
`src/apprt/win32/` and converted the three that were left. The sweep was scoped
to those two trees because that is what T738's Where section named, so the rest
of `src/` - `src/os/`, `src/termio/`, `src/terminal/`, the build helpers - has
never been asked the question, and neither have the PowerShell harnesses in
`test/win32/`, where the same shape is a retry counter around a GUI wait.

The mechanical form of the question: a loop whose exit condition is an
iteration count, containing a `Thread.yield()`/`Thread.sleep` (or, in
PowerShell, a `Start-Sleep` inside a `for ($i = 0; $i -lt N; ...)`), waiting on
something another thread or process produces. A yield-based one is a real bug
(a yield costs no measurable time); a sleep-based one is a weaker version of
the same mistake, since the budget it implies is invisible at the call site.

Measure before building: if the sweep finds nothing, the deliverable is the
report saying so, and closing this card is the right outcome.

## Where

- `src/` minus the two trees T738 covered, and `test/win32/*.ps1`.
- The pattern to imitate: `TestDeadline` in `src/remote/connection.zig` and
  `test_util.liveness_ns`.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The sweep is run and its result recorded - every count-bounded
      cross-thread wait found is converted to a clock bound or justified in a
      comment saying why the count is the right bound.
- [ ] The lane(s) covering whatever was touched are green, repeated 3x if any
      conversion landed in a lane that has ever flaked.
