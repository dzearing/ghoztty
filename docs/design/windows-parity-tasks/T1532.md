# T1532 - A dragged pane can land on another tab or in another window

## Summary

Cross-tab and cross-window drops: the tab strip makes a new tab at the index under the pointer, long-hovering a tab button for 500ms switches to it so the drag can continue into its layout, dropping over another window's panes lands there, and releasing over nothing makes a new window. Session safety is the sharp edge - a relocated pane must not be read as closed and lose its agent session (Mac's PaneMoveCoordinator.finishRelocation).
