# T2536 - Composition present: a pane that grows during a drag shows its newly exposed strip pure black on ~30 frames of a chain-fold (every grow tick of the pane left of the grabbed divider), against 0-1 frames on the WGL path - must be fixed before T2514 makes it the default

## Summary

Found by T2485's D18 A/B (2026-10-30 ~21:00, release ReleaseFast +71c6c2c25 in
temp\t2485-rel, real pointer, idle box, GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring, the T2485
chain-fold scenario: 6 panes, first divider dragged 3.4 pane widths right).
`blackcols.py <run> 60 450` (pure-black columns, max channel <= 6 on >60% of
rows) per run:

| run | GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT | frames with a black column |
|---|---|---|
| run42 | off | 1 (f140) |
| run43 | 1 | 35 |
| run44 | off | 0 |
| run45 | 1 | 27 |
| run46 | off | 1 (f140, 2 px, a frame composed mid-batch) |

The comp-path columns are the D18 shape on nearly every grow tick: the
rightmost 3-14 px of the pane that is GROWING (pane 1 during fold 1, e.g.
run43 f102 x408-413 = pane 1's new rect 91+324 against 91+318 the tick
before; again f262-f296 as pane 1 fills behind the folding pane 2), inside
the pane's NEW rect. Since D16 the window paints desk under every layered
pane, so pure black there is the pane's own surface: the window has its new
size and the composition visual's swap chain still has the old one (it is
resized on the renderer thread at its next frame). The WGL path waits for a
frame at the new size before the batch lands (T1393 `waits=1`); the comp path
evidently does not cover that gap. Frames: temp\t2485\run43, run45 (not
committed; montage strips can be cut with the T2485 tools).

## In plain terms

There is an alternative, newer way of drawing terminal panes that is about to
become the default. With it on, dragging a divider makes the edge of the pane
that is getting wider flash black on most frames of the drag. It has to stop
doing that before it can be switched on for everyone.

## Fix direction (not yet verified)

- The pane's GDI surface sits UNDER the composition visual, so under the comp
  path filling it with the terminal background on erase cannot flash (the
  T1031 reason to skip the fill does not apply): newly exposed strip shows
  background instead of black. Check whether an erase is even processed before
  DWM composes the resized window inside the batch.
- Or have the comp path honour the same new-size wait as WGL (present at the
  new size before EndDeferWindowPos returns), or size the swap chain / visual
  clip so the visual covers the new rect immediately.
