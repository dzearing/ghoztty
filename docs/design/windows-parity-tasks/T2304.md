# T2304 - Windows pane sidebar reorder has no motion: rows do not slide to open a gap (Mac 0.2s bounded shift), the dragged row is not lifted and does not follow the pointer vertically, and it does not glide to where it lands - T2275 part 3

## Summary

Mac e77422577 PaneSidebarOrder.shift (0.2s bounded, none once the drag ends; each list holds a row of room at its end for the whole drag) and 543489bca: the dragged row IS the row, following the pointer vertically only, lifted over a solid backing, gliding to where it lands. The pure shift rule lands in T2302 (pane_sidebar_order.shift); this is the paint/animation half.

## In plain terms

When you drag a pane's row up or down the sidebar today, the row stays put
and a thin line marks where it would land. On Mac the row itself comes with
your pointer, lifted over the list with a shadow, and the other rows slide
apart to open a gap where it will land; let go and it glides into place. This
brings that motion to Windows, in the expanded list and on the rail.

## Goals

- [x] the rows slide (0.2s, Mac's curve) to open a gap where the dragged pane would land and close the one it left, the tray's hairline sliding with them
- [x] the dragged row IS the row: it follows the pointer up and down only, kept inside the bar, lifted over a solid backing with a shadow
- [x] let go, it glides (0.22s) to where it landed while the lift fades; cancelled, it glides back
- [x] no caret over the list or a non-empty tray - the gap is the indicator (the empty tray's well still lights)
