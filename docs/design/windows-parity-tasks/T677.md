# T677 - Banner collapse: cross-fade the body the way Mac does, instead of a clipped reveal

## Summary

T149 landed the collapse/expand ANIMATION on win32: the card resizes with the
same easeInOut over 180ms Mac uses, while the terminal inset snaps in one
step. Mac's `89465f320` does one more thing in parallel — the body content
**cross-fades** (SwiftUI's default insertion/removal transition), so the text
below the first line dissolves rather than being uncovered.

win32 instead does a **clipped reveal**: the content is drawn in full and
clipped to the animating card, with the collapse fade (the existing
alpha-ramp of the card fill, `paintCollapseFade`) softening the cut edge. The
motion is right and the edge is soft, but the body's own opacity never
changes, so a line at the top of the clipped region stays at full strength
until the edge reaches it.

Doing it Mac's way on GDI means rendering the content into an offscreen
32bpp DIB pre-filled with the card fill and `AlphaBlend`-ing that onto the
card at a constant alpha — blending "card fill + text" over "card fill"
yields the text at that alpha exactly. The pieces are all present
(`buildCardSurface` already builds a DIB section; `paintCollapseFade`
already blends one), but `renderContent` also builds the link hit rects as a
side effect and those would need to come from the settled pass, not the
faded one.

## In plain terms

When a banner folds up, the text now slides away behind a soft edge. On the
Mac it also fades out as it goes. The difference is small and only visible
for about a fifth of a second, but it is a difference.
