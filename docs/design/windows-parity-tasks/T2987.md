# T2987 - PA-W03: every chrome paint redraws the whole caption and tab strip, allocating two full-width DIBs and re-measuring every tab title, whatever was invalidated (Window.zig:18978)

## Summary

PA-W03 (audit-1, T2960). Window.zig:18978-18979 calls paintCaption and paintTabBar on every chrome paint without consulting the clip. Each allocates a CreateCompatibleDC + full-width CreateDIBSection (:18619/:18633, :19207/:19227); paintTabBar also measures every tab title with GetTextExtentPoint32W, composites every tab shape per pixel and creates a region per tab (:19462). Whole-window invalidations that touch neither band (strip hover, PA-W04) and tab-label changes (invalidateTabBar, right = 10000, which also repaints the caption) pay all of it. Estimated: two DIB allocs + N text measurements + ~client_w x 76 px of fill per paint, 10x/s under a spinner title. Fix: skip each painter when GetClipBox misses its band; keep both DIBs across paints (grow-only); cache measured widths per title and font. Risk low; mind the merged-chrome seam and captionBandTarget.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] a paint whose clip misses the caption band does not call paintCaption (and likewise the strip) - counter or trace
- [ ] the caption and strip DIBs are allocated once per size, not per paint
- [ ] existing chrome acceptance (caption/tab strip pixel scripts) stay green
