From T3191. single-window-mode.ps1 B ends every launch with Stop-Process, so Window.deinit of a parked workspace (hwnd null, panes under the park host) at a real quit never runs in a test; and on the presenter path (opt-in, T2610) surrenderWindow -> releaseWindowBound destroys the window's presenter while the surrendering workspace's pane contexts are in its share group.
