# T2740 - Composition step 4d-1: one card's drop shadow rendered alone, as a premultiplied picture the size of the card plus its reach, that composites over a shadowless desk to the pixels the desk compose bakes today

## Summary

First split of T2511: the pure picture the later steps put on the compositor; shadow_layer.zig, unit-tested against pane_elevation's compose.

## In plain terms

Every pane card throws a soft shadow onto the coloured desk around it. Today
that shadow is painted into the desk picture itself, so whenever a card slides
the whole desk around it has to be repainted. This step makes each card's
shadow a little picture of its own that can be laid over a shadow-free desk
and look the same. Nothing changes on screen yet; it is the piece the next
steps hand to the system compositor so a shadow can travel with its card.

## Goals

- [x] One card's shadow can be drawn on its own, as a see-through picture the size of the card plus how far its shadow reaches
- [x] Laid over the desk without shadows, it gives the pixels the desk draws today
- [x] The picture depends only on the card's size and shadow strength, never on where the card is, so a card that only moves never needs a new one

## Design (2026-11-06)

The desk's shadow only ever scales the pixel under it (`pane_elevation.darken`,
a keep factor `k` per pixel). Premultiplied black at alpha `1 - k` laid over a
pixel is exactly that scale, so: lay the card's shadows over white with the
same `paintShadows` the desk uses (new `pane_elevation.paintCardShadows`), read
`k` back out of a channel, and the layer is `(255 - g) << 24`. Overlapping
shadows multiply their keeps on the desk, and layered visuals do the same.
`src/apprt/win32/shadow_layer.zig`: `render` (null for a card that casts
nothing), `Layer.origin` (the card's top-left minus the reach), `Key` (width,
height, share, scale, light, glass - everything the pixels depend on), and
`over`/`composite` (the compositor's premultiplied blend, for the tests).

Under the card the layer is cleared - wholly inside its straight sides, by
coverage along the antialiased edge and corners - because the desk compose lays
every fill over every shadow, so no shadow ever darkened a card's own pixels.

Findings carried to the next steps:

- **The antialiased edge differs by up to 9 levels on a near-white card.** The
  baked compose darkens only the desk share of an edge pixel; a layer over the
  desk darkens the fill share too. Bound: `fill * cov * (1 - cov) * alpha`.
  One-pixel ring, tested at <= 12.
- **A neighbour's rim.** On the desk one card's shadow never lands on another
  card (fills go down after all shadows). Each card's layer under its own pane
  visual would darken a neighbour's rim and edge where the shadow reaches
  across the gap - T2741 has to keep a layer off other cards (all shadow
  visuals below the desk's card pixels is not possible, since the rims are
  desk pixels), e.g. by clipping each layer away from its neighbours or moving
  the rims up with the pane visuals. Recorded on T2741.
- **A fading share can be a visual opacity.** `render(share s)` vs
  `render(1)` at opacity `s`: worst alpha gap 4 levels (solid dark), 1 level
  (solid light, glass dark/light), over s = 0.1..0.9 - measured with a probe
  of `render`. So a card gliding into its strip can fade one layer on the
  compositor instead of re-rendering per frame.

## Validation criteria

- [x] The layer over the shadowless desk matches the baked compose: desk pixels <= 3 levels, card interior exact, the antialiased edge <= 12 - dark solid at 1.5x with a share of 0.55, light solid, and dark glass at 2x (`zig test src/apprt/win32/shadow_layer.zig`, 6/6)
- [x] Negative control: without the layer the same comparison is more than 20 levels off (in the first test)
- [x] Overlapping shadows across a narrow gap multiply on the desk (<= 4 levels)
- [x] The picture is the same at any position and changes with width or share (`Key`); a card with no share renders nothing
- [x] Clear under the card, stronger below it than above, gone at the reach, premultiplied black only
- [x] floor-lane -Lane all: lib, none, win32, agent PASS (2026-11-06 01:44, side by side 1058s)
