# T2186 - Folding panes by dragging a divider jumps in one janky step instead of sliding smoothly, as on Mac

## Summary

User report 2026-10-23: resizing panes down until they collapse into strips is 'a huge janky jump, not a smooth transition'. Mac animates the fold (the carousel slide, one resize per slide - T2102 on Windows covers focus/unfold slides). Make a drag-collapse fold animate: the folding pane narrows into its strip and the neighbours glide, with no program resized mid-slide, matching the T2102 slide timing. Also check the unfold-on-drag-back path (T2124). Acceptance: a frame-sampled capture during a drag-collapse shows intermediate widths (no single-frame jump larger than one slide step), and the final layout is unchanged from today's.

## In plain terms

Squeezing a pane until it folds into a thin strip used to happen in one ugly
step: the pane vanished, the neighbours sat still for a moment with a blank
gap beside them, then snapped to their new sizes. Now the folding pane slides
away under its neighbour to the edge of the window, the other panes grow
smoothly into the room it leaves, and dragging back slides it out again - the
way it moves on Mac.

## Goals

- [x] the folding pane slides to its strip at the edge, and is hidden only when it gets there
- [x] the panes left on show grow (or shrink) through every width, instead of holding and snapping
- [x] the folding pane passes under the panes on show, not across them
- [x] while the drag goes on, the slide heads for where the hand has the layout now
- [x] the resting layout is unchanged from T2124's

## Design

Root cause: `pane_stack_slide.plan` slid only the cards on show afterwards
(the folding card was hidden at once), and `at` held every card at its old
size until the slide's end (T2102's click rule, so a ConPTY is not resized
mid-slide). Under a drag that meant a frozen layout with a hole in it for
200 ms, then a 490 -> 1000 px snap.

Mac (`PaneStageView`, `gestureSlideDuration` 0.2 s) gives each card its new
shape at once and slides every card - the tucking one included - on Core
Animation, with the content frozen. A Windows pane IS its child window, so a
card's shape and its terminal's size cannot differ. The translation:

- **A card going behind the run slides too** (`plan`): from where it was on
  show to its tucked rect, at its held size (so it is only ever moved), shown
  until the slide ends. This applies to a strip click's slide as well, which
  is the departing-card half of T2131.
- **A drag's slide is a resize slide** (`Slide.resize`, set from
  `Window.stackGesturing`): every edge moves on the curve, so cards pass
  through each width. A divider drag resizes the panes on every mouse move
  anyway, so a few more resizes in its 200 ms are the drag's own cost, not a
  new one. A click's slide keeps T2102's hold-then-resize-once rule.
- **Targets follow the hand** (`Slide.retarget`): a layout pass with no run
  change while a slide runs re-points each sliding card at its current
  placement.
- **Z-order follows the layout's paint order** at a slide's start
  (`Window.orderStackCards`, from `pane_stack_layout.paintOrder`): the run
  above, tucked cards lower the farther out they are, so the folding card
  passes under its neighbour. Only the panes are reordered among themselves.
  This is T2132's subject; closed with this commit.
