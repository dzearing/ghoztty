# T2870 - After a stall mid-glide the leading blade vanishes for ~360 ms before it lands: the strip face's declared span ends before the window's delayed glide does, and the desk paints nothing there until it lands (comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 -Arms strip .22c)

## Summary

Found by T2851's film (same setup as T2869: the pane sidebar expanding under GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLIDE_STALL_MS=500, the first of five panes a blade). The blade's face is declared with span_ms=1104 at ~290 ms and reaches its landing (frame x 330) on time; then from f140 (~1400 ms) to f183 (~1758 ms), 44 frames and 358 ms, there is NO blade at 330 - the next blade (the pane that folded, at 376) is the leftmost on screen - until the window's own glide, 500 ms late because of the stall, lands at ~1767 ms and the desk paints the strip there. Log: `stack strip faces retired after the desk paint n=1` around then. So once the face has caught up it is hidden (or dropped) while the desk still paints only `StripFace.still` (empty: the blade moves further than its width) or paints the strip where the window thread's lagging model has it - under the sidebar column, out of sight. Either way nothing stands at the landing. Fix shape: keep the face up until the desk paints the strip at its landing, or have `deskStripTab` paint the landing once the face's declared span is over.

Evidence: `powershell -NoProfile -File scripts\comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 -Arms strip -KeepFrames`, .22c (`once at its landing the leading blade stays on screen (44 frame(s) without it, f140-f183, 358 ms)`). With -StallMs 0 .22c passes, so it only shows when the thread was busy mid-glide.

## In plain terms

When the app is briefly busy while the pane sidebar opens, the thin folded pane at the edge of the grid slides to its new place and then disappears for about a third of a second before popping back. It should stay put once it arrives.

## Goals

- [x] find what takes the blade off screen between its landing and the glide's end
- [x] keep the blade on screen from its landing until the glide is over
- [x] a knob that brings the old behaviour back, so the film can show it goes red without the fix

## Root cause

The blade's face was dropped on the window thread's tick where its (late) model caught up, after the desk had painted the strip at the landing - the T2485 order. But in the composition pane tree a gliding card's CHILD WINDOW stays where the glide found it until the glide lands (T2571); only its visual moves. The pane folding past the blade still had its window at 148..444, over the desk and under the face layer, so the desk's freshly painted strip at 330..375 (traced: painted and uploaded, `rs=1`, on that very pass) was behind that window's background until the landing moved it. With no stall the blade's catch-up and the landing nearly coincide, which is why it only showed after a hitch.

Fix: `declareMovingStrip` keeps a caught-up strip's declared face - held at its landing by the compositor - while `Slide.cardSliding()` (any card of the glide still on its way); the landing pass (`stack_slide` null) retires it after the desk paint as before. A resting glide (drag held) has every card landed, so it never holds. `GHOZTTY_STRIP_FACE_HOLD=0` / the film's `-NoFaceHold` restores the old drop.
