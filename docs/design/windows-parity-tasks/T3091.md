# T3091 - A pane on a background tab still holds its full-size composition swap chain after T2975: shrink the chain while the tab is behind, without a blank frame on reveal

## Summary

T2975 frees a background-tab pane's render targets and the GL->D3D staging texture, but keeps the composition swap chain (gl_comp_present Presenter.resizeSwap never shrinks: sharedCapacity only grows), roughly two window-sized back buffers per pane. It was kept on purpose: its last image is what a reveal shows until the redraw lands, so a shrink must be paired with a reveal that waits for the first full frame (layoutSplitsLive's discrete wait, T2738) and proved with a pixel oracle. Measure in hidden-window-memory.ps1 section T.
