# T2624 - With no terminal content at all, a window-edge drag step still costs 7-9 ms in ReleaseFast (place 5.3-6.3 ms: EndDeferWindowPos of the pane children + strip faces + card region cuts; paint 0.8-1.8 ms): find which of those owns it and cut it, re-measuring on the T2610 presenter path where panes stop being GL children

## In plain terms

Even after the terminals stop resizing during a window drag (T2623), each
step of the drag still takes 7-9 ms just to move the panes' windows and
repaint the area around them. This task finds which part of that is slow and
makes it cheaper, so the window edge can keep up with a fast mouse.

## Summary

T2609 step 1's `empty` arm (`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_NO_CONTENT=empty`: a pane's
WM_SIZE does nothing) is the floor of an edge-drag step. ReleaseFast, run
20261101-150819 (`edge-drag-rate.ps1 -ContentArms -ReleaseSandbox`):

- row3 grow 8.9 ms = place 6.0 + paint 1.8; shrink 7.6 ms = place 5.8 + paint 0.9
- viewer grow 8.7 ms = place 6.3 + paint 1.5; shrink 7.0 ms = place 5.3 + paint 0.8

"place" (`frame_place_us`, `Window.zig` around `endPaneBatchFilled`) covers
the layout of the stack grid, `EndDeferWindowPos` for every pane child,
`commitStripFacesEarly`, `cutCardRegions` and `clearVacatedPaneStrips`. The
Debug `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE` stamps of one step (row3/empty, 20261101-145656)
put about 1.7 ms in the batch, about 0.7 ms in the region cut (each pane gets
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` a second time), and about 7 ms in
`clearVacatedPaneStrips`' desk paint. Debug's pixel loops inflate that last
one, so re-stamp in ReleaseFast before choosing.

Re-measure on `GHOZTTY_WINDOW_PRESENTER` (T2610/T2614) as well: once the
panes are no longer GL children, the batch may cost something else.

## Result (2026-11-06)

The 7-9 ms floor in the Summary is gone already. On HEAD 2f4061947 the `empty`
arm is 4.4-5.2 ms a step and the shipped `held` arm is 5.3-8.5 ms (ReleaseFast,
four runs). Every stage below is a median per step, read from the
`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE` ring (stamps ~1 us since T2600) with the new
`edge-drag-rate.ps1 -Trace` switch.

| stage (stamps) | row3 held | row3 empty | viewer held | row3 held, presenter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| region cut (`faces-end`->`cut-end`) | **1.15-1.9 ms** (mean 2.3-2.75) | 0.67 | 0.93 | 0.40 |
| pane batch (`batch-begin`->`defer-end`) | 0.6-0.85 (mean 1.8-2.0) | 0.74 | **2.1** | 0.66 |
| vacated desk paint (`vacated-dc`->`-painted`) | 0.85-0.99 | 0.89 | 0.73 | **0.73** |
| band paint (`gutters-end`->`paint-end`) | 0.32-0.41 | 0.38 | 0.36 | 0.36 |
| whole step (`mean_us`) | 6.2-7.7 ms | 3.8-4.6 | 4.8-7.1 | **3.4-4.2** |

**The owning stage on the shipped path is the region cut. Its cost is the GL
driver, not our code.** `pane-poschanged` (the first line of the pane's
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED`) does not arrive until that pane's renderer has passed
`synced`. `SetWindowRgn` blocks inside user32 while the renderer thread is
between `target-ready` and `synced`, which is its GL buffer and texture
uploads. Seen in pass after pass, and one wait was 10 ms. The `empty` arm wakes
no renderer, and its cut is 0.67 ms.

**Tried and reverted:** running the held resizes (`endPaneBatchFilled`'s
`applyResize`, which wakes each renderer) after the cut instead of before it.
Over two alternating A/B pairs it made no difference: new 6.9-7.7 ms against
old 6.2-7.4 ms on row3, with the cut still 1.5 ms. A renderer woken by the
previous step is still uploading when the next step's cut runs, so the order
inside one pass cannot remove the wait.

**The cut that removes it is T2610's window presenter.** With
`GHOZTTY_WINDOW_PRESENTER=1 GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT=0` the pane children carry no
GL context. The cut falls to 0.40 ms and a three-terminal held step to
3.4-4.2 ms (240-290 fps), from 6.2-7.7 ms (run 20261106-191029). T2610 is the
P0 that ships it, parked on its live run. This measurement is now on it as
evidence. What is left after it is filed: the viewer's 2.0 ms batch (T2773) and
the 0.7-1.0 ms vacated desk paint on every path (T2774).
