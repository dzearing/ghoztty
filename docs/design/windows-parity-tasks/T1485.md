# T1485 - While the loop is blocked by a quota, the watchdog should retry at the instant it clears instead of every 20 minutes regardless

## In plain terms

When the loop is stopped by a quota, it keeps trying every twenty minutes even
though the message it gets back says exactly when the quota lifts. It should
wait for that moment and try then, instead of knocking on a door it has been
told is locked until one in the morning.

## Summary

T1483 taught both supervisors to read the blocker, including
`quotaLimits.resetsAt` — the exact instant the quota clears (on 2026-09-09..12
that was 2026-09-12 01:00, and the loop resumed at 01:11, eleven minutes of pure
polling latency after a fact that had been known for 63 hours).

The retry cadence still ignores it: `-RearmMinutes` (20) governs every re-entry
equally, so a blocked loop produced 180 typings that could only ever fail, and
then recovered up to 20 minutes late anyway.

Shape of the fix:

- While `Resolve-LoopBlocker` reports `usage-limit` with a `ResetsAt` in the
  future, hold the nudge and schedule the next re-entry for that instant (plus a
  small margin), rather than every `-RearmMinutes`.
- Keep an unconditional floor — a quota message can be wrong, and a `ResetsAt`
  in the past or unparseable must fall straight back to today's cadence.
- `api-error` keeps the current cadence: a 5xx has no reset time and retrying
  IS the remedy.

Worth doing after T1484: the visible surface matters more than the polling
arithmetic, and this one is latency rather than outage.

## Goals

- [x] A quota that names its reset holds the watchdog's re-entries until then
- [x] The retry goes the moment the reset passes, not at the next 20-minute slot
      or the next 5-minute poll
- [x] Anything uncertain (past, missing, unparseable reset; a 5xx) keeps today's
      cadence, and a hold never outlasts a 6-hour floor

## Validation criteria

- [x] A blocked fixture with a future `ResetsAt` produces no nudge before that
      instant and one promptly after it. - CC61 (`ACTION hold`, no re-entry) and
      CC64 (reset 10 min ago, last nudge 15 min ago: `QUOTA CLEARED` re-enters
      although the 20m rearm has not elapsed), `test\win32\go-loop-guard.ps1`.
- [x] A `ResetsAt` in the past, missing, or unparseable falls back to
      `-RearmMinutes`, proven by a negative-control arm. - CC55/CC56/CC57 (pure)
      and CC63 (watchdog: a past reset under the same seed as CC61 re-enters).
- [x] An `api-error` blocker is unaffected and keeps retrying on the normal
      cadence. - CC58 (pure) and CC65 (watchdog says `rearm not elapsed`, no
      QUOTA line).
- [x] Acceptance arms in section CC of `test\win32\go-loop-guard.ps1`. - CC52-CC66,
      577 assertions ALL PASS on 2026-11-14.

## How it was built

- `Resolve-LoopQuotaHold` (`scripts\loop-session.ps1`) is the pure decision:
  `hold` (until reset + `-QuotaMarginMinutes` 2), `retry` (reset passed, nothing
  tried since: the rearm is waived), or `cadence` (everything else). It parses
  only the machine-readable `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm` the epoch produces; the prose
  fallback ("Sep 12, 1am") is left to the cadence on purpose.
- The floor: once `-QuotaFloorHours` (6) pass since the last re-entry, a hold
  yields to the cadence, in case the quota message is wrong (CC59).
- `go-loop-watchdog.ps1` returns a new `hold` action, records
  `recovery_hold_until` in its state, and its daemon loop sleeps to the reset
  instant when that is sooner than the next `-PollSeconds` poll, so recovery is
  about two minutes after the reset instead of up to 25.
- `-Force` still re-enters over a hold (CC66).
