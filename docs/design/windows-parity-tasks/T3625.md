## Summary

Follow-up of T3567 (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md 'Glides'). The GTK stage (SplitTree.placeStage) places every card and strip where the shared layout puts it at once: a strip click, a drag or a split jumps. Port PaneStageView.glide: retarget an eased position per frame on a GTK tick callback (gtk_widget_add_tick_callback on the stage), hold a gliding card's content at its size until the glide ends, cross-fade content/title by collapse width (PaneCardView.collapse, last 120pt), and the 50ms isGesturing tail after release. Also the paint order by Leaf.elevation and the blade tuck/shadows (strips are faces today, trimmed so they never overlap). Acceptance: a pane-stack seam --frames sampler or capture series showing intermediate positions and one resize per terminal.
