# T3625 - Linux L11e-2: stacked-panes glides on GTK - every card and strip eases toward where the layout puts it (90% in 0.15s), panes narrow into strips with content fading out and the title in, one resize per terminal after the motion

## Summary

Follow-up of T3567 (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md 'Glides'). The GTK stage (SplitTree.placeStage) places every card and strip where the shared layout puts it at once: a strip click, a drag or a split jumps. Port PaneStageView.glide: retarget an eased position per frame on a GTK tick callback (gtk_widget_add_tick_callback on the stage), hold a gliding card's content at its size until the glide ends, cross-fade content/title by collapse width (PaneCardView.collapse, last 120pt), and the 50ms isGesturing tail after release. Also the paint order by Leaf.elevation and the blade tuck/shadows (strips are faces today, trimmed so they never overlap). Acceptance: a pane-stack seam --frames sampler or capture series showing intermediate positions and one resize per terminal.

**As landed (2026-11-25):** everything above except the blade tuck and
shadows, split out to T3729. The motion is win32's pure follower
(`pane_stack_slide`, Mac's 45ms time constant: about 90% in 0.1s, as Mac
`PaneStageView.glideTime` is today), driven by a tick callback on the
`SplitTree` rather than the stage, because a split or a close rebuilds the
stage and its glide has to carry across. The task's "--frames" sampler is a
Python poller over the existing `pane-stack` seam, which now reports
`drawn`/`gliding`/`fade`/`content_w`/`resizes`.

## In plain terms

On Linux, clicking a folded pane's strip, closing a pane or dragging one shut
used to make every pane jump to its new place in one frame. Now the panes
slide there together, the way they do on the Mac: a pane folding away narrows
into its strip as its text fades and its name fades in, and the terminals
inside are not resized until the motion is over, so nothing reflows mid-slide.

## Goals

- [x] A strip click, a focus onto a minimized pane, a split, a close and a fold under a drag glide instead of jumping
- [x] A card narrows into its strip (and widens out of it) with its content held at its size and cut by the card
- [x] Content fades out and the strip's title fades in over the last 120px
- [x] Each terminal is resized at most once, after its card lands
- [x] The run paints above its strips (`Leaf.elevation`), so a card tucking away passes under the run
- [x] A drag's glide follows the hand and settles 50ms after the release
- [x] The blade tuck and shadows are filed (T3729)
