# T1990 - Panes show a hard white border on Windows: the focused card's rim and glow render as a crisp white outline, not Mac's faint backlight

## In plain terms

Every focused pane on Windows, most visibly a viewer pane (the dashboard in
the user's screenshots), has a bright white line around its rounded border.
On Mac the same focus cue is a barely visible backlight. The white outline
must go: no pane should show a hard white border.

## Summary

User reports (screenshots on 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-15): a viewer pane
showing `http://localhost:7788/#activity` has a crisp white rounded outline
on all four sides. The 2026-10-15 report followed five days with no task.
The controller session explained the cause on 2026-10-10, promised a task,
and never filed it. That miss is why this is P0 at order 0.

Cause, from reading the code (`src/apprt/win32/pane_elevation.zig`, T1834,
c4446ba8e):

- **The rim**: `RIM_DIP = 0.5`, but `rimPx` rounds up to a minimum of one
  whole physical pixel, so on Windows the hairline is 1-2 px instead of
  Mac's 0.5 pt Retina line. `rimAlpha(glass, light, focus)` brightens the
  focused card's rim to 16% (solid) or 20% (glass) white.
- **The glow**: `glowOpacity` gives 24% white on dark at the card's edge
  (`glowAlpha`, stroke 4 DIP, sigma 2). Its peak sits on the same pixel as
  the rim, so the two stack into a crisp white line.

Fix (Windows-only, no Mac change needed, since Mac reads as intended):

1. When the rim would round up past its DIP width, scale its alpha down so
   its total light matches Mac's 0.5 pt line (alpha x 0.5 DIP / drawn px).
   Do NOT brighten the rim for focus on Windows at all.
2. Start the glow OUTSIDE the rim (offset by `rimPx`), so its peak never
   lands on the rim pixel, and keep its peak low enough that no pixel just
   outside the card reads as a line.
3. Viewer panes: check whether the viewer host or WebView2 adds its own
   edge (a 1 px light border or the page's background showing at the
   rounded corners). The screenshot's line is uniform all the way round,
   including the corners.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Pixel oracle (test desktop, PrintWindow capture, never the composited
      screen): for a focused terminal pane AND a focused viewer pane in
      the elevated glass style, dark theme, at 100%, 150% and 200% DPI, the
      brightest pixel within 3 px of the card edge exceeds the backdrop
      just outside it by less than a set threshold (for example dL* < 8).
      A negative control with today's rim and glow fails it.
- [ ] The focus cue is still present: the focused card's surrounding
      backdrop is measurably lighter than an unfocused card's (a positive
      control), just not as a line.
- [ ] A side-by-side PNG of before and after, attached in the close-out.
