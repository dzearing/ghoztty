# T2541 - Composition step 4b follow-up: a translucent window's desk (background-opacity below 1) still blits through GDI under GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME - carry it as a premultiplied visual over cleared window pixels

## Summary

T2509 put the desk on the chrome host's bottom visual for OPAQUE windows only
(`Window.deskOnVisual`: `deskOpacity() == null`). On a translucent window the
desk's blit carries its own alpha (`PaneDesk.out`, `translucentSpan`), and the
visual composes over the window's redirected pixels, not the desktop: carrying
the desk there means uploading `out` premultiplied (DXGI_ALPHA_MODE_PREMULTIPLIED)
AND making sure the window pixels under it are fully transparent rather than
stale, or the old picture shows through the desk at `1 - opacity`. Mac's desk
takes `.opacity(backgroundOpacity)` on its own layer, so the visual is the
natural home. Hero tabs and rearrange-header bands also keep the desk on GDI
(GDI painters draw over it there); those lift with T2510's overlay, not here.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] `scripts\comp-desk-onscreen.ps1` grows a translucent arm (`--background-opacity=0.8`): the knob-on frame matches the GDI frame within rounding, over a known backdrop.
- [ ] The visual arm logs `chrome desk visual attached` on the translucent window, with no fallback.
