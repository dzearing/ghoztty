# T2253 - Windows split gutters show no accent bar and a drag can bring several stacked panes out at once; Mac's one gutter rule (main a81db68f9 + 5e4f30144)

## Summary

Mac a81db68f9: a row is a pure function of where its gutter is (PaneLayout.dragged); a drag only collapses panes and brings out at most one (a strip pulled by its gutter comes out alone, the other side tucks away), and dragged back the row is as it was. Draggable gutters show a 4pt accent bar (half strength on hover, full while dragged), one at a time, kept in the gutter, never lit after release (5e4f30144 fixed a late mouse-exit that left it lit - Windows must pass the same release-then-leave check). The pinned sidebar resizes from the gutter beside it. Changes in what is on show glide for drags AND clicks, retargeted per frame; a strip is the pane's own glass card at strip size, content fading out / title fading in as it narrows. Port to win32 split_geometry/PaneDesk/divider code.
