# T2253 - Windows split gutters show no accent bar and a drag can bring several stacked panes out at once; Mac's one gutter rule (main a81db68f9 + 5e4f30144)

## Summary

**Scope as built (2026-10-25):** the gutter accent bar - the part the user
named ("the missing resize gutter glow"). The rest of the original summary
below was split out: the one gutter rule -> T2263, glides for drags and
clicks + strips as glass cards -> T2264, the pinned sidebar / side panel edge
-> T2265, grip-bar acceptance -> T2266.

Original summary - Mac a81db68f9: a row is a pure function of where its gutter is (PaneLayout.dragged); a drag only collapses panes and brings out at most one (a strip pulled by its gutter comes out alone, the other side tucks away), and dragged back the row is as it was. Draggable gutters show a 4pt accent bar (half strength on hover, full while dragged), one at a time, kept in the gutter, never lit after release (5e4f30144 fixed a late mouse-exit that left it lit - Windows must pass the same release-then-leave check). The pinned sidebar resizes from the gutter beside it. Changes in what is on show glide for drags AND clicks, retargeted per frame; a strip is the pane's own glass card at strip size, content fading out / title fading in as it narrows. Port to win32 split_geometry/PaneDesk/divider code.

## In plain terms

The thin gaps between panes are what you drag to resize them, but on Windows
nothing showed that you could: hovering one only changed the mouse pointer.
Now, as on the Mac, a hovered gap shows a soft accent-colored bar down its
middle, the bar brightens while you drag, and it goes away the moment you let
go and move off.

## Goals

- [x] A hovered split gutter shows a 4 DIP accent bar with a soft glow at half strength
- [x] A dragged gutter shows the bar at full strength, one bar at a time
- [x] Letting go never leaves a bar lit (Mac 5e4f30144's release-then-leave)
- [x] Stack-grip gutters light the same way as split dividers
- [x] Both pane styles (elevated desk and flat line)
