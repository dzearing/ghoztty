# T2455 - After T2454 a fold glide at 1875x1125 still drops one frame in ~650 (a 33.5 ms gap against the 32.5 ms two-period line): find which tick owns it - the fold's whole-desk compose, a viewer resize, or a WM_PAINT - and remove it

## In plain terms

Dragging a divider so panes fold away now glides at a steady 59 frames a
second on an ordinary window, but about once per drag one frame still arrives
late, so the glide hitches once. This finds out which frame that is and makes
it as cheap as the rest.

## Summary

Split from T2454 (2026-10-29). After T2454 the release glide probe (5 panes in
a row, a viewer second, the first divider dragged 720 DIP out and back on the
background test desktop, `-ReleaseSandbox`) at 1875x1125 @1.25 reads, on three
steady runs: `late=1`, `max_gap_us` 33369-33687 against `period_us` ~16250
(the line is two periods, ~32.5 ms), `max_us` 14.7-24.0 ms, mean tick
5.8-6.1 ms, `wm_paints=1`.

Candidates, cheapest to check first:

- a **fold or unfold**: the card count changes, so `PaneDesk.Memo.dirty` returns
  `.full` and that frame composes and blits the whole desk (glass on: ~7 ms at
  4K in the T2454 bench, ~2 ms at this size) on top of the layout;
- a **viewer resize** (`viewer_ticks` ~20 per run, `viewer_us` ~57 ms total,
  so ~2.5 ms each on average and more at the worst);
- the one **WM_PAINT** (`wm_paint_us` ~1.2-4 ms).

The glide line only carries means and maxima; add the gap's own tick shape
(was it a full compose, a viewer tick, a WM_PAINT) to the stats first, so the
answer is read rather than inferred. If it is the fold frame, the change in
card count can be diffed too (match cards by key rather than index).

The probe is `glide-probe.ps1` in the T2450 session scratchpad (copied into
T2454's); T2451 is the harness that should own this shape permanently.

## What landed

- `drag_perf.GlideStats.record` takes a tick `Shape` (cost, its viewer
  resizes, whether it composed the whole desk, and its layout pass's phase
  timers plus the glide's own step and compositor flush) and a `Between`
  (WM_PAINTs and motion ticks since the last tick ended). The widest gap keeps
  the tick before it and what ran between; `Worst.owner()` names the largest
  piece (`tick`, `tick_full_desk`, `tick_viewer`, `wm_paint`, `motion`) and
  `idleUs()` the part no measured work accounts for (the clock's vblank wait).
- `Window.stackSlideTick` feeds it (`glideMark`/`glideBetween` diff running
  totals); `reportGlideCost` writes two more lines, `divider glide
  worst_gap_us=... owner=...` and `divider glide worst_tick step_us=...
  place_us=...`, and writes the main glide line at info whether or not the
  glide was smooth - a release run (which drops debug) could not read a
  `late=0` before.

## Finding

The late gap is owned by **the glide tick before it, by itself**: at 60 Hz
`worst_gap_us=33443` after a 21916 us tick, `before_desk_full=false`,
`before_viewer_us=0`, no WM_PAINT and no motion tick in between. None of the
three candidates. Inside the tick it is the layout pass's **place phase**
(14.6-22.1 ms of the worst tick): the pane batch's window moves (2.8-5.2 ms),
the card region cut (0.3-1.9 ms) and the vacated-strip desk fill (2.1-5.8 ms),
read from `GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring` stamps. The costly ticks cluster at the
start of each glide. Mid-turn the box's display went to 120 Hz (a Parsec
virtual display), where the same ticks run the glide at half rate
(`late=119-125`, `fps=106`). Both are T2732.
