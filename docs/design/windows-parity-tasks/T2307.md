# T2307 - Windows strips cast no shadow; Mac's blades cast a tight shadow on the next strip out that leans away from the run, stops at the strip's sides, and fades in with the collapse during the glide (Mac afc64dc53, 5a9a2e2a4 - shadow half of T2283)

## Summary

Split from T2283 (which landed the blade layout, the 12 DIP tuck, pill-cornered bands and the label centring). Mac PaneCardView: a stacked card's shadow mixes by collapse from the card's (radius 10, opacity 0.4 dark / 0.16 light) to the blade's (radius 5, opacity 0.6 / 0.26), offset 3pt AWAY from the run along the stack axis (stripLeads: leading strips lean left/up, trailing right/down), and the shadow mask narrows across the stack to the strip's own extent so it stops at the strip's sides. Acceptance: off-desktop pixel sample across a strip boundary showing the outer strip darker beside the inner one at rest, and a mid-glide shadow between card and blade strength.
