Mac PaneCardView: a stacked card's shadow mixes by collapse from the card's (radius 10, opacity 0.4 dark / 0.16 light) to the blade's (radius 5, opacity 0.6 / 0.26), offset 3pt AWAY from the run along the stack axis (stripLeads: leading strips lean left/up, trailing right/down), and the shadow mask narrows across the stack to the strip's own extent so it stops at the strip's sides. Acceptance: off-desktop pixel sample across a strip boundary showing the outer strip darker beside the inner one at rest, and a mid-glide shadow between card and blade strength.

## In plain terms

When several panes are folded into thin strips at the edge of a window, the
strips sat flat against each other in one undivided colour. Now each strip
casts a short shadow onto the next strip out, the way the Mac app draws them,
so a pile of strips reads as separate blades laid over one another. The
shadow also fades in smoothly as a pane narrows into its strip.

## Goals

- [x] at rest, each strip darkens the strip behind it, leaning away from the panes on show and stopping at the strip's own ends
- [x] while a pane glides into or out of its strip, its shadow moves from a card's to a blade's with nothing snapping at either end
