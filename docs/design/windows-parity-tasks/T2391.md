## Summary

Split out of T2389 (2026-10-28). `test\win32\lib\FrameLabCatalogue.ps1`
lists six interactions as `trigger` with `Driver = 'T2391'`, and every matrix
cell of theirs reads `Blocked: no driver yet (T2391)`: maximize-button,
blade-glide (sidebar collapse/expand), banner, hero, tab-switch, split-close.
They are not pointer gestures (or not only), so the input driver cannot play
them.

Build, per trigger: the action that fires it (an IPC verb - `+set-banner`,
`+split`, `+close` - or a key chord through the same SendInput path as
FrameLabInput, or a click on the maximize button located from the chrome's
own geometry), its fire time stamped on QPC beside FrameCapture's frame
slots, and what is tracked (blade-glide: the sidebar edge against the glide's
ease curve - FrameScore's `-Expected`; banner: the pane top; the rest: bleed
only). Then flip `Kind` to something the matrix runs, drop the Blocked
reason, and extend `test\win32\frame-lab-catalogue.ps1` with fake-driver
proofs for each trigger.
