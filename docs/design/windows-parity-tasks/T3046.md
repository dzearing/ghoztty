# T3046 - PA-V14: a viewer keeps its last hero or glide snapshot bitmap until the pane closes (ViewerPane.zig:5464)

## Summary

PA-V14 (audit-1, T2963). The snapshot DIB (ViewerPane.zig:5464-5468) is freed only in deinitHeroSnap from deinit (:5499-5504, :909). Cost (computed): ~5 MB at 1400x900, ~33 MB at 4K per viewer that ever glided; the viewer twin of PA-M01. Fix: free it when the glide or hero mode ends. Risk: a later glide waits for a fresh capture. Findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/V-findings.md.
