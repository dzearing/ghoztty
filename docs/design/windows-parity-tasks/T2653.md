# T2653 - A window-edge grow drag draws the terminal pane solid black on every filmed frame (knob off: 51 of 51 right-grow frames; it is green again at rest): the terminal stops presenting under the drag

## Summary

Found filming T2549 on HEAD 18627a0d4 (ReleaseSafe, scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Drags right-grow, no knobs): every frame shows the terminal card black (flat=451 on the scored row, %TEMP%\t2549-head-chrome-1122170043). With GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1 it shows on some runs (61 of 61 once, 0 another), and frame 0 shows it half-presented (green 451 px, black past it). The 2026-10-31 films had a green terminal, so it arrived after them: suspects 0a4f79a3a (T2623, terminals hold their size under a drag) and 3cdf6d4b8 (T2474, render-target memory given back). User-visible on the default path; T2516's resize sync cannot be judged while the terminal is black.

## In plain terms

A screen recording of a window being dragged wider showed the terminal as a black box on every frame, which looked like a bad new flash. It was not the app: the recordings were made while the monitor was asleep, and a terminal on a sleeping monitor never draws. Recorded again with the screen on, the very same build draws the terminal normally. The recording script now notices a sleeping screen and sets that run aside instead of reporting a fault.

## Goals

- [x] find out whether the black terminal is the app or the recording
- [x] if the recording: stop the film script from ever scoring such a run again

## Finding (2026-11-02, lane a)

**Box state, not a regression: every black film was taken on an occluded display.** All 13 T2549 film dirs from today (`%TEMP%\t2549-*`, knob on and off, right and left grow) carry FrameClock's `compositor clock wait failed (c01e0006)` line in the app's `stderr.txt` - STATUS_GRAPHICS_PRESENT_OCCLUDED, the T2645 signature - and none of the green-era films do (`b-t2516-film*` 2026-10-30, `t2623-film`/`t2623-film-ctl` 2026-11-01). The frames agree: T2549's `right-grow\f0010.bmp` shows the terminal card as the bare `#20a020` fill with black past it and **no prompt text** - the terminal never presented - while the 2026-11-02 17:0x re-film below shows the prompt.

Re-filmed on an idle box with `Get-DisplayState` = `on`, no occluded line in any app log:

- `zig-out-rsafe` - **the exact binary T2549 filmed** (ReleaseSafe, built 15:09 from 18627a0d4): right-grow `flat_background=3` of 53 (was 51 of 51), left-grow `0` of 46. The 3 are a 6 px sliver on f0002 (`flat=6`, just over the 4 px threshold), not a black terminal.
- Debug at HEAD 010c01eaa: right-grow `flat_background=0` of 53.

The `.5` geometry row stays red in both - that is T2516's own subject (terminal/bar/page agreeing per frame), not this task. Neither suspect commit is implicated: T2474's covered-window release runs from the visibility poll, which the film turns off (`GHOZTTY_TEST_NO_OCCLUSION=1`), and the lit film of the same build presents through the whole drag.

Fix landed here: `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1` reads the app's `stderr.txt` with T2645's `Get-DividerFlickerOccluded` before scoring each drag (live and `-ScoreOnly`) and SKIPS an occluded one by name; the verdict line counts skips. The broader move of that gate into a shared lib for every WGC harness stays T2650.
