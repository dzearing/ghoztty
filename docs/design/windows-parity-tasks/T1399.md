# T1399 - The design system says cards round at 8 DIP; the card renderer rounds at 14

## Summary

docs/design/win32-design-system.md 3.1 lists 8 DIP for 'cards and overlays - banner card, TOC card, popovers', but banner_card.zig RADIUS is 14 (mirroring Mac GlassCard.cornerRadius), and every card on screen - the banner, the TOC card, the viewer find card - is drawn at 14. So the table and the pixels disagree, and the table is the thing a future change is supposed to be checked against. Surfaced by T1391, which clips the find card's window to banner_card.RADIUS and deliberately did not introduce a second number. Decide which is right (14 is what ships and what Mac uses, so most likely the table is stale) and make the loser follow the winner; if the table wins, every card moves and that is a visible change, not a doc edit.

## In plain terms

The Windows design rulebook said cards have 8-pixel rounded corners, but every
card you actually see (the banner, the table of contents, the find box) is
drawn with 14-pixel corners, exactly like the Mac app. Nothing on screen
changes: the rulebook is corrected to match what ships, and a check now fails
the moment the rulebook and the code disagree again.
