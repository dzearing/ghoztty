# T2940 - A fold's first glide ticks each cost 8.4-11.7 ms at 120 Hz: while the folding pane's carried face is up, the vacated-strip desk compose runs 2.1-2.9 ms (vs ~0.5) and the after-place paint 2.3-3.1 ms (vs ~0.5)

## Summary

Split from T2732's 2026-11-12 re-measure (release sandbox, 120 Hz real, period 8268 us, T2732 glide probe, 3 runs, ~987 ticks each): 7-9 ticks a run over 8.3 ms, and 4-6 of them are ONE cluster - the ticks right after a pane folds into its strip in the last round. Each of those ticks: pane batch 0.8-2.3 ms (fine), strip face paint+commit 1.8 ms (face cost line: fill 0.31, finish 0.24, commit 1.11 = SetWindowRgn 0.27 + UpdateLayeredWindow 0.33 + show/z heal 0.52), vacated-strip desk compose 2.1-2.9 ms (stack trace vacated ready->composed; ~0.5 ms on ordinary ticks), and paint_us after place 2.3-3.1 ms (~0.5 ordinary). Find why a face pass's desk compose and paint are 5x an ordinary tick's (dirty rects of the carried face? whole-card repaint behind the face?) and bring the tick under 8.3 ms. Probe + logs: T2732 progress log 2026-11-12; GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring, 'stack trace face cost' line landed with T2732.

### Re-measured 2026-11-13 at 3a66d2c24 (after T2941) - what is left

Release sandbox (ReleaseFast, `-Dstrip=false`), the display at a real 120 Hz
(`period_us` 8223-8234), `drag-glide.ps1` scene at 1875x1125, ring trace, 2
runs (late 13 and 29 of ~940 ticks; run 2 was noisier - five non-face ticks
over budget with a 6-9 ms batch):

- **Every over-budget tick in run 1 (15 of 15) is a face tick** - a tick that
  repainted a folding pane's carried strip face. Face ticks average 6.2-6.4 ms,
  other ticks 3.7-4.0 ms.
- **The after-place paint is no longer 5x.** It is 0.42-0.43 ms on face ticks
  vs 0.33-0.35 on the others (it was 2.3-3.1 ms on 11-12). T2941's band-only
  repaint took it.
- **The compose is ~1.8x, and the extra is area, not waste.** A temporary
  dirty-rect stamp (reverted) shows a face tick composes 259k px vs 120k on an
  ordinary tick, at the same 5-7 ns/px. The rects are 3-4 full-height bands
  (70-140 px wide x 1096 tall), one at each card edge that moved, plus the
  folding card's top/bottom corners. A face tick is a fold glide, where every
  card in the row re-lays, so more edges move. Nothing composes the whole card
  behind the face.
- **On the over-budget face ticks the biggest term is the pane batch**
  (`EndDeferWindowPos`, batch qpc->end): 3.6-7.0 ms, up to 10.6-12.5 ms once a
  run, vs ~2.2-2.4 ms mean on other ticks. Next: the face paint 0.9-1.8 ms (one
  6.1 ms outlier), the vacated compose 1.4-2.2 ms, and the vacated blit/out
  0.4-0.9 ms.

The batch and the per-tick face cost are exactly T2942's levers (the same
fold-glide face ticks measured at 4K; P0, held by lane A on 2026-11-13). So
this card now waits on T2942 and re-measures at 120 Hz after it lands. If face
ticks are still over 8.3 ms then, the next lever here is the compose's
per-pixel cost (5-7 ns/px over the moved-edge bands), not its area.

## In plain terms

Right after a pane folds away into its thin strip, the next few frames of the
glide take longer than one refresh on a 120 Hz screen, so the fold looks
slightly less smooth than the rest of the drag. Most of that time now goes on
moving the pane windows, which another task (T2942) is already fixing; this one
checks the result at 120 Hz once that lands.
