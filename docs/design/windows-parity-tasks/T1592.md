# T1592 - Prove the job-ownership refusal fires: a pane-launched destructive refresh should read AGENT_OWNS_JOB=yes and be refused, not obeyed

## In plain terms

When the terminal offers to restart its background helper, it now checks first
whether doing so would take the terminal down with it, and backs out if it
would. Nobody has watched it back out. Until somebody has, "it would refuse" is
a belief about the code rather than something the product has been seen to do.

## Summary

T771 added `AGENT_OWNS_JOB` to the pre-kill diagnostic and a refusal in
`LocalAgent.terminateAgent` keyed off it. Both are proven in the directions
available on a background test desktop:

- the pure verdict (`jobIsAgentsFrom`) is unit-tested over the exact field shape
  T268 met - agent NOT a member, and yet the job is the agent's;
- `agent-upgrade.ps1` arm J (J22b/J22c) proves the term is on the real line in a
  real destructive refresh, and that it is NOT `yes` there, because that arm's
  app escaped its job at startup.

What is NOT proven is the arm that matters: the T268 shape itself. That needs
an app launched FROM A PANE (so it is auto-assigned to the agent's kill-on-close
PTY job), the startup escape suppressed, and a destructive refresh driven
through it. The expected outcome after T771 is `AGENT_OWNS_JOB=yes` followed by
`REFUSING to terminate pid N` and a **surviving app** - where before T771 the
app simply vanished mid-call with no further log line.

That arm is also the negative control the refusal is owed: a gate that has never
been observed saying anything but "carry on" is indistinguishable from a gate
that cannot say anything else.

## Where to start

- `test\win32\job-escape-startup.ps1` already knows how to build the hostile
  shape (it constructs a real kill-on-close job and proves an escaped app
  survives its teardown). The missing arm is the un-escaped app plus a refresh.
- `GHOZTTY_JOB_ESCAPE` is the knob that suppresses the startup escape; check
  `src\apprt\win32\job_escape.zig` for the exact spelling before relying on it.
- Verdict plumbing: `job_object.ownJobIsAgents` / `jobIsAgentsFrom`, and the
  refusal in `LocalAgent.terminateAgent`.
- Wire the new arm into the `job-escape-startup` guard row (it already covers
  `job_object.zig`) rather than minting a new harness.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A pane-launched (job-assigned, escape suppressed) app writes
      `AGENT_OWNS_JOB=yes` on the pre-kill line
- [ ] ... and the refresh is REFUSED, with the `REFUSING to terminate` line naming
      the pid
- [ ] ... and the app is still alive afterwards, which is the whole point
- [ ] The arm is a negative control too: it fails if the refusal is removed
- [ ] The guard row covering it re-stamps green
