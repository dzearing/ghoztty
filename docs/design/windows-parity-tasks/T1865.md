# T1865 - The pane sidebar's hover-open card on Windows has a hard right edge: Mac's glass card casts its soft shadow onto the grid, Windows clips it with a window region

## Summary

Found landing T1842. The open card is a GDI popup clipped by a window
region (the rail column OR the rounded card), so its right-hand corners are a
hard 1-bit edge and the banner-card shadow (8 DIP blur, 4 DIP drop) stops at
the card instead of falling on the terminals beside it, as Mac's glass card
does over the grid. Fix shape: render the card into a per-pixel-alpha layered
window (UpdateLayeredWindow, premultiplied: the card material plus its shadow
as real alpha over whatever is under it) instead of a region, keeping the
column strip opaque. Hit testing must then follow the card's alpha (or a
region kept for hit tests only), so the trailing margin still passes clicks
to the grid.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] the open card's right corners are anti-aliased and its shadow falls on the grid (capture of the card over a pane, looked at)
- [ ] the trailing margin still passes a click through to the pane under it (`pane-sidebar.ps1` section 7 region/hit check, adapted)
