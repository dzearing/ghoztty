# T1865 - The pane sidebar's hover-open card on Windows has a hard right edge: Mac's glass card casts its soft shadow onto the grid, Windows clips it with a window region

## Summary

Found landing T1842. The open card is a GDI popup clipped by a window
region (the rail column OR the rounded card), so its right-hand corners are a
hard 1-bit edge and the banner-card shadow (8 DIP blur, 4 DIP drop) stops at
the card instead of falling on the terminals beside it, as Mac's glass card
does over the grid. Fix shape: render the card into a per-pixel-alpha layered
window (UpdateLayeredWindow, premultiplied: the card material plus its shadow
as real alpha over whatever is under it) instead of a region, keeping the
column strip opaque. Hit testing must then follow the card's alpha (or a
region kept for hit tests only), so the trailing margin still passes clicks
to the grid.

## In plain terms

When you hover the narrow pane sidebar and its card slides open over your
terminals, the card's right side used to end in a hard, jagged cut: its
rounded corners were stair-stepped and its shadow stopped dead at the card's
edge. On Mac the card floats - smooth corners, a soft shadow falling on the
terminals beside it. Windows now draws it the same way, and clicks on that
shadow still go to the terminal underneath.

## Goals

- [x] the open card's corners are smooth (antialiased) instead of stair-stepped
- [x] its soft shadow falls on the terminals beside and below it
- [x] clicks on the shadow and the empty margin still reach the terminal underneath
- [x] the raised-card (elevated) style gets room on the right for the shadow too

## Validation criteria

- [x] the open card's right corners are anti-aliased and its shadow falls on the grid - verified by the pure layer's unit tests (`pane_sidebar_layer.zig`: corner coverage passes through partial values, shadow alpha beside and below the card, faded to clear at the window edge; none + win32 lanes green) and by what the card actually handed the window manager in `pane-sidebar.ps1` 7b (`card layer 330x826 solid=253128 partial=11332 distinct=16`: 11k partial-alpha shadow/edge pixels, ~14 px deep down an 826 px card). An on-screen photograph is NOT RUN: a layered window cannot be read back off the test desktop (the readonly-badge CAPTURE LIMIT), and capturing on the user's desktop is barred while they are present
- [x] the trailing margin still passes a click through to the pane under it - `pane-sidebar.ps1` section 7, adapted: the card is WS_EX_LAYERED with no region, and its WM_NCHITTEST answers HTCLIENT on the column and card middle, HTTRANSPARENT (-1) on the trailing margin and the rounded-off top-right corner; ALL PASS (65 assertions)
