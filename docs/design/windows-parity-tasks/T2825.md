# T2825 - Composition step 5c-5a: a strip that stays a strip and visibly moves (the grid shifting for the pane sidebar's collapse or expand, T2417) glides on the compositor - its band and name declared as a face on the glide's follower, cut to the strip's visible part

## Summary

Split of T2780. A StripGlide strip (pane_stack_slide.Slide.strips) is eased on the window thread's tick and painted into the desk by tuckedStrips/shownStripTab, so under a stall it stands still while the declared cards glide on. Declare it the way T2778/T2779 declare a face: the pane's StripFace with no content (band over the union box, name riding nameOffset), its clip running from the visible part (pane_stack_strip.visiblePart of the drawn tab and its tuck) to the landing's, re-declared from FaceDecl.reached on a retarget. The desk paints the strip only at the overlap of its start and landing (always inside the face, since every edge moves monotonically on the follower), so the desk stops changing on the tick. Finding from T2780's baseline (comp-glide-hold-onscreen -Arms stall, ALL PASS 20): that layout's one moving strip (left 91 fixed, right 150 -> 136) moves only where it is tucked under the opening card, so the stall arm cannot see it; the oracle needs a new arm whose glide translates strips on show - a pane sidebar collapse/expand (T2406) with GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLIDE_STALL_MS, scoring the strip's visible edge, -NoDeclare red. Held strips (T2312) do not move during a glide, so they stay as they are.

## In plain terms

When you open or close the pane sidebar, a pane that is folded down to a thin strip at the edge of the grid slides over with the cards beside it. That strip was moved one frame at a time by the app, so whenever the app was busy for a moment the strip stood still while the cards around it kept sliding. Now the whole slide is handed to Windows up front, so the strip keeps moving with its neighbours even when the app stalls.
