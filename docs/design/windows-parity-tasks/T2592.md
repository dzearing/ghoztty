# T2592 - Blades D22b: at the near-full window a pane the fold grows shows its old picture plus a black strip for one or two frames although its new-size frame was already swapped - the compositor shows the resized window before that present; renderer-side fixes cannot reach it

## In plain terms

On a big window, when a drag folds panes into blades, a pane that gets wider
sometimes shows a thin black stripe along its new edge for a frame or two.
When this lands that edge shows the background (or the real text) instead,
so the drag has no black flicker at any window size.

## Summary

Parent: T2485 (D22). On the near-full window (3000x1700 at 125%) a pane the
batch grows shows, for one or two composed frames, its previous width's
picture with pure black out to the new width (20261101-004809 f254/f255:
pane `2570baa` 2155 -> 2162, black x2247-2253; pane `9e10706` ~280 -> 313,
black x3431-3463; 20261101-001013 f257/f258 the same shape).

What it is NOT (measured 2026-11-01 00:48, release, ring trace,
`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_FIX=verify`): a stale GL back buffer. The verify mode reads
the back buffer's far corner back before each first swap at a new size (via a
raw 2x1 copy, sRGB-safe): 153 of 153 first swaps in the live run landed whole
on the first try (1127 of 1127 in the off-desktop probe). In the f254 case
pane `2570baa` swapped at 2162 TWICE (qpc ...225552, ...255316), 17-20 ms
before f254 composed, and f254 and f255 still show the old picture + black.
The next width (2180, swapped ...537285) shows within 5 ms. So the frame is in
the swap chain and the compositor shows the resized window's surface (the old
contents, black beyond) before it shows that present. `rebind`, `twice` and
`verify` all act on the renderer side and none can reach it; D18's
`fillUncoveredStrips` GDI fill is laid in `handleResize` before the swap and
is not what is shown either.

Candidate fixes (to be measured, best first):

1. **Keep a pane's window from changing size during a drag**: size the GL
   child to a grow-only bucket (like `Target.grow_only`, T2577) and show only
   the logical size through the card region the window already applies
   (`applyCardRegions`), with the renderer drawing the logical size and
   clearing the rest to background. A grow inside the bucket is then a region
   change plus a new frame, never a surface reallocation, so there is no
   resized-but-unpresented surface to show. Touches `surfaceSize` (logical
   size, not the client rect), mouse mapping, overlays and the D18 fill.
2. **Cover the uncovered strip from above** until the pane's present at the
   new size has been composed (a sibling above the GL child, painted
   background, retired one composition after the present).
3. Measure first whether the window's present path matters: the composition
   swap chain (`GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT=1`, T2466/T2488) resizes its own buffers
   and may not show this at all - an A/B run at near-full is the cheapest
   first experiment.

- [ ] the chosen fix: 0 black frames on two consecutive near-full runs and on two normal runs
