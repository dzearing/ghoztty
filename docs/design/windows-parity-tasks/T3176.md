# T3176 - A relaunch keeps each pane's tint and user-typed pane title (Mac dd77885aa saves Leaf.tint and Leaf.titleSetByUser in the session manifest)

## Summary

Mac dd77885aa adds additive Leaf.tint, Leaf.titleSetByUser and Entry.tabColor to its session manifest and saves at once when a tint, title or tab colour changes. The win32 session_layout.Leaf has title but no tint and no per-pane title-set-by-user flag (Tab.color already exists, T72). Verify what a Windows relaunch drops today, then carry tint + user-title through capture/restore (additive keys) and the mac_layout_blob translation, and save immediately on change. Ref: docs/reference/session-persistence.md, pane-style.md.

## In plain terms

If you colored a pane or typed your own name for it, quitting and reopening
Ghoztty used to bring the pane back plain, and the name you typed got replaced
by whatever the shell called itself. Now the color and your name come back, and
your name stays put. A layout carried over from a Mac also keeps its tab colors.

## Goals

- [x] a pane's tint is saved and comes back on relaunch
- [x] a title the user typed comes back pinned (the shell's own titles do not replace it)
- [x] a tint or user-title change saves the layout at once
- [x] a Mac layout's `tint`, `titleSetByUser` and `tabColor` translate across
