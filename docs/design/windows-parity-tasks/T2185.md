# T2185 - Folding panes by dragging a divider leaves the strips drawn past the window's right edge, out over the desktop

## Summary

User report 2026-10-23 with screenshot: after resizing panes down until they fold (T2124 drag-collapse), the folded strips (vertical 'D:\git\ghoztty' titles) render OUTSIDE the window frame, to the right of its client area, over the desktop and the window behind. Strips must be laid out inside the row's rect. Suspect the strip rects after a drag-collapse are computed from the pre-collapse row width (or the drag's unclamped extent) rather than the window's client width, or the strip child HWNDs are not clipped by WS_CLIPCHILDREN/the parent. Repro: two or three side-by-side panes, drag a divider past the 300 DIP minimum so panes fold; look at the right edge. Acceptance: on the test desktop, after a drag-collapse every strip/pane child rect lies inside the window's client rect (GetWindowRect vs client), at 100% and 150% DPI.

## In plain terms

When you dragged a divider until panes folded into strips, a grey shadow of
each folded pane appeared to the right of the window, over the desktop. It was
the "unfocused pane" dimming layer: a folded pane is kept at its old width
just off to the side, and its dimming layer floats on top of everything rather
than staying inside the window. Folded panes no longer get that layer (their
strip is already shaded), so nothing draws outside the window.

## Goals

- [x] a folded pane shows no dimming layer, so nothing reaches past the window's edge
- [x] panes on show still dim exactly as before

## Root cause

Not the strips themselves: those are painted into the window's own DC and are
clipped by it. The leak was the unfocused-split dim wash (`DimOverlay`, T74),
a layered POPUP placed over its pane's screen rect. `updateDimOverlays` asked
`dim_math.shouldDim` with no notion of visibility, so a folded pane - hidden,
but held at its last on-show width by `pane_stack_slide.heldRect` (T2105),
which for a trailing stack runs past the window's right edge - kept a wash at
that rect. Every other pane popup (banner, bell frame, key pill, read-only
badge, scrollbar) already checks its owner's visibility; the wash was the one
that did not.

Fix: `DimState.hidden` (the pane child's own `WS_VISIBLE` bit, read after the
layout batch lands - not `IsWindowVisible`, which would read every pane hidden
in a window not yet shown) and `shouldDim` refuses a hidden pane.
