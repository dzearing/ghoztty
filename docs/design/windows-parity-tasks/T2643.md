# T2643 - divider-flicker.ps1's dark-frame reading has no live negative control: a T2400_NEUTERED build (SLWA banner resize) scores 0 dark frames in 6 cells, so find or build a knob that restores a banner black strip

## Summary

`divider-flicker.ps1` (T2476) scores DARK frames (near-black pixels over the pressed frame's count + 300) on every live cell, and the synthetic section proves the reading. But its live negative control, a build with `BannerOverlay.T2400_NEUTERED = true` (the banner resizes through SLWA instead of the ULW present, confirmed in the app log: `banner present refused; painting through SLWA`), scored 0 dark frames in 7 cells on 2026-11-02 (max 140 px under the 300 slack). T2400 measured that control at 1 and 68 frames on 2026-10-28; later banner work (the region-first corner cut, the pre-render) evidently closes the strip on its own. So the control no longer adjudicates anything, and the dark reading is undefended live.

Find a control that restores a real black strip during a drag (candidates: the banner's region cut after the resize - T2400 cause 1, `applyCardCornerSized` order - or a knob that skips the synchronous paint after a resize), add it as `-Control`, and show it red. Then either re-point `T2400_NEUTERED`'s claim at what it still controls or retire the flag.

## In plain terms

The check that catches black flashes during a divider drag works on test images, but the switch that should make the real app flash black no longer does, so nothing proves the live check would notice a black flash if one came back.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] a `-Control` knob that makes `divider-flicker.ps1 -Live` count DARK frames above 0 while the shipped build reads 0.
- [ ] `T2400_NEUTERED` re-claimed or retired; neuter-audit green.
