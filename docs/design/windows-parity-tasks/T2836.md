# T2836 - stack-fold-slide F13 goes red with T2793's chrome_comp z-order change: the strip-face popup photographs 100% black (000000) mid-glide; A/B with HEAD's chrome_comp.zig is green; find the mechanism and fix without losing the lit-rim-over-body order

## Summary

T2793 moved the chrome overlay visual and the glow visuals in
`chrome_comp.Host` from `AddVisual(desk, vis, TRUE, NULL)` (the BOTTOM of the
desk's children, under the card-shadow root) to `FALSE, NULL` (the top), so
the focused card's lit rim shows over a card riding whole. With that change,
`test\win32\stack-fold-slide.ps1` (default, flat, not `-Tree`) goes red on
one assertion:

    FAIL  F13: the face's commonest pixel 000000 is the strip band the face logged (191c21, ...)
    face photo 228x826: top colour 000000 (100%)

Evidence (2026-11-08, T2793's turn):

- Red 3 of 3 with the T2793 build: the due sweep, a solo re-run, and a solo
  re-run with `GHOZTTY_COMP_SHADOWS_NOBODY=1` (so the whole-riding bodies are
  NOT the cause).
- Green with `src/apprt/win32/chrome_comp.zig` swapped to HEAD (533fce09a)
  and everything else of T2793 kept: ALL PASS (45), photo top colour 23262d
  (94%).
- Every other assertion stays green (F10/F11 face alphas and strengths, F12,
  F14, F19).

What is NOT yet understood: the photo is the `GhozttyStripFace` popup's
WM_PRINT (`StripFace.printInto` BitBlts its glide DIB), and the face's paint
(`Window.paintStripFace`, ~Window.zig:9680) never reads the overlay or glow
visuals. A 100% black print means `mem_dc` was null or `px_w` 0 when the
print arrived (DIB released at `hide`, or not yet committed), so it may be a
timing race the z-order change shifts, rather than a picture defect.
Places to look: the frame the photo lands on (log the WM_PRINT vs
commit/hide order), and whether the overlay or glow visual on top changes
when the face is first committed or hidden.

## In plain terms

One automated check photographs the title strip a folding pane slides
into, to prove the strip shows the right colour. On a busy machine that photo
sometimes came back solid black and the check failed. Nothing was wrong with
what a user sees: Windows itself was occasionally handing the test an empty
picture. The check now notices a blank photo and takes another one.

## Goals

- [x] Find why the photo is black
- [x] Make the check retake a blank photo without losing its ability to fail
- [x] Stop the check from going red on a busy machine

## Mechanism (2026-11-09)

Not a z-order or picture defect, and not the app's WM_PRINT at all:

- The face popup is an UpdateLayeredWindow window, and the system answers
  `PrintWindow(hwnd, dc, 0)` for it from the layered surface. A receipt added
  to the face's WM_PRINT/WM_PRINTCLIENT handler (`strip face printed` /
  `strip face print empty`) logged **0 times in 9 runs**, green and red alike,
  while the same logger's `strip face dib` debug line printed in every one.
- Under box load (32 PowerShell CPU burners beside the script) the system's
  answer is sometimes the caller's untouched `CreateCompatibleBitmap`, i.e.
  pure 000000: red on 3 of 5 loaded runs with the HEAD test, 0 of 5 unloaded.
  That is why the red tracked sweeps (box load) and looked tied to whichever
  change was being swept, T2793 included; A/B runs at different moments
  sampled different load.
- Fix (test side, `test\win32\stack-fold-slide.ps1`): a capture that is
  >=99% 000000 is retaken on a later sample, at most 5 times; the 6th black
  photo is kept and scored, so a face that truly draws nothing still fails
  F13 (the face's band is never pure black: every logged band is >=191c21).
  The receipt was removed again (it can never fire); `StripFace.zig`'s
  WM_PRINT comment now says the handler is not reached once the face is
  layered.
