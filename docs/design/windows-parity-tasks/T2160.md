# T2160 - Stacked panes: with glass panes on, a tucked strip is resolved over the solid terminal background instead of the glass sheet Mac lays it over

## Summary

T2121 resolves the strip (82% over the scrimmed pane) against the configured background. On a glass pane Mac's strip sits over the translucent sheet, so the desk tints it; Windows paints it opaque. Resolve it over the glass sheet mix (pane_elevation.sheetMix) in the desk composite when glass is on, and add a -Glass leg to pane-stack-strip.ps1.

## In plain terms

With see-through ("glass") panes turned on, a pane folded away into a thin
strip was painted as a solid dark bar, so the coloured backdrop behind the
panes never showed through it the way it does on the Mac. Now the strip
picks up a faint tint of the backdrop under it, as Mac's does: darker where
the backdrop is dark, a touch of teal where it is teal.

## Goals

- [x] On glass, each strip pixel is the strip laid over the tucked card's
      thin glass sheet on the desk under it (Mac: 82% strip over the 45%
      scrim over `PaneGlass`), hover included
- [x] Rounded leading corners, square trailing edge and the hover lightening
      all keep working on glass
- [x] Solid panes (glass off, or the flat style) are unchanged
- [x] `pane-stack-strip.ps1` gains a `-Glass` leg; its elevated leg pins glass
      off, since glass defaults on and that leg had been asserting the opaque
      colour over a glass pane

## Validation criteria

- [x] Unit tests (`pane_stack_strip.zig`, `zig test` on the file, 113/113):
      over a desk equal to the background the glass strip IS the opaque one;
      over blue/red desks it leans blue/red; it stays darker than the sheet;
      hover lightens it; the LUT equals the per-pixel layers; a glass band
      follows a vertical desk gradient row by row and keeps its rounded
      leading / square trailing corners.
- [x] `test\win32\pane-stack-strip.ps1 -Glass` (test desktop, 125% DPI): ALL
      PASS (35). Each strip reads exactly the colour the desk beside it
      predicts (desk rgb(18,24,32) -> rgb(23,26,31), 6 levels from the opaque
      rgb(25,28,33)); D7 rest/hover over the teal mid-desk rgb(19,49,52) read
      rgb(23,28,33) -> rgb(25,31,35), exact.
- [x] Flat ALL PASS (31) (stamps the guard), `-PaneStyle elevated` ALL PASS
      (31), `-NegativeControl` FAILS (2).
- [x] Negative control: a build with the glass layer disabled
      (`.glass = if (glass and false)`) FAILS the `-Glass` leg 9 times (D1
      colour, not-opaque and trailing corner on both strips, D7 x3).
