# T3560 - Linux: pane banners survive session restore and a banner title names its pane on GTK

## Summary

Banners are per-pane session state on Mac and win32 (session-layout manifest keyed by pane id; core Surface.pane_banner_restored). On GTK T3339 keeps the banner only in PaneBanner.zig memory, so a restore or relaunch drops it. Persist it in src/apprt/gtk/SessionLayout.zig and restore it; and use the banner title (first line, or a ## heading) as the pane label wherever GTK names a pane (tab title fallback, the future sidebar), per banners.md and stacked-panes.md Strip labels.

## In plain terms

On Linux, a pane's banner (the card at the top of a pane that says what it is
working on) disappeared whenever the app restarted or its background helper
crashed, and a "Session interrupted" notice could take its place. Now the
banner comes back with its pane, and the notice only appears in panes that had
no banner of their own. A pane with a banner too tall for it still starts its
shell.

## Goals

- [x] the session layout records each terminal pane's banner and a relaunch puts it back
- [x] the session-interrupted notice keeps out of a restored banner's slot
- [x] in-place agent recovery keeps the banner too
- [x] a restored banner taller than its pane cannot stop the pane's shell from starting
- [x] the banner title as the pane's name: already the sidebar's (T3557); a stacked pane's strip is T3567. The Mac never titles a TAB from the banner (docs/reference/banners.md, stacked-panes.md "Strip labels"), so the summary's "tab title fallback" is not built, by platform symmetry
