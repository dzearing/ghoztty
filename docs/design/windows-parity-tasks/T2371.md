# T2371 - Mac parity check for T2365: PaneStage steps its glide from a 120 Hz Foundation Timer, which is not display-locked either; confirm on a 60/120 Hz Mac that glide frames land evenly (or move it to CADisplayLink/CVDisplayLink as Windows moved to the compositor clock)

## Summary

T2365 found that Windows' 16 ms timers fired at 16 or 31 ms about half the
time each, so glides stuttered. It moved every Windows animation onto the
compositor's own clock (`DCompositionWaitForCompositorClock`), one step per
displayed frame. Mac's `PaneStage` steps its glide from a 120 Hz Foundation
`Timer`, which is also not tied to the display: on a 60 Hz panel, or under
main-thread load, its steps can land unevenly against vsync too.

Check it on a Mac: log the interval between glide steps (and when each frame
is shown) during a stack glide on a 60 Hz and a 120 Hz display. If the steps
land evenly, record that and close this. If they do not, step the glide from
`CADisplayLink` (macOS 14+) or `CVDisplayLink`, the Mac counterpart of what
Windows now uses.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Glide step intervals measured on a Mac at 60 Hz and 120 Hz, with the numbers recorded here.
- [ ] Either they land one display frame apart (no change needed), or the glide is moved to a display-linked clock and re-measured.
