# T1939 - The pane sidebar's hover-open glass card is see-through with no blur: terminal text and the pane banner show through and garble its rows

## In plain terms

When the pane sidebar slides open over a terminal, you can read the
terminal right through it. Its rows ("Windows parity - task status",
"Machine reboot...") are drawn on top of the terminal's text and the
pane's banner, and both are legible, so neither is readable. On Mac the
same card is frosted glass: the colors behind it show, the letters do not.

## Summary

User report (2026-10-14, screenshot): "bug ... you can see through this
pane". The hover-open sidebar card (PANES) sits over a diff in Claude Code.
The terminal's text, and the pane banner's "Prompt" / "Status" rows, show
crisply through the card and overlap its rows.

Evidence: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ghoztty\ghoztty.log` on the user's 1.39.7+ build:
`pane sidebar card glass=true interior_alpha=85` (33% opaque). The card is
a layered window presented with per-pixel alpha
(`PaneSidebar.zig` `presentCard`, T1865) and NO blur behind it. A 33% sheet
with no blur leaves text behind it fully legible.

Mac reference: the unpinned sidebar card is tinted glass (main ec71db251,
733b03e58) over a material, so what is behind it is blurred to color only.

Two defects:

1. **No blur.** The glass needs what is behind it blurred, as the panes'
   own glass gets with T1788's acrylic (DWMSBT_TRANSIENTWINDOW). If the
   card's popup cannot take a system backdrop (it is a layered
   per-pixel-alpha popup), then until it can, raise the interior opacity
   so text behind it is not legible. Measure that: the luminance contrast
   of terminal text seen through the card must fall below a legibility
   floor (for example < 1.2:1) against the card's own background.
2. **The banner is above the card.** The pane banner's text draws over
   the open sidebar card (the "Prompt"/"Status" rows cross its rows in the
   screenshot), so the banner window sits above the card in z-order, or
   its own translucency adds to it. An open sidebar card must be above
   every per-pane chrome window (banner, badges, dim) of the panes it
   covers (`overlay_zorder.zig`).

## Goals

- [x] Nothing behind the hover-open card is legible through it: the
      terminal's text and the pane banner are gone, the colour stays
- [x] The card's rows read at 4.5:1 or better, dark and light
- [x] Find out whether the banner really sits above the card (it does
      not - it was showing through the see-through sheet)

## Resolution

**Defect 1 (no blur) - fixed.** The card is a per-pixel-alpha layered
popup, and DWM's blur materials cannot be measured on the test desktop, so
the sheet now carries what a blur would have left: past the column it lies
on the panes as a blur leaves them - the desk with `GLASS_TINT` (40%) of the
terminal background over it (`pane_sidebar_layer.frostPanes`) - and
`buildGlass` backs the sheet with that at its own coverage. Inside the card
it is opaque (interior_alpha 85 -> 255); its antialiased edge and its soft
shadow are still translucent over the grid. What is lost against Mac's live
blur: a strongly coloured region behind the card (an image, a coloured
diff) no longer tints it. The live blur behind the card is T1997,
gated on an on-screen measurement like T1808.

**Defect 2 (banner above the card) - disproved.** Section 5b of
`pane-sidebar-elevated.ps1` shows a banner SHOWN while the card is open
(the reported sequence) and enumerates the z-order: the banner sits below
the card and above its own window. A build with a z-order fix in place and
a build without it both measured that, so no z-order change ships: the
banner's text was coming through the 33% sheet exactly as the terminal's
did, and the opaque sheet ends both. 5b stays as the regression check.

## Validation criteria

- [x] Nothing behind the card shows through it: `pane-sidebar-elevated.ps1`
      section 5 reads the card's interior alpha from the app (`interior_alpha=255`,
      was 85 - the T1887 assertion at 70-100 is the negative control this
      replaces); unit test "nothing behind the open card's sheet can show
      through it" holds every fully covered pixel at alpha 255 while the
      shadow stays partial. A screen capture of the composite is NOT RUN:
      the test desktop has no screen composite, and the user's desktop is
      off limits (go.md focus rule); with alpha 255 the composite cannot
      depend on what is behind.
- [x] The banner window is below the open card in z-order (enumerated,
      section 5b: card=0 banner=2 window=3).
- [x] Rows stay readable: unit test "the rows read at 4.5:1 on the frosted
      card" - the row ink against the frosted interior over the whole ocean
      desk, two dark and two light backgrounds.
- [x] Pinned (non-hover) sidebar is unchanged: sections 3 and 6 of
      `pane-sidebar-elevated.ps1` green (the pinned panel and the rail do
      not use the past-the-column path; `opaque_w = w`).
- [x] `floor-lane.ps1 -Lane all` green (lib/none/win32/agent), `ipc-p1/p2/p3`
      ALL PASS, `-Lane harness` PASS, and every due harness green except
