# T3651 - Linux L11k4: single-window mode on GTK - the desk gradient crossfades from the old workspace's to the new one's on a switch (0.35s, elevated style only)

## Summary

Port the shade fade (Mac deskEntrance/PaneDeskFade; win32 T3183 over desk_fade.zig) onto the GTK desk (T3566). Nothing fades in the flat style. Screenshot seam frames mid-fade. Spec: docs/reference/window-modes.md (The hand-off step 4).

## In plain terms

In single-window mode every "window" is a workspace shown in one shared
window, and each can have its own background gradient. Until now, switching
to any workspace other than the first one showed no gradient at all (a flat
grey header and gutters), and nothing animated. Now the shared window always
shows the gradient of the workspace you are looking at, and a switch between
two different gradients fades smoothly from the old one to the new one in
about a third of a second, as on the Mac and Windows.

## Goals

- [x] the shared window draws the desk of the workspace on show, not its own (the desk was missing entirely for every other workspace)
- [x] a switch between two different gradients crossfades over 0.35s on the ease-in-out curve (the old desk over the new, fading out)
- [x] no fade between two workspaces with the same gradient, in the flat style, or with animations off
- [x] the fade can be captured mid-way (debug-only clock stretch + `workspaces` seam counters)
