# T3192 - Single-window mode: a parked workspace counts as hidden - its terminals stop drawing and give back render targets, its overlays hide, its viewers stop polling; all catch up on show

## Summary

Child of T3181 (split 2026-11-16; P0 by user directive via T3136). Spec docs/reference/window-modes.md; decisions come from src/apprt/win32/workspace_host.zig. Acceptance: a section of test/win32/single-window-mode.ps1.

## In plain terms

With all windows in one window, the workspaces you are not looking at
should cost nothing: no drawing, no GPU memory, no banners or dim washes
floating over the workspace you switched to. When you switch back, everything
is drawn fresh at once. Before this, a workspace you had switched away from
could wake up and start drawing again whenever something happened in it (a
split, a resize), and its pop-up banners could be left on screen over the
workspace that took the window.

## Goals

- [x] a parked workspace's terminals stop drawing and give back their render targets, and stay that way through layout passes, +split and new tabs made while parked
- [x] a workspace parked from birth never draws
- [x] its pane popups (banner, dim wash, badges, search bar) go off screen on surrender
- [x] a viewer in it is paused (WebView hidden, diff polling gated) and catches up on show (the existing `ViewerPane.setVisible` gates)
- [x] shown again, everything presents at once - or stays held when the window itself is covered/minimized (the occlusion answer moves with the window)

## Design notes

- `PaneView.setVisible` holds a pane hidden when its window is parked, the same
  way it holds an occluded window's panes (T2914) - so no layout pass can wake
  a parked workspace.
- `Window.holdParkedPanes` (every pane of every tab: release targets, hide,
  `hideOverlays`) runs on surrender, at the end of every `layoutSplits` of a
  parked workspace, and on a `stay_parked` first presentation.
- `WindowBound.occluded` carries the visibility poll's answer with the HWND;
  `adoptWindow` runs `refreshOcclusion` before laying the tab out.
- Seam: `workspaces --op=state` rows gain `presenting`, `holding_targets`,
  `overlays_shown` (a composition-path banner counts when its pane is shown).
