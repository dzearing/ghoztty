# T3232 - Each pane's own GL context costs ~10 MB of NVIDIA driver arena the driver never gives back: measure per-context cost and whether panes could share one context per window (T3130 follow-up)

## Summary

T3130 found that ~196 MB of the ~224 MB of private_rw the first 10-pane burst leaves behind is ONE 1 GB reservation owned by the NVIDIA OpenGL driver (`nvoglv64.dll`), committed in place by the driver's own `VirtualAlloc` (cdb conditional breakpoint on `NtAllocateVirtualMemory` into the range: 7/7 hits called from `nvoglv64`; one of them 10.06 MB under `ghoztty!threadEnter -> opengl32!wglMakeCurrent -> nvoglv64!DrvSetContext`). The driver keeps the arena after the context is deleted and reuses it for the next context, which is why later cycles settle at ~+0.6 MB/cycle.

The lever we own is how many GL contexts the app makes: today every pane's renderer thread makes and binds its own. Measure the driver cost per context (open 1, 2, 5, 10 panes; arena commit per step, from perf-baseline's `private_rw_allocs`), and evaluate whether panes in one window could render from fewer contexts (one per window, or a small pool) without costing frame latency or robustness (device-loss recovery, T2615 presenter share group). Only then decide whether it is worth building.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] per-context driver arena cost measured on this box (Release, 1/2/5/10 panes), with the method written down
- [ ] a decision recorded: build fewer contexts (filed design task) or keep per-pane contexts, and why
