# T2084 - Stacked panes: strips stay put on focus, drags chain-collapse, resizers live only in the gutters, and a dragged strip gets its own drop targets

## In plain terms

Folded strips stay folded unless you ask for them: focusing a pane already
on show never shuffles the row. Dragging a divider past a pane's minimum
folds the next pane too, and dragging back unfolds them in reverse. Every
resize handle sits in the gaps between panes. A strip you drag gets drop
targets of its own.

## Summary

Mac 2811a3043 (`fix(macos): harden stacked panes - strips stay put, chain
collapse, gutter-only resizers, strip drop targets`):

- Strips no longer come back out unexpectedly. Focusing a pane already on
  show never moves the row (`PaneLayout.stickyRun`). A column that empties
  into its row keeps its place. Undo restores the stack state saved with
  the tree. Stash, trash-kill, viewer close and window activation never
  hand the caret to a strip. A pane dropped on a strip stays minimized
  there, and a former strip is a drop target again once on show.
- Dragging a divider or grip past a pane's minimum now chains: the next
  pane collapses as the drag goes on, and dragging back restores them in
  reverse.
- Every resizer (dividers, stack grips, run-edge grip, sidebar edge) lives
  in the gutters only. Strips get gutters too; the flat style gets a 6pt
  gutter.
- A dragged strip gets its own targets: slots between the cards and strips
  of its row, the window's edges, a screen's edge (tiles a new window), and
  the sidebar. It never splits or swaps over a pane. Each target has a
  preview (`PaneDropResolver.swift`, `PaneDropFeedbackView.swift`).
- Mac tests: PaneStackFuzzTests (random operation sequences in both
  styles), PaneDropResolverTests.

Windows translation: the same rules in T2082's pure layout and stack state.
Hit-testing for resizers is restricted to gutter rects (`split_resize.zig`).
Strip drag targets come in through the existing rearrange/drag machinery
(PaneDesk, rearrange-* harnesses) and its drop highlight (`DropHighlight.zig`).

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A fuzz test (`none` lane) over random sequences of split, close,
      stash, focus, drag and undo never ends with the focused pane on a
      strip, or with a strip re-opened that nobody asked for. A port of
      PaneStackFuzzTests.
- [ ] Acceptance (posted messages): a chained collapse while dragging, and
      a strip dropped between two cards. Resizer hit-tests answer only in
      gutters (enumerated points).
