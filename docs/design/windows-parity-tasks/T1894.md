# T1894 - In the elevated style the mini rail's soft shadow stops at the sidebar column's edge: the card is flush with it, so the right half of the shadow Mac casts over the grid margin is cut off

## Summary

Follow-up of T1850. In the elevated style the rail's card is flush with the
sidebar column's trailing edge (Mac 557e4f978: no trailing inset, the grid's
margin is the gap). The card is painted by the sidebar child window
(`banner_card.renderOn` in `PaneSidebar.ensureMaterial`), which cannot paint
past its own rect. So the part of the card's soft shadow that falls right of
the card, onto the grid margin, is clipped to a hard edge. Mac's SwiftUI
shadow spills onto the gradient there.

It is faint on the dark ocean desk: the T1850 capture shows no visible seam.
A light theme makes it easier to see. Same family as T1865, the hover-open
card's clipped shadow. One fix could serve both: have the window's desk paint
(`Window.paintPaneDesk` -> `pane_elevation.paintCards`) cast the sidebar
card's shadow onto the grid margin, the way it already casts the panes'.

## In plain terms

With the panes drawn as raised cards, the sidebar on the left is a raised card
too, and on Mac its soft shadow falls onto the strip of background between it
and the first pane. On Windows that shadow was cut off in a hard line at the
sidebar's edge, so the sidebar looked pasted on rather than lifted. Now its
shadow falls onto that strip just as each pane's does, collapsed or expanded,
on dark and light themes.

- [x] The expanded card's shadow does too (T2290 made it the same card)
