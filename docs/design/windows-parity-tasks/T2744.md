# T2744 - A partial desk compose (most canvas resize steps since T2577) still copies the whole desk rectangle and runs the glass sheet over a copy: skip solid interiors there too, and write sheet(desk) directly where no neighbour's shadow or glow reaches

## Summary

Follow-up from T2539. Two pieces of the window-edge resize compose are still
left.

1. **Partial composes.** Under T2577's canvas, most resize steps are partial
   composes (`PaneDesk.composeIn`). Each one still `@memcpy`s the desk under
   every rect into the scratch, including under solid interiors that the fill
   overwrites unread. T2539's `pane_elevation.copyDeskUnder` does this skip
   for the full compose only. A strided variant (desk row stride != rect
   width) would cover composeIn. In T2539's A/B (ReleaseSafe,
   resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 + GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE), composing steps had a
   median of 390 us, mostly partial.
2. **Glass interior fusion.** The glass sheet reads `buf` after the glows and
   shadows. Neighbours' shadows reach into a glass interior (37 px at 1.5x
   against a 15 px gap), so writing `sheet(desk)` straight into the
   interior is only exact where no other card's shadow or glow reach box
   touches. Inside that region the copy-then-sheet could become one pass,
   saving the copy's write and read there.

Validate both with the T2539 bench (`GHOZTTY_BENCH=1 zig test
src/apprt/win32/pane_elevation.zig -OReleaseSafe --test-filter T2539`) and the
same in-app A/B. Hold them bit-exact the way T2539's "copying the desk only
where the paint reads it" test does, with a sentinel-filled buffer and a
skip control. The existing partial == whole compose test in PaneDesk must
stay green.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] composeIn skips the desk under solid interiors; partial == whole compose stays bit-exact
- [ ] glass-interior fusion is bit-exact against copy-then-paint (sentinel buffer, neighbours whose shadows reach into the interior, plus a control)
- [ ] compose_us drops on the in-app A/B (resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 + GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE, ReleaseSafe)
