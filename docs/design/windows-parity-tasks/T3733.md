# T3733 - Linux stack-glide.sh G11/G13 are intermittently red: a terminal is resized while its card is still gliding (2 of 5 runs at 2162cf1f2, before T3626)

## Summary

Measured 2026-11-25 during T3626: test/linux/stack-glide.sh G11 (no terminal resized while its card is on the move) and G13 (each terminal resized at most once by the whole glide) fail together in about 40% of runs, both at 2162cf1f2 (the T3625 close, before T3626: 2 of 5 red) and at 12dad6b6f (1 of 6 red). The failing sample shows one pane going 0 -> 2 size changes and another 1 -> 2 during the strip-click glide, so either a hold is dropped for a frame (Private.holds rebuilt per placement; a second placement in one frame?) or the first sample races the glide start. Find which, fix the product or the sampler, and prove it with 10 green runs in a row.

## In plain terms

On Linux, when several panes are stacked and one was split in and folded
away before it ever drew, bringing it forward made its terminal change size
twice: once partway through the slide (at a size it was never really given)
and again when it landed. Now it gets its size once, the size it lands at.
The glide test that caught this failed in roughly one run out of six; it now
passes every time.

## Goals

- [x] find whether the hold is dropped or the sampler races the glide start
- [x] fix the product so a never-sized pane is sized once by a glide
- [x] stack-glide.sh green 10 runs in a row

## Root cause

Neither hypothesis in the Summary. In the failing runs the fourth pane is
split in and folded into a strip before the GL area is ever allocated
(`resizes` 0, `content_w` 0 in the before sample). `planGlide` held a
minimized pane at "its last size on show" from `Private.placed`, the layout's
planned rect (570px), which this terminal never actually had. Brought
forward, it was held and drawn at 570 (0 -> 1, mid-glide: G11) and took its
552px slot on landing (1 -> 2: G13). Fix: a minimized pane whose terminal was
never sized (`Surface.sizeChanges() == 0`) is held at its slot and no `held`
entry is recorded, so it tracks the layout until it is sized; the glide sizes
it once, at the landing size. G11 now accepts exactly that first size (0 -> 1
at the landing width) for a never-sized terminal; G13 still caps every
terminal at one change.
