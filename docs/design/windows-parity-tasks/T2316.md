# T2316 - No on-box check that a pane dragged by its grab handle onto ANOTHER window's sidebar joins that window: above the hairline at that list place (T2303 sidebar_order), below it stashed - pane-sidebar-drag.ps1 lost its row-based cross-window section when row drags became list drags

## Summary

Until T2303, pane-sidebar-drag.ps1 section F dragged a pane's SIDEBAR ROW onto another window's rail and asserted it moved there stashed (`commitStashDrop` -> `commitCrossWindowDrop` with a stash index). A row drag is now a list drag held to its own sidebar (Mac 0a547522d), so that section was rewritten as G (a row released over another window stays home) and the cross-window paths lost their only on-box check. They are still reachable from the grab handle (`beginGrabHandleDrag`) or a rearrange header: onto another window's sidebar above its hairline is `.sidebar_order` -> `commitListDrop` -> `commitCrossWindowDrop` + `dest.placeInPaneSidebar`; below it is the stash path. Add a section driving a grab-handle drag (as pane-grab-handle.ps1 does) onto a second window's rail at both heights and assert the HWND re-parents, stashed or on screen, and (above) its place in that window's list from the `pane sidebar rows` log line.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] grab-handle drag onto another window's list (above the hairline): same HWND now that window's child, on screen, at the list index dropped on
- [ ] grab-handle drag onto another window's tray (below the hairline): same HWND now that window's child, stashed
