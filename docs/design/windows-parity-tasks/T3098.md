# T3098 - PA-M15/PA-R19: GPU cell buffers and T2972's new CPU shadow copies grow to 2x the high-water need and never shrink - up to ~3.7 MB GPU plus ~3.7 MB CPU per pane after one 4K maximize

## Summary

Audit-2 (T3072) areas M and R (one defect, folded). opengl/buffer.zig:113-121 doubles self.len on growth and never reduces it; prepareShadow (:83-91) sizes the shadow to that capacity (upload_diff.zig:46-55), so the shadow added by T2972 (60ae989a0) doubles the retained cost. ~0.55 MB per pane at 170x45 (computed). Fix: reallocate oversized buffers and their shadows on the storage-trim frame (T2974) when use falls under a quarter of capacity; consider a smaller growth factor. Related: PA-M10 (T3010).
