# T2132 - Stacked panes: a card sliding in can pass beneath the card beside it - mid-slide stacking order should follow the layout's paint order, as on Mac

## Summary

Pane child windows are placed with SWP_NOZORDER, so while T2102 slides a card in over its neighbours the overlap is drawn in whatever z-order the children happen to have. Mac paints cards in PaneLayout paint order (pane_stack_layout.paintOrder on Windows), so the arriving card should pass over the right card and under the left as the layout says. Fix: order the moving children by paintOrder during a slide. Acceptance: a pixel probe mid-slide (stretched GHOZTTY_STACK_SLIDE_MS) of the overlap region.

## Validation criteria

- [x] fixed by T2186's `Window.orderStackCards`: at the start of every slide the pane windows are restacked in `pane_stack_layout.paintOrder` (run on top, tucked cards lower the farther out), only among themselves
- [x] `test\win32\stack-fold-slide.ps1` F3: mid-slide, the pane on show is above the card tucking away in the window z-order in 55 of 55 samples (read from the child enumeration order, since pixel probes are dead on the test desktop); flat and elevated
