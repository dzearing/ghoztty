# T2715 - edge-drag-rate -Burst (Debug): the viewer scene's burst shrink step still sits at its 150% budget after T2695/T2711 - 13952us vs 13785us in 1 run of 3 on 2026-11-05, 13691 vs 13792 in another - so the row flips red on box noise

## In plain terms

With the experimental "burst" resize on, shrinking a window that has a web
viewer pane costs about one and a half times what the normal path costs per
step - right on the line the test allows, so the test goes red on ordinary
box noise and the burst cannot be turned on by default.

## Summary

Found validating T2713 (2026-11-05, Debug, `test\win32\edge-drag-rate.ps1 -Burst`,
three runs): the viewer scene's E row "a burst shrink step costs ... budget
(150% of ...)" read 13952us vs a 13785us budget in run 1 (red), 12976 vs 13266
and 13691 vs 13792 in runs 2-3 (green by under 1%). The row3 scene has ~15%
headroom. T2695 (bar glass cache) and T2711 (one band paint per step) already
took the obvious costs out; what is left is the viewer-specific part of a burst
shrink step. The ReleaseFast run (`-ReleaseSandbox`, zig-out-perf) was green
at 18893 vs 19516 (3% headroom), so it is close there too.

Next: read the `resize trace burst-sheet` / `burst-nav` lines from a viewer
burst run's stderr log (under `%TEMP%\ghoztty-edge-drag-rate\`) and find
what the burst shrink step spends that the shipped step does not.

## In plain terms (update, 2026-11-05)

Part one landed: the title bar's readability check got about six times
cheaper, which takes roughly a millisecond off every resize step on a glass
window (burst or not, grow or shrink). The viewer scene's shrink row now
usually clears its budget by 4-10%, but one run in five still lands within 1%,
so the noise problem is smaller, not gone.

## What is left (measured 2026-11-05, Debug, temporary trace laps)

What the burst pays INSIDE its timed step, which the shipped path pays after
`WM_SIZE` (so outside its measured step):

- **The burst's `RDW_UPDATENOW` paint of the caption band**: ~2.7 ms after the
  fix. The viewer's glass sheet is ~1.3 ms of it (`burst-sheet`, T2702's
  render), the caption ~0.4 ms (was ~1.35: `glassChromeBand`'s
  `scrimAlpha`, fixed here), `paintChromeRaw`'s flat/overlay tail ~0.5 ms.
- **The pane desk is painted ~4 times per step** (319 `paintPaneDesk` calls
  over 80 steps): the vacated-strip paint, the band paint after `gutters-end`
  (kept on this box because `vacatedPaintIsFinal` is false without
  `noredir`), and the burst paint. Each costs 0.2-1.7 ms (`pane_desk.paint`
  ~1.0 ms mean, `syncGlassBackdrops` up to ~0.9 ms, `paintDeskUnderPanes`
  ~0.15 ms).

The vacated-strip paint is already filed as T2712, now a dep of this task:
re-run this task's validation once it lands. The cheapest remaining lever is
probably the repeat desk paints; dropping one
is a frame-correctness change (T2516/T2577), so it needs the film
(`resize-sync-onscreen.ps1`), not just this rate harness.
