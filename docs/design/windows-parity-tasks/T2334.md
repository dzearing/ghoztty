## In plain terms

The test that checks the folded-pane "strips" (the thin labelled edge a pane
shrinks to when a row is too narrow) was failing even though the strips
themselves draw correctly. It was measuring in the wrong places: it mistook two
neighbouring strips for one wide strip, looked for a square corner where the
design now shows a rounded one, and looked for a banner window that the app no
longer uses. After this, the test passes on all three pane styles and still
fails when the strips are missing.
