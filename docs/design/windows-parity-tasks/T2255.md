# T2255 - A pane banner on Windows is a white-washed slab over the glass; Mac's banner card is now a pane of glass of its own (rim and shadow only, the pane's sheet thinned to 25% under it) (main a81db68f9)

## Summary

Mac a81db68f9 made the banner card over a glass pane a pane of glass of its
own: the pane's sheet thins out under the card (`PaneGlass.bannerTint`, 25%
against the focused pane's 40%, never below an unfocused pane's 15%; the card
publishes its frame to `PaneCard` as `PaneBannerCardKey`), and the card itself
is rim and drop shadow only, with no white wash and no sheen
(`SurfacePaneBanner.isGlass`). A white lift over glass greyed the gradient into
a flat slab about 17 levels lighter than the pane (`bannerOnGlassLetsTheGradientThrough`).
`flat` and elevated-without-glass keep the wash.

Windows still paints Mac's old wash over glass: `BannerOverlay.zig` builds the
card as "a wash over" the pane's glass sheet (`lighten(0.06)` on a dark pane,
`darken(0.04)` on a light one, see the comment near line 490). Port: drop the
wash and sheen on glass, keep rim + shadow, and thin the pane's glass tint under
the card's rect to 25% (floored at the unfocused 15%). Doc: `docs/claude/cli.md`
Glass panes paragraph (ported in T2249).

## Validation criteria

- [ ] On a glass pane the banner card shows the gradient through it (pixel probe: card middle within a few levels of the desk at 25% tint, not ~17 lighter).
- [ ] `flat` style and elevated-without-glass keep the wash (control).
- [ ] Acceptance in `test/win32/` on the test desktop.
