# T2788 - A pane the glide resizes takes 1-6 ms (median 3.8) from its resize wake to its swap, and the next tick's pane batch that moves it while it is still drawing costs 3-4 ms instead of 0.5: the slow-tick tail of the fold glide at 120 Hz

## In plain terms

While a divider drag folds panes away, the two panes being resized redraw
themselves after every frame of the glide. That redraw often is not done by
the time the next frame moves the panes again, and moving a pane while it is
still drawing makes that frame several times slower. Those are the frames
that miss the screen's refresh on a 120 Hz display.

## Summary

Split out of T2732 (2026-11-07). `GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring` stamps added in
T2732 (`stack trace filled ... defer_end= sizes= size_qpc= fill_qpc=`, and
`began=` on each `stack trace present`) break the live pass's pane batch
down. Release sandbox, 120 Hz, the T2732 glide probe (5 panes, a viewer
second, 1875x1125 px @1.25):

- the panes' own `WM_SIZE` work inside `EndDeferWindowPos` (including the
  uncovered-strip fill) is ~0 (under 0.01 ms a batch): the batch's time is
  all the OS;
- `SwapBuffers` itself is short (mean 0.14 ms, p99 0.6 ms) and almost never
  overlaps the batch;
- the batch is slow (over 2.5 ms; 27% of batches) when **no** swap landed in
  the 4 ms before it - i.e. a renderer is mid-draw on the frame the previous
  tick's held resize woke - and fast (0.44-0.6 ms mean, 0-1% slow) when the
  panes swapped 1-4 ms before;
- wake (`filled`, the held resizes applied) to that pane's next swap begin:
  p10 1.2 / p50 3.8 / p90 5.7 ms, against an 8.3 ms period whose batch runs
  ~1-2 ms into the tick.

Refuted in T2732, so not to be retried as the fix: holding a renderer's swap
while a batch runs (bounded futex wait; A/B late 60/92/52 vs 140/54/49, no
win - the collision is the draw, not the swap), and the T2418
`GHOZTTY_PRESENT_RETIRE` RW gate (removes the overlap, the UI then waits on
the in-flight swap+finish: late 57, mean tick up 4.7 -> 4.9 ms).

Directions: (a) why a resized pane's frame takes 3.8 ms to draw when the grid
is held (T2459) - the renderer's work at a new viewport size (target realloc,
full rebuild?) under `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE`'s frame stages; (b) make the
batch not wait on a drawing surface (move a pane that only moves by its
composition visual, T2732 direction c); (c) wake the renderers earlier in the
tick (before the strip faces / desk fill) so the draw is done by the next
batch.
