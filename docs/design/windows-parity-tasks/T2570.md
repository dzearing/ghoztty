# T2570 - Composition step 5c-1: in pane-tree mode a pane's visual takes its card's shape - cut to what a sliding card shows and rounded to the card's corners - instead of the whole client rect, so a folding pane never draws at full size over its neighbour

## Summary

Split of T2554. Surface.syncTreeVisual clips the pane's tree visual to the child window's whole client rect (comp_tree.placement), but the window path shapes the child with SetWindowRgn: Window.applyCardRegions cuts a sliding card to pane_stack_slide.clip (PaneView.card_region_cut, T2187/T2287) and rounds the elevated style's card corners (createCardRegion). A region does not clip a DComp visual on a topmost=TRUE target, so in GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE mode a pane going into its strip draws at full size over the pane sliding in, and elevated panes lose their rounded corners. Fix: carry the plan's cut as the visual's clip rect (offset into the visual's own pixels) and the card radius as an IDCompositionRectangleClip, set where applyCardRegions sets the region. Oracle: scripts\comp-present-onscreen.ps1 tree arm with a stack fold stretched by GHOZTTY_STACK_SLIDE_MS, WGC grab mid-fold matches the on arm at the strip edge; elevated corners match at rest; -NegativeControl red.
