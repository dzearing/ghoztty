# T3854 - Composition: on a translucent window the busy lights and focus glow still share the desk (T2626 gated the lights layer to opaque windows) - decide whether the glow layers and the lights layer hold on a premultiplied desk

## Summary

T2626 lifted the busy lights into their own compositor layer over the focus glow's, but only on an opaque window: a translucent window's desk goes up as out (re-premultiplied over the desk at background-opacity), and the glow layers' unblend (T2574) assumes an opaque picture under them. Check whether a translucent window takes the glow layers at all (glowOnCompositor does not test opacity), and if it does, whether the ring still sits under the glow there; then either extend both layers to the premultiplied desk or keep the glow in the desk on a translucent window. Oracle: the unit-test shape of T2626 over a translucentSpan picture.
