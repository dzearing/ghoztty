# T3729 - Linux L11e-3: stacked-pane strips are blades on GTK - each reaches 12px under the strip in front with a light shadow leaning away from the run, the shadow following collapse as a card narrows in

## Summary

Follow-up of T3625 (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md 'Gutters' - Strips are blades). GTK strip faces are trimmed by their tuck (pane_stage_rules.stripFace) so they never overlap; T3625 now paints by Leaf.elevation, so a face can draw its whole tab and overlap the one behind it. Port: draw each face at its full tab (rounded edge under the strip in front), a tight shadow (30% dark / 14% light) cast from a shape pulled in by the corner radius at each end and leaning away from the run, and fade the shadow in with collapse during a glide. Acceptance: a capture-pane of a 3-strip stack showing the overlap and shadow, plus stacked-panes.sh/stack-glide.sh still green.

## In plain terms

When panes don't fit side by side, the extra ones fold into narrow strips at
the edge. On Linux those strips sat edge to edge like flat tiles; on the Mac
they look like a fanned deck of blades - each slides a little under the one in
front and casts a soft shadow on the one behind. Now the Linux strips look and
fade in the same way.

## Goals

- [x] Each strip is drawn whole and tucks 12px under the strip in front, its title centred on what shows
