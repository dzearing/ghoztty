# T2661 - Dragging a collapsed blade back out leaves its pane banner hidden until the pane goes idle; it should come back with the pane

## In plain terms

When you drag a collapsed pane (a "blade") back open, its banner - the
status card at the top of the pane - was missing for the whole time the pane
slid open, and only popped in once the pane had finished opening. Now the
banner slides out together with the pane, showing exactly as much of itself
as the opening pane does.

## Goals

- [x] the banner is on screen for every frame of a blade opening, not only when it lands
- [x] it is cut to the part of the pane that is open, never painted over the neighbouring strip or past the window's edge
- [x] the acceptance harness films it (banner on show while the pane is still carried by its face)

## Summary

**Defect.** A card opening out of its strip (T2458, "opening carried") keeps
its live pane window HIDDEN for the whole unfold: the window is presized at
its new slot size and cut to the card, and the strip face draws the card
from the frame it rendered at that size. `BannerOverlay.updatePosition` hid
the banner whenever its owner was not wholly in view - hidden or partly out
of the window (T2227's guard against popups bleeding over the desktop) - so
for every frame of the unfold the banner was off, and it reappeared only on
the layout pass after the glide landed and the live window was shown. That is
exactly the user's re-report: "the banner still doesn't paint while expanding
and only shows up when done".

**Fix.**
- `PaneView.openingCarried()` names the opening-carried state (also used by
  `Window.paintStripFace`, which had it inline).
- `BannerOverlay.updatePosition` no longer hides the banner of a partly-cut
  pane or of an opening-carried one. It computes the strip's columns on show
  (`banner_layout.stripSpan`): cut by the window where the pane runs past its
  edge (`pane_clip.ownerClip`), and by the pane's card mid-slide
  (`PaneView.card_region_shown`); none on show, or the pane's top above the
  window, still hides it.
- The strip's window region now combines its rounded top corners with that
  column cut (`banner_layout.stripRegionKey`, keyed so a widening cut is a new
  region every frame), so the popup paints only over the open card - which is
  the T2227 rule satisfied by clipping rather than by hiding.
- The debug oracle line `banner cut span left= right= width= at= owner_shown=`
  records each cut placement.

Only the popup path changed; the composition (visual) path is clipped by the
pane's tree already. The fold direction (a card going INTO its strip, carried
by its face with the live window hidden) still hides the banner - filed as a
follow-up (see the progress log).

## Validation criteria

- [x] Pure math unit-tested in every lane: `banner_layout` tests "strip span:
      ..." (whole, cut to the card, cut by card+window, nothing on show) and
      "strip region key" - `floor-lane.ps1 -Lane all` ALL LANES PASS
      (lib/none/win32/agent) 2026-11-03.
- [x] While the blade opens, its banner is on show over the carried card and
      widens with it: `stack-fold-slide.ps1` F19 - on show in 45 samples while
      s2's live window was off show, cut to 28 distinct partial widths
      (45 -> 254 px), on show at rest. Green alone 2026-11-03.
- [x] Nothing bleeds past the window: F6 now probes a popup's own window
      region, and only counts a popup that stood still across the probe (a
      gliding popup re-cut between the rect read and the region read was a
      sampling race, confirmed from the oracle trace: at=1280 cut to 117 px
      ends exactly at the window edge). F6 green on the same run.
- [x] No regression in the stack/banner harnesses run by `acceptance-parallel.ps1 due`
      (pane-stack-unfold, close-overlays, rearrange-*, stash/sidebar divider,
      pane-banner, stack-collapse-*, pane-stack-*): see the progress log for the
      run. Reds there are the filed T2438 (overlay-zorder B) and T2495
