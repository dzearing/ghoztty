# T2574 - Composition step 5c-5: the elevated style's focus glow cross-fades on the compositor - the old and new glow as their own visuals with opacity animations - instead of repainting the whole desk on every FOCUS_GLOW tick

## Summary

Split of T2554. focusGlowTick calls repaintPaneDesk every tick for GLOW_FADE_MS, a full desk paint and upload per frame on the UI thread. On the desk-visual path (GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME) paint each pane's lit glow once into its own visual under the pane layer and cross-fade glow_from -> glow_to with comp_overlay.Layer.animateOpacity-style declared curves (T2551), repainting the desk only when the glow's geometry changes. Oracle: WGC grab of the faded-in glow matches the GDI arm at rest (comp-desk-onscreen.ps1 shape); during the fade, desk uploads per fade = 0 (debug line) instead of one per tick; advance >= 0.98 through a 500 ms UI stall (idle box).

## In plain terms

With split panes, the focused pane has a soft glow round its edge that slides to
whichever pane you click. Until now every frame of that quarter-second fade
redrew and re-sent the whole window background, on the same thread that handles
your typing and clicks. Now the glow is its own layer that Windows' compositor
fades by itself: clicking between panes costs one small drawing, and the fade
keeps going even if the app is busy.

## Goals

- [x] the glow is drawn once per lit pane into its own layer, over a background drawn without it
- [x] a focus move hands the fade to the compositor once; nothing is redrawn while it runs
- [x] the layer at any fade level shows the same picture the window drew itself at that level
- [x] a stepped A/B arm (`GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLOW_STEPPED=1`, debug builds) keeps the old path for comparison
- [ ] on-screen film of the fade, and the fade advancing through a UI stall (T2627, needs an idle box)

## Design (as built)

- `PaneDesk.glowLayer`: composes the lit card's glow rect twice (lit at level 1,
  unlit) and unblends: per pixel the least alpha `a` with A = P + (1-a)B, colour
  in range. Faded to L the compositor shows (1-L)B + L A, the GDI arm's picture
  at level L (glow, rim and anything over them are linear in the level).
- `chrome_comp.Host` glow layers (2): child visuals of the desk visual, below
  the overlay, each with an effect group whose opacity is animated by an
  `IDCompositionAnimation` from `comp_overlay.fitCurve(pane_elevation.fadeCurve)`.
- `Window`: on the compositor path the desk is composed with no glows;
  `syncGlowLayers` builds/rebuilds a layer when the desk was recomposed
  (`PaneDesk.compose_gen`) or the card moved, and declares the move's fade
  once; the frame clock is dropped for a single end tick. Off the compositor
  (`dropGlowLayers`) a move under way goes back on the stepped clock.
- Known deviation, filed as T2626: a busy light's ring on the focused card now
  sits under the glow layer (GDI paints it over).

## Validation criteria

- [x] Layer faded to L == desk composed at glow level L, within 3/255 per channel, dark/light, glass/plain, scale 1/1.5, levels .25/.5/1 - unit test `T2574: the glow layer faded to L over the unlit desk is the desk composed at glow level L` (win32 lane, `-Dtest-filter=T2574`, green).
- [x] Unblend rebuilds the lit pixel within 1/255 over 20k random pairs - unit test `T2574: unblend ...` (same run).
- [x] Desk uploads per fade = 0, fade declared once per move (1 layer for the first light, 2 for a move between panes) - `test\win32\focus-glow-comp.ps1` A2-A5, ALL PASS (12), stamped; 4 moves, `desk_uploads=0,0,0,0`.
- [x] Negative control: the stepped arm declares no compositor fade - same script, B2.
- [ ] WGC grab of the faded-in glow matches the GDI arm; fade advances >= 0.98 through a 500 ms UI stall - NOT RUN (user present; needs the input desktop): T2627.
