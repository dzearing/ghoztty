# T1926 - The viewer feedback composer bar stays a solid band on a glass pane; Mac's ChromeBarBackground gives it the same material as the nav bar

## Summary

Mac's ChromeBarBackground (ViewerSplitLeaf.swift) is shared by the nav bar and the feedback composer beneath it, so the two read as one stack of chrome. T1916 made the Windows nav bar a material over the glass sheet (pane_elevation.glassHeadRegion over the host's GlassBackdrop); ViewerFeedbackBar.paint still fills bar_rgb, and its carousel edge cue fades to solid bar_rgb. Paint the band with glassHeadRegion at the bar's offset in the host (it is not at the host's top-left), make the cue fade to the material, and invalidate it from setGlassBackdrop. Validate like T1916's host-floor nav check.

## In plain terms

With glass panes on, a viewer's address bar already lets the window's colour
show through, but the feedback box under it was still a flat grey strip, so
the two bars did not look like one piece. Now the feedback box has the same
see-through material as the address bar, and the fade at the edge of its
picture strip fades into that material instead of into grey.

## Goals

- [x] The composer band paints the nav bar's glass material at its own place in the pane (under the nav bar, not at the pane's top-left)
- [x] The carousel's overflow fade blends into the material on glass, and into the solid band off glass
- [x] Going on/off glass, or the band moving down when the nav bar grows, repaints it
- [x] Off glass, the band is the solid colour exactly as before
