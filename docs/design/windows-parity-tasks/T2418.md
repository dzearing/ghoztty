# T2418 - Dragging the divider beside the left pane to the right leaves stale copies of the divider bar behind it and the widening pane does not repaint while the drag is in progress; each frame must show the bar once and the pane painted out to it

## Summary

User report 2026-10-28 ~16:45 on the installed build (1.49.4 or 1.49.5): dragging the sizer beside the LEFT pane to the RIGHT, to make that pane wider, the growing pane does not redraw while the drag goes on, and stale copies of the sizer are left behind ("copies of the sizer bleeding") across the area the bar has passed over. Assumption, recorded because the controller does not ask: "the sizer next to the left pane" is the split divider between the leftmost terminal pane and its right neighbour. It may instead be the pane sidebar's drag edge; check both, the T2401/T2406 sidebar-edge change 033534b4a is not in a release yet. What the user expects: every presented frame shows the growing pane painted out to the divider's current position, and the bar exactly once, at the pointer. No trail of old bar positions and no strip of stale or unpainted content (the same "a game never presents a half-drawn frame" bar as T2400). Likely shape: the area the bar uncovers (old divider rect plus the newly exposed pane strip) is never invalidated or painted in the same present as the moved bar. Find which surface owns those pixels during a live divider drag (the parent's background, the pane child, the divider window) and whether ScrollWindow, BitBlt or a copied-bits move (SWP without SWP_NOCOPYBITS) is copying old bar pixels forward. The oracle is a frame capture of a rightward drag with zero frames showing more than one bar column and zero stale columns in the growing pane. That capture can run on the background desktop via TestDesktop, so it does not need an idle box.

## In plain terms

Dragging the line between two panes to make the left one wider leaves a trail
of old copies of the pink line behind it, and the pane that is growing does not
draw itself until the drag stops. When this is fixed, every picture on screen
during the drag shows the line once, where the pointer is, and the pane drawn
out to it.
