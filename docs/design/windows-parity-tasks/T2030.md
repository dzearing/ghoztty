# T2030 - A pane's banner moves a frame behind the pane during a divider drag: place banner, badge and key-state popups in the same DeferWindowPos batch as the pane (T2012 follow-up)

## Summary

T2012 made a closing pane's popups leave with it. Its second goal - move and resize the banner strip in the SAME batch as its pane - is not done: updatePaneBanners, updateReadonlyBadges and updateKeyStateIndicators place their popups with their own SetWindowPos after endPaneBatch (Window.zig updateDimOverlays). Batch them with the dim washes during a live pass, then measure with banner-resize-repaint.ps1 / drag-perf.ps1 that a banner rect matches its pane's top band on every sampled drag step.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] During a posted-message divider drag, every banner rect matches its
      pane's top band on each sampled step (no one-frame lag).
- [ ] The readonly badge and key-state pill ride the same batch.
- [ ] drag-perf.ps1 shows no regression in the `overlay` term.
