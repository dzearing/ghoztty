# T2577 - Cut the window-resize pass from frame change to last pane present under one 120 Hz compose (~6 ms; today ~14-16 ms: batch 4, regions 6, paint 1, wait 1-3), so the content can make the compose that shows the new frame

## Summary

T2575 lined the film's compose times up against `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE` (ReleaseSafe, CHROME+PRESENT+NOREDIR, right-shrink, 120 Hz). With the frame change paced to just after a compose (DwmFlush in WM_NCCALCSIZE, experiment), the new frame is on screen exactly one compose later (+10-15 ms after the change), every step. The content reaches the screen one compose after THAT, every step, because the last pane present lands +12-16 ms after the change and DWM latches about one period (~8 ms) after the compose it just finished. So the content makes the frame's compose only if the whole pass - children moved, regions cut, desk painted, every pane presented at the new size - finishes within ~6-7 ms of the frame change.

Phase costs from the trace, per step (size-begin is ~1 ms after the frame change): pane batch (DeferWindowPos) ~4 ms, batch-end -> cut-end ~2 ms, cut-end -> regions-end ~5-6 ms (clearVacatedPaneStrips + cutCardRegions), overlays + paint ~1 ms, frame wait 0.5-3 ms; then ~5 ms after the wait before WM_SIZE returns (the NOREDIR UpdateWindow), which does not delay the content but delays the next drag step.

Do: profile each phase (`resize trace` already stamps them), take the regions and the batch down first, and move anything that does not change what the new frame shows (the post-wait work) out of the way. Re-film with `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1` (a pacing knob is ~15 lines: see T2575's findings) and score bad frames per step, not per film - pacing changes the frame count.

## In plain terms

When you drag a window edge, the inside of the window is redrawn one screen refresh after the outline moves. The redraw takes about twice as long as one refresh on a 120 Hz screen; this task makes it fit in one.

## Goals

- [x] the desk (the background around the cards) stops being rebuilt from scratch on every drag step
- [ ] the pane batch (~4 ms) and the region cut (~2 ms) come down
- [ ] frame change -> last pane present fits in ~6 ms

## Step 1 landed (2026-10-31): the desk paints on a canvas that holds still

Where the regions phase went: `clearVacatedPaneStrips` paints the whole desk on
the elevated style, and a new window size was a whole new desk every step - the
gradient rendered and stretched (~1 ms), every card composed over a full-window
copy (~1.5 ms), and the whole bitmap uploaded to a new composition surface
(~1.3 ms). Now `PaneDesk.liveResize` (WM_ENTERSIZEMOVE / WM_EXITSIZEMOVE) keeps
the desk and `comp` on a CANVAS through the drag: it starts as the desk already
cached and grows by an eighth (64 px at least) when the window outgrows it. The
size no longer changes per step, so the existing memo's partial compose
(T2454 `addEdges`) recomposes and uploads only the card edges that moved; the
visual's clip / the window DC keep the canvas past the window off screen. The
gradient is laid out for the canvas during the drag, and WM_EXITSIZEMOVE
invalidates the desk so it is laid out for the window again (trace: a `desk
build` at the exact size right after every `msg=0x232`). A shrink drag renders
no desk at all; a grow re-renders every ~12 steps. `GHOZTTY_DESK_NO_CANVAS=1`
is the A/B knob.

## Step 4 (2026-10-31 23:00): a live pass moves the panes without the system's redraw

New stamps (`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE`): `pane-poschanged` / `pane-size` on the
terminal's `WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` / `WM_SIZE`, `viewer-move(d)` /
`viewer-size` / `viewer-synced` around the viewer's WebView2 calls. What they
say about the batch, per step:

- `EndDeferWindowPos` delivers `WM_SIZE` in z-order (topmost first), not in
  the order the panes were deferred: deferring terminals last was tried and
  changed nothing, so it was reverted. In the film the viewer is on top, so the
  terminal's `WM_SIZE` comes last, after the viewer's move notify (~0.4 ms) and
  bounds sync (~1.4 ms).
- The terminal's own `WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` costs ~0.85 ms before its `WM_SIZE`
  (`syncTreeVisual`, T2553).
- 1.5-3 ms of the batch passes before any message reaches us: the system
  moving the windows and invalidating / erasing what they expose (a parent
  `WM_ERASEBKGND` lands there).
- Releasing D18b's hold for panes without their own layer (so the renderer
  wakes at the terminal's `WM_SIZE` rather than at the batch's end) gained only
  ~0.5 ms, because that `WM_SIZE` is already last; neutral A/B, reverted.

Landed: with no redirection surface, a live layout pass places every pane that
stays on show with `SWP_NOREDRAW` - the desk is the pass's own paint and every
pane presents itself, so the system's invalidate + erase of what each move
exposes only costs time. A pane being shown or hidden keeps the redraw.
`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_REDRAW=1` restores it for A/B. `SWP_NOCOPYBITS` was tried with
it and adds nothing.

## Step 5 (2026-11-01 01:00): two of the three named levers do not apply - both reverted

Same rig as step 4 (ReleaseSafe @58b018602, CHROME+PRESENT+NOREDIR+RESIZE_TRACE,
four drags, 128 steps), alternating runs.

**The viewer's page bounds ahead of the batch: neutral to worse, reverted.**
`ViewerPane.presize(w, h)` called from `placeLeaf` before `DeferWindowPos`
sent the controller its new `put_Bounds` (page rect worked out from the nav
bar's and the composer's heights without placing them), and the host's
`WM_SIZE` skipped the same bounds. Bad frames, new vs old:
151/146, 162/154, 159/157 (new is never better). Worse in kind too: frames
where the page's right edge no longer matches its address bar's
(`pageR=1350/barR=1357`) rose from 10-11 per run to 13-17, because the page
now moves ahead of the bar, which still moves at `WM_SIZE`. Moving the bar
ahead as well is unsafe: it paints itself, and a child painted while the
host still clips it to the old size is left unpainted when the host grows
under `SWP_NOREDRAW`. The page's lag is the browser process's frame time, not
when the bounds are sent.

**Skipping the terminal's rounded-region cut during an edge drag: does not
apply to this configuration, reverted.** Built and filmed (deferred to a
settle at `WM_EXITSIZEMOVE`): bad 153/152, 162/150 (neutral), cut phase
1.71-1.88 ms in both arms. A trace stamp showed why - it never fired: with
CHROME+PRESENT the terminal is NOT in the window's composition tree
(`tree_visual` null on every pass). Its swap chain is the child window's own
composition target, and that IS clipped by the window region, so the region is
visually load-bearing. Only `GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE=1` puts the pane in the
tree (where the region becomes input-only). The shipped config
does not, so the region cut stays.

What that leaves of the ~6 ms: the batch (~4.3 ms) and the cut (~1.8 ms) are
the system's own work on two child windows. The remaining levers are
structural, not more trims: (a) the pane tree (`GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE`,
T2553), where the region and the child's own target both drop out of the
visible path, and (b) the render thread's first frame at a new size (~6 ms
from draw-begin to present, step 3), which today cannot begin before the
batch ends.
