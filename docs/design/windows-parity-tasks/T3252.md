# T3252 - Relaunch after a full quit shows 'error starting IO thread: ConnectionClosed / Timeout' in panes - reproduce and fix (split from T3251)

## In plain terms

After closing Ghoztty completely and opening it again, some panes came back
showing "error starting IO thread: error.ConnectionClosed" or "error.Timeout"
and a sentence blaming "exhausting a system resource" instead of your shell.
Those panes now say what actually happened (the background terminal service
dropped the connection, or did not answer within 10 seconds) and what to do,
and the log records enough to find the cause the next time it happens. The
underlying cause is not yet reproduced.
