# T2899 - A dropping remote link hides tabs on a narrow window: the pill's status text takes the tab strip's room

## Summary

Found by T1435's new harness. On a merged titlebar row the tab strip stops one
group gap left of the remote connection pill, so the pill's width is taken out
of the tab run. When the link drops, the pill grows from `127.0.0.1` (92 px at
1.25) to `127.0.0.1 - Reconnecting... n/5` (242 px): +150 px, and almost all of
that is the STATUS text (about 120 DIP), which `remote_pill.width` deliberately
never clamps. The name is already capped at 72 DIP and squeezes to a 24 DIP
floor, so squeezing it further saves at most ~30 DIP - not enough.

Measured: a 620 DIP wide remote window with 4 tabs lays out all 4 while
connected (tabs 74 px) and only **2 of 4** while reconnecting - the other two
get no rect, so they cannot be seen or clicked until the link returns. At 760
DIP all 4 survive (80 px each). Mac has no such conflict: its tabs are a native
bar below the titlebar accessory.

The fix is an experience call - what gives way when the row is short: the
status wording (e.g. `n/5` alone, with the tooltip carrying the sentence), the
tabs (today), or the status moving out of the row. Filed as a decision (see
the Activity feed, linked to this task). Assumed meanwhile: nothing changes.

Repro (red today):

```
powershell -NoProfile -File test\win32\remote-pill-tabs.ps1 -WidthDip 620
```
