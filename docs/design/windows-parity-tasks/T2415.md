# T2415 - Collapsing panes into blades is janky and flickery on Windows: the panes do not glide smoothly into their blade positions as they do on Mac

## Summary

User report 2026-10-28 on win-v1.49.1: collapsing panes into blades (the stack fold/collapse into narrow blades, T2283/T2307/T2312) is 'janky as hell': it does not glide gracefully into place, it is flickery and weird. The user cannot name a single defect, so treat it as several until measured: frames where a pane is black/unpainted or shows stale-size content, panes that jump instead of moving, panes moving on different frames from each other, the blade strip or banner/overlays a frame behind, uneven step sizes (judder) even after T2365's vsync clock, and terminal content reflowing at intermediate widths (resizing the grid every frame) instead of being held/clipped while it slides. First record a WGC capture (T2373 camera, release build) of a collapse and an expand at 2, 3 and 4 panes, and list every bad frame by class, then compare frame by frame with the Mac glide (read the Mac stack fold animation code for duration, curve and what it does with content during motion - CoreAnimation slides a layer snapshot, it does not reflow). Fix on the current stack, compatible with T2360. Acceptance: the capture shows every frame fully painted, all moving panes advancing on every refresh with a smooth eased curve matching Mac's duration, no single-frame jumps, no grid reflow mid-glide; and the user sees a graceful glide in the installed build.

**Rescoped 2026-10-28 (split):** this task carries the two defects that
reading the code proved; the frame-by-frame capture its acceptance asked for
needs the input desktop on an idle box (go.md's focus rule - the user was at
the machine), so it moved to **T2416** with the rest of the acceptance.

### What was found and fixed

1. **A GL repaint on every frame of a fold.** The pane going into its strip
   is re-cut to its narrowing card on every glide tick, and in the flat style
   every `SetWindowRgn` asked for a redraw - a full GL repaint of a pane whose
   content had not changed (the T1906 cost, ~20 ms a pane in Debug), on every
   frame of the glide: 56 forced repaints in one `stack-fold-slide.ps1` fold.
   A cut that only shrinks shows nothing of the pane that was not on show
   already, and the layout pass's own paint covers what it gave back, so it is
   now set without a redraw (`pane_stack_slide.cutNeedsRedraw`); a growing cut
   (unfold) still redraws. Measured: 1 redraw in 56 cuts folding, 57 of 57
   unfolding.
2. **Stale pixels left in a gliding card's slot (flat style).** The gutter
   paint excluded every on-show card's FINAL slot from the background fill.
   Mid-glide a card is not there yet: the gap a folding pane is leaving and
   the backdrop beside a pane held at its old size sat inside that excluded
   slot, so nothing painted them and whatever was last drawn there stayed - a
   trail. A card still gliding no longer excludes its slot; the panes
   themselves are clipped off by `WS_CLIPCHILDREN`. (The elevated style's desk
   already painted cards at their glide position.)

## In plain terms

When you collapse panes into narrow blades, the motion stuttered and left
flickering leftovers on screen. The pane sliding into its blade was being
fully redrawn on every frame of the motion, which is slow enough to skip
frames, and in the flat look the space a pane was leaving was not repainted,
so old pixels hung around. Both are fixed; whether the glide now looks as
smooth as on a Mac is measured by T2416.
