# T1422 - The hero-mode divider has no real grab target on Windows

## In plain terms

The line between the big hero pane and the strip of thumbnails can be dragged
to resize them. On Windows the mouse had to land inside a narrow 6-pixel gap
to grab it, noticeably tighter than the dividers between ordinary split panes
in the same window, and tighter than the Mac, which just widened its own. Now
the hero divider can be grabbed a few pixels into either side, exactly like a
split divider, and it moves with the pointer from wherever you grabbed it.

## Goals

- [x] Verify the win32 hero divider's hit rect against what it paints
- [x] The grab band is comfortably wider than the drawn line, matching the Mac feel
- [x] The resize cursor appears over the whole grab band, not only the hairline
- [x] The wider hit area does not steal clicks from the panes on either side

## Resolution

**As filed, the defect did not reproduce.** The win32 hit area was never the
hairline: it was the whole 6 DIP band, a gap between the hero pane and the
carousel that the parent window owns outright, and the cursor, hover, drag and
double-click all asked the same `heroHitDivider` question. What Mac's
83e6359be actually changed was more than "stop being a 1pt target": its new
handle is 9pt over the 6pt band, "the same ~4pt-into-each-pane grab zone the
regular splitter uses". Windows stopped at the band, so it was the narrower of
the two and narrower than the split dividers in the same window.

What landed:

- `hero_math.dividerGrab` - the band's center plus/minus
  `split_geometry.grabHalfPx`, the split divider's own grab half (4.5 DIP), so
  the hero divider and a split divider are now grabbed the same way. Clamped to
  the content and never narrower than the band.
- `Window.heroHitDivider` asks that zone. It is the single question behind the
  resize cursor, the hover highlight, the drag and the double-click reset, so
  the cursor cannot promise a target that is not there (the exact Mac bug).
- `App.surfaceWndProc` `WM_NCHITTEST`: the hero pane answers `HTTRANSPARENT`
  inside the zone, the same T94 fall-through the split divider uses, so the
  hero-side edge is reachable by a real pointer and not just by a posted one.
- The drag records where in the zone it was grabbed (`hero_drag_grab_dx`), so
  a press a few DIP off the line moves the divider with the pointer instead of
  snapping its center there on the first move - Mac's cumulative-from-mouse-down
  shape.

Viewer panes as the hero: the fall-through is on terminal surfaces only, which
is the same coverage the split divider has today.
