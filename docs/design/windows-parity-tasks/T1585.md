# T1585 - The rest of tab-strip.ps1 still measures the strip after a fixed sleep, which is the shape T756 just proved flaky

## In plain terms

One of the automated UI checks used to fail now and then on a build with
nothing wrong with it. The two places that caused it are fixed; the same
risky habit is still in the rest of the script, so the next false alarm is
only a matter of time. This is the cleanup that stops it recurring.

## Summary

T756 fixed two arms of `test\win32\tab-strip.ps1` that measured the strip a
FIXED SLEEP after an action and then asserted on what they measured. Both were
intermittently red on correct builds, because the strip re-fits on its own
schedule (the ratchet, T249/T737) and a tab widens when its shell publishes a
title (T1396) - and a shell publishes twice, hundreds of ms apart.

The two fixed arms now wait on observable state: `Wait-TabTitles` asks the app
over `+list --json` whether the titles have agreed, and `Settle-Shot` requires
two consecutive captures to agree on the selected extent. The REST of the
script has not been converted:

- `Strip-Click` ends in a flat `Start-Sleep 300` and every caller measures
  straight after it.
- Section 5 ("many tabs shrink") sleeps 800ms after creating `$want` tabs -
  the case with the MOST title traffic in the script.
- Several sections do `Close-TestWindowPixels $shot; $shot = Get-Shot` with no
  settle at all.

Worth deciding while doing it: `Settle-Shot`'s two-captures-agree rule was
MEASURED insufficient on its own (a transient held still across two captures
150ms apart and the arm settled on it), so the title oracle is the real gate
and the pixel settle is the second belt. If other harnesses under
`test\win32\` carry the same two-captures idiom, they inherit the same hole.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Every measurement in `tab-strip.ps1` that follows an action waits on
      observable state (titles agreed, or a settled capture), not a bare sleep.
- [ ] 10 consecutive runs are ALL PASS, and the teeth are re-demonstrated:
      `T204_NEUTERED` / `T206_NEUTERED` still score the sections that own them
      red.
- [ ] `test\win32\` is swept for other users of a bare
      "two captures agree" settle, and each is either gated on an app-side
      oracle or recorded as safe with the reason.
