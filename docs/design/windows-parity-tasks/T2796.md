# T2796 - Composition step 4d-5: wire blade_layer - each tucked strip's blade shadow (and a card gliding into its strip) goes up as a shadow-root visual kept off the bands in front, the desk composes those strips with no blade shadow, and an on-screen match of a stack with tucked strips (riding vs GHOZTTY_COMP_SHADOWS=0) proves it

## Summary

T2743 built the picture (`src/apprt/win32/blade_layer.zig`: `Key.inStack`,
`render`, `Layer.origin`), unit-tested against the baked compose. This task
puts it on screen, the way T2741 did for card shadows:

- **Owner and lifetime.** Strips are not pane visuals, so a blade layer has
  no `Surface` to own it the way `Surface.tree_shadow` is owned. Hold one
  `chrome_comp.ShadowVis` per tucked strip on the `Window` (keyed by the
  strip's view, like `glow_layers`), under `Host.shadow_root`, re-uploaded
  only when its `blade_layer.Key` changes and offset to `Layer.origin`.
- **Order under shadow_root.** Blade layers multiply with the card shadow
  layers, so their order among themselves does not change a pixel; the mask
  (bands in front) is what reproduces the desk's paint order.
- **Desk side.** `PaneDesk.composeOver` lays `blade_shadow.paint` for every
  strip; a strip whose blade went up needs a flag on `pane_stack_strip.Band`
  (or `collapse` 0 for the shadow only, keeping `band`) so the compose skips
  the shadow but keeps the band. Same eligibility as `syncTreeShadows`
  (desk on the compositor, nothing GDI-drawn over it, no lit gutter bar).
- **The gliding card.** Its collapse changes every frame (and its rect with
  it), so its key changes every frame: re-render per step, or keep it baked
  while gliding and ride only resting strips. Decide from a measurement of
  the per-step render cost; a stalled glide (comp-glide-hold-onscreen stall
  arm) shows whether a re-rendered layer lags its card visual.
- **Oracle (owed since T2741).** An on-screen match of a stack with tucked
  strips, riding vs `GHOZTTY_COMP_SHADOWS=0`, in the shape of
  `scripts\comp-shadow-onscreen.ps1`, with a negative control (the desk keeps
  baking a blade that also went up, or `_NOMASK`).

## In plain terms

Panes folded into a stack show as thin strips, each casting a small shadow
on the strip behind it. That shadow is still painted into the background
picture. This task moves it onto the system compositor beside the pane
shadows that already moved, so the background needs repainting less often.
Nothing should look different.

## Goals

- [x] Each resting strip's blade shadow is its own compositor picture, kept off the strips in front
- [x] The background is drawn without those shadows, so none is there twice
- [x] A card gliding into its strip: decided (ride or stay baked) from a measurement, and recorded
- [x] An on-screen comparison with the old painted shadows, with a negative control

## What landed

- `src/apprt/win32/blade_rides.zig` (new): `Set` of up to 32 slots on the
  `Window`, keyed by the strip's pane; each holds a `chrome_comp.ShadowVis`,
  re-rendered only when its `blade_layer.Key` changes, offset to
  `tab - reach`, opacity 1. `ridable`: band at rest (`collapse` 1), solid.
- `chrome_comp.Host.bladeUpload`: a blade visual goes on TOP of the shadow
  root (card visuals go to the bottom). The order matters after all, against
  the task's guess: the desk lays strips after every card, so a blade shadow
  lands on a card beside it, and a card riding whole (T2793) is darkened only
  by a layer above it.
- `pane_stack_strip.Band.blade` (default true): `PaneDesk.composeOver` skips
  `blade_shadow.paint` when false; `Rects.addStrip` treats a flip as the whole
  strip both places. The paint passes the desk a copy of the bands
  (`desk_bands`); the glow layers keep the originals, since they unblend
  against the picture with every shadow in it.
- Eligibility: the tree shadows' terms (`on_visual and !over and bar == null`)
  plus `!glass`, and `GHOZTTY_COMP_SHADOWS` on. Knobs: `GHOZTTY_COMP_BLADES=0`
  (blades baked, cards ride), `GHOZTTY_COMP_BLADES_DOUBLE`/`_NOMASK` (Debug).
- **Gliding cards stay baked** (decision, from a measurement): one render of
  a 400x900 card at collapse 0.5, scale 1.5 is **2247 us** (Debug, this box,
  `blade_rides` test "what a gliding card's layer would cost per frame"),
  before its upload - every frame of the glide, for a strip the desk's
  partial compose is already redrawing because the card moves.
- **Glass stacks stay baked**: the mask is proven against solid bands only
  (T2743); follow-up filed.
- Oracle: `scripts\comp-blade-onscreen.ps1`.
