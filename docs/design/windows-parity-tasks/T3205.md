# T3205 - Single-window mode: prove a click on a parked workspace's row in the pane sidebar's all-windows list puts it on show (the deferred switch T3182 added), and that the adopter's sidebar comes back expanded in all-windows scope

## Summary

T3182 routed PaneSidebar.raiseGroup/focusForeign for a PARKED workspace through App.showWorkspaceLater (WM_APP_SHOW_WORKSPACE on msg_hwnd), because the hand-off destroys the clicked sidebar inside its own wndProc. single-window-mode.ps1 H proves new_tab and goto_tab only; nothing yet clicks a row. Add section I: expand the sidebar + all-windows scope (pane-sidebar-all-windows.ps1 Get-ToggleCenter/Get-ScopeCenter/Read-Outline, note that test runs --macos-pane-style=flat), click a parked workspace's W head and a foreign pane row, assert the log 'workspace switch from the sidebar name=' + seam active + same HWND + no crash, and that the adopter's sidebar catches up (expanded, all_windows=true outline line after the switch). Milestone M1 (child of T3136).

## In plain terms

In single-window mode every window is a "workspace" sharing one window on
screen, and the pane sidebar's all-windows list is one way to switch between
them. Nothing proved that clicking a workspace in that list actually switches
to it, and the workspace you switched to could come back with its sidebar
collapsed even though you had just expanded it. Now the switch is proven by
clicks, and the sidebar stays the way you left it.

## Goals

- [x] A click on a parked workspace's header in the all-windows list puts it on show, in the same window
- [x] A click on a parked workspace's pane row does the same
- [x] The workspace that takes the window shows the sidebar expanded and in all-windows scope (fix: `Window.adoptWindow` catches the sidebar up, as Mac's does)

- [x] Header click switches (log `workspace switch from the sidebar name=swm-jb`, seam active, same HWND, windows_with_hwnd=1) - single-window-mode.ps1 K2, test desktop, ALL PASS 236
