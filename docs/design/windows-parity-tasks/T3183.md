# T3183 - Single-window mode: the desk gradient crossfades from the old workspace's to the new one's on a switch (0.35s, elevated style only)

## Summary

Mac deskEntrance/PaneDeskFade: draw the outgoing workspace's desk over the incoming one and fade it out over 0.35s; nothing fades in the flat style. Reuse the win32 desk renderer (desk_variant, T1832). Child of T3136 (split 2026-11-16). Design (T3136 Details): ONE shared top-level HWND; every workspace keeps its own Window controller; the workspace on show owns the HWND (GWLP_USERDATA, chrome, sidebar), a parked Window has hwnd=null and its pane child HWNDs are reparented (adoptPane-style SetParent, Window.zig:7278) under a hidden park host so they survive the shared HWND closing. Spec: docs/reference/window-modes.md. Acceptance goes in test/win32/single-window-mode.ps1 (one section per child).
