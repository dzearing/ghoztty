## In plain terms

On the new, faster drawing path a pane with a status banner now reaches up
under the banner. Until this lands, the things drawn over the terminal (the
scrollbar, the flashing bell frame, the key-sequence pill, the read-only
badge, the dimming of an unfocused pane) would have measured from the top of
the banner instead of the top of the text, so they would sit too high, and
the banner's collapse animation was switched off on that path. Afterwards each
one sits exactly where it does on the classic path, and the animation is back.
