# T3612 - Hidden owned popups (a faded pane scrollbar, the grab handle) pick up a stray WS_EX_TOPMOST nobody in the app set

## Summary

Found in T3609. On the test desktop, during menu-bar.ps1 (after View>Terminal Read-only and a New Tab), EnumWindows by owner showed a HIDDEN GhozttyScrollbar at ex=0x80800a8 and, in another run, the hidden GhozttyPaneGrabHandle at 0x8080088 and the IME window at 0x8, while their top-level owner was not topmost. Nothing in Scrollbar.zig or PaneGrabHandle.zig sets the bit, and healOverlayZOrders does not re-seat a hidden popup, so it sits there until the popup shows (healOverlayZOrderAfterMove clears it then). T3609 made w32.setTopmost demote such strays when a promotion does not take, which is what blocked Float on Top; this task is the SOURCE. First step: log ex-style of every owned popup at each menu open/close and after each SetWindowPos on the top-level in a menu-bar.ps1 run, and find the step at which the bit appears (candidates: TrackPopupMenu's topmost #32768 popup, the test desktop having no foreground window, an OS drag of owned windows during a band change).
