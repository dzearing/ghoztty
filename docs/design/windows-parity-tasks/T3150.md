# T3150 - PA-M17: moving focus between a large and a small pane frees and re-allocates the focus-glow buffer each time, faulting fresh pages in on the GUI thread

## Summary

Audit-3 (T3101), area M, verified at d0d874326. glowLayer calls fitList(&self.glow_px, 2 * n) per focus change (src/apprt/win32/PaneDesk.zig:2336); fitList (:328-340) gives back slack whenever capacity exceeds 2x the need, by a fresh allocation and a release. Alternating focus between panes whose glow rects differ more than 2x in area therefore shrinks then regrows the buffer on every switch, and the fresh pages fault in on the GUI thread during the focus animation. Fix: hysteresis - shrink only after the buffer has stayed oversized for a while (an idle trim), not on the focus change itself. Detail: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-3/M-findings.md.
