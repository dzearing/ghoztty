# T2276 - Stashed panes on Windows have no tray in the pane sidebar: Mac lists them after the last pane under a divider, in the same scrolling list, and dragging a pane in stashes it and out restores it (Mac e77422577, d85e4c0e0, 543489bca)

## Summary

Mac e77422577 + d85e4c0e0: the tray of stashed panes is part of the one scrolling list, under a divider that follows the last pane (not pinned to the bar's bottom); a drag slides panes, the tray header and stashed rows as one sequence, so a row crossing between them moves the divider too. Drag any pane in to stash it, out to restore it (sidebarOrder split from .stash at the tray divider). 543489bca: an EMPTY tray is still a visible drop target (dashed well + tray glyph). 0a547522d: the tray divider reads on glass (22% white, was 9%). Windows has +stash/+restore and stash keybinds (T1827) but no tray in the sidebar.

**What was left when claimed (re-verified 2026-10-26).** Most of this card
had already landed: the stashed rows sit after the last pane under a hairline
and a STASHED caption in the one scrolling list (T1827, T1878), a pane dragged
onto the sidebar stashes at that place in the tray and a stashed row dragged
into the grid restores (T1844, `pane-sidebar-drag.ps1`), and a click restores.
What Windows still lacked:

- Mac 543489bca: an **empty tray is always visible** - the STASHED caption
  over a dashed well with the tray glyph and "Drag a pane here to stash it",
  brightening to "Drop to stash - it keeps running" during a drag. Windows
  showed its slot only while a drag was live, so the target was invisible
  until you were already dragging.
- Mac 0a547522d: the tray divider at 22% of the text color (was a ~9% shade).

Dragging a row up or down the list (the `sidebarOrder` half of the drop split
at the tray divider) and the rows sliding as one sequence are T2302-T2304.

## In plain terms

When nothing is stashed, the Windows pane sidebar gives no hint that you can
put a pane away by dragging it there. After this, the bottom of the list
always shows a faint dashed "Drag a pane here to stash it" box under a
STASHED heading, and the line above it is easier to see on glass.
