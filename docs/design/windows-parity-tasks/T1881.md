# T1881 - Look at the pane grab handle on screen beside Mac's: size, translucency, the solid state and its shadow, over dark and light themes

## Summary

T1830 gave every pane a hover grab handle. Its behavior is covered by
`test\win32\pane-grab-handle.ps1` and its pixels by the pure tests in
`pane_grab_handle.zig`, but nobody has LOOKED at it on a real screen: the
acceptance run is on the background desktop, where a layered window's pixels
cannot be read back, and the T1830 turn could not open a window on the user's
desktop. Open a debug build when the user is away (or on request), hover a
pane's top edge, grab the handle, and compare with Mac's handle over the same
theme: the capsule's size and position (48x16 DIP, 4 DIP inside the pane top),
the hinted translucency, the solid fill and its shadow, the dots' weight.
Also confirm the real-cursor paths the harness cannot drive: moving from a
viewer's page into the band (the 50 ms sample), and the handle hiding while
typing hides the pointer.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] screenshots of hinted and engaged, dark and light theme, beside Mac's
- [ ] the handle appears when the pointer enters the band from a viewer's page
- [ ] the handle hides while typing hides the pointer
