# T3303 - PA-C23: the reader's flood coalescing always waits to its 1 ms deadline, so the tail of a short burst lands about 1 ms late (termio/Exec.zig:1635-1650)

## Summary

Once shouldWait (read_coalesce.zig:38-42) says stream, the loop at Exec.zig:1640-1649 waits slice by slice until 16 KiB or max_wait, with no exit when a top-up brings nothing new. A burst that ends early still pays the full ~1 ms. Fix: stop waiting after a slice that added no bytes. From T3060. Audit-4 (T3151), Fable 5.2 area finding at c9af15802; cites read at HEAD by the owning turn. Detail: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-4/C-findings.md
