# T2121 - Stacked panes: elevated strips round their leading corners and the tucked card's scrim lightens on hover, as Mac's PaneStackTabFace does

## Summary

T2109 paints the strip as a square-cornered opaque band. Mac rounds the strip's leading corners to the card radius (UnevenRoundedRectangle) in the elevated style and lightens the card scrim while the strip is hovered (onHover). Antialiased corners like pane_elevation's card paint; hover ink is already in.

## In plain terms

When a row of panes is too narrow and some are folded away into thin strips,
those strips were plain square bars, and nothing changed when the pointer
went over one. Now, in the raised-card look, a strip's outer corners are
rounded like every other card's, and pointing at a strip brightens it a
little - the hint Mac gives that it can be clicked to bring the pane back.

## Goals

- [x] In the elevated style a strip's two leading corners are the card's
      11 DIP curve, antialiased against the desk; the trailing edge stays square
- [x] The strip's colour includes the tucked card's scrim (Mac: black 45% dark
      / 16% light), which lightens to 20% / 6% while the strip is hovered
- [x] The flat style keeps square corners and gains the same hover
