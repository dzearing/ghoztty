# T1553 - relay-account.ps1 scores a false red when the fake relay still holds its hits log

## Summary

Observed 2026-09-13 during T712: section 10 of `test\win32\relay-account.ps1`
scored `FAIL the sign-out revoked this machine (T1421 still holds)`, and the
three lines directly above it were `(could not read ...hits-a.log - held by the
relay job for 1s)`. The very next run of the same script over the same build was
ALL PASS.

So the assertion did not measure the product: it measured whether the fake relay
job had let go of its hits log within the retry window. A red that means "the log
was locked" is indistinguishable in the verdict from a red that means "sign-out
stopped revoking", which is the expensive one this section exists to catch.

## In plain terms

A test of the sign-out flow fails now and then for a reason that has nothing to
do with signing out - it could not read its own scratch file in time. Anyone
reading the result sees a broken feature that is not broken.

## Goals

- [ ] a hits-log read that loses the race is reported as an arm that could not be
      measured, not as a failed product assertion
- [ ] the race stops happening in practice (longer retry, or a writer that does
      not hold the handle between appends)

## Details

The reader already retries and already NAMES the miss, which is why this was
diagnosable at all; what it does not do is refuse to score the assertion that
depends on content it never got. `Write-TestAssertedNothing` is the established
shape in this suite for "this arm could not be measured". Check the writer side
too - `test\win32\lib\FakeRelay.ps1` - if the job keeps the handle open longer
than an append needs.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] With the hits log deliberately held open, the section reports an
      unmeasurable arm rather than a FAIL.
- [ ] Ten consecutive runs of `relay-account.ps1` are green.
