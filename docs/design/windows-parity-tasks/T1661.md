# T1661 - The harness lane's default 30-minute budget no longer fits its own suite, so a full sweep can end in TIMEOUT instead of a verdict

## In plain terms

Running the harness checks can take half an hour and then stop with "took too
long" instead of saying whether anything is wrong - so the half hour buys
nothing and has to be spent again.

## Summary

`scripts\floor-lane.ps1` defaults `-TimeoutSeconds` to 1800 (line 162). Measured
on 2026-09-18: the harness suite itself is **22m 10s** for 29 audits, and the
confirm pass re-runs every non-green audit ALONE on top of that - on this run
three of them, including `test-reach-audit.ps1` at 5m 27s. Total 29m 26s the
first time, which the wrapper killed at 1800s with
`FLOOR SUMMARY: harness#1=TIMEOUT` and no verdict at all. The re-run with
`-TimeoutSeconds 3600` completed in 1766s and reported the real answer
(`HARNESS FLOOR: 1 FAILURE(S) of 29 audits, 2 PENDING`).

The budget is not per-lane: 1800s is generous for a zig lane and tight for this
one, and the gap grows every time an audit is added. Options to weigh: a
per-lane default (the harness lane knows it is the long one), a budget that
scales with the member count, or a confirm pass that is accounted for
separately from the sweep. Whatever the shape, the wrapper should distinguish
"the suite is long" from "the suite is wedged" - which is the whole reason the
wrapper exists.

Seen on the T861 turn; the turn paid 30 minutes for a TIMEOUT and then 30 more
for the answer.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A full `floor-lane.ps1 -Lane harness` on this box reaches a verdict
      without a hand-passed `-TimeoutSeconds`.
- [ ] A genuinely wedged harness lane is still killed and still reports STALL /
      TIMEOUT rather than running to the new ceiling silently.
- [ ] The acceptance script for the lane wrapper covers the new budget rule.
