# T2519 - A folded blade on a raised card without glass is darker than Mac's: Mac's card-style strip is the card itself (bg colour), Windows still lays the flat style's darkened 82% fill and 45% scrim

## Summary

In the elevated (raised card) style with glass off, a tucked pane's strip was
painted as the flat style's bar: the background darkened 35%, laid at 82% over
the pane under a 45% scrim (`pane_stack_strip.stripRgb`), rgb(25,28,33) on the
default #282c34, and lightened on hover. Mac draws no fill there at all
(`PaneStackTabFace.isFilled = !style.isCard`) and has no scrim any more, so its
strip is the tucked card itself - the background colour with the card's rim -
and hover changes only the name's ink.

Fix: `pane_stack_strip.faceRgb(bg, hovered, card)` (card -> bg, else the old
bar), `Rim.card` (the opaque card's rest rim, `rimAlpha(false, ...)`) and
`paintRimmedBand`. `PaneDesk` (elevated-only) lays the opaque band rimmed;
`Window.stripFace` feeds the desk band, the names' ink reference and the three
glide/fold painters, so a glide lands on the colour the resting strip shows.
The flat style and the glass strip are unchanged.

## In plain terms

With raised cards and glass turned off, a pane folded into a stack showed as a
noticeably darker bar than on the Mac, and the bar brightened under the mouse.
Now the folded pane looks like its own card, the same colour as the pane, and
hovering only brightens its name - as on the Mac.
