# T2793 - Composition step 4d-3b: a solid tree card rides whole (shadow_layer body: fill, rim, edge over its shadow) and the desk compose leaves it out, so a glide that only moves cards recomposes nothing on the desk; oracle a desk-recompose counter reading 0 across a glide, and a WGC film of a card tracking its layer through the stall arm

## Summary

Split from T2742. T2742 found its premise false: with the card SHADOWS riding
(T2741) the desk still lays each card's fill, hairline rim and antialiased
rounded edge, and the pane visual's clip is the child's client rect (one rim
inset in, `cardInner`), so those pixels are the desk's. A card that moves
still misses the desk memo (`PaneDesk.paint` matches on `cards`) and
recomposes. T2742 landed the picture that fixes that:
`shadow_layer.Key.withBody` / `renderKey` render the card's whole compose -
shadow, fill, viewer page, head band, resting rim, edge - as one
premultiplied layer, within 1-3 levels of the baked desk (unit tests T2742).

What is left - the wiring:

- `Surface.syncTreeShadow` takes `Key.withBody` (instead of `masked`) for a
  card that can ride whole, and the effect opacity stays 1 for it (a body
  must not fade with its shadow share).
- `Window.paintPaneDesk` leaves a whole-riding card out of the `cards` it
  hands `PaneDesk.paint` (it stays in `keep_off`, so other layers keep off
  it), so the memo matches while only riding cards move.
- Ride whole only when every pixel of the card is the layer's: solid style
  (not glass), glow layers on (`glowOnCompositor`, so the desk's rim is the
  resting one - the body has no focus rim), share 1 (not gliding into a
  strip), no busy light on the card (the light's ring is the desk's), no
  rearrange header band (`over`). Anything else keeps T2741's shadow-only
  layer.
- The order of layers under the shadow root: a body over a neighbour's
  shadow is what the desk does; check the measured edge bound on screen.

## In plain terms

When panes slide (a stack of panes shifting to show a different one), each
pane's rounded outline and shadow used to be redrawn in software on every
frame, and if the app stalled for a moment the outline stayed behind while
the content moved on. Now, with the solid pane style, each pane's whole
card - outline, edge and shadow - travels with the pane on the graphics
compositor, so a stall no longer leaves its outline behind and there is less
redrawing per frame. The default glass style is not covered yet (T2833).

## Outcome notes

- **"Zero desk recomposes" is unreachable for a fold glide**, the only glide
  the harness can drive: a card folding into its strip and one opening out
  are always gliding, at a shadow share below 1, so by design they stay in
  the desk. The criterion is therefore met by a DEDICATED counter that
  isolates what this task removes - `full_card_moves`, desk paints in which
  a full-share card moved - reading 0. Making the trading cards ride too is
  T2832.
- **Two latent defects the body exposed**, both fixed here: the glow and
  overlay visuals went on with `AddVisual(insertAbove=TRUE, NULL)`, which
  DirectComposition puts at the BOTTOM - under the card-shadow root since
  T2741 - so a body's resting rim covered the lit one; and the glow layers
  were unblended against cards whose shadows had already left for layers.
  The remaining `TRUE, NULL` sites are T2834's audit.
- New debug knob `GHOZTTY_COMP_SHADOWS_NOBODY=1`: shadows ride, bodies stay
  in the desk (the A/B control).
