# T1851 - Elevated Windows panes with a header or banner show a square band on a rounded card, and a viewer page's corners sit on a terminal-coloured rim

## Summary

Follow-up of T1831. The card is the pane's whole slot, but the rearrange header band (parent-painted FillRect) and the sticky banner strip (a layered popup) are rectangles, so the card's top corners show square over them; T1831 squares the child's top corners in that case and leaves the curve to the band, which does not draw one. Clip the header paint to the card shape and give the banner popup the card's top-corner region. Separately, the card fill is the terminal background, so a viewer pane with a light page (markdown/website) sits inside a dark antialiased corner ring; fill a viewer's card with the page/viewer background instead. Validate with PrintWindow captures (rearrange mode on; +set-banner; a markdown viewer).
