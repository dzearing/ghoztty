# T2767 - The viewer nav bar is still ~0.53 ms of every resize pass after T2550, almost all of it the cost of being a child window (move ~0.2 ms, BeginPaint/EndPaint+blit ~0.1 ms): hide the bar while a drag is moving it, paint its band in the host's own per-step paint, and put the window back a beat after the drag settles

## Summary

T2550 measured what the viewer nav bar still costs every pass of a window-edge
drag (ReleaseSafe, `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1`, `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`,
128 passes, medians): nav 532 us = bar move 195 + paint 282 (glass 84,
buttons+field 107, BeginPaint/EndPaint + the back buffer's blit ~90) + tip 28.
Flags do not move the move (`SWP_NOSENDCHANGING` and a handled
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` measured 201 -> 202 us): it is the price of being a child
window, as is the second BeginPaint/EndPaint.

The host already paints its whole client on every step of a drag on glass
(`ViewerPane.paintUnderPage`, after its own EndPaint, chrome children clipped
out). Proposed shape - T2538's deferred-EDIT trick one level up: when `place`
sees the width change while a drag is moving the window (or on any change),
hide the bar window, let the host paint the band with the bar's own
`paintBuffered` into its DC at (0,0) (it is a pure function of width and
state, and the glass slice is already the host's top-left), and move + show the
bar `edit_sync_ms`-style a beat after the band stops moving. Off glass the
host must be asked to repaint the band rect itself.

Risks to settle first: harnesses that read the bar's rect or visibility right
after a layout change (viewer-nav-pin, viewer-narrow-pane, anything clicking
relative to the bar's right edge) must wait for the settle or the settle must
be immediate when no drag is in progress; tooltips and hover must not fire on a
hidden bar; focus in the address field (editing) must keep the live window
shown and moving (no hide while editing). Target: nav under 0.3 ms per pass.

## In plain terms

When you drag a window's edge, every viewer pane's top bar used to be moved
and redrawn as its own little window on every step of the drag. Now the bar
steps aside for the duration of the drag and the pane draws an identical
picture of it in the same paint it was already doing, then puts the real bar
back the moment you let go. It looks the same; each step of the drag costs
about a third as much for the bar.

## Goals

- [x] the bar window is neither moved nor painted on its own during a window-edge drag
- [x] the host paints a pixel-identical band in its own paint, on and off glass
- [x] the bar comes back at its true rect when the drag ends (or pauses), and never hides under the user's typing
- [x] measured before/after on one build, with an A/B switch for future films

## Result

ReleaseSafe build, `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1`, `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`,
four drags, 128 passes each, medians in us, same build both ways
(`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_NAV_NODEFER=1` keeps the old shape):

| | nav (place) | bar move | bar paint | tip | host-painted band | nav total | sheet paints / run | bad / frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| live bar window (old) | 654 | 284 | 288 | 31 | - | **654** | 114 | 137 / 178 |
| hidden bar, host band | 22 | 9 | 0 | 0 | 172 | **195** | 117 | 154 / 211 |

- The bar window hides at the first step of a size loop (`deferWhileSizing`:
  in the loop and not editing) and the host lays the band with the bar's own
  `paintBuffered` in its WM_PAINT (off glass), `paintUnderPage` (on glass) and
  WM_PRINTCLIENT. `settle` moves + shows it at `WM_EXITSIZEMOVE`, on a 120 ms
  timer after the last step (a paused drag, a loop whose end never comes) and
  before the field goes live.
- The band is invalidated only when its size changed (or at the hide): doing
  it every pass cost +26 whole-client glass paints over the run.
- `resize trace viewer` now carries `nav_host_us`/`nav_host_paints` (the band
  the host painted since the last line) and the running sheet-paint totals.
- The `.4` flat-background rows are red in BOTH shapes on this build (4/52/20/40
  old vs 4/47/22/36 new on the first pair); that is T2666/T2774's defect, not
  this change.
- Runs: four films this turn, each with the ghost window
  (`GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GHOST=bottom`, never activated); row F ("the ghost never
  took the foreground") passed in every one. T2666 parks further films for
  the user's absence, and no more were run.

## Validation criteria

- [x] nav share of a resize pass under 0.3 ms median (ReleaseSafe resize-sync-onscreen with GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1), bad-frame counts not worse than T2550's run - 195 us (place 22 + host band 172) vs 654 us old on the same build; bad frames per frame 0.73 vs 0.77 (Result table)
- [x] the band painted by the host during a drag is pixel-identical to the bar's own paint (win32 lane or WM_PRINTCLIENT comparison) - ViewerPane host-floor test: 0 of 11520 band pixels differ between the host's WM_PRINTCLIENT and the bar's own
- [x] the bar window is back at its true rect and visible after the drag settles; viewer-nav-pin, viewer-panes, viewer-narrow-pane green - the same host-floor test asserts WS_VISIBLE and the bar's rect after `settleMove`; viewer-nav-pin ALL PASS (29), viewer-panes ALL PASS (205), viewer-narrow-pane ALL PASS (70) on the Debug build, 2026-11-07 20:31
