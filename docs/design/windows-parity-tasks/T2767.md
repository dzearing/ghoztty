# T2767 - The viewer nav bar is still ~0.53 ms of every resize pass after T2550, almost all of it the cost of being a child window (move ~0.2 ms, BeginPaint/EndPaint+blit ~0.1 ms): hide the bar while a drag is moving it, paint its band in the host's own per-step paint, and put the window back a beat after the drag settles

## Summary

T2550 measured what the viewer nav bar still costs every pass of a window-edge
drag (ReleaseSafe, `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1`, `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`,
128 passes, medians): nav 532 us = bar move 195 + paint 282 (glass 84,
buttons+field 107, BeginPaint/EndPaint + the back buffer's blit ~90) + tip 28.
Flags do not move the move (`SWP_NOSENDCHANGING` and a handled
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` measured 201 -> 202 us): it is the price of being a child
window, as is the second BeginPaint/EndPaint.

The host already paints its whole client on every step of a drag on glass
(`ViewerPane.paintUnderPage`, after its own EndPaint, chrome children clipped
out). Proposed shape - T2538's deferred-EDIT trick one level up: when `place`
sees the width change while a drag is moving the window (or on any change),
hide the bar window, let the host paint the band with the bar's own
`paintBuffered` into its DC at (0,0) (it is a pure function of width and
state, and the glass slice is already the host's top-left), and move + show the
bar `edit_sync_ms`-style a beat after the band stops moving. Off glass the
host must be asked to repaint the band rect itself.

Risks to settle first: harnesses that read the bar's rect or visibility right
after a layout change (viewer-nav-pin, viewer-narrow-pane, anything clicking
relative to the bar's right edge) must wait for the settle or the settle must
be immediate when no drag is in progress; tooltips and hover must not fire on a
hidden bar; focus in the address field (editing) must keep the live window
shown and moving (no hide while editing). Target: nav under 0.3 ms per pass.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] nav share of a resize pass under 0.3 ms median (ReleaseSafe resize-sync-onscreen with GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1), bad-frame counts not worse than T2550's run
- [ ] the band painted by the host during a drag is pixel-identical to the bar's own paint (win32 lane or WM_PRINTCLIENT comparison)
- [ ] the bar window is back at its true rect and visible after the drag settles; viewer-nav-pin, viewer-panes, viewer-narrow-pane green
