# T2335 - An unfocused pane's read-only badge, bell frame and search bar can sit above its dim on Windows; Mac's dim covers them (the key-state pill excepted)

## Summary

Found while fixing T2308. Mac's `SurfaceView` ZStack puts the unfocused-split
dim `Rectangle` after the `ReadonlyBadge`, `SurfaceSearchOverlay`,
`BellBorderOverlay` and `HighlightOverlay`, so all of them dim with the pane;
`KeyStateIndicator` carries `.zIndex(1)` and stays above the dim, and
`SurfaceGrabHandle` is deliberately last. On Windows every one of those is a
popup owned by the surface HWND, and two such popups sit in whatever order
last moved them (T2261), so an unfocused pane's badge, bell frame, hover tint
or search bar may or may not be dimmed. T2308 gave `DimOverlay.seatOver(card)`
for the banner strip; extend the same read-then-seat to the badge, the bell
frame, the search bar and the hover tint, and seat the key-state pill above
the dim. First measure the order today (EnumWindows z-order, as
`pane-banner.ps1` 7c does). Validation: a z-order row per popup on an
unfocused pane, with a negative control.
