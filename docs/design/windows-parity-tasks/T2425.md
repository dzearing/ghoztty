# T2425 - A dragged Windows pane-sidebar item lifts on a clear glass card, a dragged pane row keeps its indent, and on release it glides from where it was dropped without jumping back first (Mac 6bd2962eb)

## Summary

Mac main 6bd2962eb: the dragged item lifts on clear Liquid Glass; a dragged pane row keeps its indent while dragged; on release the row glides into place from where it was let go rather than snapping back to its origin first. Also: no dividers in the collapsed rail. Windows: port the lift look in the Windows-native acrylic/glass idiom of the win32 design system, keep pane-row indent during drag, and start the settle animation from the drop point. Ref PaneSidebarRow.swift / PaneSidebarView.swift / PaneDragSource.swift in 6bd2962eb; docs/design/win32-design-system.md.

## In plain terms

When you drag something in the pane sidebar, the piece you are holding used
to sit on a solid grey slab. Now it floats on a clear glass card, as it does
on the Mac. When you let go of a pane row, it glides from where you dropped
it straight to its new spot, instead of first lurching back toward where it
came from. The collapsed rail no longer draws lines between windows.

## Goals

- [x] the held item (pane row, window, or group) lifts on clear glass: no fill, a rim lit from above, a shadow around its shape only
- [x] let go, a pane row holds at the drop until its new place is laid out, then glides from there
- [x] a dragged pane row keeps its indent (already true here: the held row is painted from the layout's own indent; now logged)
- [x] no dividers between windows in the collapsed all-windows rail
