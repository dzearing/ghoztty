# T3165 - PA-I05 part 2: an ATTACH still builds its multi-MB grid snapshot under the agent-wide store lock, stalling every other session's output (server.zig handleAttach -> gridSnapshotAlloc)

## Summary

T3019 moved the per-chunk VT parse off `SessionStore.mutex` onto a per-session
`Session.emu_mutex`, taken hand over hand from the store lock. `Server.handleAttach`
(src/remote/agent/server.zig, `gridSnapshotAlloc` / `gridOnAltScreen` / the ring
replay slice) still runs entirely under the store lock, so a fresh attach that
serializes a scrollback-bearing snapshot (multi-MB) stalls every other session's
output for that long.

Why it was not done in T3019: the hand-over-hand rule (only the session's own
reader may hold `emu_mutex` without the store lock) is what keeps the emulator
in ring order and makes the snapshot consistent with `snapshot_at`. Releasing the
store lock during the serialize breaks that rule: the reader would block on
`emu_mutex` WHILE holding the store lock, stalling everyone anyway. A fix needs a
different shape - e.g. capture `snapshot_at` + bind under the store lock, then
serialize under `emu_mutex` alone with the session's reader parking its feed (an
`emu_busy` flag the reader checks before taking the store lock), or a
copy-on-snapshot of the emulator's pages.
