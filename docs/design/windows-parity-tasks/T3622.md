# T3622 - Linux L11d-2: glass panes on GTK - each elevated pane a translucent sheet tinted with its terminal's background, the desk reading through, the banner card and template viewers on glass, no unfocused dimming wash on glass

## Summary

Follow-up of T3566 (which landed the desk, solid elevated cards, focus rim/glow and the flat style). Port the glass half of docs/reference/pane-style.md: macos-pane-glass (default true, elevated only) makes every pane a translucent sheet at PaneGlass.tint 40% (25% under a banner card), the renderer stops drawing the terminal background (generic.zig pane_glass is gated to macOS; GTK needs its own gate plus a transparent GL area), the banner strip skips its base fill and becomes a rim+shadow card, template viewer pages get the pane-glass class, and upstream's unfocused-split-fill wash is not drawn on glass (flat and elevated-without-glass keep it). win32 counterparts: chrome_glass.zig, viewer_glass.zig, pane_elevation.zig. Acceptance: extend test/linux/pane-style.sh (the desk must read through a pane's glass; focused and unfocused panes identical but for rim/glow).

**As built (2026-11-25):** the renderer's `pane_glass` gate now covers GTK as
well as macOS (`generic.zig` `pane_glass_sheet_in_apprt`; win32 keeps its
backdrop path), and the pane's `Surface` draws the sheet in its own `snapshot`
before its children - the background (the pane's `--color` tint, else the
configured one) at 40%, banded so a banner card's frame gets 25%
(`pane_style.sheetBands`). The tree carries `pane-glass`, which turns the banner
into a rim-and-shadow card with no wash; a viewer's template pages get a
transparent web view and the `html.pane-glass` class (win32's pure
`viewer_glass.zig`, reused); the unfocused-wash closure binds the config so a
reload re-reads glass.

## In plain terms

On Linux each pane was a solid card sitting on the window's coloured
gradient. Now, as on the Mac and Windows, each pane is a sheet of tinted glass:
the gradient's light and shade show through every terminal and every markdown,
code or diff viewer, the focused pane differs from the others only by its
brighter rim and glow, and a pane's banner sits on it as a thinner pane of
glass of its own.

## Goals

- [x] Every elevated pane is a 40% sheet of its terminal's background over the desk (default on, `macos-pane-glass`)
- [x] Focused and unfocused panes are the same glass; no unfocused dimming wash on glass (flat and solid cards keep it)
- [x] A banner card thins the sheet to 25% under it and draws only its rim and shadow
- [x] Our own viewer pages (markdown, code, diff) are see-through over the sheet; websites keep their own background
- [x] Flat style stays solid, and a reload switches glass on and off live
