# T1544 - A pane moved into another window comes back in that window after a restart

## In plain terms

Panes can now be dragged from one window into another. Sessions survive a
restart, so after you rearrange your windows and then quit and come back, the
panes should be where you left them - not back in the windows they started in.
Nothing measures that today.

## Summary

`commitCrossWindowDrop`, `commitCrossWindowNewTab` and `commitPopOutDrop` all
call `App.markLayoutDirty`, so the manifest is re-pushed with the new shape, and
the source window's collapse goes through `removeTabSlot` (which marks it too).
That is the mechanism being right by construction; the claim is unverified.

The gap is an acceptance claim, not a suspected defect: relocate a pane, let the
layout settle, restart the app, and assert the pane is re-attached under the
window it was moved INTO - with its session, per the T89f contract. The
interesting edge is the window that CLOSED because its last pane left: its
layout uuid must not come back as an empty window.

`test\win32\session-layout-preserve.ps1` and `restore-late-agent.ps1` are the
shapes to follow.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A pane dragged into another window is restored under THAT window after an
      app relaunch, with its session re-attached.
- [ ] A window emptied by the move does not come back as an empty window.
- [ ] A popped-out window comes back with its own pane, at its own frame.
