# T2442 - A pane folding into its blade keeps its terminal content fully visible until the last frame instead of fading it out while the title fades in over the last 120 dip, as on Mac; T2288's fade never reaches the visible terminal surface

## Summary

User report 2026-10-28 ~20:45 (installed 1.49.6): blade collapse is "annoyingly janky because you can literally see the content in the blade until like the last frame", with flicker and weirdness, "absolutely not smooth". Mac spec (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md, Glides): a pane going into its strip narrows into it as a card, its content pinned to its far edge and cut by the card. Content fades OUT and the title fades IN as it narrows, over the last 120pt (PaneCardView.collapse), so at the strip there is nothing left to change; the terminal is resized once, after the motion. T2288 (29ef0f725) claimed this fade was ported: pane_stack_slide.collapse feeds strip_face.zig. But the user sees the terminal content at full opacity in the narrowing card until the final frame, so the fade does not reach the pixels that show. Hypothesis to check first: the fade is drawn on the strip-face/chrome surface while the terminal's own GL child HWND sits above it unfaded, and that child is only hidden or resized when the glide lands. That is the separate-surfaces problem T2360 names. Fix: during the fold glide the terminal content must actually fade (alpha on the content's composition visual, a faded snapshot standing in for the live child, or the child hidden behind a faded capture of its last frame), it must be clipped by the narrowing card every frame, and the title must fade in on the same frames. Prove it with a WGC capture of a fold on the RELEASE build (portable zip in temp): luminance of the content region falls smoothly to zero across the last 120 dip, the title rises over the same frames, no frame where content pops off, and every frame passes the T2427 doubled-text oracle. Close only on that capture.

## In plain terms

When a pane folds away into its thin blade, its text should fade out while the
blade's name fades in, so by the time it is a blade nothing is left to switch.
On the user's machine the fold moved so far in each frame that the whole fade
fell inside a single frame: the text was at full strength one frame and gone
the next. Now the fade always takes at least three frames, however slowly the
window is drawing; at a normal frame rate nothing changes.

## Goals

- [x] find why the fade never shows: no frame of the glide ever lands inside the fade
- [x] the fade spans three frames at least, at any frame rate, folding and unfolding
- [x] at 60 fps the glide is Mac's, frame for frame
- [x] an acceptance check that fails without the fix
- [ ] the release-build screen capture confirmation (T2416, needs the user away)

## Root cause and fix

Evidence: the user's own release log (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ghoztty\ghoztty.log`,
1.49.6; display scale 1.25 from the face rects). Over the last 40 folds the
strip face was FIRST put on screen at alpha 175-255 - the card already ~100 px
wide or in its strip - and never lower, while that window's layout passes
landed 60-100 ms apart (the viewer pane in the row logs each one). Mac's
follower (45 ms time constant) closes 75-90% of the remaining way in a frame
that long, so it crossed the 120-DIP fade in one step: the face never had a
partial frame and the content went from full to gone. This rules out "the face
is never shown" (the log says it is); whether it composites above the GL
child at the pixel level is still only proven through WM_PRINT (F13), which is
why the screen capture goes to T2416.

Fix: `pane_stack_slide.limitFade` - a strip card's `collapse` moves at most a
third per frame, stopping exactly on the follower's own line;
`Slide.fade` comes from `strip_face.fadeOf(scale)` at both plan sites in
`Window.planStackSlide`. A 16.7 ms frame closes 31% of the way, under the cap,
so at the display rate the glide is unchanged. The frame rate itself is T2448.

## Validation criteria

- [x] unit: a fold at 70 ms frames crosses the fade in 2+ partial frames, none more than 1/3, and lands on its strip; an unfold at 100 ms frames likewise; at 16.7 ms frames the capped glide equals the uncapped one frame for frame; `limitFade` stops on the line (4 tests, win32 lane `-Filter T2442`; red with the cap disabled)
- [x] on-box: `stack-fold-slide.ps1 -SlowFrames` (F15, every glide tick forced to 80 ms at Mac's 45 ms glide): face alphas 85 > 170 > 255, ALL PASS 7; the same run on a build with the cap disabled logs 202 > 255 and FAILS F15 twice - the user's log shape
- [x] no regression: default `stack-fold-slide.ps1` ALL PASS 33; `floor-lane.ps1 -Lane all` ALL LANES PASS
- [ ] release-build WGC capture of a fold: content luminance falls over 2+ frames as the name rises - moved to T2416 (an input-desktop capture cannot run while the user is present, go.md)
