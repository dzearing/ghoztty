# T2704 - Dropping a dragged pane on a divider does nothing on Windows; Mac now inserts the pane in that gap with a third of the split space

## In plain terms

On the Mac you can now drop a pane right onto the line between two panes, and
it slots in between them. On Windows letting go over that line does nothing.
After this lands, the divider is a drop target on Windows too.

## Summary

main intake 2026-11-04, from 30d13ea14. Mac's new row in the drop table:

| Where released | Result |
|---|---|
| A **divider** (10pt either side of the gap's middle) | **Insert** in its gap, spanning its length, with a third of the space its split had; the two sides each give up their share |

- Precedence: the window edge band beats a divider, and a divider beats the
  pane under it.
- A divider hides between panes overlapping in a stack - there is no gap to
  drop in (`PaneDropCandidate.Divider.all`).
- The hover preview fills the insert area with seams where the new dividers run.

Windows today: `src/apprt/win32/pane_drop.zig` resolves "inside the window but
between panes" to nothing (test "a gap between panes is a divider and resolves
to nothing"). Port the resolver rule (pure, unit-tested in the `none` lane)
and the tree insert, plus the preview.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] pane_drop.zig unit tests: divider band 10 DIP either side of the gap's middle resolves to an insert; edge band beats divider; divider beats pane; stacked panes have no divider target
- [ ] Split-tree insert gives the new pane a third of the split's space, taken equally from both sides (unit test)
- [ ] Acceptance: dragging a pane onto a divider in A | B gives A | C | B with the expected ratios
- [ ] Preview shows the inserted area with seams
