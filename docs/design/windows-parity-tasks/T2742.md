# T2742 - Composition step 4d-3: with card shadow visuals live the desk compose lays no card shadow, so a glide that only moves cards recomposes nothing on the desk; oracle a desk-recompose counter reading 0 across a glide, and a WGC capture of a resting card's shadow matching the GDI desk

## Summary

Third split of T2511: the payoff - zero desk recomposes while cards glide.

**Re-verified 2026-11-07: the title's premise is false.** With the shadows
riding (T2741) the desk still lays every card's fill, hairline rim and
antialiased rounded edge: the pane visual's clip is the child's client rect,
one rim inset in (`Window.cardInner`, `CardRegionPlan.treeShape`), so the rim
and the corners outside the clip are desk pixels. `PaneDesk.paint` matches its
memo on `cards`, so a card that moves still recomposes, shadow or no shadow.
Zero recomposes needs the card's BODY to leave the desk too.

So this task became the picture that lets it (as T2740 was for the shadow),
and the wiring plus the counter/film oracle moved to **T2793**:
`shadow_layer.Body` + `Key.withBody` + `renderKey`'s body path render the
card's whole compose - its shadow, fill, viewer page, head band, resting rim
and edge - as one premultiplied layer, by composing the card over a black
desk and a white one (the compose is `cov * colour + (1 - cov) * k * desk`, so
black gives the premultiplied colour and the step to white is what of the
desk shows). Null on glass, whose sheet is the desk seen through a tint.
`clearUnderCard` now scales every premultiplied channel, so a neighbour mask
clears a body's colour as well as a shadow's black (a no-op for shadow-only
layers, whose colour is 0).

## In plain terms

Each pane sits on the coloured desk as a raised card: a soft shadow, rounded
corners and a thin outline. Moving the shadows onto the compositor (the last
step) was meant to let cards slide without repainting the desk, but the
outline and the rounded corners were still painted into the desk, so it still
had to repaint. This step makes the picture of the whole card - outline,
corners and shadow together - so the next step can slide the card as one
piece. Nothing changes on screen yet.
