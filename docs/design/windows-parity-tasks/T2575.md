# T2575 - With no redirection surface (GHOZTTY_COMP_NOREDIR) the whole client still lands one drag step after the frame: time the size message from the frame change to its last commit against the film, and find what puts the new size and the content in one compose

## Summary

T2548 filmed GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME+PRESENT with and without NOREDIR (ReleaseSafe): redirected 41/60/41/65 bad of 65-68 frames, no-redirection 35/66/42/76 of 77-84. Without the surface the page and its address bar always agree and the shape is pure lag: right=2 on shrink is the whole client exactly one 12 px step behind the frame, even the layered nav-bar child moved inside the same WM_SIZE. So DWM shows the new frame before the size message finishes. Measure (GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE QPC vs frames.txt compose times) when the frame change lands against the last commit of the pass, then try what can hold the frame to the content: a size message short enough to fit before the next compose, DwmFlush/compositor-clock pacing now that the chrome is one commit, or presenting the panes and the tree at the new size before the window takes it. Re-film with scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1.
