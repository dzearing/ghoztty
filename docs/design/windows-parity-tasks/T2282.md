# T2282 - When a Windows glass pane with a banner collapses into its strip, the thinner sheet under the banner card must fade with the banner, or the strip shows a pale patch (Mac 8aedaae61)

## Summary

Mac 8aedaae61: a glass pane's sheet is thinner under its banner card; that patch did not fade with the banner as the pane went into its strip, so the strip showed a pale rectangle where the card had been. The card's preference now carries how much of the banner is showing, scaled by the pane's collapse, and the patch fades with it (test aStripWithABannerIsTheSameGlassTopToBottom). Windows does not yet thin the sheet under the banner (T2255); when it does, it must scale the thinning by the collapse fraction from the start.
