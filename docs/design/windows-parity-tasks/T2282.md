# T2282 - When a Windows glass pane with a banner collapses into its strip, the thinner sheet under the banner card must fade with the banner, or the strip shows a pale patch (Mac 8aedaae61)

## Summary

Mac 8aedaae61: a glass pane's sheet is thinner under its banner card; that patch did not fade with the banner as the pane went into its strip, so the strip showed a pale rectangle where the card had been. The card's preference now carries how much of the banner is showing, scaled by the pane's collapse, and the patch fades with it (test aStripWithABannerIsTheSameGlassTopToBottom). Windows does not yet thin the sheet under the banner (T2255); when it does, it must scale the thinning by the collapse fraction from the start.

## In plain terms

On a Mac, a pane folded away into a thin strip at the side of the window could show a pale rectangle at the top of the strip, where its status banner used to be. Windows must not show that patch either. On Windows the banner and the lighter glass behind it are one floating piece that disappears whenever its pane is folded away, so the strip is the same glass from top to bottom; this task proves that with a check on the real app.

## Goals

- [x] Confirm how Windows carries the thinner sheet under a banner card, and whether a folded pane can leave it behind
- [x] An on-box check, Mac's `aStripWithABannerIsTheSameGlassTopToBottom`: a tucked pane with a banner shows no card over its strip and the strip is the same glass where the card was as further down
- [x] A positive control proving the check can see a banner card when one is there

## Validation criteria

- [x] No banner card is on show over a tucked pane's strip - `pane-stack-strip.ps1 -PaneStyle elevated -Glass` D8 PASS (0 `GhozttyBannerOverlay` windows over the strip)
- [x] The strip reads the same glass 30 DIP down (where the card sat) as 150 DIP down, beyond what the desk beside it changes - D8 PASS: `rgb(25,29,35)` vs `rgb(24,29,35)`, desk beside `rgb(17,20,24)` at both rows
- [x] Positive control - D8 PASS: the same pane clicked on show has its banner card back (1 window), and the same two-row comparison down that card differs by 72 levels
- [x] Harness floor (scoped) PASS 278s, stamped
