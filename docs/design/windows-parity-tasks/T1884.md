# T1884 - Glass Windows panes still sit under an opaque banner strip and rearrange header: Mac drops the banner's base fill so the desk reads through it

## Summary

Follow-up of T1833 (main 8174d96fc). T1833 made the terminal itself a glass sheet; the sticky banner strip (layered popup, BannerOverlay) and the rearrange header band (parent FillRect) above a pane still paint an opaque base fill, so a pane with a banner shows a solid band over a glass card. Mac removed the banner strip's base fill in the glass style. Give both bands the glass mix of the desk under them (pane_elevation.glassMix over the PaneDesk slice) when Window.paneGlass() is on; pairs with T1851 (their corners). Validate with capture-pane + PrintWindow like the glass run in test\win32\pane-elevation.ps1, with +set-banner and rearrange mode on.

## In plain terms

With see-through ("glass") panes on, the coloured backdrop shows through each
terminal, but two strips above a pane were still painted as solid blocks: the
sticky banner strip and the header that appears while you rearrange panes. They
looked like opaque lids on a glass card. Now both let the backdrop through the
way the Mac app does, so the whole pane reads as one sheet of glass.

## Goals

- [x] The banner strip's margin round its floating card is the pane's glass, and the card is a light wash over that glass rather than a solid fill
- [x] The rearrange header is the chrome bar colour as a material over the glass (70% bar), with the desk's colour carried through; a carried (dragged) pane keeps its solid accent band
- [x] Solid panes (`macos-pane-glass = false`) and the flat style are unchanged

Note on the header check: on the dark ocean desk the glass band is only ~4
levels off the bar (the material is 70% bar), so the assertion is "matches the
glass model and is nearer it than the opaque bar", and it SKIPs rather than
passes on a desk within 3 levels of the bar. Pre-fix code paints the bar
itself, which fails it.
