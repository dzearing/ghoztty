# T1483 - A 63-hour loop outage was reported as a bare stall 716 times while the reason sat in the transcript

## In plain terms

The loop went quiet for two and a half days and the terminal gave no hint why.
It was not broken and nothing had crashed: the account had hit its monthly spend
limit, so every attempt to get it working again was refused instantly by Claude
itself, and the loop started again on its own the minute the limit reset. The
supervisor watching it repeated the same useless rescue 180 times and reported
nothing but "no turn has completed", which is a symptom, not an answer. Now both
status readouts say the real thing — "blocked: you've hit your monthly spend
limit, clears 2026-09-12 01:00" — and they count how many rescues have already
achieved nothing, so a repetition this size can never look like the first one.

## Goals

- [x] Both supervisors name an EXTERNAL blocker (a spend/usage limit, a 5xx)
      instead of narrating the stall, and say when it clears.
- [x] Repeated re-entries inside one unmoved turn are counted and escalate into
      a single loud line, rather than 180 identical ones.
- [x] The count belongs to a turn, so a completed turn clears it.
- [x] One classifier, called by the watchdog and by the health line, so the
      actor and the observer cannot disagree (AF's rule).
- [x] Acceptance coverage with negative controls in both directions.

## Summary

**The loop did no work between 2026-09-09 09:46 and 2026-09-12 01:11 — 63.5
hours.** The watchdog logged `STALLED(by=turn)` 716 times over that gap, with
`turn_age` climbing from 604.8m to 3,804.5m against a 180m limit, and it nudged
the pane 180 times. None of it helped.

**Root cause (verified from the session transcript, not the watchdog log): the
loop was never wedged.** Claude Code had hit the account's **monthly spend
limit**, and every one of those 180 nudges was answered inside a second with

> You've hit your monthly spend limit · raise it at claude.ai/settings/usage ·
> your weekly limit resets Sep 12, 1am (America/Los_Angeles)

Those transcript entries carry `isApiErrorMessage: true` and
`quotaLimits.resetsAt = 1789200000` — which is **2026-09-12 01:00 local**, the
minute the loop came back. The 01:11 nudge worked because the quota reset, not
because it differed from the 179 before it.

**Two of the three diagnoses this task was filed with do not survive contact
with the code**, and are recorded here so nobody re-implements them:

- *The rearm gate is not starvable by the stalled session.* Its input is
  `Get-MinutesSinceAction` — time since the watchdog's own last **action** — not
  `age(by=transcript)`. The 5m/10m/15m readings are that clock restarting after
  each nudge; the two numbers matched only because typing into the pane also
  touches the transcript. The watchdog was re-entering every ~20 minutes exactly
  as designed.
- *`go-loop-health.ps1` does not print `HEALTHY` over a stale turn clock.*
  T1290's gate is at `scripts\go-loop-health.ps1:358` and is covered by sections
  AA and AF of `test\win32\go-loop-guard.ps1`; a fixture with `turn_age=14.5h`
  reads `DEGRADED`.

**What was actually missing is the third bullet, and it is the whole defect:**
the watchdog repeated one ineffective action 180 times and neither supervisor
could say **why** — while the reason sat, machine-readable, in the transcript
the lock already names.

So:

- `Resolve-LoopBlocker` (`scripts\loop-session.ps1`) classifies an externally
  blocked session from the transcript — `usage-limit` (a quota, with the instant
  it clears, from the epoch where there is one and from the prose where there is
  not) or `api-error` (transport, 5xx) — with the pane tail as the fallback for a
  lock with no transcript. Only the session's **most recent** answer counts, so a
  blocker it has already recovered from is history rather than a park.
- The watchdog prints `BLOCKED(usage-limit, by=transcript): … - clears
  2026-09-12 01:00` on the decision line, and keeps a **repetition clock** keyed
  on `turn_started`: past `-IneffectiveLimit` (3) re-entries inside one unmoved
  turn it logs `RECOVERY INEFFECTIVE: N re-entries … Typing cannot clear it; the
  loop resumes when it clears.` A completed turn resets the count.
- `go-loop-health.ps1` carries `blocked=<kind|no>` on its one line, the same
  three fields in `-Json` for the dashboard, and a note quoting the message —
  through the **same** classifier, per AF's rule that the actor and the observer
  must not reach different conclusions about one session.
- The retry itself is unchanged: nudging is still how the loop discovers the
  block has lifted. What changed is that it can no longer do it silently.
