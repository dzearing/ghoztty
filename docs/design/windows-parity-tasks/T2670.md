# T2670 - After collapsing and expanding blades, an expanded pane keeps an unpainted band along its bottom edge (a flat gray strip ~120px tall under the terminal in the right pane, a thin strip in the left), so the pane's content never reaches its bottom again

## Summary

User report, 2026-11-03 16:15 (screenshot in the lane-b session): "after collapsing and expanding some panes, they have artifacts on the bottom of the pane (see right bottom panes)". Two cmd.exe panes side by side, one with a banner. After a fold/unfold, the right pane shows the window gradient for most of its height and then a flat, opaque gray band (`~#282c34`, ~120 px at 1x) across its full width at the bottom. The left pane has the same defect, smaller: a thin flat strip just above its bottom border. The band does not repaint by itself afterwards.

Likely shape (unverified): after an unfold, the pane's surface or swap chain stays at an intermediate glide size, or the band fill/backdrop under the grid is painted for the old rows and the expanded area is left as the plain card fill. Compare against T2459 (open cards hold their content size during a drag and resize once) and T2566 (banner panes span their whole slot on the composition path): the larger band is on the pane that has a banner.

Same report also said the banner still does not paint during the expand; that half is T2661 (note added there).

## In plain terms

Folding panes away and opening them again leaves a gray strip along the bottom of the panes that come back. The terminal never draws into that strip until something else redraws the pane.
