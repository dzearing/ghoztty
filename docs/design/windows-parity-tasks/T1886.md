# T1886 - The Windows title bar and tab strip are an opaque band above the desk: Mac runs the window gradient up under the titlebar with glass panes

## Summary

Follow-up of T1833 (main 8174d96fc: titlebarAppearsTransparent + fullSizeContentView, so the window is one surface). On Windows the custom caption and tab strip paint their own chrome color above the desk. With glass panes on, paint the desk (the window's variant) under the caption band and draw the tabs over it, keeping the T306 chrome-contrast floor (caption text >= 4.5:1 over the gradient, or a scrim). Experience-visible: if contrast cannot hold on some variants, that is a decision to file. Validate with a PrintWindow capture of the caption band (desk pixels, readable title).

## In plain terms

On a Mac with glass panes, the window's colourful background runs all the
way up behind the title bar and the tabs, so the whole window reads as one
sheet. On Windows the title bar and tab strip were a flat grey band sitting
on top of it. Now the background runs up under them too, the tabs and
buttons sit on it, and the title stays as easy to read as before.

## Goals

- [x] With glass panes the caption band and the tab strip show the window's own gradient, laid out over the whole window as on Mac
- [x] Tabs, the "+" and the caption buttons draw over it unchanged; the gradient meets the margin under the chrome with no step
- [x] The title keeps its 4.5:1 floor: a bar scrim at the least strength that clears it wherever it would not (zero on every shipped gradient)
- [x] Solid panes, the flat style, translucent windows and hero tabs keep the solid bar

## Decisions recorded

- **The desk runs under the chrome, rather than the chrome sampling a desk
  laid out below it.** Mac's gradient is laid out over the full window
  (`.ignoresSafeArea(.top)`), so `deskRect` is the whole client area with
  glass panes and the chrome painters copy their rows of the same cached
  desk (`chrome_glass.fillBand`). The desk blit is clipped off the chrome
  rows so it never paints over them; the pane sidebar's slice is offset by
  the chrome height (`Backdrop.below`); terminals, viewers and banners take
  their slices relative to the desk, so they follow without change.
- **Contrast is guaranteed by measurement, not by palette.** The title's
  floor is checked against the band's own pixels and the bar is laid over
  them in 10% steps until it clears, so a chrome theme that disagrees with
  the gradient's light/dark flavour (`window-theme = light` on a dark
  terminal) falls back toward the solid bar instead of shipping unreadable
  text. With the default theme the two always agree and the scrim is zero
  (unit-tested across 64 gradients x 5 backgrounds).
- **Tabs keep their chiclets.** The active tab is still the terminal
  background and inactive ones a lift of the bar, now over the gradient -
  the same material-over-glass treatment as the rearrange header (T1884).
