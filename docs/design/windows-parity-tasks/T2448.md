# T2448 - Under a divider drag the fold glide steps at 10-15 fps in the user's own window (release 1.49.6, a viewer pane in the row resized on every pass, passes 60-100 ms apart): measure the per-pass cost with a viewer pane and get glide frames back to the display rate

## Summary

Found working T2442 (2026-10-28). The user's installed release log
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ghoztty\ghoztty.log`, 2026-10-29T05:20:19-22Z) shows a
divider drag folding panes in a 5-pane window with a viewer pane in the row:
the viewer's `viewer nav tips` line - written each time a pass resizes it - lands
every 60-100 ms, and every logged fold showed its strip face first at alpha
175-255, i.e. one glide step crossed most of the 120-DIP fade. A 45 ms follower
stepping at 60-100 ms closes 75-90% of the way per frame, so the glide reads as a
jump regardless of how it is drawn. T2442 capped the fade at a third per frame so
the content can no longer pop; this task is the frame rate itself.

What to find out: what a drag pass costs with a WebView2 viewer pane in the row
(controller bounds, the frame wait batch over the other panes, the strip face
DIB), whether the glide's FrameClock ticks are starved by the mouse-move passes
(each move is a full `layoutSplitsLive`), and whether a pass that only moves
cards still resizes the viewer. Related: T2143/T2241 (8-pane drag budget, no
viewer), T2373 (frame lab), T2360 (composition). Measure with `GHOZTTY_PERF`'s
`drag ticks=` line on a row that includes a viewer pane (drag-perf.ps1 has no
viewer variant today), on the release build where possible.

## In plain terms

Dragging a divider far enough that panes fold into strips made the fold
animation stutter at about 15 frames a second. Every animation frame was being
drawn twice over, plus once more for each mouse move. Now each frame is drawn
once, and the remaining cost (the soft card shadows, which are slow on big
windows) has its own task.

## Goals

- [x] measure it: per-pass cost broken down, glide frames timed, with and without a viewer pane
- [x] name the cause of the 60-100 ms gaps
- [x] fix the terms that double each frame
- [x] split what is left into its own task (T2450)

## Findings (2026-10-29, debug build, test desktop, 1600x900, 5-pane row, viewer second)

New perf lines at button-up under `GHOZTTY_PERF`: `divider glide ticks=...`
(`drag_perf.GlideStats`: gap between glide ticks, cost, late frames, the viewer's
share, deferred moves, window `WM_PAINT`s, desk composes vs reuses) and
`divider drag viewers=...` (viewer `syncBounds` split into nav / controller
bounds / TOC). Reproduced the user's numbers: **glide 16 fps, mean gap 62 ms, max
103 ms, 232 of 234 ticks late.**

- **The viewer pane is not the cause.** Same numbers with a 5th terminal in its
  place (`fps=16 mean_gap_us=61559`). A viewer resize costs ~2.8 ms debug (nav
  bar place 1.9 ms, controller bounds 0.3 ms, TOC 0.6 ms).
- **The cost is the elevated desk paint.** `mean_paint_us=26108` of a 26937 us
  drag tick; inside it `paintPaneDesk` -> `pane_elevation.paintCards` ~22-24 ms
  debug. ReleaseFast bench: 6.4 ms at 1582x891@1x, 22.4 ms at 3800x2050@2x, ~90% of
  it the card shadows (T2450 has the table).
- **And every glide frame paid it twice or three times.** Glide tick pass (34 ms)
  + the window's `WM_PAINT` (27.6 ms, `wm_paints=238` for 240 ticks) recomposing the
  identical desk = the 62 ms gap; the mouse-move passes queued in between on top.

Fixes:
1. `layoutDragStep`: a divider motion tick under a running glide leaves its
   layout to the glide's next frame (`deferred_moves=120`); a motion still
   pending when the drag ends and the glide stops is laid out at button-up.
2. `PaneDesk` memo: a compose whose inputs (cards, strips, style, glows, bar,
   light geometry, desk generation, size) equal the last one's blits the cached
   composite; busy-light rings are still re-applied at the paint's head.
3. After the layout pass paints the elevated desk through `GetDC`, it validates
   the desk rect below the chrome, so the paint its own pane moves queued has
   nothing to redraw (`wm_paints` 238 -> 1). Flat style untouched (its divider
   paint skips unsplit/zoomed tabs).

After: **mean gap 48 ms, max 62 ms (was 62 / 103), wm_paints=1.** 48 ms is three
vsync periods for a 33 ms debug tick: the tick is now the whole bill, and the
tick is T2450's shadows.
