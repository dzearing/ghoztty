# T1957 - Low-power auto-switch re-loads the config into every pane: per-pane overrides (wait-after-command of +new-remote-window --command) are re-derived when a no-GPU launch or an RDP switch flips the effects

## In plain terms

When Ghoztty switches its visual effects off or on by itself (a PC with no
graphics card at startup, or a Remote Desktop connect/disconnect), it re-reads
its settings into every pane. A pane opened with special one-off settings, such
as a remote window told to stay open after its command ends, could lose those
settings at that moment.

## Summary

`App.refreshLowPower` (T1951) re-loads the config and calls
`core_app.updateConfig`, which re-derives every surface from the app config -
the same path as a user's hard reload. The `.reload_config` handler's own
comment records why that is dangerous for per-surface overrides
(`+new-remote-window --command` sets `wait-after-command` on its surface only).
On a no-GPU box this now happens once on every launch, shortly after the first
GL context, so a restored remote window could be affected.

Options: re-apply the per-surface overrides after the push (the surface keeps
them in `pending_surface_overrides` style), or push only the effects keys to
each surface on top of its OWN current config instead of the app config.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] An acceptance arm: `+new-remote-window --command` (or a local
      `wait-after-command` override) survives a forced low-power flip
      (`GHOZTTY_TEST_REMOTE_SESSION_FILE` + `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE`), and the
      window stays open after its command exits.
