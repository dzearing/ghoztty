# T1536 - A pane drag says nothing about itself: no drag cursor and no mark on the pane being moved

## Summary

T1531 landed the drag and the drop preview, but the gesture gives no feedback about its SOURCE. The cursor stays an arrow for the whole drag (nothing answers WM_SETCURSOR while rearrange_drag is live), and the pane being carried looks exactly like the panes that are staying put - so in a four-pane window, a user who looks away mid-drag cannot tell which pane is in their hand. Mac's rearrange mode marks the dragged pane and changes the cursor. Two small arms on state that already exists: a WM_SETCURSOR arm in Window.zig's wndproc keyed on rearrange_drag, and a dimmed / outlined treatment for the source pane's header while its drag is active (the header paint already knows which view it is drawing). Validation is the existing rearrange-drag.ps1 shape plus a cursor-handle assertion.

## In plain terms

In rearrange mode you can pick a pane up by its header bar and move it, but
until now nothing told you the pane was in your hand: the pointer stayed an
ordinary arrow, and the pane you were carrying looked exactly like the ones
staying put. Now the pointer turns into the four-way "move" arrow while you
drag (and while you hover the bar, to say it can be grabbed), and the header
of the pane you are carrying lights up in your accent color until you let go.

## Goals

- [x] The pointer says "this moves" over a header's grab surface and for the whole of a live drag.
- [x] The pane being carried is visibly marked for as long as the drag is live, and only then.
- [x] The mark comes off on the drop and on a cancel; a click that never travels marks nothing.

## Mac parity, and the translation

Mac (`SurfaceDragSource.swift`) shows `NSCursor.openHand` over the grab handle
and `closedHand` for the drag, and hands the drag a scaled snapshot of the
pane that follows the pointer. Windows has no open/closed hand pair -
`IDC_HAND` is the LINK pointer and would promise a click - so both states take
`IDC_SIZEALL`, the pointer Windows itself shows when a window is moved from the
keyboard. Mac's "which pane is in my hand" answer is the snapshot riding the
pointer; the Windows answer is the carried pane's header painted in the accent
(with `on_accent` ink for the grip, title and pop-out glyph), which stays put
where the eye left it. The pop-out button keeps the arrow at rest: it is the
one control in the band that does not drag.

  `Treatment` / `treatment`, 4 new `none`-lane tests.
- `src/apprt/win32/Window.zig` - the header paint takes the treatment;
  `updateRearrangeDrag` repaints on activation and sets the pointer every move
  (the capture keeps `WM_SETCURSOR` away for the whole drag);
  `endRearrangeDrag` repaints to take the mark off; `WM_SETCURSOR` answers for
  a live drag and for header hover; the `GhozttyDragCursor` window property

  `WM_DESTROY`), because no test on the background desktop can read the real
  cursor (T228). A win32-lane test pins `Cursor.move.idc()` to `w32.IDC_SIZEALL`.

- `test/win32/rearrange-drag.ps1` - claims F (pointer) and G (mark).
