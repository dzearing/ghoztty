# T2216 - printclient-audit enforces WM_PRINTCLIENT arms that the synchronous capture never calls, and dark-menus takes the torn capture on the same false premise

## In plain terms

Some of the screenshot checks in the test suite were built on a wrong belief
about how Windows takes a picture of a window. One audit forces every window
to carry code that the picture-taking never actually runs, and at least one
check takes the less reliable kind of picture because it thought the reliable
kind was impossible. Nothing a user sees changes; the tests get more honest.

## Summary

T1287 measured on a background desktop that `PrintWindow(hwnd, dc, 0)` -
the `-Sync` capture in `test/win32/lib/TestDesktop.ps1` and the in-app hover
capture in `src/apprt/win32/ipc_hover.zig` - sends **neither `WM_PRINT` nor
`WM_PRINTCLIENT`** to popup, layered or child windows. It re-runs the window's
`WM_PAINT` into a surface of its own and copies that over the caller's DC. A
fixture with a `WM_PAINT`-only red fill captured red; one with a
`WM_PRINTCLIENT`-only green fill never showed green.

Two things still rest on the old premise:

1. `test/win32/printclient-audit.ps1` (T940) sections A/B treat "a class with a
   `WM_PAINT` arm and no `WM_PRINTCLIENT` arm" as a finding ("paints but cannot
   pose"). Under the measured mechanism that is not a capture defect at all. The
   audit should either be re-based on what the sync capture really needs, or
   retired with the reason recorded, keeping section C (the call-site rule
   for `-Sync`/`-TornReason`, which is still valid).
2. `-TornReason` call sites that justify the async capture by "does not answer
   WM_PRINTCLIENT" - `test/win32/dark-menus.ps1:92` (user32 popup menu
   `#32768`) is the first. Try `-Sync` on each. Where it reproduces the pixels,
   switch to it and drop the reason; where it does not, rewrite the reason to
   name the real cause.

Also check whether the `WM_PRINTCLIENT` arms in `Window.zig` and
`BannerOverlay.zig` are reachable by anything else before calling them dead.
Removing them is a product change and needs its own evidence.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] printclient-audit.ps1 no longer reports a finding the sync capture cannot hit, and it stays green (or is retired with its guard-due row)
- [ ] every `-TornReason` that cites WM_PRINTCLIENT is re-tested under `-Sync`, then converted or re-reasoned
- [ ] the harness floor stays green
