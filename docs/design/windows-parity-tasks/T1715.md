## Resolution

Not a regression, and not T1711: BB17's fixture turn is 200m, past the 180m
backstop where the background-wait exemption stops applying (AI4 proves that).
The red came from running go-loop-guard at the same time as the loop's own
acceptance run, which T401 forbids. A clean-slate reset in the other run killed
the debug app. Section L lost its windows, and the watchdog in BB17 then saw no
app and correctly took `launch-app` rather than `new-window`. BB17 now accepts
either last-resort action, because its subject is the decision to re-enter.
H11 separately covers the `launch-app` choice. Section L prints a NOTE when the
app stops answering.
