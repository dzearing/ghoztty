# T2798 - Composition step 5d-3b: the banner card's collapse/expand runs on the compositor - today each frame re-renders the card at its animated height with the body dissolve and the tail fade, so it needs a layered card first (a clip-animated body visual plus an offset-animated bottom edge, body alpha as an opacity animation) before the COLLAPSE_TIMER_ID tick can go

## Summary

Split from T2763, which moved the frozen terminal copy's glide onto the
compositor (`BannerFreeze.glideOnCompositor`). The card itself is still
stepped: `onCollapseTick` (BannerOverlay.zig, `COLLAPSE_TIMER_ID` on
FrameClock) re-renders the whole card each ~16 ms at
`paintedCardHeight()` with `paintedBodyAlpha()` and `paintCollapseFade`, and
re-uploads it to the banner layer. None of that is an offset or a clip of
one bitmap: the rounded bottom corners and shadow move with the height, the
body dissolves, and the tail fade sits at the moving edge.

Design to settle first: render the card at the larger of the two heights
once, split into (a) the card body (content + fill) clipped by an animated
clip rect on its visual, (b) the bottom edge strip (rounded corners, shadow,
tail fade) as its own visual with an animated offset Y on the same curve
(`banner_layout.collapseHeight`'s ease, via `comp_overlay.fitCurve` /
`tailOf`), and (c) the body below the collapsed line as a visual whose
opacity animates on `collapseBodyAlpha`'s curve. The grid band must not
relayout per frame (it already reserves the settled height once, T149).
Needs `Layer` support for an animated clip (IDCompositionRectangleClip).

Until this lands, under a UI stall the copy glides on while the card holds
still (T2763's note); with no stall they stay in step.

Oracle: comp-present-style declared/ui_steps log rows (stepped A/B seam,
like T2763's J10/J11), `pane-banner.ps1`'s T149/T833/T677 sections still
green, and a stall-seam film on an idle box.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The collapse and the expand are declared to the compositor once (log oracle), ui_steps=0 on the visual path
- [ ] A stepped A/B seam reddens exactly those rows
- [ ] pane-banner.ps1 T149/T833/T677 sections stay green (card height, no stale expand frame, body dissolve)
- [ ] Film under a UI stall on an idle box: the card keeps moving
