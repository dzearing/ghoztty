# T3805 - Linux: a pane stashed at relaunch in a tab that is not on show never starts its terminal

## Summary

Follow-up of T3667. A GTK terminal starts when its GL area is first realized, and a stashed pane gets no card, so SplitTree.adoptRecordedStash waits (PendingStash, 50 ms ticks, 10 s cap) until every recorded pane has started before re-stashing. A restored tab that is NOT the selected one never realizes its panes until it is shown, so the wait times out and the stash applies over a pane that never started: its shell never runs and its agent session is never re-attached until the user restores the pane by hand (and the next capture may then record it with no session id). Fix: start a stashed pane's core surface without a card (or keep the wait open per tab until the tab is first shown), and extend test/linux/session-restore.sh with a stashed pane in a second, unselected tab: after a SIGKILL relaunch it is stashed AND +list reports its re-attached pid. Also: during the wait (up to the first frames after restore) the once-a-second capture records the pane as on screen; a crash in that window loses the stash.
