# T2872 - Opening the pane sidebar, the gutter beside the grid gapes ~90 px for the first ~80 ms and then runs ~9 px tight: the declared column's crossfade starts near-invisible and its clipped edge drops the card's resting margin (comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 -Arms sidebar .24d)

## Summary

Found by T2855's film (comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 -Arms sidebar): two flat panes beside the rail, the toggle expands the sidebar, and the gutter between the sidebar card's visible right edge and the leading pane's prompt is measured every frame (14 px at rest). The declared column (T2827) crossfades the panel frame over the rail frame starting at opacity ~0.09, so for the first ~80 ms the wider card is too faint to see (within 6 levels of the background) and its visible edge stays at the rail while the grid moves ~90 px: the gutter reads 24, 35, 46 ... 104 px. Once visible, the edge is the clip, cut at the column's width with no margin, while at rest the card stands ~16 px inside its window: the gutter runs 5-7 px against 14 at rest until the landing. .24d (within 2 px of rest on every frame) is red declared (worst 90 px) AND with -StallMs 0 (worst 83 px), so it is the crossfade's shape, not the stall's. Compare against Mac (PaneStageView.didStep / the column's card): its card edge rides the grid's with the gutter constant. Fix shape: start the crossfade where the new card already reads (or draw the panel frame's card at the clip with its margin and corner), so the visible edge is the declared edge from the first frame.

## In plain terms

When you open the pane sidebar, the panes start sliding right a moment before the sidebar's panel becomes visible, so a wide empty gap flashes between them. Then, for the rest of the slide, the panel's edge runs a little too close to the panes. On a Mac the gap stays the same width the whole way.
