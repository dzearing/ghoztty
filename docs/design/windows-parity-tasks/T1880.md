# T1880 - The pane sidebar's scope switch and folds snap on Windows: Mac slides the list sideways between this window and all windows, and reveals a folding group top-down

## Summary

Split from T1829. Mac animates the sidebar's list: switching between this
window and all windows slides like a navigation push - this window's list lives
on the LEFT, all windows on the RIGHT, each sliding 48 pt and fading over 0.3 s
on an ease-out (0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) - and folding a group reveals or covers its
rows from the top down over 0.22 s while the rows below slide to their place.
The Windows outline snaps. Wanted: the same two motions in the panel and open
card, honoring the system's "show animations" setting (SPI_GETCLIENTAREAANIMATION).

## In plain terms

In the pane sidebar, switching between "this window" and "all windows", or
folding a group of panes open and shut, used to jump instantly on Windows. On
a Mac the list slides sideways when you switch, and a folding group unrolls
or rolls up from the top while the rows beneath glide into place. Windows now
moves the same way, for the same length of time. With "Animation effects" off
in Windows settings (or over Remote Desktop) it still just jumps.

## Goals

- [x] the scope switch slides 48 DIP and crossfades over 0.3 s on Mac's ease-out: to all windows the old list leaves left and the new arrives from the right, back is the mirror
- [x] a fold reveals / covers the group top-down over 0.22 s while the rows below slide, with header and material holding still
- [x] both motions in the pinned panel and the open (layered, glass) card
- [x] animations off (or a remote session): both land with no transition

## Design (win32)

SwiftUI animates two live view trees; GDI has one frame. So on the click the
surface is **pictured** as presented (`snapshot`: the card in each of its
three paints - plain, and over white and black for its glass), the change is
made, and for 0.3 / 0.22 s each paint composes three frames: the picture
from before, the frame after painted live, and the bare material
(`paint_bare`: no header, no rows). A list moves as its difference from the
material, laid back over the material where it lands, so the glass, the
desk gradient and the card's edge hold still while the rows travel - the
material is what Mac's list slides OVER. Fold geometry is measured at paint
time against the list after the change (the folded head's bottom and the
content's bottom); if the head moved - the list re-scrolled under the fold -
the transition is dropped and the fold simply lands.

- `pane_sidebar_transition.zig` (pure, every lane): Mac's bezier, the slide
  offsets/opacities and the slide/fold compositors, unit-tested.
- `pane_sidebar_xfade.zig`: the running transition and its pictures.
- `PaneSidebar.zig`: `paint` wraps `paintFrame`; `snapshot` /
  `startTransition` / `onTransitionTick` / `paintTransition`; a 16 ms
  `TIMER_XFADE` per surface.
