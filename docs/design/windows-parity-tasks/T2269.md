# T2269 - A Windows card going into its strip vanishes and a dark band appears at the end of the glide; on Mac the card narrows into its strip, its content fading out and its title fading in (Mac a81db68f9)

## Summary

Mac PaneCardView.collapse = clamp(1 - (min(w,h) - peek) / 120): a tucked card is its own glass card at strip size; the content alpha follows 1 - collapse and the strip title alpha follows collapse, both ways, with the content held at size and pinned to the far edge (glideAnchor), clipped by the card. Win32 today keeps the pane shown at full card size until the slide ends, then hides it and PaneDesk paints the band. Needs: the card target for a tucked pane = its strip rect, the held pane anchored and region-clipped (slideClip exists), and a layered fade layer over the narrowing card (DimOverlay is the precedent) carrying the strip colour + title at collapse alpha.
