# T2997 - PA-W13: the key-state pill and read-only badge re-render and reallocate on every tick and every move, and the pill ticks while its window is occluded (KeyStateIndicator.zig:567)

## Summary

PA-W13 (audit-1, T2960). KeyStateIndicator.tick (KeyStateIndicator.zig:567-571) checks only visible/layout.hidden on its 100 ms timer; each repaint re-renders the pill with a fresh CreateCompatibleDC + CreateDIBSection (:676/:688) and UpdateLayeredWindow. update repaints on a pure move (:300/:322) and calls SetWindowPos unconditionally (:310). ReadonlyBadge.zig:189/208 repaints on moves and measures its label through GetDC(null) on every update (:175, :226-235). Only while a key sequence is pending or a pane is read-only. Fix: keep the DIB; skip ticks when !window.inUse(); adopt the chrome_fanout move/blit guards Scrollbar uses. Risk low.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] pill ticks stop while its window is occluded/minimized
- [ ] a pure move repositions without re-rendering (counter)
