# T2104 - With glass panes on, hero mode on Windows is the same glass card a focused pane is, not an opaque carousel

## Summary

Mac 89848fd59 (HeroPaneView): with macos-pane-glass the hero is the one pane on show, so it wears the focused pane's glass card - the container paints nothing (bgColor = .clear) and the PaneCard sheet is its background. Windows hero mode (T59a) lays its own carousel out from surfaceRect with no desk and no card, and glassChromeBand explicitly skips a hero tab. Owed: on glass, a hero tab shows the desk with the selected pane as a 40% glass card (Sheet default) and the strip over the desk; the title bar's glass band runs over it like any other glass tab. Split out of T2086.

## In plain terms

With glass panes on (the default look), hero mode - one big pane with a strip
of thumbnails beside it - used to drop the glass look entirely: the big pane
and the strip sat on flat solid colour. Now the big pane is the same
see-through rounded card a focused pane is, floating on the coloured
background, the strip is that background under a light shade, and the title
bar stays see-through too - matching the Mac.

## Goals

- [x] The hero pane is a glass card: inside the window margin, rounded and
      rimmed, its sheet laid over the desk at the hero's own position.
- [x] The carousel strip is the desk under Mac's 30% black shade; the divider
      band beside it is the bare desk.
- [x] The glass title bar (and the sidebar beside the tabs) stay glass in a
      hero tab, so toggling hero mode moves nothing above the content.
- [x] Solid panes (and flat style) keep today's hero exactly (every change is
      gated on paneGlass(); elevated-solid's margin is T2149).
