# T3001 - PA-M01: a pane keeps two full-size pixel copies of itself (snapshot buffer + DIB, 10-66 MB) for its whole life after any glide, drag ghost or hero capture (Surface.zig:2663)

## Summary

PA-M01 (audit-1, T2961). snapRequest allocates snap_buffer = w*h*4 (src/apprt/win32/Surface.zig:2655-2670) and heroSnapPublish creates a same-size DIB (:2820-2844); both are freed only in Surface.deinit (:1385-1395). Full-size requests come from PaneView.glideSnapRequest (PaneView.zig:470), glideSnapRequestExact and the drag ghost. Estimated 2*w*h*4 per pane that ever glided: 10 MB at 1400x900, 29 MB at 2560x1400, 66 MB at 4K. Fix: free both (under snap_mutex) when the glide lands and when hero mode ends; drop snap_buffer straight after publish. Risk low (re-fault on the next glide's first frame). win32-only, no Mac half. Baseline: docs/design/perf-audit/baseline-1.json (T2958); findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/M-findings.md.

## In plain terms

Every time a pane folded into a strip, opened back out, was dragged, or showed in
hero mode, Ghoztty took a full-size picture of it and then kept that picture
(twice over) until the pane was closed - 10 MB a pane at a normal window size,
66 MB at 4K. Now the picture is thrown away as soon as the animation that needed
it is over, so memory goes back down after you rearrange panes.

## Goals

- [x] the renderer's landing buffer is freed as soon as its pixels are copied out
- [x] a glide that lands gives back the frame it carried
- [x] ending hero mode gives back every pane's thumbnail
- [x] the dragged pane gives back its ghost's picture when the drag ends

## Validation criteria

- [x] a dedicated meter instead of perf-baseline: the app now logs `pane snapshot released ... buffer= dib=` when it gives the pixels back; stack-fold-slide.ps1 F20 (new) asserts the carried frame is released once the fold lands - 1 release, 2,059 KB, at the harness's window size. A private_mb before/after reading at 1400x900 was NOT taken (perf-baseline has no glide phase).
- [x] hero mode, stack fold and drag ghost still animate correctly: hero-mode.ps1 (91, incl. new "hero off: thumbnails given back", 3 panes), hero-nav, hero-viewer-tile, pane-drag-ghost (31), pane-stack-unfold (15), viewer-fold-carried (13), stack-fold-slide (47) all green via acceptance-parallel.
