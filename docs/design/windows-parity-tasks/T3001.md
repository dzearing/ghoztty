# T3001 - PA-M01: a pane keeps two full-size pixel copies of itself (snapshot buffer + DIB, 10-66 MB) for its whole life after any glide, drag ghost or hero capture (Surface.zig:2663)

## Summary

PA-M01 (audit-1, T2961). snapRequest allocates snap_buffer = w*h*4 (src/apprt/win32/Surface.zig:2655-2670) and heroSnapPublish creates a same-size DIB (:2820-2844); both are freed only in Surface.deinit (:1385-1395). Full-size requests come from PaneView.glideSnapRequest (PaneView.zig:470), glideSnapRequestExact and the drag ghost. Estimated 2*w*h*4 per pane that ever glided: 10 MB at 1400x900, 29 MB at 2560x1400, 66 MB at 4K. Fix: free both (under snap_mutex) when the glide lands and when hero mode ends; drop snap_buffer straight after publish. Risk low (re-fault on the next glide's first frame). win32-only, no Mac half. Baseline: docs/design/perf-audit/baseline-1.json (T2958); findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/M-findings.md.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] perf-baseline.ps1 -Phase cycle with a glide/fold step (or a dedicated meter): private_mb after the glide lands returns to within 1 MB of before it, at 1400x900
- [ ] hero mode, stack fold and drag ghost still animate correctly (their acceptance harnesses green)
