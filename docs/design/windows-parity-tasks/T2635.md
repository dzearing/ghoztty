# T2635 - Blades: a viewer pane opening out of its strip carries the frame its page draws at the new size, like a terminal does (T2458 viewers)

## Summary

T2460 carries a FOLDING viewer's frame; the unfold path (T2458) is still terminal-only: armGlideSnaps keys the opening carry on view.surface() and PaneView.glideSnapRequestExact returns false for a viewer, so an opening viewer is its live WebView2 under a fading face. A viewer resized while hidden needs its CapturePreview after the page has re-laid-out at the new size (and must not take an older capture), then the same glide_snap_want size check.

## In plain terms

When you click a folded markdown, HTML, website or diff pane's strip to bring
it back, the pane used to slide out as the live page with the strip's label
fading over it on a separate clock, and it snapped to its real size only at
the end. Now it slides out the way a terminal does: one picture of the page
already laid out at the size it is heading for, fading in as the card widens,
and the live page takes over at the end with no resize.
