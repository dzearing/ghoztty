# T2822 - Composition step 5c-4d: a strip card whose live pane still shows under its face glides on the compositor - the pane visual's offset/clip and the face's blend at Mac's last-120 collapse declared together on one follower (the live half T2779 split out)

## Summary

Split of T2779 (a slice of T2640). T2778 declared a FOLDING card's carried face and T2779 an OPENING card's (band until its frame lands, then the frame fading in). The third face shape is still stepped from the window's thread: a LIVE strip card (glide_snap not ready, no carried frame), whose face is blended over the live pane at strip_face.alphaOf(collapseOf(vis)) - Mac's last-120 collapse - so a busy window thread freezes it. The pane visual's offset/clip (T2572's paneGlide, skipped for strip cards in declareGlideTreePane) and the face's opacity curve must be declared together on one follower. Oracle: a comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 stall arm on a live-face fold (force glide_snap off), -NoDeclare red; stack-fold-slide.ps1 tree arms unchanged.

What landed (2026-11-09):

- `Window.liveFaceDeclarable`: a fold not carrying its frame (the frame not landed yet, or never asked for), in the composition pane tree, with pane and face both declarable. `declareGlideTreePane` now hands such a card's pane visual (offset and clip, T2572's `paneGlide`) to the compositor instead of skipping every strip card.
- `paintStripFace` declares that card's face from the fold's FIRST frame (`declareStripFace` with no carried frame and not opening = live): band and name, the whole face's strength on `strip_face.liveLine` - Mac's last-120 collapse, linear in the card's extent, unclamped, held at 0 until it rises in and at 1 once the card is narrower than a strip. Same tau and the same follower as the pane, so the two stay together through a stall (the follower is memoryless, so the face declared a frame later tracks the pane exactly).
- `chrome_comp.FaceGlide.live`: the strength animates the face CONTAINER's effect (the whole face fades over its pane) rather than a carried frame's. A live face that then takes its carried frame is made again, container and all (`declared_live`), because the container's live fade otherwise kept the carried face invisible.
- **Two pre-existing bugs fixed on the way:** (1) `ensureFaceRoot` added the face layer with `insertAbove` TRUE and no reference, which is the BOTTOM of the pane layer, so a face over a still-visible pane was hidden under it (nothing showed it before, because a fold's or opening card's pane is hidden). (2) `placeGrid` hid a folding card's live pane as soon as ANY face was shown once its frame was ready; with the face up live from the first frame that left the card blank for two frames before the carried face was declared. `StripFace.carrying` now says the face is actually carrying the frame.
- Oracle: `scripts\comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 -Arms live` (debug knob `GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLIDE_LIVE_FACE=1`: a fold never asks for its frame).

## In plain terms

When a pane folds into its strip, for the first moment the card still shows the live terminal, with the strip's colour fading in over it as it narrows. That part was still driven frame by frame by the app, so if the app was busy for a moment the folding card froze and then jumped while the panes beside it kept sliding. Now Windows runs that part too, so the fold keeps moving smoothly. (Only in the new composition rendering path.)
