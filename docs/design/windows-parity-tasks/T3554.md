# T3554 - Linux: a subframe on a GTK viewer page can post a quote, headings or a find count on the viewerTOC bridge; win32 accepts only linkMenu from a subframe

## Summary

links.js is injected into ALL frames (Viewer.zig installLinkScript), so every iframe - an ad on a website - has window.webkit.messageHandlers.viewerTOC, and WebKitGTK's script-message-received carries no frame. Viewer.onScriptMessage therefore acts on a subframe's quote (T3545: opens the composer and files the passage into the report), headings, active and find exactly as on the top frame's. win32 bounds this natively (ViewerPane.onFrameMessage acts on a subframe's linkMenu and nothing else; viewer_bridge subframe_js). Fix: give subframes their own handler name (register a second handler, inject links.js into subframes with a guard that posts there, keep the top-frame scripts on viewerTOC) or use webkit_user_content_manager_register_script_message_handler with a world and check the frame, so only linkMenu is accepted from a subframe. Test: a website fixture with an iframe that posts {type:quote} must leave the composer closed and quoted=0.
