# T2764 - Composition step 5d-4: the viewer TOC panel's slide runs on the compositor - the panel (a WS_CHILD of the viewer) needs a visual of its own first, then its offset is declared once instead of stepped on SLIDE_TIMER_ID

## Summary

ViewerTOCPanel.zig slid by SetWindowPos per FrameClock tick. Needs the panel's pixels on a composition visual (or its slide expressed as a clip/offset of the viewer's visual) before the motion can be declared. Oracle: film with stall seam and stepped control.

## In plain terms

In a narrow viewer pane the contents list is a card that slides in from the
left when you press the contents button, and slides back out when you pick a
heading. That slide was timed by the app itself, so a busy moment could make
it hitch or freeze halfway across the page. Now Windows plays the slide, so
it stays smooth however busy the app is, and the card lands exactly where it
did before.

## Goals

- [x] the slide is declared to the compositor once, as the exact path the old timer walked (same 150 ms ease-out, same start and end)
- [x] a toggle halfway through reverses from where the card actually is, with no jump
- [x] the card's own window waits hidden at its resting place during the slide and is shown before the moving picture is taken down, so the hand-back shows the same pixels
- [x] the old stepped slide stays as the fallback (no device, any composition failure, `GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT=0`) and as the oracle's A/B arm (`GHOZTTY_DEBUG_TOC_SLIDE_STEPPED`); a stall seam `GHOZTTY_DEBUG_TOC_SLIDE_STALL_MS` for the film
- [ ] the on-screen film at UI stalls of 0/50/500 ms -> T2800 (needs CopyFromScreen on the real desktop, which this loop cannot run)

## Design

The card is a `WS_CHILD` of the viewer host floating over the WebView2 child,
and the viewer has no composition tree of its own (HoverTint's T2762 shape -
a child visual of the pane's presenter - does not exist for a viewer). So the
host gets a small DirectComposition target of its own
(`viewer_toc_comp.zig`), `topmost=TRUE` so its visual composes over the host's
child windows exactly as the card's window does as `HWND_TOP`. It is built on
the pane's first slide and torn down with the panel; any failure deinit()s it
and the slide steps the old way, and the next slide builds it again (a 150 ms
motion is not state worth recovering across a device loss).

For the length of a slide the card's PICTURE is the visual: the panel's own
`paint` into a DIB, the filter EDIT printed in at its place (`WM_PRINT`), and
the window region's rounded corners cleared from a region mask, so the
corners show the page as the region does. The motion needs no fitting:
`t` walks linearly in time (`slideStep`) and `slideEase` is a cubic in `t`,
so x(time) is exactly one cubic (`viewer_toc_layout.slidePlan`), one
`AddCubic` segment plus `End`. A reversal reads where the card is off the
clock (`slideStep(t0, want, elapsed)`) and declares a fresh plan from there.
A bounds sync that changes nothing mid-slide is a no-op; one that moves the
resting rect re-declares from the reached value. `play` waits for the commit
to reach DWM before the window is hidden (slide out), and the end timer shows
the window (and paints it) before the visual is removed (slide in).

Known limit, accepted: the picture is the card as it stood when the slide was
declared, so a row highlight that changes during the 150 ms shows at the
hand-back rather than mid-slide.
