# T2308 - An unfocused pane's banner stays at full brightness on Windows while the terminal below it dims; Mac's unfocused-split dim covers the whole pane, banner included

## Summary

Found while fixing T2261. Mac's `SurfaceView` puts the unfocused-split dim `Rectangle` last in the pane's ZStack, over the terminal, the frozen terminal and the `SurfacePaneBanner` alike (and none at all on glass). On Windows `DimOverlay` is placed on the surface HWND's window rect, and the banner band (`BannerOverlay`, opaque, painting the pane background plus the card) sits ABOVE that rect, so the band and its card stay undimmed. Measured order today: card above dim (T2261's negative control read `dim=under` for a copy seated under the card). Fix direction: extend the wash over the band (dim placement = slot, not surface) and stack it over the card, or paint the dim into the banner; keep the T2261 rule (copy under the dim). Validation: pane-banner.ps1 - pixel of an unfocused pane's card versus the same card focused.
