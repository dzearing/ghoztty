# T2826 - Composition step 5c-5b: in the composition pane tree a gliding pane's window region is re-cut on every glide tick (cutGlideTreeRegion keys the region on the visual's offset), one SetWindowRgn per moving pane per frame - keep the desk bookkeeping, cut the window once at the landing

## Summary

Split of T2780. Window.cutGlideTreeRegion (Window.zig ~10392) hashes plan.key with dx/dy - the offset of the pane's visual from its unmoved window - so the key changes on every tick of a glide and SetWindowRgn runs per moving pane per frame, a window-manager transaction for a window that paints nothing itself while its visual glides (the visual takes its shape from setTreeShape). What the region still decides is the desk's bookkeeping (card_region_cut / card_region_shown) and what a GDI desk paint is clipped out of. Keep the bookkeeping per tick, skip the SetWindowRgn while the pane's visual is gliding (tree_glide_at set) wherever the desk does not paint through the window DC, and cut the real region once when the window is placed at the landing. Oracle: the glide line's cards_us term (drag-perf / GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE 'cards_us') falls to ~0 on the tree's stall arm, a counted 'region cut' debug line per glide is at most one per pane, and comp-glide-hold-onscreen -Arms tree,stall plus stack-fold-slide / pane-stack-slide with the tree knobs stay ALL PASS (the desk never shows a stale card corner).
