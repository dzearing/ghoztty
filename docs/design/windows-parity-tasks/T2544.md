# T2544 - Composition step 4c-ii: rearrange header bands and an elevated hero tab stay on the compositor - painted into a full-client overlay mirror whose pixels the desk's upload rects clear first, so a desk repaint uncovers exactly what GDI would

## Summary

T2510 split, part ii. Today a window with rearrange headers or a hero tab drops the desk back to GDI (deskOnVisual eligible=false). Erase semantics: every deskUpload rect clears the overlay mirror there (alpha 0), then the over-desk painters source-over into it, then the union is uploaded. Oracle: WGC match knob on/off with headers showing and with a hero tab; a header drag leaves no stale band.

## In plain terms

With the compositor switch on (`GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1`), a window showing the
pane headers of rearrange mode, or a hero tab with its column of thumbnails,
used to give up on the compositor and draw its whole background by hand. Now
those windows stay on the compositor too: the headers and the thumbnail
column go up as a layer just above the background, and they look exactly the
same as before. When a pane closes or moves, its old header disappears with
it, as it always did. The switch is still off by default.

## Goals

- [x] A window with rearrange headers keeps its desk on the compositor, with the headers painted into an overlay above it
- [x] A hero tab (carousel, and a running selection slide) does the same
- [x] Erase semantics match GDI: where the desk repaints, the overlay clears first and the painters draw again, so nothing is left stale
- [x] On screen, the composed headers, a closed pane's header and the hero carousel match the hand-drawn picture

## Design (2026-10-31)

`chrome_comp.Overlay` holds two buffers the desk's size: `desk`, a copy of
what the desk visual holds (each sink upload copies its rects in), and `px`,
the overlay - premultiplied, every pixel 0 or opaque. It goes up as the desk
visual's only child (`Host.ovlUpload`), so it inherits the desk's offset and
its below-the-caption clip, and it dies with the desk.

A pass (`Window.overlayPass`) runs the painters over a region R:
1. R = what desk uploads erased since the last pass, plus the paint's clip
   (WM_PAINT) or every header band (`paintMovedBands`); the whole overlay
   when the visual is new.
2. Prefill the scratch DIB over R with what is on screen (`px` where opaque,
   else `desk`), so a painter that blends (ClearType, the glass band shade,
   the slide's desk gap) blends against the real picture.
3. The painters draw into it in client coordinates, clipped to R.
4. Merge: a pixel that differs from the desk becomes opaque, one that equals
   it clears (the desk under it is the same colour). Upload R.

Erase rule, matching the GDI blit's coverage: every sink upload clears the
overlay where it landed; a paint that is NOT blitting only what changed
(WM_PAINT, and every `GetDC` pass but the glow's) clears its whole clip box
first, as the GDI desk blit would have covered it. Neither clears inside a
"keep" rect: a hero tab's carousel and a running slide's hero rect, which the
GDI blit is clipped away from. A keep rect that stops being kept (a slide
that ended, a carousel that moved) is erased. A busy-light frame's upload
does not erase (`deskSinkUploadLight`): on GDI it lays the ring over a header
until the next paint, which is a flash rather than a picture.

Found while filming: a glass header lays no fill of its own (the desk draws
the band as material), so without the clip-box erase each pass drew the title
over its own last copy - 1334 px of over-bright, fringed text. With it the
headers frame matches GDI to the pixel.

`paintMovedBands` now batches its uploads (desk + overlay) into one commit,
as the paint does. A failing overlay step logs `chrome overlay visual failed
at <stage>` and puts such a window's desk back on GDI (`ovl_failed`) until
the host is rebuilt. Debug seam for the oracle's negative control:
`GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME_OVERLAY_FREEZE=1` drops every dirty-rect overlay upload.
