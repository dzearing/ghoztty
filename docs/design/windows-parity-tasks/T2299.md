# T2299 - Pushing the Windows sidebar panel's edge far in only stops it at 180 DIP; on Mac a push under 120 tucks it back into the rail and a rail pulled past the panel minimum comes out at that width, both within one drag, from a grip in the gutter beside the sidebar in both modes (Mac 543489bca PaneSidebarState.edgeDrag/edgeGrip)

## Summary

Mac 543489bca replaced the edge drag: edgeDrag(proposed, isExpanded) -> past min (180): expanded at clamped width; under expandThreshold (120): collapsed; between: unchanged (expanded at min, or still the rail) - so one drag can expand AND collapse. The grip is the gutter beside the sidebar in BOTH modes (edgeGrip: grid margin elevated, the rail card's own margin flat), never a band on the rail's tiles. Windows (pane_sidebar.edgeDrag after T2289): the rail expands past 120 and an expanded panel only clamps at 180, never collapsing; the rail's band sits inside its card. Port edgeDrag/edgeGrip and update pane-sidebar-resize.ps1 (sections 1, 3, 4 assert the old never-collapse rule).

## In plain terms

Dragging the edge of the Windows pane sidebar could make it wider or narrower, but never put it away again: pushed far in, it just stopped at its narrowest. On Mac the same drag tucks it back into the slim icon rail once you push well past the narrowest width, pulls the rail back out once there is room for the full panel, and does both in the same drag. The grab spot is also the gap beside the sidebar on Mac, never on top of the rail's icons. Windows now behaves the same.
