# T3626 - Linux L11e-3: the gutter bar and double-click on GTK - a 4px accent bar with a soft glow in the gutter on hover (half) and drag (full) that stays in the gutter, and double-click evens a run (grip) or a split in its direction (divider)

## Summary

Follow-up of T3567 (docs/reference/stacked-panes.md 'The gutter bar', 'Double-clicking a grip'). The GTK stage takes gutter drags (stagePress/stageDrag) and sets a resize cursor, but draws no GutterBar and has no double-click. Draw the bar in SplitTree's snapshot from priv.grid's divider/stack-handle gutters (one at a time; moves to the replacing gutter on a snap), and wire a GtkGestureClick n_press==2 to stack_drag.evenRun (grip) or SplitTree.equalized(only:) (divider; a horizontal divider evens heights only). Acceptance: pane-stack seam --press twice reports evened shares; a capture probe finds the accent bar in the gutter while held.

## In plain terms

On Linux, the thin gaps between split panes can be dragged to resize, but
nothing shows they can be: on the Mac a blue bar glows in the gap when you
point at it and brightens while you drag. Double-clicking a gap on the Mac
also evens the panes out; on Linux it does nothing. When this lands, Linux
shows the same bar and the double-click evens panes out the same way.

## Goals

- [x] a 4px accent bar with a soft glow in the gutter: half strength on hover, full while dragged, one at a time
- [x] the bar stays in the gutter and moves to the gutter that replaces the dragged one
- [x] double-click a divider: even out the panes across it, in its direction only
- [x] double-click a grip between two cards: even out the run on show; a run's edge has none
- [x] acceptance in the headless harness, pure logic in the none lane
