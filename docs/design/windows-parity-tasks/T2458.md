## Summary

T2457 landed the COLLAPSE half: a folding terminal card hides its live window and the strip face carries the pane's last frame (renderer readback, `PaneView.glideSnapRequest`/`glideFrame`), pinned, cut by the card and faded over the whole tween (`strip_face.tweenCollapse`/`blendContent`). EXPAND still shows the live pane held at its old size under a face at the last-120 collapse, and resizes it once at the end. Build the reverse: at glide start place the hidden pane at its FINAL slot size (keep the renderer awake while hidden, as hero mode does - `setVisible(true)` on a hidden HWND), request a frame at that size, and draw the card from it (content alpha rising per tweenCollapse, 0 until the frame lands); at the end show the live pane at its final rect (no resize) and only then hide the face. Oracle: the `stack fold carried` line plus a resize counter; stack-fold-slide.ps1 F5/F9/F11 move to it the way F1/F8/F10 did.

## In plain terms

When a folded-away pane (a blade) opens back up, the terminal used to show its
text at its old size under a fading cover and then jump to its new size at
the very end, reflowing in front of you. Now the terminal is resized first,
out of sight, draws itself at its new size, and the opening card slides out
carrying that picture, fading it in as it widens. The live terminal comes
back only once the card is fully open, already the right size, so nothing
reflows or resizes while it moves.
