# T2288 - A Windows card narrowing into its strip should fade its content out and its title in as it goes, and the reverse coming out (fade half of T2269, Mac a81db68f9)

## Summary

Mac a81db68f9 fade half of T2269 (needs T2287's narrowing geometry). Mac PaneCardView: the strip is the pane's own card at strip size; content alpha = 1 - collapse, the title face alpha = collapse, collapse = clamp(1 - (min(w,h) - 36)/120) of the DRAWN card, both directions, nothing switched at the end. Win32: a per-pane layered face popup over the narrowing card (DimOverlay.zig is the precedent: WS_EX_LAYERED|TRANSPARENT popup owned by the pane HWND, LWA_ALPHA, region matched to the owner via matchOwnerRegion, pane_clip.ownerClip for off-window parts). Its pixels = the strip band colour (pane_stack_strip.stripRgb, or the glass band over the sheet) plus the strip label (paintStripLabel / labelOrigin over the drawn rect), at LWA_ALPHA = collapse*255 - so the content reads through at 1 - collapse. Hide it at collapse 0 and once the glide ends (the desk band + paintStackStrips take over, same pixels, so no pop). The unfocused dim overlay of the same pane yields to it while it is shown. Acceptance: off-desktop pixel sample of a stretched fold (stack-fold-slide) showing a mid-glide alpha between pane colour and strip colour, plus the face hidden at rest.

## In plain terms

When a pane folds away into the thin strip at the edge of its stack, the Windows build used to show the pane's own content shrinking right up to the end and then swap to the strip with its name. On Mac the content fades out and the strip's name fades in while it narrows, so nothing switches at the end. Windows now does the same, both on the way in and on the way back out.

## Goals

- [x] as a card narrows into its strip, the strip's band and name fade in over it while the content fades out (Mac's 36/120 collapse)
- [x] the reverse when it widens back out
- [x] nothing left on screen at rest: the window's own strip takes over, and a card on show wears no face
- [x] the face matches the strip it becomes: the solid band in the flat and solid styles, the glass band over the desk in the glass style

## Validation criteria

- [x] folding, the face is on show over the card at 2+ partial alphas, rising - stack-fold-slide.ps1 F10 (flat: 34 partial alphas 5 -> 255; elevated: 21, 19 -> 255)
- [x] unfolding, it fades back out at 2+ partial alphas, falling - F11 (flat 7 partial alphas 255 -> 7; elevated 4, 255 -> 25)
- [x] at rest, folded and unfolded, no face is on show - F12, both styles
- [x] the face's pixels are the strip band - F13 (flat): a WM_PRINT photograph mid-glide is 100% the logged band 191c21
- [x] the check can fail - stack-fold-slide.ps1 -NegativeControl (0 ms slide) is red on F10, alongside F1/F2/F7/F8
- [x] pure logic unit tested in the none lane - strip_face.zig (collapseOf, alphaOf, axisOf, fillSolid/makeOpaque, fillGlass) and pane_stack_slide.zig's stripFace test
