# T2674 - Folding a pane into its strip, its banner vanishes at the first frame instead of sliding into the strip with the card (the carried fold hides the live window, and the banner with it)

## Summary

Follow-up to T2661 (which fixed the opposite direction). A card folding into its strip whose frame is carried (T2457, glide_snap=ready) hides its live pane window, so BannerOverlay.updatePosition hides the banner at the first carried frame; Mac's banner rides the card into the strip. Show it over the folding card the way T2661 does for an opening one - mind the fold's window pull (PaneView.fold_pull): the face draws the card at the window rect minus the pull, so the banner must be placed there too, cut to the card. Validate with a fold-direction twin of stack-fold-slide.ps1 F19.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] folding s2 under the hand, its banner is on show in 2+ samples while the live window is off show, cut to 2+ narrowing widths (stack-fold-slide.ps1, a fold twin of F19)
- [ ] F6 stays green: the banner never reaches past the window while it rides the card out past the edge
