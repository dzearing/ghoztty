# T1404 - a synchronous window capture can silently omit its child controls, and nothing in the harness says so

## Summary

`Get-TestWindowPixels -Sync` photographs a window through `PrintWindow`. What
the window paints itself is deterministic; a `WS_CHILD` control inside it is
not. T1400 measured eight captures of one unmoved viewer nav bar, at a constant
width, with the address field's rect reading a constant 122px from
`GetWindowRect`: the field's 35 full-height rows were in six of them and absent
from the other two. Both are valid bitmaps and the harness returns both without
comment, so the only symptom is an assertion downstream that flips verdicts on
identical code - which read for a day as *the working directory decides*.

T1400 wrote the rule into `docs/claude/testing.md` (measure a child through its
rect; bound an ink scan at its edge) and fixed the one script that was counting
a child's pixels. What does not exist is anything that CHECKS it. A probe that
starts measuring a child's pixels tomorrow gets the same day of investigation.

## In plain terms

When a test takes a picture of part of the app to check how it looks, the boxes
and text fields inside that part sometimes come out of the picture and sometimes
do not - and the test is handed the picture either way, with nothing to say a
piece is missing. One test was caught believing such a picture. Nothing stops
the next one.

## Details

Two shapes worth considering, in order:

1. **Make the omission LOUD.** A capture helper that knows the window's child
   controls could compare their rects against the bitmap and refuse (or report)
   a capture where a child's region is entirely the parent's background. That
   turns a silent wrong answer into a named one, which is the same move
   `Test-TestCaptureUniform` already makes for a blank capture.
2. **Make the capture deterministic**, if it can be: establish whether the
   omission is a `PrintWindow` child-enumeration race, a `WM_PRINT`
   `PRF_CHILDREN` behaviour of the system `Edit` class, or a DWM artifact, and
   whether a retry-until-children-appear is honest rather than a heuristic that
   hides a real product paint bug.

Note that `-AllowUniform` disables the one guard that exists here; a probe
carrying it has nothing left that notices a capture that drew less than it
should have.

## Goals

- [x] Measure what the omission actually is
- [x] Make a read of a child's pixels through a parent capture say so
- [x] Prove the check fires, and stays quiet on the window's own paint

## What it is, measured (2026-11-10)

A scratch probe ran viewer-find's setup (find card with "needle" typed into its
child Edit) and took 30-40 back-to-back `-Sync` captures of the card per
configuration, hashing the Edit's rect region each time:

| configuration | field missing |
|---|---|
| as shipped (card has no `WS_CLIPCHILDREN`) | 4/30, 5/30, 8/30, 10/30 across runs - the card's own paint ("1/5" count, field background) shows through |
| `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` forced on the card via `SetWindowLongPtr` | 8/30 twice - a solid BLACK hole instead |
| capture gap 0 / 265 / 530 ms | 4/40, 7/30, 3/30 (at 530 ms the caret's blink phase alternates visibly; the omission is independent of it) |
| focus moved off the field (no caret at all) | 4/40 |

So the omission is the CHILD's own paint missing from the surface PrintWindow
hands back - not the parent overpainting it (clipping the parent turns the
omission into a hole rather than removing it), not the caret, not capture
spacing. `PrintWindow` on the child Edit itself fails outright, so the child
cannot be photographed alone either. Why user32 sometimes leaves a same-thread
child's paint out is not observable from the harness, and that is why the
retry-until-present shape (option 2) was rejected: with no named cause it is
the heuristic the Details warned about.

## What landed

- `Get-TestWindowPixels` shots carry `.Children` (visible descendants, class +
  SCREEN rect) - `Get-TestCaptureChildren`.
- `Get-TestPixel` warns once per child per shot when a read lands inside a
  child; `Get-TestBrightness` warns when a child covers >= 25% of an explicit
  rect (a dialog's whole-client average with buttons in it is not the defect).
  `-ChildOk` marks a deliberate read.
- `Get-TestCaptureChild -Shot -X -Y` - the bound for a hand-rolled scan.
- `test-desktop-harness.ps1` section P2 + a `PaintFixtureChild` fixture
  (red parent, green WS_CHILD): warns inside, silent outside, once per child,
  `-ChildOk` silences, brightness warns, whole-shot average does not.
- That harness's own `Measure-TitlebarLuminance` now starts the caption band
  past the pane-sidebar child that reaches into it - the one real hit the new
  warning found on its first run.
- `docs/claude/testing.md`: the measurement and the helpers, under the T1400
  rule.

A warning rather than a refusal: `Get-TestPixel` has 161 call sites in 35
scripts, and an unknown number read inside children today; turning them all
red in one turn would bury the signal. The follow-up to promote it is filed
below.
