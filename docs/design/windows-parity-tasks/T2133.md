# T2133 - Mac: dragging a divider whose side is already under its minimum folds that whole side on the first pixel and it cannot come back during the drag

## Summary

Found by Windows T2124 (the port of 2811a3043's chain collapse), reasoned from
the shared code rather than reproduced on a Mac. In
`BaseTerminalController.splitDidResize` the fold thresholds are
`leading = minimumExtent(split.left, stacking: false)` and
`trailing = dimension - minimumExtent(split.right, stacking: false)`. When a
side is ALREADY under that side-by-side minimum at the press (a window too
narrow for it, so the layout shows it stacked), `trailing` is left of the
divider: the first pixel of movement is past `trailing + restackDistance`, the
whole side folds, and `back` (position within leading...trailing) can never be
true again, so it stays folded for the rest of the drag. Windows hit it in
drag-perf.ps1's 8-pane window: a 72 px nudge of the root divider folded seven
panes into a strip.

Windows fix (T2124, `stack_drag.zig` `RowDrag.press`): measure from the press
when it is already past the minimum - `leading = min(leading, press)`,
`trailing = max(trailing, press)`. A side that fits at the press behaves
exactly as before; an over-full side folds only once pushed 48 pt further than
where it was grabbed, and unfolds back at the press. Port the same to Mac
(the press is `divider.position` at `.began`), with a PaneStackWindowTests
case: a 3-pane right half in a window too narrow for it side by side, nudge the
root divider 20 pt toward it, assert nothing folded.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] a nudge of a divider whose side is already stacked does not fold that side (PaneStackWindowTests)
- [ ] pushing 48 pt beyond the press folds it, and returning to the press unfolds it
