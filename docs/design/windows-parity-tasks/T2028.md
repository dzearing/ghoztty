# T2028 - A pane's banner and overlays are placed AFTER its layout batch, not inside it: a divider drag can show a banner one frame off its pane (T2012 criterion 2)

## Summary

T2012 made a pane's popups hide with it; they are still re-glued by updatePaneBanners/updateDimOverlays after the pane's DeferWindowPos batch ends, so a resize can show them a frame behind. Fold the overlay placement into the same batch (or prove with a sampled drag that it never lags) - the remaining half of T2012's 'move and resize with the pane in one batch', and a step toward T1350.
