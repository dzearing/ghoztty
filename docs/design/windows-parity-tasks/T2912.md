# T2912 - Hidden windows hold title changes on Windows too: a covered or minimized window's title lands when it shows or every 5s at most, while +list reads the held title at once (port of Mac 68a8344f0, split from T2909)

## Summary

Part (3) of the Mac 68a8344f0 port, split from T2909. Mac holds NSWindow.title while a window is hidden (pendingWindowTitle) and lands it the moment the window shows, or after hiddenWindowTitleInterval (5s) so accessibility readers and the Window menu are never far behind; a window's first title always lands; +list and AppleScript read BaseTerminalController.currentWindowTitle (the held title) at once. Windows: find where Window.zig calls SetWindowTextW / repaints the caption for a title change, hold it while `occluded` (or minimized) with a 5s cap, land it in applyOcclusion's reveal, and make sure +list reads the held value, not GetWindowText. Reference: docs/reference/background-windows.md#titles. Acceptance: a covered window retitled 10x/s produces at most one caption update per 5s, +list shows the newest title immediately, and revealing lands it.

## In plain terms

A program that animates its title (Claude Code spins a glyph there several
times a second) made every Ghoztty window repaint its title bar and notify the
taskbar on every frame - even windows that were minimized or buried under
others, where nobody could see it. Now a hidden window keeps the newest title
aside and shows it the moment the window comes back (or every 5 seconds, so
the taskbar never falls far behind). Scripts asking `ghoztty +list` still get
the newest title instantly.

## Goals

- [x] a minimized or covered window holds its title changes
- [x] the held title lands on restore / uncover, and at most every 5 s while hidden
- [x] a window's first title always lands
- [x] `+list` (and copy-title) report the held title at once
- [x] the tab strip's repaint for a changed label waits too, and is paid on landing
