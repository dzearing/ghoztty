# T2711 - An edge-drag burst step paints the desk again after the frame change (1.65 ms ReleaseFast) and its own WM_PAINT costs 1.8 ms with no viewer: paint each band once per step, so a burst shrink fits edge-drag-rate's 150% budget

## In plain terms

The experimental "burst" resize (T2666) makes the window's outline and its
contents arrive together, but it paints the window's background three or four
times on every step of an edge drag. That extra work drops a shrinking drag
from about 66 to about 43 frames a second, which is too slow to make the burst
the default. This makes each step paint the background once.

## Summary

Under `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_BURST` one edge-drag step paints the desk bands in
`layoutSplitsLive` (`bands-desk` twice in the trace: the vacated strip and the
band pass), then again in `releaseBurst`'s `RedrawWindow(RDW_UPDATENOW)`
(WM_PAINT, 1.82 ms ReleaseFast in the 3-pane scene with no viewer, so it is
not the viewer sheet), then AGAIN after the frame change in
`finishPrelaidSize` (`prelaid-paint` -> `bands-desk`, 1.65 ms ReleaseFast vs
1.08 ms on the shipped pre-layout). Find what the burst's WM_PAINT actually
repaints when nothing new is exposed, and skip whichever repeat paints the same
pixels; a shrink exposes nothing outside the old client, so its post-frame
repaint should be removable outright.

## Details

Measured 2026-11-04 ~20:00, `test\win32\edge-drag-rate.ps1 -Burst`, off-desktop
(logs under `%TEMP%\ghoztty-edge-drag-rate\<stamp>\`):

| | Debug | ReleaseFast sandbox |
|---|---|---|
| burst WM_PAINT (`burst-paint` -> `burst-window-painted`), row3 | 2.11 ms | 1.82 ms |
| same, viewer scene (includes the T2702 sheet) | 4.37 ms | 2.88 ms |
| post-frame `prelaid-paint` -> `bands-desk`, row3 burst | ~3.5 ms | 1.65 ms |
| same, shipped pre-layout | - | 1.08 ms |
| row3 shrink step, burst vs shipped | 15.7 vs 9.7 ms | 23.2 vs 15.0 ms |

The shrink budget compares a burst shrink, which lays out before the frame
change (T2580's shrink), with a shipped shrink that does not, so some of the
gap is inherent. The repeat paints are the part that is not. Visual
correctness of skipping a paint can only be judged on film (T2666's
`resize-sync-onscreen.ps1`); off-desktop, the rate harness is the gate.

## Goals

- [x] name what the burst's WM_PAINT repaints in the 3-pane scene: the caption/tab band only (`burst-invalid l=0 t=0 r=<width> b=40` on every step, new trace line) - chrome laid out at the pending width, not the desk, so it stays
- [x] paint each desk band once per burst step: a shrink's post-frame desk paint is gone (`prelaid-skip`, 40 of 40 shrink steps); the layout pass's second band paint stays because with a redirection surface the vacated paint blits only the strips (T2516), and a grow still repaints because the old frame clipped its new strip
- [x] edge-drag-rate -Burst section E step-cost rows green in ReleaseFast

## Fix

`finishPrelaidSize` skips the desk paint (keeping the T2448 validation) when
`resize_prelayout.deskPaintNeeded(laid, under)` says the rect the pre-layout
laid out fits inside the client of the frame it painted under - a shrink.
`prelayout_under` records that client in `prelayoutForPos`. Unit test in
`resize_prelayout.zig` (none lane). Shipped path unaffected: it lays out only
grows unless `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_PRELAYOUT_SHRINK` or `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_BURST`.
