# T2349 - Windows all-windows sidebar: the folds a window/group drag takes at pick-up, and gives back after the drop, snap instead of animating like Mac's fold (foldAnimation)

## Summary

T2337 folds every group (group drag) or every window (window drag) when an item is picked up, and restores them once it settles. Mac runs both through PaneSidebarOutline.foldAnimation; Windows applies them with a plain refresh, so the list jumps. Use the fold transition (startTransition .fold) or an equivalent without fighting the held item's motion; the held header must stay under the pointer while the list folds beneath it (window_order.clamp grabbed).

## In plain terms

When you pick up a group or a window in the all-windows sidebar list to
reorder it, every group (or window) closes up to one line so it is easy to
place, and opens back up after you let go. On Mac those closings and
openings glide; on Windows the list jumped. Now it folds and unfolds
smoothly, and the item you are holding stays under the pointer meanwhile.

## Goals

- [x] the folds a drag takes at pick-up animate (every band at once)
- [x] the folds given back after the drop animate the other way
- [x] the held item is painted live over the fold, under the pointer
- [x] a fold the held item or a re-scroll would fight just lands
