# T3020 - PA-I06: every 30 s the agent re-serialises each changed session's whole history and rewrites it with fsync, building it under the store lock (session.zig:1729, :3064)

## Summary

PA-I06 (audit-1, T2962). src/remote/agent/session.zig:1729 snapshot_every_ticks = 30; in snapshotRingsWith the ring is journaled incrementally (append_from, T997) but on the timer pass `s.historyAlloc(alloc)` (session.zig ~:3064) serialises the emulator's whole history BEFORE the store mutex is released, and ring_snapshot.writeHistoryAtomic writes the full file with file.sync() (atomic_write.zig:90). Cost (estimated): a pane with a clock or spinner and a full 4 MB scrollback writes 1-3 MB with fsync every 30 s; five such panes is on the order of 20 GB/day of SSD writes, plus a tens-of-ms lock hold per pane each pass. Fix: rewrite history only on detach/stop/checkpoint, on a much longer timer, or when the scrollback (not just the screen) changed; serialise outside the store lock. Risk medium: lengthens the reboot-recovery window for history (the ring journal still covers recent bytes). Shared agent code: Mac half filed. Findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/I-findings.md.

## In plain terms

The background service that keeps your terminal sessions alive was re-saving each busy pane's entire scrollback to disk every 30 seconds, even when only a clock or spinner on screen had changed. Now it saves the full scrollback every 5 minutes (and always when you disconnect or shut down), while the recent output keeps being saved every 30 seconds. After a crash or reboot you still get your old scrollback plus whatever came after it.

## Goals

- [x] the full history is rewritten every 5 minutes on the timer, not every 30 seconds
- [x] disconnect and shutdown still save it immediately
- [x] a restore after a crash between saves keeps the older history and adds the newer output after it
- [ ] serialising outside the store lock (needs an emulator lock of its own) - filed as T3166
