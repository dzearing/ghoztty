# T2593 - Film the window-edge drag with each pane in the window's composition tree (GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE=1), and with it on, skip the terminal's per-step region cut: with the tree off the region clips the visible swap chain, so T2577 could not drop it

## Summary

T2577 step 5 tried to skip the rounded-region `SetWindowRgn` on terminal panes during a window-edge drag (~1.8 ms cut phase per step). It never fired in the filmed configuration (CHROME+PRESENT+NOREDIR): there the terminal's swap chain is the composition target of its OWN child window, which the window region clips, so the region is visually load-bearing. Only with `GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE=1` (T2553) is the swap chain a visual in the window's tree, where the child HWND is input-only and its region decides nothing on screen.

Do: (1) film `scripts/resize-sync-onscreen.ps1` (ReleaseSafe, CHROME+PRESENT+NOREDIR) with and without `GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE=1`, alternating, bad frames per step + the `resize trace` phases; (2) with the tree on, defer the terminal's card region to a settle at `WM_EXITSIZEMOVE` (the T2577 step 5 sketch: skip when `noredir and in_size_loop and in_live_layout and surface.tree_visual != null`, leave `card_region_key` stale, `cutCardRegions()` on exit) and A/B that; (3) if the tree wins, say what stands between it and on-by-default.

## In plain terms

Resizing a window by its edge still shows the inside lagging a frame. One way the app draws terminal panes might remove two slow steps from every resize frame; this measures whether it does.

## Goals

- [ ] tree on vs off filmed and scored
