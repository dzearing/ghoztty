# T2874 - A fold-glide tick's card region cut (SetWindowRgn) stalls 5-8.7 ms once or twice a run, against a 0.25 ms mean, and that tick drops a 120 Hz frame

## Summary

**Closed 2026-11-11 (lane b):** the stall is the UI thread blocked off-CPU inside window-manager / GDI calls (see below). T2932 (composition path) and T2933 (default path, behind `GHOZTTY_DRAG_CARD_CLIP=1`) took the drag's region cuts off the frame. The last stall T2933's probe still showed (1.61 ms) was after mouse-up: the glide the drag leaves settling ran without the hold, so every settling tick re-cut 2-4 panes. This task's change holds the regions through that glide and cuts them once when it lands. Turning the knob on by default is T2935. Original summary follows.

Split from T2732 (2026-11-09). Release, 120 Hz, the T2732 probe, 4 runs: cards_us (cutCardRegions, timed around applyCardRegions) mean 0.23-0.30 ms, p99 0.77-1.29 ms, max 1.45-8.68 ms; 0-3 ticks a run over 3 ms, each of them a late tick (place 8-12 ms). T2732's 2026-11-07 cut reorder already moved the cut before the held resizes, which took the mean from 1.30 to 0.40 ms; the stall left is sporadic. Next: per-pane stamps around each SetWindowRgn on a stalled tick (which pane, was its renderer mid-present) - same contention suspect as the 11-07 finding.

## In plain terms

While you drag a divider between panes, the app re-shapes each pane's
rounded card every frame. Once or twice a drag, one of those re-shapes takes
5-9 ms instead of a quarter of a millisecond, and on a 120 Hz screen that
frame is skipped - a tiny hitch in an otherwise smooth drag. This task found
out where that time goes, and with the drag's shape hold switched on the
re-shaping now waits until the panes have finished settling after you let go,
so it happens once instead of every frame. The hold ships switched off until
someone has looked at the corners on a real screen (T2935).

## What the per-pane stamps found (2026-11-11, lane b)

Release sandbox, 120 Hz, the T2732 glide probe, 12 runs, with a new
`stack trace rgn` line per `SetWindowRgn` (pane, kind, QPC span, the pane
window procedure's own time and message count, and the UI thread's cycles):

- **Not the renderer.** No present on the stalled pane overlaps a slow cut
  (nearest 1-14 ms either side), so the 11-07 present-contention suspect is
  ruled out for the residual stall.
- **Not our message handling.** `SetWindowRgn` sends the pane 3 messages
  (WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGING / WM_NCCALCSIZE / WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED); our window
  procedure spends 3-20 us on them inside a 1.5-8 ms call.
- **The thread is off-CPU.** A normal cut is ~140 us of the UI thread on CPU
  in the kernel (cycles / wall = 1.0, ~3000 cycles/us). A stalled one runs
  130-300 us of cycles over 1.7-6.8 ms of wall: blocked, on a 32-thread box
  at ~6% load - most likely a session-wide lock (win32k / GDI / DWM). Naming
  the holder needs an elevated kernel trace: **T2931** (parked for the user).
- **Not specific to SetWindowRgn.** Several slow `cards_us` ticks spend most
  of the phase outside the calls (9.1 ms with 3.1 ms in them; 5.4 / 1.3;
  3.8 / 0.3) - the region build (GDI) and the rest of `applyCardRegions` are
  system calls that stall the same way.

So no reorder of our own work removes it: the lever is making fewer
window-manager / GDI calls per drag frame - **T2932** (keep the card shape
off `SetWindowRgn` while the drag runs, cut once at its end). This task waits
on it.

Probe + analysers: `glide-probe.ps1`, `rgn3.py` (wall vs thread CPU per slow
cut) and `tickrgn.py` (cards_us vs time inside the calls) in session
97a4449e's scratchpad.
