# T2773 - With a viewer pane open, a window-edge drag step spends 2.0 ms in EndDeferWindowPos (vs 0.6 ms for three terminals) on both present paths: find what the viewer's WebView2 child costs inside the pane batch and take it out of the per-step path

## In plain terms

Dragging the edge of a window that has a document or web page open beside a terminal
is slower per step than a window of terminals alone. Most of that extra time goes to
moving the page's window. When this lands, a window with a viewer resizes as
smoothly as one without.

## Summary

T2624's stage table (ReleaseFast, `edge-drag-rate.ps1 -ContentArms -Trace
-ReleaseSandbox`, phases from the `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE` ring) puts the pane
batch (`batch-begin` -> `defer-end`, i.e. `EndDeferWindowPos` of the pane
children) at a 2.0-2.1 ms median per step in the `viewer` scene (a terminal
beside a markdown viewer). In `row3` (three terminals) it is 0.6-0.85 ms. The
numbers are the same on the default per-pane present (run 20261106-185545) and
on `GHOZTTY_WINDOW_PRESENTER=1` with `GHOZTTY_COMP_PRESENT=0` (run
20261106-191029), so it is the viewer's own child: WebView2's host window and
what its `WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED`/`WM_SIZE` does inside the batch
(`ViewerPane.sync_cost` is printed on the `resize trace viewer` line but read 0
here, so the cost is not in our bounds sync). `resize_paint.liveViewerMoves`
(T2577) already defers the parent-position refresh. Find what is left and get
it out of the per-step path.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A ReleaseFast stage breakdown of the viewer's share of the batch, naming the call that owns it.
- [ ] The viewer scene's `batch-begin` -> `defer-end` median measurably lower in `edge-drag-rate.ps1 -ContentArms -Trace -ReleaseSandbox`, with the viewer still tracking its pane during and after the drag.
