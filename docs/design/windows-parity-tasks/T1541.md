# T1541 - The tab under the pointer shows nothing while the drag dwell clock runs

## In plain terms

When you drag a pane over the tab bar and hold still on a tab, Ghoztty waits
half a second and then opens that tab so you can carry the pane into it. While
you are waiting, nothing on screen says so - the tab looks exactly as it did,
and half a second with no feedback reads as "this is not going to do
anything", so people move on before it fires. The tab you are resting on
should say that it is about to open.

## Summary

T1537 landed the dwell (`Window.armTabDwell` / `onTabDwell`, 500ms,
`TAB_DWELL_MS`) and it works, but it is invisible until it fires. Mac draws the
hovered tab in its pressed state for the duration; the win32 strip has the
`rearrange_hover` machinery already and paints nothing for this.

The new-tab caret IS drawn during the same gesture, so the strip is already
showing SOMETHING - which makes the silence worse, not better: the caret says
"release opens a tab here" while the tab you are actually resting on says
nothing about the other thing that is about to happen.

## Goals

- [ ] The tab button under a dwelling pane drag is drawn in a distinct state
      for the duration of the clock.
- [ ] The state clears when the pointer leaves it, when the dwell fires, and
      when the drag ends.
- [ ] It never appears outside a drag - resting a plain pointer on a tab is
      still just a hover.
