# T2824 - Frame lab: decide what the banner cell's pane-top motion is scored against (T2784 follow-up): a trigger with no glide has no expected path, so Measure-FrameMotion counts every refresh between its two jumps as due and the banner can fail advance rate for holding still

## Summary

T2784 wired trigger cells into `Get-FrameLabSceneScore`
(`test\win32\lib\FrameLabLiveScene.ps1`): a trigger's tracked edge is measured
on its fire stamps through `Get-FrameLabTriggerMotionArgs`. For a plan with a
glide (blade-glide) the eased curve is the expected path and the T2373
smoothness targets apply. The banner has no glide, and
`GhozttyMotionScore.Measure` (`lib\FrameScore.ps1`) with neither a pointer nor
an expected path counts EVERY refresh from the first advance to the last as
due - so a banner that drops in on one frame and holds for 700 ms reads an
advance rate near 0.3 and fails, whatever the app did. Verified by reading
`Measure` (the `due = adv || !ptr || ...` line) and by
`frame-lab-live-scene.ps1` H13b, where the same frames under a plan with a
glide read as misses.

Interim (T2784): a trigger with no glide has its motion REPORTED, never held
to the targets. What is left: decide what the banner's pane-top is held to.
Find out how the banner appears on Mac (`macos/` banner view: does it slide
the pane down over a duration, or jump?) and on win32, then either give the
plan a glide (duration + curve read back from the app's constants, the way
blade-glide reads `pane_sidebar_transition.zig`) so it is scored like
blade-glide, or define a jump target (the edge reaches its new position
within N refreshes of the fire and holds it with no intermediate frame).

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The banner cell's motion is held to a target that a correct banner passes and a stalled or torn one fails, proven on synthetic frames in `frame-lab-live-scene.ps1` section H (positive and negative control).
- [ ] The target is the app's actual banner behaviour (Mac and win32 read, constants cited).
