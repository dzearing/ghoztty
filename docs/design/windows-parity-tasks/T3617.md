# T3617 - Linux L11b5: a pane drag carries a ghost card of the pane under the pointer

## In plain terms

On Linux, dragging a pane to rearrange it showed only a generic drag icon, so
you could not see what you were carrying. Now the pointer carries a small card
of the pane - its colour, its name in a title strip, and a shrunken picture of
what is on its screen - the same card the Mac shows. Where the pane will land
and the hover handle are separate follow-ups.

## Goals

- [x] a pane drag carries a card of the pane (background, title strip with the label, its own content scaled in, rim, shadow), at most 220x150 in the pane's shape, held by the middle of the title strip
- [x] a tinted pane's card is drawn in its tint with dark ink
- [x] a headless seam reads the very card a drag-begin sets, so it is tested without a seat
- [x] the drop preview (+ dwelling tab lit) and the hover grab handle are filed as their own cards (T3708, T3709)

## Summary

Scoped on 2026-11-24 to the GHOST CARD; the drop preview moved to T3708 and the hover grab handle to T3709.

Follow-up of T3564. Today GTK draws its default drag icon and no drop preview.
