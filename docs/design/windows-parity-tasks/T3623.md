# T3623 - Linux L11d-3: a window's gradient survives a relaunch, and the desk runs under the pane sidebar and the header bar

## Summary

Follow-up of T3566. (1) The window's desk variant seed is not persisted: GTK SessionLayout does not record it, so a relaunched hand-opened window comes back with a NEW random gradient and a --color=random window loses its own. Mac persists the seed (session-persistence.md, Leaf.tint and PaneDeskVariant), win32 records desk_variant in its layout (App.zig ~4075/6286); record it additively in the GTK session layout and give it back on restore and in-place agent recovery. (2) The desk is painted only by the split tree, so the pane sidebar sits on the stock window background and the header bar is solid; Mac runs the gradient under the expanded sidebar (which goes translucent) and up under the titlebar. Move the desk to the window's content and make the sidebar card sit on it. Acceptance: extend test/linux/pane-style.sh and session-restore.sh.

## In plain terms

On Linux, a window opened by hand gets its own coloured gradient behind its
panes. Until now that colour was forgotten on every relaunch - the window came
back with a different one - and the gradient stopped at the panes: the title
bar and the pane list on the left sat on plain grey. Now a window keeps its
colour across a relaunch, and the gradient fills the whole window, title bar
and pane list included, the way it does on the Mac.

## Goals

- [x] the session layout records each window's gradient seed and a restore gives the same one back (never a new random one)
- [x] the window, not the split tree, paints the desk, so it runs under the header bar and the pane sidebar
