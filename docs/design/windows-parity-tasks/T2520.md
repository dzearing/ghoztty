# T2520 - A window-edge resize step rebuilds the whole desk from scratch: ensureDesk re-renders the gradient and HALFTONE-stretches it to full size on every new size, 3.2-4.0 ms per step at ReleaseSafe - a third of the budget the pass needs to fit in one 120 Hz frame (T2516)

## Summary

Measured 2026-10-30 with GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE (desk trace desk_us=) on resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Exe zig-out-rsafe: desk_us 3160-4000 per step, compose+blit another ~2 ms, of a ~15 ms pass. PaneDesk.paint sees a new w/h on every step, so ensureDesk destroys the desk DIB, renders the quarter-size gradient and StretchDIBits(HALFTONE) it up. Make a live resize step cheap: e.g. keep the desk at the largest size of the gesture and read the w x h window out of it (stride) during a live resize, re-rendering at WM_EXITSIZEMOVE; or a fast integer bilinear upscale in place of HALFTONE. Validate: desk trace under 0.5 ms per step, resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 bad-frame count down, pixel test that the settled desk is unchanged.
