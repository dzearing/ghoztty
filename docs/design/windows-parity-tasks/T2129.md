# T2129 - Stacked panes: a fuzz test over random split, close, stash, focus, drag and undo sequences never leaves the caret on a strip or reopens a strip nobody asked for

## Summary

Split from T2084: port of Mac PaneStackFuzzTests (2811a3043) into the none lane, both styles (glass and flat), driving pane_stack_state + pane_stack_layout with random operation sequences and asserting after each step that the focused pane is on show and no strip re-opened unasked. Lands after the rules it checks (T2124 drag chain, T2126 undo, T2127 caret rules).

## In plain terms

Folding panes into strips has a lot of rules: a strip stays a strip until
you ask for it, the pane you are typing in is always on show, and undoing a
close puts the row back exactly. Each rule had its own tests, but nothing
checked them all together over the long, messy sequences people actually
make. This adds a test that plays thousands of random sessions (split,
close, stash, bring back, focus, drag a divider, undo, swap, resize) and
checks every rule after every step. On its first run it found a real bug:
drag a divider far enough to fold two panes, then whip it straight back to
where you started, and one pane stayed folded. That is fixed.

## Goals

- [x] A fuzz test over random split, close, stash, unstash, focus, drag, undo, swap and resize sequences, both styles, at 100/125/150%
- [x] After every step: the focused pane is on show, no strip came out unasked, every resizer is in a gutter, nothing on show is under the minimum
- [x] Whatever it finds is fixed or filed
