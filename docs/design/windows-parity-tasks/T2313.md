# T2313 - Windows stash tray shows a 'Stashed' caption over an empty tray and a divider in the all-windows list; Mac drops both - the empty well alone says it, and in all-windows a window's stashed panes just follow its others, with the empty well only while a pane is dragged (Mac 82b9c5501, 47b39b1cb, b7de64ce8)

## Summary

Mac 82b9c5501: an EMPTY tray has no 'Stashed' caption - its dashed drop well says it; the caption (with count) shows only while the tray holds panes. 47b39b1cb: in the ALL-WINDOWS scope the tray has no divider or caption at all, in either mode - a window's stashed panes simply follow its other panes, so collapsing or expanding moves nothing. b7de64ce8: in all-windows the empty tray's well shows only while a pane is being dragged. (ref: main docs/reference/pane-sidebar.md stash tray.) Windows tray landed in T2276; port these three to PaneSidebar.zig.

## In plain terms

The pane sidebar's "stashed" area had extra labels and lines that the Mac
version no longer draws. When nothing is stashed, the Mac shows only the
dashed "drag a pane here" box, with no "Stashed" heading over it; in the
all-windows list it shows no heading or line at all before a window's stashed
panes, and the dashed box appears there only while you are dragging a pane.
After this, the Windows sidebar looks the same.
