# T2485 - Blades: a drag that chain-folds three of 5+ panes must be smooth frame for frame - an already-folded blade must not flicker while the next pane folds, the folding pane must not carry a light border that vanishes on the last frame, and nothing may change colour or jump on the final frame (release-build capture, every frame validated)

## Summary

User report 2026-10-29 16:5x on release 1.50.8: with 5+ panes left to right, dragging so that 3 of them collapse to the right one after another is "completely stupidly janky". Observed: (1) after the first pane has folded into a blade, while the SECOND one folds the FIRST blade flickers and "does weird things"; (2) the pane being folded carries a light border on every frame of the tween, which disappears only on the final frame; (3) things that move change colour harshly on the last frame. The user asked for this exact scenario to be recorded frame for frame and validated.

Assumption (recorded, not asked): "drag 3 of them right to collapse them" = one continuous divider/grip drag toward the right that chain-collapses three neighbouring panes into blades in turn (T2084/T2091 chain-collapse), on a 5-6 pane window; 1x and the 4K window both.

CLOSE CONDITION (no debug-harness substitute; the release build, real pointer, WGC capture of every composited frame):
- Release build (ReleaseFast portable copy in temp - never the installed app), 6 terminal panes with text, one real-pointer drag that chain-folds three panes; capture every frame from drag start to the last fold settling. Run it at 1x and on the 4K window.
- Frame-to-frame oracle over the whole capture, scored per region (each blade, each pane face, each gutter/border strip):
  a. an already-folded blade is pixel-stable (only its position may change, by a smooth glide) while later panes fold - no flicker, no repaint flash, no content reappearing;
  b. the folding pane shows NO border/rim colour that is absent from both its start and end state (the light border must not exist mid-tween);
  c. every region's colour and position move monotonically from start state to end state, with no single-frame step larger than the tween's per-frame share - specifically no jump on the final frame (last-frame delta must be no bigger than the median delta of the tween);
  d. no frame over 16.7 ms between composited frames during the folds.
- Attach the frame montage (all frames of the 2nd fold) and the per-region delta table to this task. Close only when a-d hold on two consecutive runs at both scales.
- The capture script lands as an acceptance harness (opt-in -Live, real cursor; never sweep-run) so the scenario stays guarded.

## In plain terms

flash black, text flickers on and off, and the folded strips blink while the
next pane folds. When this lands the drag will look like one smooth motion,
and a recording of every frame will prove it.

## Root causes so far (2026-10-29 19:20, off-desktop probe, no pointer)

The probe: a debug build on the background test desktop, 6 panes, divider 1
dragged right in 160 posted mouse steps, the app's oracle lines counted (the
probe script is `docs/design/t2485-chainfold/tools/chainfold-probe.ps1`). It
reproduces the fold-face misbehaviour without touching the user's pointer,
though it cannot photograph frames, so black faces (A1/A2) are out of its
reach.

- **D1, the folding blade's text blinking on and off (A4, last fold) -
  FIXED.** The fold reads the pane's last frame back at the live window's
  size (282 px) and then holds the hidden window at a rect that rounds to
  281 px. `paintStripFace` refused any size mismatch, which dropped the
  carried face and fell back to the live pane, and the next layout hid it
  again. The pane went live and hidden on alternate frames (`stack fold
  carried dropped ... live=1,0,1,0` x17 in one drag). Fix:
  `strip_face.carriedFits` allows 2 px of slack, since `blendContent` pins
  and clips the frame anyway. After the fix: 0 drops, all 74 carried frames
  `live=0`.
- **D2, the already-folded blade "does weird things" while the next pane
  folds (A4/A5) - FIXED (see the note below the paragraph).** When a new fold starts, the blade that
  is already in the stack is classified by the glide as UNFOLDING (`stack
  unfold carried ... card=1924,45 -> 1909,60`, then `stack unfold landed`).
  Its card widens 45 -> 60 px as the stack makes room, and that widening
  reads as coming out of the strip. So a card that stays a strip goes through
  the opening path: its live pane is placed hidden at a slot size, a frame is
  requested, and a band-only face is drawn over it. Every subsequent fold
  repeats this for every blade already in the stack. Next: make
  `pane_stack_slide`'s folding/unfolding classification ask whether the card
  is tucked at BOTH ends of the glide (strip -> strip is a move, not an
  unfold), with a unit test on the 5-blade chain shape.

  Fix: the cause was one step earlier than the classification. A blade that
  had folded and caught up still sat in the resting glide as a strip CARD,
  so `carryStripCard` carried it into the next glide as a card again, and
  `folding`/`unfolding` decided direction by width (drawn 45, headed 60 =
  "opening"). Now `StripCard.into` records the direction from the tucked
  state (in when tucked at the end, out otherwise), the two predicates read
  it, and a folded card that has caught up is left to `moveStrip`, which
  glides it strip-only like any strip that stays a strip (T2417). Unit test
  "a blade already in its strip moves strip-only as the next pane folds"
  was red at `!unfolding(2)` before the fix. Probe after: `stack unfold
  carried` 0, `stack unfold landed` 0, carried drops 0, 79 carried frames
  all `live=0`.
- **D3, blade names vanishing after the pointer stops (A6) - FIXED.** Not a
  skipped label: the probe's strip paints at rest all had the children hidden
  and the label present. The names were laid by `paintStackStrips` (TextOut)
  as a separate GDI pass AFTER the desk's BitBlt of the nameless bands, with
  `syncGlassBackdrops` in between. 150 ms after the hand rests, the drag hold
  releases and the six terminals reflow, the gap widens, and DWM composited
  the window between the two passes (run3 f306-308 + f310, release; run4
  f324, debug). Fix: `PaneDesk.names` lays the names into the composite for
  the blit and restores those pixels after, so the cache never holds them;
  `paintStackStrips` skips names the desk laid. Opaque windows only - a
  translucent window still alpha-blends the names in their own pass. run6
  (release, real pointer): no dropout after the hand stopped
  (`run6-settle-fixed.png`, `run6-dropout.txt` vs `run3-dropout.txt`).
