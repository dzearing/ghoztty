# T2485 - Blades: a drag that chain-folds three of 5+ panes must be smooth frame for frame - an already-folded blade must not flicker while the next pane folds, the folding pane must not carry a light border that vanishes on the last frame, and nothing may change colour or jump on the final frame (release-build capture, every frame validated)

## Summary

User report 2026-10-29 16:5x on release 1.50.8: with 5+ panes left to right, dragging so that 3 of them collapse to the right one after another is "completely stupidly janky". Observed: (1) after the first pane has folded into a blade, while the SECOND one folds the FIRST blade flickers and "does weird things"; (2) the pane being folded carries a light border on every frame of the tween, which disappears only on the final frame; (3) things that move change colour harshly on the last frame. The user asked for this exact scenario to be recorded frame for frame and validated.

Assumption (recorded, not asked): "drag 3 of them right to collapse them" = one continuous divider/grip drag toward the right that chain-collapses three neighbouring panes into blades in turn (T2084/T2091 chain-collapse), on a 5-6 pane window; 1x and the 4K window both.

CLOSE CONDITION (no debug-harness substitute; the release build, real pointer, WGC capture of every composited frame):
- Release build (ReleaseFast portable copy in temp - never the installed app), 6 terminal panes with text, one real-pointer drag that chain-folds three panes; capture every frame from drag start to the last fold settling. Run it at 1x and on the 4K window.
- Frame-to-frame oracle over the whole capture, scored per region (each blade, each pane face, each gutter/border strip):
  a. an already-folded blade is pixel-stable (only its position may change, by a smooth glide) while later panes fold - no flicker, no repaint flash, no content reappearing;
  b. the folding pane shows NO border/rim colour that is absent from both its start and end state (the light border must not exist mid-tween);
  c. every region's colour and position move monotonically from start state to end state, with no single-frame step larger than the tween's per-frame share - specifically no jump on the final frame (last-frame delta must be no bigger than the median delta of the tween);
  d. no frame over 16.7 ms between composited frames during the folds.
- Attach the frame montage (all frames of the 2nd fold) and the per-region delta table to this task. Close only when a-d hold on two consecutive runs at both scales.
- The capture script lands as an acceptance harness (opt-in -Live, real cursor; never sweep-run) so the scenario stays guarded.

## In plain terms

With six panes side by side, dragging a divider to the right squeezes the
panes next to it shut one after another. Right now that looks broken: panes
flash black, text flickers on and off, and the folded strips blink while the
next pane folds. When this lands the drag will look like one smooth motion,
and a recording of every frame will prove it.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] (a) an already-folded blade is pixel-stable while later panes fold: no frame where its title text disappears or jumps (oracle: per-blade text-pixel count never dips >50% against both neighbours)
- [ ] (b) the folding pane shows no rim/border colour absent from both its start and end state
- [ ] (c) no region steps by more than the tween's median per-frame delta on the final frame
- [ ] (d) no composed-frame gap over 16.7 ms during the folds, on a capture with zero queue drops
- [ ] no frame where a pane face is solid black (A1/A2 class)
- [ ] all of the above on two consecutive runs, normal and near-full-monitor window

## Root causes so far (2026-10-29 19:20, off-desktop probe, no pointer)

The probe: a debug build on the background test desktop, 6 panes, divider 1
dragged right in 160 posted mouse steps, the app's oracle lines counted (the
probe script is `docs/design/t2485-chainfold/tools/chainfold-probe.ps1`). It
reproduces the fold-face misbehaviour without touching the user's pointer,
though it cannot photograph frames, so black faces (A1/A2) are out of its
reach.

- **D1, the folding blade's text blinking on and off (A4, last fold) -
  FIXED.** The fold reads the pane's last frame back at the live window's
  size (282 px) and then holds the hidden window at a rect that rounds to
  281 px. `paintStripFace` refused any size mismatch, which dropped the
  carried face and fell back to the live pane, and the next layout hid it
  again. The pane went live and hidden on alternate frames (`stack fold
  carried dropped ... live=1,0,1,0` x17 in one drag). Fix:
  `strip_face.carriedFits` allows 2 px of slack, since `blendContent` pins
  and clips the frame anyway. After the fix: 0 drops, all 74 carried frames
  `live=0`.
- **D2, the already-folded blade "does weird things" while the next pane
  folds (A4/A5) - FIXED (see the note below the paragraph).** When a new fold starts, the blade that
  is already in the stack is classified by the glide as UNFOLDING (`stack
  unfold carried ... card=1924,45 -> 1909,60`, then `stack unfold landed`).
  Its card widens 45 -> 60 px as the stack makes room, and that widening
  reads as coming out of the strip. So a card that stays a strip goes through
  the opening path: its live pane is placed hidden at a slot size, a frame is
  requested, and a band-only face is drawn over it. Every subsequent fold
  repeats this for every blade already in the stack. Next: make
  `pane_stack_slide`'s folding/unfolding classification ask whether the card
  is tucked at BOTH ends of the glide (strip -> strip is a move, not an
  unfold), with a unit test on the 5-blade chain shape.

  Fix: the cause was one step earlier than the classification. A blade that
  had folded and caught up still sat in the resting glide as a strip CARD,
  so `carryStripCard` carried it into the next glide as a card again, and
  `folding`/`unfolding` decided direction by width (drawn 45, headed 60 =
  "opening"). Now `StripCard.into` records the direction from the tucked
  state (in when tucked at the end, out otherwise), the two predicates read
  it, and a folded card that has caught up is left to `moveStrip`, which
  glides it strip-only like any strip that stays a strip (T2417). Unit test
  "a blade already in its strip moves strip-only as the next pane folds"
  was red at `!unfolding(2)` before the fix. Probe after: `stack unfold
  carried` 0, `stack unfold landed` 0, carried drops 0, 79 carried frames
  all `live=0`.
- **D3, blade names vanishing after the pointer stops (A6) - FIXED.** Not a
  skipped label: the probe's strip paints at rest all had the children hidden
  and the label present. The names were laid by `paintStackStrips` (TextOut)
  as a separate GDI pass AFTER the desk's BitBlt of the nameless bands, with
  `syncGlassBackdrops` in between. 150 ms after the hand rests, the drag hold
  releases and the six terminals reflow, the gap widens, and DWM composited
  the window between the two passes (run3 f306-308 + f310, release; run4
  f324, debug). Fix: `PaneDesk.names` lays the names into the composite for
  the blit and restores those pixels after, so the cache never holds them;
  `paintStackStrips` skips names the desk laid. Opaque windows only - a
  translucent window still alpha-blends the names in their own pass. run6
  (release, real pointer): no dropout after the hand stopped
  (`run6-settle-fixed.png`, `run6-dropout.txt` vs `run3-dropout.txt`).
- **Still open after run6:** f210 (fold start) shows pane 4 as a flat grey
  slab with the blades' names gone for one frame (`run6-f210-slab.png`), and
  f209 has doubled glyphs (A3). Dropouts in the blade region during folds:
  44 small ones plus 7 wide ones (f135, f168, f210, f266-f279), about the same
  as before D1/D2.
  The detector is `tools/dropout.py` (a column bin that falls under 25% of
  both neighbouring frames).
- **D4, a folding pane vanishing for frames at a time (run6 f210, run8
  f213-f214) - FIXED.** Debug build + real pointer + app log (run8). On the
  third fold the pane's frame was read back at 301 px, and the drag then
  went on squeezing the held window to 295. D1's 2 px slack refused that,
  so the fold fell back to the live pane. But the fold had already hidden
  that pane, so the frame showed no card at all, then the live one, then
  none again (`stack fold carried dropped frame=301x914 at=295x914
  live=1/0/1/0`, `run8-f213-hole.png`). Fix: a fold carries its frame
  whatever the window does after, pinned and clipped (the terminal is held,
  so that is what it shows); a misfit is only logged (`stack fold carried
  misfit`). run9 (debug, real pointer, same drag): 3 folds carried with
  misfits of up to 17 px and the pane stayed hidden under its face
  throughout. Wide dropouts dropped from 8 to 2 (`run8-dropout.txt`,
  `run9-dropout.txt`).
- **Still open after run9:** the 2 wide dropouts left are A1: at a fold's
  START (f150 first fold, f210 third fold) one pane shows as an empty card
  for one frame, with no text and no name (`run9-a1-empty-card.png`; pane 6 at
  f150, the folding pane 4 at f210). Doubled glyphs in the squeezed pane
  (A3, run8 f212/f215) are still there too.
- **D5, an empty card for one frame at a fold's START (A1) - FIXED for
  folds 1, 3 and 4.** Every fold shows its pane's strip face (a layered
  window) fresh, and the live pane was hidden in the same tick. DWM puts a
  layered window it has just been told to show on screen one composition
  later, so for one frame there was neither the face nor the pane. A first
  try kept the pane up until the next tick. That fixed fold 1, but at folds
  3/4 the squeezed held pane then drew blank (#282c34) and unclipped over the
  blades for two frames (`run10-f216-live-slab.png`). The fix: on the tick
  that first shows a face, `DwmFlush` once, then hide the pane in the same
  tick. run11 (debug, real pointer): folds 1, 3 and 4 start with no dip.
  **Still open: fold 2** dips for one frame (run11 f187 114 -> 27 -> 81,
  `run11-f187-fold2.png`; run9 f183 was the same before this change). Its face
  comes up while fold 1's face is still shown (fold 1's is hidden only
  later, run11 log 1246 vs 1311), so the cause is not the same as D5. Also
  seen in run11: a black column at x~1915-1960 over f186-f188 (A2-like), and
  f202 pane 2 drawn offset (A3).
- **D6, fold-start ordering measured (2026-10-30, run12/run13, debug, real
  pointer) - NOT fixed; one approach ruled out.** With a QPC stamp on every
  strip-face pass (`tools/qpc-stamp.patch`, matched to frames by
  `tools/corr.py <run> <stderr> <from> <to>`), the pass that first shows a
  fold's face and hides its pane reaches the screen 30-70 ms later, and the
  pane HIDE lands 1-3 frames BEFORE the face does: run12 fold 1 hide at f143,
  face at f144; fold 2 hide at f179, face at f182
  (`run12-fold2-hide-before-face.png`). So D5's DwmFlush does not order the
  two; it only won the race in run11. Fold 1 dipped again in run12.
  Ruled out: warming the face before the frame lands (shown 1x1 at alpha 1, so
  the carrying commit updates a face that is already up). run13 was worse: a
  blank grey (#282c34) slab with no face for 4 frames at folds 1 and 2
  (`run13-warm-grey-slab.png`), so the change was reverted. Also: `dropout.py`
  missed all of it, because a dip that lasts 3+ frames is not a dip to it, and a
  carried face at content_alpha ~110 sits under its brightness threshold anyway.
  Judge fold starts by eye (`tools/crop.py`) or with `corr.py`.
  Next candidates: (1) do not hide the pane until a LATER pass confirms the
  face is on screen (DwmGetCompositionTimingInfo frame counter past the
  commit), with the squeezed pane's blank repaint (run10 f216) handled by
  freezing its size, not by hiding it; (2) commit the face with
  UpdateLayeredWindow (per-pixel, applied atomically by DWM) instead of
  SetLayeredWindowAttributes + WM_PAINT into the redirection surface, which is
  the likely source of the lag.
- **D7, the fold-start empty card (A1, all folds) - FIXED by committing the
  face with UpdateLayeredWindow (2026-10-30, run14/run15, debug, real
  pointer).** `StripFace.commit` now hands DWM pixels, place and alpha in one
  `UpdateLayeredWindow` (constant alpha, `AlphaFormat` 0) instead of
  `SetLayeredWindowAttributes` + a WM_PAINT into the redirection surface; a
  copy of the frame still answers WM_PRINT, so the off-desktop probes
  photograph it (stack-fold-slide, pane-stack-unfold, pane-stack-slide,
  stack-collapse-drag, stack-collapse-carry all PASS). run14: folds 1-3 start
  with no empty card; run15: all four folds start clean
  (`run15-ulw-fold-starts.png`, compare run12's hide-before-face).
  **Seen once, in run14 only:** at fold 4 (f251-f252) the folding pane was
  still live, moved over the blade stack, with a blank gap left of it, for two
  frames before its face landed (`run14-f251-fold4-live-over-blades.png`; the
  run10 f216 shape). The log shows that pass with `live=1` before `strip face
  shown`. It did not recur in run15; watch for it on the next captures.
- **Still open after run15:** A3, doubled/offset glyphs in the squeezed pane
  (run15 f142, f178, f214 - visible on most frames near a fold start), the A2
  black column, and the near-full-monitor run.
- A1/A2 (one-frame black pane faces): A1 is D5/D7 above. A2 is not yet
  root-caused. The probe saw no frame-wait timeouts
  or stale presents in the debug build, so the black frames are more likely
  a shown-while-hidden toggle (the D1/D2 shape) than a slow renderer. That
  gets re-checked on the next real-pointer capture, after D2.
