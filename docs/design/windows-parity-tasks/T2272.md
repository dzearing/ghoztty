# T2272 - The unpinned pane sidebar's open card shows no accent gutter bar on its resize edge, unlike the pinned panel and the rail (Mac 5e4f30144)

## Summary

Split from T2265. Mac's SidePanelResizeHandle carries the GutterBar on every sidebar edge, the open card's included. T2265 painted it on the pinned panel (both styles) and the rail; the open card is a layered window (presentCard, per-pixel premultiplied alpha), and gutter_bar_dc.paintOn assumes an opaque DC, so PaneSidebar.paintEdgeBar skips the .card surface. Compose the bar into the card's premultiplied buffer instead (gutter_bar.paint already treats alpha as coverage) and extend pane-sidebar-resize.ps1 section 6 with the hover/drag/release bar rows.
