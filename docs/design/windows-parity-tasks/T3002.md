# T3002 - PA-M02: each window keeps its desk, scratch, glow and overlay pixel buffers at their largest size until it closes (25-40 MB at 1400x900, 165-265 MB at 4K; PaneDesk.zig:2508)

## Summary

PA-M02 (audit-1, T2961). PaneDesk desk_buf ('Grown, never shrunk', src/apprt/win32/PaneDesk.zig:2508-2510), scratch, scratch_desk, scratch_grad, quarter_buf, glow_px (2n) and the DIBs live on std.heap.page_allocator (:295) and are freed only in deinit (:1140-1152); chrome_comp.zig:359-362 Overlay.desk/.px likewise. A window maximized once keeps that size. Estimated 5 MB per buffer at 1400x900 (33 MB at 4K) x 5-8 live per window (count inferred). Fix: trim to current size at drag/resize end or on minimize/occlusion (the DIB's under-a-quarter rule), free glow/overlay/scratch when their feature idles. Risk low-medium: these exist to avoid page faults mid-drag (T2520, T2946) - trim only at gesture end or idle. win32-only. Baseline: docs/design/perf-audit/baseline-1.json (T2958); findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/M-findings.md.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] maximize then restore a window: private_mb after restore + idle settle drops by at least the maximized-minus-restored buffer size (measured, numbers in the task)
- [ ] drag-perf.ps1 / divider drag timing does not regress against its last recorded run
