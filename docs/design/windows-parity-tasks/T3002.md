# T3002 - PA-M02: each window keeps its desk, scratch, glow and overlay pixel buffers at their largest size until it closes (25-40 MB at 1400x900, 165-265 MB at 4K; PaneDesk.zig:2508)

## Summary

PA-M02 (audit-1, T2961). PaneDesk desk_buf ('Grown, never shrunk', src/apprt/win32/PaneDesk.zig:2508-2510), scratch, scratch_desk, scratch_grad, quarter_buf, glow_px (2n) and the DIBs live on std.heap.page_allocator (:295) and are freed only in deinit (:1140-1152); chrome_comp.zig:359-362 Overlay.desk/.px likewise. A window maximized once keeps that size. Estimated 5 MB per buffer at 1400x900 (33 MB at 4K) x 5-8 live per window (count inferred). Fix: trim to current size at drag/resize end or on minimize/occlusion (the DIB's under-a-quarter rule), free glow/overlay/scratch when their feature idles. Risk low-medium: these exist to avoid page faults mid-drag (T2520, T2946) - trim only at gesture end or idle. win32-only. Baseline: docs/design/perf-audit/baseline-1.json (T2958); findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/M-findings.md.

## In plain terms

Once you maximized a window, Ghoztty kept that window's background and pane-compositing memory at the maximized size until you closed it, even after restoring it to a small size. On a 4K screen that was tens of megabytes per window, held for nothing. Now a restored window gives that memory back, while dragging a window edge stays as smooth as before.

## Goals

- [x] the desk's CPU buffers fit the window again outside a drag
- [x] the chrome overlay's two buffers do the same
- [x] the desk's bitmap sections are given back once twice the bitmap, outside a drag
- [x] an acceptance harness that measures it, with a negative control

## Validation criteria

- [x] maximize then restore a window: private_mb after restore + idle settle drops by at least the maximized-minus-restored buffer size (measured, numbers in the task) - test\win32\maximize-restore-memory.ps1 ALL PASS on a 4K test desktop, 1000x700 restored, bar 28.1 MB: every restore gave back >=54.8 MB (kept-buffers control: 26.2, red), restored settles 309.5 MB vs 342.9 kept; flat after the first cycle
- [x] drag-perf.ps1 / divider drag timing does not regress against its last recorded run - under a drag `live` is set, so fit=false and tight=false: the exact pre-T3002 path. drag-perf run: worst batched mean 6387us (budget 33334), 8-pane tree mean 13.8 ms, pointer-to-commit 333us; its 4 reds are the zero-baseline asserts owned by T2690 (noted there)
