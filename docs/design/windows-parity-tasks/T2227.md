# T2227 - Folding a pane by dragging it to the window's edge: during the slide its background colour bleeds out past the window, over the desktop

## In plain terms

When you dragged a divider until a pane folded away to the window's edge,
a block of the pane's background colour slid out past the window and over
your desktop for the length of the animation, and the pane's scrollbar went
with it. Now the parts of a pane that sit on top of it - its dimming, its
scrollbar, its banner and badges - stay inside the window the whole way,
folding and unfolding.

## Goals

- [x] the unfocused-pane dimming never paints outside the window, at any
      frame of a fold, unfold or strip-click slide
- [x] the scrollbar, banner, read-only badge, key pill and bell frame never
      show outside the window either
- [x] an acceptance claim that samples every frame of the slide, red before
      the fix and green after

## Summary

User report 2026-10-24: "when a pane collapses to the edge, during the
animation it BLEEDS OUT OF THE WINDOW!! the bg color does!" - drag a divider
until a pane folds into a strip at the window's edge; while the fold slide
(T2186) plays, a block of the pane's background colour is drawn past the
window frame, over the desktop. It goes away when the slide ends (the resting
state was fixed by T2185).

Nothing painted by a pane may ever appear outside its window's client rect,
at any frame of any slide.

## Lead (unverified - confirm before fixing)

T2185's root cause was a layered POPUP (the unfocused-split `DimOverlay`)
placed over a folded pane's held rect, which for a trailing stack runs past
the window's right edge; popups are not clipped by the parent. The fix keyed
off the pane child's `WS_VISIBLE` (`DimState.hidden`): a hidden pane gets no
wash.

T2186 (1de51f746) then changed `pane_stack_slide.plan` so a card going behind
the run stays SHOWN at its held size until the slide ends, sliding to its
tucked rect. During those ~200 ms the pane is visible, so `shouldDim` passes
again and the wash (or another pane popup - banner, bell frame, key pill,
read-only badge, scrollbar, elevated-style shadow) tracks the card out past
the edge. Alternatively the pane child itself is not being clipped (check
the window has WS_CLIPCHILDREN and the card is a true WS_CHILD, not a popup)
- if "bg colour" is the terminal background rather than a grey wash, this
branch is the more likely one.

Fix direction: clip every pane popup to the intersection of its pane rect
and the window client rect (or hide it while its card is in a departing
slide), rather than adding another per-state exemption.
