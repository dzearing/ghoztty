# T1842 - The mini rail on Windows never opens: Mac's rail grows into the full card over the grid while the pointer is on it (140ms intent, ~280ms grace), without resizing any terminal, and each tile has a tooltip

## Summary

Follow-up of T1828 (Mac 597a193cc / e628f3933 / 51573daf0). Hover-open card floats over the grid, the column stays 68 DIP; never contracts under an open menu, a drag or the pointer on the pin just clicked (unpin leaves it open until the pointer leaves). Tiles carry a tooltip with the pane title.

**What landed.** The open card is a second window, a passive popup owned by
the top-level (`GhozttyPaneSidebarCard`), glued to the rail column's top-left
and clipped to the column plus the rounded card. Mechanism calls, recorded
here rather than filed (experience test: none of these is visible as a
choice):

- **A popup, not a wider child.** A child sidebar widened over the grid would
  sit UNDER the panes' own popups (dim overlay, banner strip, scrollbar), so a
  dimmed left pane would paint over the open card. As an owned popup seated
  above its siblings (`pane_sidebar_hover.seatAfter`, below the first window
  that is not ours) it covers them, and never floats over another app.
- **Timing from main, not the intake note.** Mac's `hoverOpenDelay` is 0.1 s
  on main today; the 140 ms in this title is an earlier revision. 280 ms grace.
- **The pointer is re-checked at timer time** (`GetCursorPos` +
  `WindowFromPoint`, honoring the region), as Mac re-checks
  `pointerIsOverCard`; a leave message alone cannot say whether it came back.
- **Held open by a press on the card** (capture), retried every grace period;
  drag (T1844) and the right-click menu (T1845) must add themselves to the
  same hold - noted on those tasks.
- **Tooltips**: a tile's tip is the pane's title, then Mac's click hint
  (`Click to focus` / `Click to restore`) - the rail shows only a monogram,
  so the tip is where the name is read. Rows and row buttons in the panel and
  the open card carry Mac's hint / `Stash` / `Restore`, the pin Mac's
  pin/unpin wording.
- **The tooltip control is UNOWNED.** Owned by the window, the topmost
  tooltip - created on every window's first layout - lifted a window opened
  in the background above the user's frontmost Ghoztty window
  (`cli-focus-policy.ps1` arm R: z=6 over z=9, deterministic; green with the
  tooltip disabled, green unowned). The other owned tooltips are T1866.
- Hard region edge: the card's right-hand corners are clipped by a window
  region, so the card's soft shadow does not spill onto the grid as it does
  on Mac. Filed as a polish follow-up (see Progress log).

## In plain terms

With the sidebar unpinned, Windows showed only the slim column of pane
icons and nothing else - you had to pin it to read a pane's name. Now resting
the pointer on that column opens the full list over the terminals for as long
as you stay on it, then folds back, and no terminal jumps or redraws while it
does. Hovering any icon also shows that pane's name.
