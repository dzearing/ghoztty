# T1907 - A glass pane's desk slice is re-rendered on the UI thread for every pane on every divider-drag mouse move: about 4.7ms per large pane in Debug

## Summary

syncGlassBackdrops keys each pane's backdrop on its rect, so a divider drag re-renders (desk_palette.renderDeskRegionRgba, quarter resolution, per-pixel gradient math) and ships a new slice for both resized panes every move: 4658us for a 1250x1350 pane in Debug (microbench 2026-10-13), inside drag-perf's paint term. Options: hand the renderer the whole desk once and a sub-rect per move; or crop from PaneDesk's cached quarter-size desk instead of re-evaluating the gradient; or defer the slice to drag end. Must keep the glass seam-free during the drag.

## In plain terms

With the default glass look, dragging the gap between two panes made each
terminal redraw its piece of the background gradient from scratch on every
mouse move, a few milliseconds a pane. Each terminal now copies its piece out
of the background the window has already drawn, so the drag does less work per
move, and the glass under a terminal is now the same pixels that sit around it.

## Goals

- [x] Stop evaluating the gradient for a terminal's glass slice on every drag move
- [x] Keep the glass seam-free (the slice is the window's own desk pixels)
- [x] Measure the drag before and after

## Approach

Of the three options in the Summary, cropping from the cached desk was taken:
`Window.syncGlassBackdrops` passes `PaneDesk.backdrop` (the full-size desk the
paint just blitted, cached, so it costs nothing) to `Surface.setPaneBackdrop`,
which now fills the quarter-size slice with `desk_palette.cropDeskRegionRgba` -
one pixel read per texel at its centre - instead of `renderDeskRegionRgba`.
The analytic path stays as the fallback when the desk is missing or a
different size. Handing the renderer the whole desk once would add a second
GPU copy of it per pane; deferring the slice to drag end would show a stretched
or stale sheet under the moving edge, which is the seam the task forbids.

The viewer's glass sheet has the same shape at full resolution and is filed as
T1923 (its spec is stored and painted later, so it cannot borrow the slice).
