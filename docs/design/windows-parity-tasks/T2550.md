# T2550 - After T2538 the viewer nav bar is still ~0.75 ms of every resize pass: its own move (~0.25 ms) and its one paint (~0.43 ms incl. glass ~0.11) remain; find what in BeginPaint/EndPaint and the band paint is left and whether the bar can ride the pane's own paint instead of being a child window

## Summary

What is left of the viewer nav bar's share of a resize pass after T2538, measured
part by part, and what can come out of it while the bar stays a child window.

Measured with a ReleaseSafe build, `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1` and
`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1` (four drags, 128 passes, medians in us):

| | nav | bar move | paint | glass | buttons+field | paint overhead | tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (HEAD 83c36c899) | 600 | 201 | 345 | 105 | 150 | ~90 | 28 |
| back-buffered band + lean redraw | **532** | 195 | **282** | 84 | 107 | ~90 (incl. the one blit) | 28 |

- **Paint.** The band was painted straight into the window DC: a
  `StretchDIBits` of the glass, then a GDI call per button glyph, then the
  field's two blits, all against the redirection surface. Now it is painted
  into a back buffer (a top-down 32bpp DIB section, grown in 256-column steps
  and never per drag step), the glass is copied into the section's own pixels
  with no GDI call at all, and the window DC sees ONE `BitBlt`. `RedrawWindow`
  no longer walks the field's frame and client (`RDW_FRAME | RDW_ALLCHILDREN`)
  while the field is idle - since T2538 it is a hidden window then.
- **The move** (~0.2 ms) does not respond to flags: `SWP_NOSENDCHANGING` plus a
  handled `WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED` (no WM_SIZE/WM_MOVE) measured 201 -> 202 us, so
  that was reverted. It is the cost of being a child window at all.
- **Riding the pane's own paint** is the only route to the 0.3 ms target: the
  host already paints its whole client on every step of a drag on glass
  (`paintUnderPage`), so a band painted there pays neither the move nor a
  second BeginPaint/EndPaint. The bar window carries input, tooltips, the
  EDIT's parenting and every harness's handle on the chrome, so it cannot just
  go; the shape that keeps all of that is the T2538 trick one level up - hide
  the bar while a drag is moving it, paint its band in the host, put it back a
  beat after the drag settles. Filed as T2767.

Viewer total per pass 952 -> 909 us. Bad-frame counts in the same run are
unchanged against the baseline: every `.4` row 0, and the four `.5` rows red
in both, which is T2516's open defect.

## In plain terms

When you drag a window edge, every viewer pane's top bar is redrawn at the new
width on every step. This trims what that redraw costs - the bar is now drawn
off screen and put up in one go - and works out what it would take to stop
paying for the bar as a separate window during a drag at all.
