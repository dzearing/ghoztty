# T1906 - An elevated divider drag still takes about 60ms a mouse move in Debug, and 40ms of it is outside every timer drag-perf reports (layout minus place, paint and overlay)

## Summary

After T1856 cut the desk paint from 42ms to 16ms per move (Debug, drag-perf 2026-10-13), mean_layout_us is still ~60ms against a 33ms two-frame budget, and ~40ms of it is accounted to none of place/paint/overlay/resize - the same 40ms at HEAD before T1856 (88ms layout, 42ms paint). Candidates: applyCardRegions' SetWindowRgn(redraw) on every resized child each move, the sidebar's deskPainted repaint, the dim overlays. Instrument the pass, find the 40ms, and make drag-perf's 'two frames or better' assertion go green.

## In plain terms

Dragging the gap between two panes in the default (rounded-card) look lagged
behind the mouse: each mouse move took about 60 ms in a debug build, so the
drag ran at roughly 15 frames a second. Two thirds of that went on re-cutting
each pane's rounded corners in a way that made Windows redraw the pane on the
spot. The corners are now cut just before the window paints its background,
which fills them for free, and a mouse move takes about 21 ms.

## Goals

- [x] Find the 40 ms no drag-perf timer accounted for
- [x] Remove it without changing what the corners look like
- [x] drag-perf's "two frames or better" assertion goes green

## Root cause

`applyCardRegions` ran as a `defer` at the end of `layoutSplits`, AFTER the
desk paint, and called `SetWindowRgn(h, rgn, TRUE)` for every resized pane.
The redraw is synchronous work on a GL child: a new `cards=` term in the drag
line measured it at 41.5 ms of a 62.6 ms move at 2 panes (~20 ms a pane).

Fix: `cutCardRegions` runs right after `endPaneBatch` and before the desk
paint, with `bRedraw = FALSE` for the elevated style. The desk paint goes
through `GetDC` on a `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` parent, so it is clipped by the regions
as they now stand and paints the corners they gave back; the pane's own pixels
come from the frame its resize already waits on. The flat style keeps the
redraw (no desk paint follows to cover a dropped region), as does the hero
carousel's region clear.

Found on the way: `IpcServer.logPerf` panicked in `Instant.since` under
`GHOZTTY_PERF` when the GUI thread's `gui_start` stamp landed after the
listener's `now` (crashed drag-perf's first run); it now reads 0 for an
inverted pair.
