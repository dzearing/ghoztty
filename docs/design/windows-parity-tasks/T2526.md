# T2526 - Windows pane sidebar drag: a window dragged past a group's end (or a folded group's header) stays in that group, leaving only when dragged up among the ungrouped windows; the joined group's header glows (soft accent wash, no outline) only in the window dragged into (Mac 0100ae3db, cb9e28ff2)

## Summary

Mac 0100ae3db: the top-level slot after a group used to split at its middle, so a group's last window, grabbed, started in its lower half and wanted out at once. Now right under a header, between a group's windows, or anywhere past its last row (or a folded group's header) the item is in that group; it is out of a group only up among the ungrouped windows (above the first group). Mac test PaneSidebarCrossDropTests.pastAGroupsEndItStaysInThatGroup. Mac cb9e28ff2: the join highlight (PaneSidebarJoinHighlight) becomes a soft accent wash + glow instead of an accent outline, and only in the sidebar of the window being dragged into, not every window's sidebar. Windows: src/apprt/win32/PaneSidebar.zig join outline (~line 317, 4174) and the banded drop logic.
