# T2723 - The stepped focus-glow fade still costs ~3.4 ms a tick in Debug (compose ~1.9 ms of thin bands + ~1 ms fixed paint overhead): interpolate cached lit/unlit band pictures instead of re-composing every tick, to get frame-clock.ps1's glow cost under 3 ms

## In plain terms

When Ghoztty cannot hand the focus glow's fade to the compositor (an older
box, the test desktop, a failed layer), it repaints the glow itself every
frame. That repaint is now small and drops no frames, but it still uses about
a fifth of each frame in a debug build; this makes it cheaper again.

## Summary

Left over from T2370 (2026-11-05). The stepped glow fade (`Window.focusGlowTick`
when `glowOnCompositor()` is false) re-composes only thin bands round the two
lit cards now (`PaneDesk.Memo.Rects.addGlowBands`, ~5.6% of a 1182x791 desk),
and `frame-clock.ps1` measures the glow tick at a mean ~3.0-3.5 ms in Debug
(was 15.5 ms at filing, 4.4 ms after T2454). Traced breakdown
(`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE`, median per tick): compose+blit ~1.9 ms (of which
`paintGlows` ~0.78, `paintShadows` ~0.47, the cards ~0.39 - about 34 ns a
pixel in Debug), desk setup `desk-enter`->`desk-pre` ~0.27 ms, the blit under
layered panes ~0.17 ms, WM_TIMER->desk ~0.16 ms.

Fix idea: when a paint differs from the memo ONLY in the glow levels of the
same lit cards, do not compose. Cache, once per fade (keyed on
`compose_gen`/geometry), the band pictures at the corner levels - a blend
chain over two glows is bilinear in (L1, L2), so four pictures (00/10/01/11)
interpolate it exactly up to rounding, shadows and solid cards are affine
over it - and per tick write `bilerp` into `comp` over the bands and blit
them. Glass edge pixels go through the sheet LUT (non-linear), so measure the
error; at the fade's end the levels reach 0/1 and the normal partial compose
restores exact pixels. T2574's `glowLayer` test shows the same linearity
argument and its tolerance.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] `test\win32\frame-clock.ps1` reports the glow's `cost mean` under 3 ms (Debug, test desktop), doubled share still 0-1%.
- [ ] A unit test holds the interpolated band within a stated tolerance of a full compose at several levels, glass and solid, scale 1 and 1.5.
- [ ] The fade's last frame is pixel-exact against a full compose (the normal path takes over).
