# T3310 - PA-M20/V32: a glass viewer keeps a pane-sized buffer (x1.25) that never shrinks and is not freed while hidden (ViewerPane.zig:7990-8002)

## Summary

glassPixels (ViewerPane.zig:7990-7997) only grows glass_buf (n + n/4, page allocator); releaseGlassBuf (:7999-8002) is not called on hide or shrink. About 6 MB at 1400x900, 41 MB at 4K, per glass viewer. Regression of T3041 (663e4a850). Fix: free on hide, and shrink when the pane is much smaller than the buffer. Reported by areas M and V. Audit-4 (T3151), Fable 5.2 area finding at c9af15802; cites read at HEAD by the owning turn. Detail: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-4/M-findings.md
