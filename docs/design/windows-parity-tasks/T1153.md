# T1153 - The feedback composer's buttons slide off the left edge of a narrow viewer pane

## In plain terms

Open the feedback composer in a viewer pane and then squeeze the pane narrow,
and the composer's buttons walk off the left-hand side of the pane. They are
pinned to the right and laid out leftwards from there, and nothing stops them
once there is no room left.

## Summary

Found while fixing T1130, which took the same class of defect out of the
contents card and the nav bar strip. `viewer_feedback_layout.zig` pins the
action buttons to the pill's bottom trailing corner and walks LEFT:

```zig
var right = pill.right - pill_pad;
while (i > 0) { i -= 1; const left = right - m.target; ... right = left - pill_pad; }
```

Nothing floors `left` at the pill's own left edge, so a pane narrow enough gives
negative lefts and the leading buttons are outside the pill and outside the
pane. The text rect immediately below IS floored
(`@max(buttons[0].left - gap, text_left)`), which is what makes the omission
read as an oversight rather than a choice.

The remedy should follow the rule T1130 settled for the nav strip (D83): a
control that does not fit WHOLE is not painted - an empty rect that costs no
pixels and answers no hit - rather than one parked outside the band. Check the
composer's paint and hit paths for empty-rect handling first; the nav bar has
that convention already and the composer may not.

## Goals

- [x] No composer control is ever placed outside the pill, at any pane width.
      An action that does not fit whole is dropped (send is kept last).
- [x] Paint and hit testing both ignore a dropped control (they already
      skipped empty rects, as do the tooltips), and the Tab walk now skips one
      too (`Layout.reachable`).
- [x] Pure-module tests walk the width band one pixel at a time, as the T1130
      tests do.
