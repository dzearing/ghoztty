# T2450 - The elevated desk's card shadows are recomputed in software over the whole window on every layout pass (6.4 ms at 1x, 22 ms at 4K/200% in ReleaseFast), so a fold-glide frame under a drag still overruns a 60 Hz period on a large window

## In plain terms

Dragging a divider so that panes fold away still does not glide smoothly on a
big window. Every frame of the glide redraws the soft shadows under every pane
from scratch, and on a 4K screen that alone takes longer than one screen
refresh. When this lands the glide keeps up with the display at any window size.

## Summary

Split from T2448 (2026-10-29). T2448 removed the two passes that doubled every
glide frame (the mouse move's own layout, and the `WM_PAINT` that recomposed the
identical desk), which took the debug build from a 62 ms to a 48 ms glide frame
with 0 window paints during the glide. What is left is the tick itself, and the
tick is almost entirely `PaneDesk.paint` -> `pane_elevation.paintCards`.

Measured with a ReleaseFast `zig test` bench of `paintCards` (6 cards, one glow):

| window | all | no glow | shadows off (fill only) | full memcpy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1582x891 @1x | 6.4 ms | 6.2 ms | 0.47 ms | 0.19 ms |
| 3800x2050 @2x | 22.4 ms | 21.8 ms | 2.4 ms | 1.7 ms |

So the shadows are ~90% of it: two Gaussian layers, `erfc` in the corners, and
two float `blend`s per shadow pixel, over every card's whole shadow ring, on
every pass, though most cards did not move.

Directions, not yet chosen: (a) the shadow ring of a rounded rect is four
size-independent corner patches plus 1-D edge profiles, so cache them per style
and composite with one integer blend per pixel; (b) recompose only the rects of
cards whose geometry changed (old and new bbox plus shadow reach), keeping a
light-free base buffer, since the busy lights write into `comp`; (c) both. The
`T2448` memo in `PaneDesk` (skip the compose when the inputs are identical)
stays either way.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] the bench above, before and after, at both sizes; shadows under 2 ms at 4K @2x
- [ ] `divider glide` line (GHOZTTY_PERF) on a folding row with a viewer pane: `late=0`, i.e. no glide tick more than two periods after the last, on a release build (T2448's carried criterion)
- [ ] pane_elevation pixel tests unchanged (or a documented, sub-1/255 rounding delta)
