# T2083 - Stacked panes: a collapsed row keeps its strips minimized when a pane in it closes, is stashed, moves away, or a split adds one

## In plain terms

When a row is folded into strips, closing, stashing or moving one of its
panes, or adding a split to it, must keep the row folded. Before these Mac
fixes, the row sprang back open and every strip came out.

## Summary

- Mac 3090ac2aa (`fix(macos): closing a pane in a collapsed row keeps its
  strips minimized`): a row's collapse ("pin") is keyed by its set of
  panes, so a pane leaving the row made the lookup miss. Fix:
  `PaneStackState.rowsDidChange` carries the pin over to the row's smaller
  key on every tree change (`Pin.removing`). The panes on show close up
  over the slot, strips stay on their sides, and slot sizes are kept. If
  the only pane on show closes, the strip before it comes out. The old pin
  is kept, so an undo or a restored stash brings the row back as it was.
  After a close the caret lands on a pane that is on show.
- Mac d26e51183 (`fix(macos): adding a pane to a collapsed row keeps its
  strips minimized`): the carry-over generalised to `Pin.carrying`, which
  covers panes that are gone, joined, or moved within the row. A pane that
  joins beside or inside the run is on show; one that joins among the
  strips stays a strip until focused. Reorders are measured from the
  laid-out order. Focus arriving before or after the tree change gives the
  same row.

Windows translation: the same pin-carrying rule in T2082's stack state
(pure, unit-tested), applied on every SplitTree mutation (close, stash,
`+rearrange`, a drag to another window or tab, split, undo).
