# T2703 - A dragged pane shows no ghost card under the pointer on Windows; Mac now hangs a small card of the pane (background, title strip, content) below the pointer from the first move

## In plain terms

When you drag a pane by its grab handle on the Mac, a small picture of the
pane now follows the pointer so you can see what you are carrying. On Windows
nothing follows the pointer: only the drop highlight moves. After this lands,
Windows shows the same little card.

## Summary

main intake 2026-11-04, from 30d13ea14 (`feat(macos): drag a pane by its grab
handle, with a ghost and clearer drop targets`), `PaneDragGhost.swift` and
`docs/reference/pane-rearrange.md`:

- From the drag's first move, a ghost card hangs **below** the pointer, held by
  its title strip, so the target under the pointer stays visible.
- The card is the pane's background + a title strip + its content, in the
  pane's aspect, at most 220x150 (pt; scale by DPI here). It must be a card,
  not a bare snapshot: a terminal surface alone is glyphs on nothing.
- Applies to terminal and viewer panes, from both entry points (hover grab
  handle and rearrange header).
- A strip (minimized pane) and a sidebar-row drag draw **no** ghost - the real
  thing moves with the pointer. A stashed pane drags its sidebar row instead.
- Mac also gives the grip an open-hand / closed-hand cursor. Windows already
  decided its grip cursor in T1536 (`IDC_SIZEALL`; Windows has no open hand that
  does not mean "link") - no parity owed for the cursor.
- Perf on Mac: the drag publishes nothing per move. Windows has its own drag
  perf harness (`test\win32\drag-perf.ps1`); the ghost must not add per-move
  redraws of panes (a layered window moved with `SetWindowPos`/ULW only).

Where: `src/apprt/win32/PaneGrabHandle.zig`, `rearrange_header.zig`,
`DropHighlight.zig` (likely home for a layered ghost window), the drag loop
that feeds `pane_drop.zig`.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A grab-handle drag of a terminal pane shows a card below the pointer from the first move, at most 220x150 DIP, in the pane's aspect, with background + title strip + content (acceptance script, off-desktop capture)
- [ ] Same for a viewer pane and for a rearrange-header drag
- [ ] A strip drag and a sidebar-row drag show no ghost
- [ ] The ghost is gone after drop and after a cancelled (Escape) drag
- [ ] drag-perf.ps1 shows no per-move pane redraw added by the ghost
