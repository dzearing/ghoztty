# T3744 - Linux: closing a pane beside a collapsed row's strip focuses the strip and brings it back out on GTK - the close's focus target must be the pane on show (PaneStackState.paneOnShow), as win32 and Mac do

## Summary

Found during T3627. GTK's closeConfirmationClose (src/apprt/gtk/class/split_tree.zig) picks next_focus as the tree's previous/next leaf (goto), with no regard for stacking: when that neighbour is minimized in a collapsed row, the rebuild focuses it and propSurfaceFocused relays the stage, so the strip comes back out and the user's collapse is undone by an unrelated close. win32 routes the close's caret through PaneStackState.paneOnShow(candidate, old_rows, new_rows) (Window.paneOnShowAfter) and Mac does the same. Port it: compute rows before/after the removal (SplitTree.stackRowsNow, added by T3627) and take paneOnShow's answer. Acceptance: a test/linux/stack-persist.sh section - three panes, the user collapses the leading one, close the focused middle neighbour: the caret goes to the pane on show and the strip stays a strip.
