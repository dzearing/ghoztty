# T1590 - A config reload does all of its window work three times over

## Summary

Measured in T765: one reload_config chord runs the whole per-window rebuild THREE times - applyChromeTheme, applyBackgroundBlur, updateDimOverlays, refreshAllDividerBands, createTabFont/createPillFont (two HFONTs deleted and recreated each pass), invalidateTabBar and tabTipReset, once per pass. The fan-out is one app-level config_change plus one per surface, so it scales with pane count: a window with six panes rebuilds its chrome seven times for one keypress. Decide whether the per-surface config_change should reach the app-level handler at all, or whether onConfigChange should coalesce.
