# T2524 - Windows pane sidebar: an Ungrouped header pins over the lone windows whenever a group exists, folds like a group, and its +, x and pencil open an ungrouped window, close them, or name them all as a group (Mac 7bee055c9)

## Summary

Mac 7bee055c9 (PaneSidebarWindowGrouping.headsUngrouped): with at least one group and one lone window, the lone windows sit under a sticky 32pt 'Ungrouped' header with the group header's count and hover tools. Pinned on top, never dragged, not in windowOrder; a click folds it (shared fold state, not persisted; a group drag leaves it as it is). Its + opens a new untitled window from its first window as listed; its x closes its windows (the matching ones while searching); its pencil names them as a group: an empty field (placeholder 'Group name') retitles each '<name>: <title>', pinned, and the new group stands first among the groups, or, named as an existing group, they join it ahead of its windows. Not in the rail, not in this window's own Panes list. No group or no lone window: flat list, no header. Mac reference: docs/reference/pane-sidebar.md; PaneSidebarSyncWindowTests.ungroupedHeaderCaptures.
