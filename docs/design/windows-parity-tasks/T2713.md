# T2713 - A burst grow step that rebuilds the desk backdrop (desk build ~2.6 ms + compose ~5.8 ms, Debug viewer scene) holds its pane's frame past the renderer's hold timeout: edge-drag-rate -Burst E 'no held frame ran out the hold timeout' goes red 1 run in 2

## In plain terms

With the experimental "burst" resize on, a window-edge drag sometimes has one
step where the window's background has to be rebuilt from scratch. That step
takes long enough that a terminal's waiting frame gives up and goes out on its
own, a moment ahead of the window outline - the thing the burst exists to stop.

## Summary

Found while validating T2711 (2026-11-04 22:3x, Debug, `test\win32\edge-drag-rate.ps1 -Burst`,
second of two runs). In the viewer scene, one GROW step's vacated-strip desk
paint did a full backdrop rebuild (`desk build render_us=699 scale_us=1894
(1566x791)`, then `desk trace desk_us=2614 compose_us=5792`): `vacated-dc` ->
`bands-desk` was ~11.6 ms against the usual ~2.5. The pane had been
`swap-held` since before that paint, so its hold ran out
(`swap-hold-timeout`) during the burst's WM_PAINT, before `burst-release`.
T2711's change (skip the post-frame desk paint on a shrink) does not run on a
grow step, so this is the existing path. The first Debug run of the same
build was clean on that row, as was the ReleaseFast run.

Options: rebuild the backdrop outside the held window (before arming any hold,
or on the first step of the drag at the drag's max size), or arm the holds
after the band paints rather than during the pane batch. Decide from the
trace which leaves the burst intact.

## Goals

- [x] Find why a held frame outlived its hold: the timeout's budget left out the rest of the pre-layout pass
- [x] Budget the pass tail explicitly and size the hold timeout to cover it
- [x] A hold that does time out costs one frame, not every frame at that size

## Fix (2026-11-05)

The hold starts when a pane's renderer lands its new-size frame, which is
DURING the pass's pane batch - so the rest of the pass (the vacated-strip desk
paint, the popups) is held time as well as the gather, the latch step and the
bar paint. `resize_burst.hold_timeout_ms` (16) was sized for those three only.
A step whose desk paint outgrew the drag's canvas spends ~11.6 ms Debug in
that tail, and ran the hold out.

- `resize_burst.pass_tail_us` (14 ms) names the tail, and `hold_timeout_ms`
  is now 32, held above tail + gather + span + margin by a `none`-lane test.
- `Surface.holdSwap` drops the hold it timed out on (compare-exchange, so a
  hold the UI thread re-armed meanwhile stays): a release that never comes
  now costs one frame, where before every frame drawn at that size waited the
  full timeout again until the next pass.

Considered and not taken: rebuilding the canvas before the holds are armed.
It moves no cost out of the step (the rebuild is in the same
`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGING`) and covers one cause of a long tail only; any other
slow pass tail would still run the hold out and send the content up a compose
ahead of the frame.
