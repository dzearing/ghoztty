# T2538 - The viewer nav bar is still ~1.1 ms of every window-resize pass after T2531: the address EDIT's resize (~0.33 ms) and its share of the band repaint are the largest part left; owner-draw the field while it is not being edited so a drag moves no EDIT at all

## Summary

T2531 painted the bar and its address field once per pass instead of twice (nav_us 1445 -> 1144 us median, ReleaseSafe resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 with GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1). Left: bar SetWindowPos ~0.2 ms, EDIT SetWindowPos + ShowWindow ~0.33 ms, the one band repaint ~0.53 ms (glass 0.11 + buttons 0.13 in our code, the rest BeginPaint/EndPaint, the EDIT's own paint and the GDI flush). Two child HWNDs cannot reach the 0.3 ms target. Direction: paint the address text as part of the bar while the field does not have focus (cue banner, text from its first visible char, border, colours - pixel-identical to the EDIT), and keep the real EDIT hidden until it is focused, so a resize pays one move and one paint. Oversizing the bar to skip its move is ruled out: harnesses click relative to the bar's right edge and viewer-narrow-pane asserts chrome sits inside the host.

## In plain terms

When you drag a window edge, every viewer pane's top bar has to be redrawn at
the new width on every step, and the address box in it used to be a separate
little window that had to be moved and redrawn on its own each time. Now the
bar draws the address box itself while you are not typing in it, so a drag
moves nothing but the bar. Clicking the address (or ctrl+l) brings the real
text box up exactly where the picture of it was.

## Goals

- [x] a drag moves no EDIT: the field is a window on screen only while it is being edited
- [x] the idle field is the EDIT's own pixels (cache printed once, spliced to the width by two blits), not a hand-drawn imitation
- [x] clicking the field, ctrl+l / alt+d, and focus restores all bring the live control up; losing focus puts the picture back
- [x] nav bar share of a resize pass measured before/after

## Outcome (2026-10-31)

ReleaseSafe, `scripts\resize-sync-onscreen.ps1` with `GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`,
four drags, 256 trace lines, medians in us:

| | nav | bar move | edit | paint | glass | buttons(+field) | viewer total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T2531 (before) | 1144 | 199 | 335 | 535 | 110 | 130 | ~1600 |
| first cut: print the hidden EDIT every paint | 1203 | 261 | 223 | 585 | 113 | 324 | 1599 |
| landed: cached print + deferred EDIT move | **747** | 248 | **5** | 427 | 106 | 153 | 1176 |

The first cut (hidden EDIT, printed into the band on every paint) bought
nothing: a hidden EDIT still pays ~0.22 ms to be moved (it re-lays itself out)
and its print added ~0.19 ms to the paint. What landed: the EDIT is printed
ONCE into a memory bitmap wide enough for its text, re-printed only when its
text, theme, font or height changes, and each paint is two blits - the cache's
left part, then the control's clip tail (its inner inset, read off the control
as `EM_GETRECT.left` less the left margin). The hidden EDIT itself is moved
120 ms after the band stops moving (`syncEdit`), or at once when editing
begins, so its rect is still the truth every reader and harness uses.

Two facts about the comctl32 v6 EDIT the splice depends on, both found by the
new win32-lane test rather than assumed: it draws a `WS_BORDER` border inside
its client rect (window and client rects agree), and it clips text at its
inner inset on the right, not at its right margin (a long address has ink in
the right-margin columns). The 0.3 ms target in the triage reason is not
reachable by this change alone: what remains is the bar's own move (~0.25 ms)
and its one paint (~0.43 ms, glass included).
