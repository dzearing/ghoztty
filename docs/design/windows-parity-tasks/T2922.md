# T2922 - A fold-glide pass that resizes a viewer pane still costs ~2 ms more than one that does not: ~1 ms of EndDeferWindowPos is the WebView2 host's own cross-process resize, and the held bounds sync after it is ~1.5 ms (nav bar move + paint, put_Bounds)

## In plain terms

Dragging a divider next to a markdown or web viewer still makes each frame
that resizes the viewer about 2 ms slower than other frames. T2838 moved
most of that work out of the way of the terminals. This card is about the
cost that is left.

## Summary

Left over from T2838 (2026-11-11). Release build, T2732 glide probe (5
panes, viewer second, 1875x1125, ring trace, A/B via
`GHOZTTY_VIEWER_SYNC_INLINE=1`), the batch split at the `stack trace
filled` stamp:

| arm | EndDeferWindowPos (viewer resized / not) | after it | pass total |
|---|---|---|---|
| inline (before) | 2.91 / 0.46, 3.31 / 0.48 | 0.29, 0.36 | 3.20, 3.67 |
| held (T2838) | 1.17 / 0.48, 1.49 / 0.48 | 1.69, 1.82 | 2.86, 3.31 |

What is left on a viewer-resized pass:
- **~0.7-1.0 ms inside `EndDeferWindowPos`**: the viewer host HWND's own
  resize. A windowed WebView2 parents the browser's HWND from another
  process, so sizing the host synchronises with it. Look at whether the host
  can be moved without a size change mid-glide (size once at settle, content
  clipped or scaled by the compositor), or a visual-hosted WebView2
  (`CreateCoreWebView2CompositionController`) that has no cross-process
  child at all.
- **~1.3-1.5 ms of held sync after the batch**: nav bar move + band paint
  (~0.5 ms) and `put_Bounds` (0.3-0.6 ms, with rare 10 ms outliers). The
  T2767 deferred-band path (host paints the band, bar hidden) cuts the first
  half on window-edge drags; extending it to divider/fold glides needs a
  frame-exact check that the band never lags or blanks mid-glide.

Probe and splitter: `glide-probe.ps1` + `vb2.py` in session 83cd587d's
scratchpad.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] viewer-resized passes' total within 1 ms of other passes on the glide probe (release, ring trace, `vb2.py`)
- [ ] the viewer's band and content never lag or blank mid-glide (viewer-panes acceptance plus a frame check of the band)
