# T2654 - With GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1 a right-edge grow leaves a 5-6 px flat column past the viewer page's right edge on most frames (page and address bar a step short of the frame)

## Summary

Found filming T2549 on HEAD 18627a0d4: right-grow scored flat=5/6 on 3-6 frames per drag, a thin column to the right of the viewer page. **Re-measured 2026-11-28 on 5eaac0760 it is much more frequent, and the default path shows it too**: `resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 -Drags right-grow` (ReleaseSafe) scores flat on 28-47 of ~55 frames with `GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1` and 17-22 with the knob off. The column is the **window background (the desk), not the viewer's own paint**: the pixels are the config background, and they sit between the page's right edge and the card's page-coloured rim, which is already at the new width. So the card's rim moves with the frame, but neither the viewer host nor the card body under it does. What is left: find which surface lags the card rim by ~6 px (half a 12 px step) and bring it into step.

## In plain terms

Dragging the window's right edge outward with a web page open in a side pane, a thin stripe of window background flickers between the page and the pane's right edge on most frames. It goes away when the drag stops.

## Findings (2026-11-28)

Per-frame pixels at row 300 (`%TEMP%\t2654-film-a-59104\right-grow\f0009.bmp`, w=1096): magenta page to x=1074, six columns of `32,160,32` (the film's config background), then at 1081 a 1 px `255,11,255` card rim, then desk. In the settled frame the page runs right up to the rim (1369), so there is no gutter by design. Some frames also show a 10 px black column next to the green (a surface that had grown but was not painted yet).

**Tried and reverted** (patch kept in the session scratchpad, no effect on the film, so not kept):

1. The host's off-glass `paintRect` fill took the page colour (`cardColors().fill`) instead of `bg`. No change: the film's viewer is on glass, so that path never runs there.
2. On glass, `paintUnderPage` filled the opaque `page_rect` (which T3041 skips) with the page colour. Frames with a green band went 45-50 to 35-36, and frames with black went 11-13 to 9-14. Both are within run-to-run noise, so the strip is not the host's unpainted area.

That points at the desk: T2577 holds a fixed desk canvas through a drag, and the card body is the desk's paint (`pane_elevation.paintCards`, `Card.fill` = the page colour). A rim at the new width with config background inside it means the canvas the card was painted into, or the host's position, is a step behind the rim. Next step: line the `resize trace` (`GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1`, `viewer-size`/`desk trace`) up with frames.txt QPC stamps for one flat frame and name the surface that is late.
