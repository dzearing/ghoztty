# T3840 - Linux L11b6a: undo puts back a lone pane's tab that a tab-bar drop moved to another index, as the Mac's undo of that drag does

## Summary

Follow-up of T3706. A pane that is its tab's only one, dropped on the tab bar of its own window, reorders its tab (Window.dropOnTabBar, pane_drop_apply.tabDrop .reorder) instead of making a new tab. On the Mac the same drag is moveToNewTab inside one undo group, so Cmd+Z puts the tab back where it was; on GTK that reorder is recorded nowhere, so Ctrl+Shift+Z does nothing for it. Record the tab and its old index on CloseUndo (a reorder entry, or a move entry that carries a tab position), undo/redo it, and add the check to test/linux/pane-drag-undo.sh.
