# T2855 - The pane sidebar column's declared glide (T2827) has no on-screen film: the gutter between the sidebar card and the grid's leading card through a GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLIDE_STALL_MS stall is proven by log only

## Summary

T2827 hands a followed pane sidebar column glide to the compositor (Window.declareSidebarColumn) and proves it by log and window geometry (pane-sidebar-glide.ps1 -Tree section 7). Add a sidebar arm to scripts/comp-glide-hold-onscreen.ps1 on T2851's setup (sidebar toggle with GHOZTTY_DEBUG_GLIDE_STALL_MS, composition pane tree): film the column's right edge and the grid's leading card's left edge frame by frame and assert the gutter between them stays within 2 px of its rest width on every frame of the stall. -NoDeclare (GHOZTTY_GLIDE_DECLARE=0) must go red: the stepped column freezes while the declared grid glides on.

## In plain terms

A test that films the pane sidebar opening while the app is deliberately made busy, and checks that the sidebar's panel and the panes beside it slide together instead of the gap between them opening up. Building it showed that the test's "busy" moment was landing halfway through drawing a frame, something a real busy app never does; with it moved to between frames, the slide holds together. The film also caught a smaller glitch that happens on every open, busy or not: the gap flashes wide for a moment at the start (filed as T2872).
