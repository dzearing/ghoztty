# T2569 - Flat style: dragging a divider paints the terminal background over the bottom of the tab bar, and the smear stays after release - the gutter bar's glow reach is not clipped to the grid on the GDI path

## Summary

Found filming T2545 (scripts\comp-flat-onscreen.ps1). paintFlatGutterBar StretchDIBits gutterBarReach(core), which extends ~11 px (at 1x) past the gutter on every side; at the top of the grid that is the bottom 11 rows of the tab bar. The bar buffer is prefilled with the terminal background, so a held divider drag leaves a background-coloured patch across the tab bar wherever the bar travelled, and nothing repaints the band (still there 1.5 s after release and after a later split). Knob-off (default) path only: the compositor's desk visual is clipped below the caption. Fix: clip the bar blit to gridRect (or the desk area below chromeHeight); oracle: comp-flat-onscreen.ps1 can then match the drag frames whole instead of below the caption.
