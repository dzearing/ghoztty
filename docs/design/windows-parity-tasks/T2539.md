# T2539 - A resize step's compose is still ~1.2 ms after T2537: the glass terminal's interior sheet (four table reads a pixel over half the window), the 4 MB desk copy it then overwrites, and two page-allocator allocations per paint

## Summary

After T2537 (flat runs vectorized, bit-exact) compose_us is 1229 us median at ReleaseSafe on resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 (was 1668). What is left, by the 1500x800 micro-bench: the glass terminal's interior sheet LUT ~250 us (0.45 ns/px, scalar table reads), the desk memcpy ~150 us that the glass interior then reads back and rewrites, the shadows ~150 us, and paintCardsWith/paintShadows each taking fresh page_allocator memory (VirtualAlloc + first-touch faults) every paint. Ideas: fuse the desk copy into the glass interior (write sheet(desk) straight into comp, copy only outside the interiors), keep the scratch and the shadow arena on PaneDesk across paints, and measure whether a run cache (desk rows are upscaled, neighbours often equal) beats the LUT. Validate with the same trace (compose_us) and the T2537 bit-exact unit test still green. Feeds T2516's 8.3 ms per-step budget.
