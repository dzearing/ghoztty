# T2539 - A resize step's compose is still ~1.2 ms after T2537: the glass terminal's interior sheet (four table reads a pixel over half the window), the 4 MB desk copy it then overwrites, and two page-allocator allocations per paint

## Summary

After T2537 (flat runs vectorized, bit-exact) compose_us is 1229 us median at ReleaseSafe on resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 (was 1668). What is left, by the 1500x800 micro-bench: the glass terminal's interior sheet LUT ~250 us (0.45 ns/px, scalar table reads), the desk memcpy ~150 us that the glass interior then reads back and rewrites, the shadows ~150 us, and paintCardsWith/paintShadows each taking fresh page_allocator memory (VirtualAlloc + first-touch faults) every paint. Ideas: fuse the desk copy into the glass interior (write sheet(desk) straight into comp, copy only outside the interiors), keep the scratch and the shadow arena on PaneDesk across paints, and measure whether a run cache (desk rows are upscaled, neighbours often equal) beats the LUT. Validate with the same trace (compose_us) and the T2537 bit-exact unit test still green. Feeds T2516's 8.3 ms per-step budget.

## In plain terms

Dragging a window's edge repaints the soft desk and the pane cards at every
new size. That repaint was taking over a millisecond each time, which is time
a smooth drag doesn't have. Now it does about a quarter less work for the same
picture, pixel for pixel. Nobody sees a different image. The drag just has
more room to keep up.

## Goals

- [x] Measure where the remaining compose time goes, on a real desk
- [x] Take out the work the picture never needed (desk copied under solid fills, glow corners recomputed every paint, fresh memory every paint)
- [x] Keep every pixel identical (bit-exact tests, with controls)
- [x] Confirm the gain in the app's own resize trace

## What landed

All of it is in `pane_elevation.zig`, plus one line in `PaneDesk.compose`. A new
opt-in micro-bench (`T2539: bench`) composes the way the app does: a real desk
rendered small and upscaled, then a glass terminal and a viewer with a glass
nav bar, a focus glow and shadows, at 1500x800 @1.5x. Before the change it
measured copy 161 us, glows ~160, shadows ~150, cards ~350.

- **The glow's corners are a table now** (`GlowCorner`, built once per style
  in `Prepared`). Where both axis terms are positive, `qx`/`qy` are the
  pixel's half-integer offsets from the corner, exact in f32 for any card on
  the pixel grid whose corner is the style radius. So the alpha depends on the
  offset alone and the table holds the per-pixel bits. That was two `erfc`
  calls a pixel over four corner squares, on every paint.
- **The desk is not copied under solid interiors** (`copyDeskUnder`). A
  viewer's page, or every card in the solid style, is `memset` over and never
  read. The glows and shadows that land there first are covered too.
  `interiorSpan` is shared with the painter, so the two cannot disagree about
  where an interior is.
- **The glass interior skips repeated pixels** (`GlassLut.sheetSpan`). The
  stretched desk repeats a pixel often, and a repeat costs one compare.
- **No fresh OS memory per paint.** The axis-table scratch is kept per thread
  and grown, never shrunk. The shadow pass's side profiles sit on the stack.

Tried and rejected, from evidence:

- **Composing in L2-sized stripes.** Bit-exact, but 16-row stripes were slower
  and 128-row stripes no faster. The passes are compute bound, not memory
  bound.
- **Fusing the desk copy into the glass sheet** (the summary's first idea).
  Neighbouring cards' shadows reach into a glass interior: 37 px of reach at
  1.5x against a 15 px gap. The sheet shows the shadowed desk through it, so
  `sheet(desk)` written early would be a different picture. A restricted form
  (only where no neighbour reaches) is filed as follow-up.

Bench (best of 60): 902 -> ~750 us.

In the app, A/B with only these two files differing (HEAD in
`zig-out-t2539base`, this change in `zig-out-rsafe`, both ReleaseSafe),
resize-sync-onscreen.ps1 with GHOZTTY_RESIZE_TRACE=1, 131 composing steps each:

| | composing steps median | p90 | mean | full composes (>600 us, n=17) median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 428 | 1296 | 565 | 1427 |
| after | 390 | 987 | 502 | 1040 |

Since T2577 most steps are partial composes over the canvas. The full ones
(this task's subject) are 27% cheaper.
