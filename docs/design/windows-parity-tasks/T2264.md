# T2264 - Windows cards and strips snap instead of gliding when a drag or a click changes what is on show, and a strip is a dark bar rather than the pane's own glass card at strip size (Mac a81db68f9)

## Summary

Split from T2253. Mac a81db68f9: every change in what is on show glides - drags AND clicks - with each card and strip easing toward its target every frame, retargeted as the hand moves. A strip is the pane's own glass card at strip size; its content fades out and its title fades in as it narrows (and the reverse), with no snap to a dark bar. The drag's gutter bar follows the shown (mid-glide) gutter (PaneStageView.shownGutter). Compare with win32 pane_stack_slide.zig (T2102) and pane_stack_strip.zig.
