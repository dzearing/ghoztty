# T2843 - With GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1 a fold-glide tick costs 10.7 ms mean against 3.2 ms on the GDI desk (late 607-630 of ~680 ticks at 120 Hz, glide at half rate; worst tick place_us 40 ms): the compositor chrome cannot become the default until its glide is at least as fast

## In plain terms

The newer way of drawing the window's background and pane cards (handing
them to Windows' compositor as layers) is still opt-in. With it switched on,
dragging a divider so panes fold makes every frame of the glide late, so the
glide moves at half the screen's rate. It has to be at least as smooth as
today's drawing before it can be turned on for everyone.

## Summary

Found by T2732 (2026-11-08, lane b). Same probe as T2732 (release build,
`-ReleaseSandbox`, background test desktop, 5 panes in a row with a viewer
second, the first divider dragged 720 DIP out and back twice, 1875x1125 px
@1.25, 120 Hz), alternating arms, two runs each:

| arm | ticks | mean tick | max tick | late | desk composes / mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1` | 673 / 690 | 10.63 / 10.79 ms | 41.2 / 27.4 ms | 607 / 630 | 675 / 0.94 ms, 2293-2349 rect uploads |
| default (GDI desk) | 1283 / 1280 | 3.22 / 3.17 ms | 23.2 / 16.5 ms | 24 / 21 | 1068 / 1.06-1.08 ms |

The compositor arm's worst tick is `owner=tick`, `place_us=40303`,
`layout_us=8653`, `paint_us=928`: the cost is in the layout pass's place
phase, not the desk compose (which is about the same per compose). Half as
many ticks ran in the same drag, i.e. the glide ran at half rate.

Where to look first: a ring trace (`GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring`) of the
compositor arm, split the same way T2732 split the GDI one (batch / faces /
cut / vacated), plus the per-layer sync cost (`syncTreeShadows`, the blade
layers of T2796, the glow layers of T2793, the desk rect uploads).
Probe: `glide-probe.ps1` in session 06458412's scratchpad (`-Release`,
`-OutLog`; set `GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1` for the arm).

## Goals

- [ ] the compositor arm's place phase broken down to its phases
- [ ] its glide tick at or under the GDI arm's on the same probe

## Validation criteria

- [ ] probe A/B x3 (release, 120 Hz): `GHOZTTY_COMP_CHROME=1` mean tick and late count at or under the default arm's
