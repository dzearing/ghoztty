# T3016 - PA-I02: every inbound agent frame is parsed, re-encoded, queued and parsed again on an extra thread in the app, and the four buffers on that path keep their largest size (client_mux.zig:209-216)

## Summary

PA-I02 (audit-1, T2962). src/remote/client_mux.zig:209-216 parses each frame from the agent, re-encodes it with protocol.writeFrame into `wire`, writes it to a lane fifo, and connection.zig:4030-4062 (dataReaderLoop) reads the fifo in 16 KiB slices into a second protocol.Reader and parses again. Every win32 dial (local pipe, TCP, relay) goes through tcp_dial.dialConnected, so it is on every agent-backed pane's output path. Cost (estimated): 3 extra memcpys, 1 extra parse and 1 condvar hop per chunk, plus 1 extra thread per connection (16 MB under PA-W01). Buffers: client_mux.zig:291-293 and :209 clearRetainingCapacity, protocol.zig:2315-2317 compact; a relaunch replay is one DATA frame up to the 2 MiB ring (16 MiB max), so ~8 MB (up to ~64 MB) stays held for the app's life on the shared local connection. Fix: dispatch data-lane frames from the pump straight into routeInboundData (drops the data fifo and its reader thread), or at least forward the raw frame slice; shrink past a threshold as inbound_ring.zig:441-442 does. Risk medium: Connection is shared with the Mac build (embedded.zig); wire unchanged. Mac half filed. Findings: docs/design/perf-audit/audit-1/I-findings.md.
