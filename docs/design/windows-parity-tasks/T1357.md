# T1357 - viewer-narrow-pane section E flakes on its first band capture

## Summary

Section E of test\win32\viewer-narrow-pane.ps1 measures the nav strip at five pane widths and asserts that narrowing never ADDS ink. About one run in two the FIRST (widest) band reads fewer inked slots than the next one down - 4 then 5, where a settled bar reads 8 - so the monotone claim fails on a bar that is correct and simply had not finished painting. The band is captured 1200ms after the +rearrange with no wait for the strip to settle, and the ink count is the settling signal itself.

## In plain terms

A test that checks the viewer's toolbar looks right at five different pane
widths fails about half the time on a build where nothing is wrong. It
photographs the toolbar 1.2 seconds after resizing the pane and sometimes
catches it mid-repaint, so it reports that squeezing the pane ADDED buttons.
A test that cries wolf every other run is a test nobody reads.

## Where

- `test\win32\viewer-narrow-pane.ps1`, section E (`Measure-NavBar`, the
  `foreach ($ratio in @(55, 72, 80, 86, 92))` loop and the `$monoSlots` check).

## Details

Observed on 2026-09-05 across four runs of the unmodified script: the widest
band (ratio 55) read `inked slot boxes 4` on two runs and `8` on the other two,
with every narrower band stable. The fix is to make the capture wait for the
strip to settle — re-measure until two consecutive captures agree, or until a
deadline — rather than to loosen the assertion, which is the one that catches
the defect the section exists for.
