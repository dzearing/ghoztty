# T1850 - In the elevated style the Windows pane sidebar is an opaque slab beside the desk: Mac runs the gradient under a translucent sidebar and gives the mini rail the panes' 10pt gutters

## Summary

Follow-up of T1831 (main 91ba7c08c: PaneSidebarHost background at 38% opacity in elevated, no 1px rule; 557e4f978: equal gutters). On Windows the rail and the pinned panel paint their own opaque column and the desk starts at the grid, so the gap right of the rail is the grid margin plus the rail card's own margin. Mac elevated: the desk runs under the sidebar, and the rail takes the panes' 10pt margin leading/top/bottom and none trailing (the grid's margin is that gap). Also from 557e4f978: a rail tile for a pane titled with a path shows its last component (and '~' itself). Validate with a PrintWindow capture like test\win32\pane-elevation.ps1.

## In plain terms

In the default look, the window has a soft blue gradient behind raised panes,
but the pane list on the left was painted as a solid dark strip, so the
gradient stopped abruptly at its edge, and the mini rail sat further from the
panes than from the window edge. Now the gradient runs under the list the way
it does on Mac: the pinned list is see-through, with no line between it and
the panes, and the mini rail is spaced evenly on both sides.
