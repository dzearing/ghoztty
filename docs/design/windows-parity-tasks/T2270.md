# T2270 - While a Windows drag's cards glide, the gutter accent bar sits at the layout's gutter rather than the gutter as drawn mid-glide (Mac PaneStageView.shownGutter, a81db68f9)

## Summary

Mac stepGlide calls GutterBar.refreshActive every frame and shownGutter(rect) moves the dragged gutter's bar beside the card whose edge it is, as far as that card has got. Win32 gutter_bar.zig (T2253) places the bar from the layout's gutter only.

## In plain terms

When you drag the line between two panes far enough that a pane folds away,
the panes glide to their new places instead of jumping. The coloured bar that
marks the line you are holding stayed where the panes were going, not where
they were, so mid-glide it floated in empty space beside them, and once the
fold began it vanished altogether. Now it rides along with the pane edge it
belongs to and stays lit for the whole drag, the way the Mac's does.

## Goals

- [x] the dragged bar is drawn beside the gliding card whose edge it is, as far as that card has got (Mac `shownGutter`)
- [x] the bar stays lit when a divider drag folds its row into a stack and the divider is replaced by a stack grip (Mac `GutterBar.replacement`)
- [x] an acceptance claim that sees the bar off its laid-out gutter mid-glide, with a negative control

## Validation criteria

- [x] `Slide.shownGutter` moves a gutter by the bordering card's remaining offset, on both axes, from either side, and leaves an unbordered gutter alone - 4 unit tests (`pane_stack_slide.zig`), win32 lane
- [x] mid-fold the dragged bar is drawn at 2+ places short of its laid-out gutter, and mid-unfold at 2+ places past it - `stack-fold-slide.ps1` F7 (36 and 55 places)
- [x] F7 goes red when there is no glide - `stack-fold-slide.ps1 -NegativeControl` (F7 FAIL, 1 place)
- [x] the rest of the fold/unfold claims (F1-F6) still hold - `stack-fold-slide.ps1` ALL PASS (19)
