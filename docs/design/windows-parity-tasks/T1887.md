# T1887 - The unpinned pane sidebar overlay is an opaque card over glass panes: Mac tints it as a lighter glass sheet (30%) with no lifting wash

## Summary

Follow-up of T1833 (main ec71db251 SidePanelCard isGlass, 733b03e58 PaneGlass.overlayTint 0.3 and no lifting wash). In the glass style the hover-open sidebar card on Windows is an opaque slab over the panes. Paint it as glass: 30% of the terminal background over what lies beneath (the desk and the glass panes under it), plus Mac's soft shadow. Pairs with T1850 (pinned sidebar on the desk). Validate with a PrintWindow capture of the open overlay over a glass window.

## In plain terms

With the default glass look, the panes are see-through sheets over the
window's gradient, but the pane list on the left - the slim rail and the card
it opens into when you hover it - was a solid grey slab, the one opaque thing
in the window. Now both are a light glass sheet like on Mac: the gradient and
the panes beneath show through, and the list's text sits on top.

## Goals

- [x] The rail's card is glass over the gradient (30% terminal background, sheen and rim, no wash)
- [x] The card the rail opens is see-through over the panes beside it, its rows and text solid on it
- [x] `macos-pane-glass = false` keeps the opaque card, both rail and open card

## Decisions recorded

- **How the open card is see-through.** It is already a per-pixel-alpha
  layered window (T1865), so the sheet is real translucency: the window
  manager composites it over the live GL panes. The glass layer (tint at
  Mac's `PaneGlass.overlayTint` 0.3, sheen, grounding shade, rim, the card's
  drop shadow and the sheet's own soft shadow, both kept off the sheet) is
  built once per shape in `pane_sidebar_layer.buildGlass`. The rows are GDI,
  which writes no alpha, so they are painted twice - over black and over
  white - and `composeOver` takes each pixel's coverage from the difference.
  That keeps ClearType text edges partial instead of guessing them from a
  key colour, which would have left a dark fringe round every glyph.
- **The column strip stays opaque**, the sheet composited over the desk
  there, as before: under it is the sidebar's own column (the rail), which
  must not show through the open card.
- **The rail** sits in the opaque column over the desk alone, so its glass
  is composited in-process (`Material.overlay_glass`), the same layers.
- **Row fills stay opaque** (selection, hover), as on the pinned panel
  (T1850).
- **No blur.** Mac's pre-26 path (`PaneGlass` without `glassEffect`) does not
  blur either.
