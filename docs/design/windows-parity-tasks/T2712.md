# T2712 - The vacated-strip desk paint costs 2.7-3 ms ReleaseFast (4-5 ms Debug) on every edge-drag step, burst or shipped: the biggest single UI-thread cost left in a step after T2695/T2702

## In plain terms

Each step of dragging a window edge spends about 3 ms repainting the strip of
background the panes just moved off. That is a large share of a 60-frames-a-second
budget, and it is paid on every build, not only the experimental one.

## Summary

In the `-Burst` trace of `test\win32\edge-drag-rate.ps1`, `vacated-dc` ->
`vacated-painted` (the vacated-strip desk paint in the layout pass, T2577)
averages 3.00 ms on the shipped pre-layout and 2.70 ms under the burst in a
ReleaseFast sandbox, 5.3 / 5.1 ms Debug, in the 3-pane scene (2.1 / 4.0 ms with a
viewer). The renderer threads draw during that window, so part of it may be
the UI thread being starved rather than paint cost: measure the paint alone
(thread CPU time, not wall clock) before optimizing, then cut it (paint
only the strip's actual area, cache the desk's glass by size, the T2695 way).

## Goals

- [x] measure the vacated paint's own CPU time apart from its wall time
- [x] find what inside it costs the most, and cut it

## Findings (2026-11-05, Debug, `edge-drag-rate.ps1 -Burst`, trace on)

**It is real work, not starvation.** A new `resize trace cpu vacated` line
(`CpuLap`: `QueryThreadCycleTime` over the lap, calibrated by a 3 ms spin)
reads the UI thread's CPU at 96-98% of the wall clock: row3 5444 us wall /
5214 us CPU, viewer 3903 / 3706 (shipped pre-layout arm).

**The biggest part was not the desk.** New trace points inside
`paintPaneDesk` (`desk-enter/-pre/-paint/-glow/-under/-glass`) split the row3
lap: desk compose+blit 2352 us, **`syncGlassBackdrops` 2188 us**, desk under
panes 351, setup 295+123. Under a window-edge drag the desk is T2577's canvas,
larger than the window, so `Surface.setPaneBackdrop`'s `d.w == full_w` check
failed on every step and each terminal's glass slice was EVALUATED (the
gradient per texel) instead of cropped from the desk already rendered - ~0.7 ms
per terminal per step, and laid out for a window-sized desk that was not the
one on screen round the cards.

**Fix:** crop whenever the rendered desk covers the pane's rect
(`desk_palette.deskCoversRegion`, none-lane test), and put the desk's size in
the slice key so the window-sized desk the drag's end paints re-sends.

| (Debug, trace on) | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| row3 vacated lap wall / CPU | 5444 / 5214 us | 3847 / 3651 us |
| viewer vacated lap wall / CPU | 3903 / 3706 us | 3026 / 2868 us |
| row3 `syncGlassBackdrops` in the lap | 2188 us | 478 us |
| row3 shipped grow / shrink step | 11656 / 10716 us | 10193 / 8873 us |
| viewer shipped grow / shrink step | 11045 / 9393 us | 9801 / 8207 us |

What is left in the lap is the desk's partial compose (~2.3 ms Debug, the
T2454 memo's moved-edge rects): filed as T2718. ReleaseFast not
re-measured this turn.
