# T2732 - A fold-glide tick's pane placement costs 8-23 ms (pane moves 3-5 ms, region cut 0.5-1.9 ms, vacated-strip desk fill 2-6 ms): at 60 Hz the first ticks of a glide overrun a period and drop a frame, and at 120 Hz the whole glide runs at half rate

## In plain terms

Dragging a divider so panes fold away glides, but each frame of the glide
spends most of its time moving the pane windows and repainting the strips
they left behind. On a 60 Hz screen that makes the first frames of a glide
miss a refresh once per drag; on a 120 Hz screen (the user's display is one)
nearly every frame misses, so the glide moves at half the screen's rate.

## Summary

Split from T2455 (2026-11-05), which added the glide line's worst-gap
breakdown (`divider glide worst_gap_us=... owner=...` and
`divider glide worst_tick ...`, GHOZTTY_PERF) and measured it.

Measured (release, `-ReleaseSandbox`, T2450 glide probe - 5 panes in a row, a
viewer second, the first divider dragged 720 DIP out and back, 1875x1125 px
@1.25 on the background test desktop):

- **60 Hz** (period 16171 us): `late=1`, `worst_gap_us=33443`, `owner=tick`:
  the tick before the gap cost 21916 us by itself; not a full-desk compose,
  not a viewer resize, no WM_PAINT or motion tick between. Same shape as
  T2455's summary numbers.
- **120 Hz** (period 8259 us; the box went to a 120 Hz Parsec display
  mid-turn): `late=119-125` of ~1170 ticks, `fps=106-107` - most gaps are
  16.55-16.75 ms, i.e. a tick of 8-13 ms skips every other vblank. Worst tick
  19-26 ms, again `owner=tick`; `place_us` 14.6-22.1 ms of it.
- Expensive ticks cluster at the **start of each glide** (17-23 ms, resizes=3),
  then settle at 6-13 ms.

`GHOZTTY_STACK_TRACE=ring` stamps break the place phase (`layoutPanes`, from
`place_timer` to the end of `clearVacatedPaneStrips`) down, for the 98 ticks
over 9 ms in one run (QPC at 10 MHz):

- the pane batch (`endPaneBatchFilled`, `EndDeferWindowPos` of 5 panes, one a
  WebView2 viewer): **2.8-5.2 ms**;
- strip faces (`commitStripFacesEarly`): ~0 normally, 1-3.7 ms in some ticks;
- `cutCardRegions` (SetWindowRgn per card): **0.3-1.9 ms**;
- `clearVacatedPaneStrips` (the vacated-strip desk fill + blit): **2.1-5.8 ms**;
- then `paint_us` after place: ~1.1 ms, 4-8 ms in some ticks.

Directions: (a) a region cut only for cards whose size or corner changed
(a pure move keeps its region); (b) the vacated-strip fill composes the same
rects the T2454 partial compose already found, so check it is not composing
the strips a second time; (c) the pane batch: which pane's move costs (the
viewer's WebView2 bounds are already timed in `viewer_us`, ~0 on the worst
ticks), and whether a pane that only moves (no size change) can be moved by
the compositor visual instead of `SetWindowPos`.

Also seen once (run 1, first drag after the release build was written): one
glide tick of **1.01 s**. Not seen in four later runs; likely a cold-start
cost (first desk compose / first pane move after launch, or the new exe being
scanned). Re-check it on a cold launch before ruling it out.

The probe is `glide-probe.ps1` in the T2450 session scratchpad (a copy with a
`T2455_TRACE` switch is in T2455's); T2451 is the harness that should own this
shape permanently.

### What is left (2026-11-08 afternoon, lane b, re-measured at e961d2e97)

With lane A's T2793 in, HEAD reads **late 8-11 of ~1300** at 120 Hz (one
run 31), mean tick 2.8-3.1 ms (3 untraced runs + 2 instrumented). A
per-tick log (temporary, reverted) of every tick over 5 ms or after a gap
over 12 ms shows the late ticks are NOT spread out. They come in two
clusters per round:

1. **The fold's first ticks (the main lever).** At `divider drag
   run=1..4 of 5` the first pane folds. In the same tick
   `row drag moved focus to the pane on show`, the desk is composed whole
   once (legitimately: the row re-lays, so the dirty area is over 3/4 of
   the window: cards 6->6, strips 1->1, both lists align), and
   `stack fold carries its frame` puts up a face that is then **painted
   from frames every tick** (`face_paints` +1 per tick, so not the
   declared T2778 path). For ~10 consecutive ticks: paint ~2.2 ms (vs ~0.5
   otherwise), place 6-9 ms, tick 8-11 ms, each one late. That 17-20 ms
   whole-compose tick is the run's worst. Suspects to check next: the
   focus glow fade starting mid-glide (desk glow bands every tick), and why
   this face is not handed to the compositor whole.
2. **Viewer-resize ticks.** A pass that resizes the viewer pane costs 4.8
   ms in the pane batch vs 0.84 for the rest; ~2.5 ms of it is the
   viewer's synchronous bounds sync inside `EndDeferWindowPos`. Split to
   **T2838**.

### What is left (2026-11-08 late, lane b, after the compose cuts)

A microbenchmark of one glide frame's partial compose (1875x1060 @1.25,
first divider +12 px, a card gliding into its strip; 5 dirty rects, 13% of
the desk) put the per-rect cost in the blade shadow (338 us) and the card
painters (309 us), with the copies at 80 us. Two cuts, both pixel-exact:

- the blade shadow's table rows (most of a gliding card's edge) are blended
  as two runs, eight pixels at a time, with the per-pixel loop's float
  steps (`shadeRun`; `test_per_pixel_rows` holds them to it);
- `composeRects` lays each dirty rect less the ones before it (`Pieces`):
  the rects overlap on purpose, and the overlaps sit on the shadows'
  corners, the costliest pixels.

`composeRects` on that frame: 619 -> 493 us. The T2454 bench at 3800x2050:
gliding partial 1.28 -> 0.99 ms (glass 1.41 -> 1.10), whole 3.07 -> 2.6 ms.
Left in the compose: the corners' per-pixel alpha (`erfc` + two
round-rect distances, ~60 us per 425x40 band) and the card painters' ~1.1
ns a pixel in the shadow bands between cards.

In the app it does not show: release sandbox, 120 Hz, alternating A/B x3
against a clean HEAD build, mean tick 3.17/3.19/3.25 -> 3.24/3.23/3.18 ms,
late 15/23/21 -> 22/20/21 of ~1280, max tick 26/39/22 -> 23/24/18 ms. So
the probe's glide frames are not dominated by the benchmark's shape (a card
gliding into its strip with overlapping bands); before the next compose cut,
stamp which rects a real glide tick composes and what each costs, rather
than optimizing the benchmark further.

### What is left (2026-11-08 night, lane b): the worst frame is not ours

The frame that breaks a 60 Hz period (the run's `max_us`, 20-48 ms) is
always ONE `EndDeferWindowPos`, 4-7 times a run, and it is not the compose,
the faces or the fold capture. Release, 120 Hz, the probe: the pane window
procedures spend 0.15 ms of a 28.5 ms batch; switching the fold capture off
leaves the 26-28 ms worst tick (A/B x3); an independent process's 5-panel
batch moved during the same glide never passes 3.4 ms; a five-terminal row
with no viewer still stalls 34-44 ms. Split to **T2845** (suspect: each
terminal's DirectComposition target on its own child window; the structural
lever is `GHOZTTY_COMP_PANE_TREE`, gated on T2843), which this now waits on.
