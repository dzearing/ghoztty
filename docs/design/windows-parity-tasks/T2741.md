# T2741 - Composition step 4d-2: in pane-tree mode each card's shadow is a compositor visual just under its pane visual, placed and glided in the same commit (panePlace/paneGlide carry it), re-rendered only when the card's size or shadow share changes

## Summary

Second split of T2511: the shadow visual that moves with its card, including through a declared glide while the UI thread is stalled.

Inputs from T2740 (`src/apprt/win32/shadow_layer.zig`): `render` gives the
card's premultiplied layer, `Layer.origin` where it goes, `Key` when it must be
re-rendered (size, share, style - never position). Two design points it left
open for this step:

- **Keep a layer off its neighbours.** On the desk no shadow lands on another
  card (fills go down after every shadow); a layer under each pane visual would
  darken a neighbouring card's rim and edge wherever the reach crosses the gap.
  Clip each layer away from neighbouring cards, or carry the rims up with the
  pane visuals - decide here, and prove it with a WGC capture of two cards
  closer than the reach.
- **A fading share as opacity.** A card gliding into its strip changes its
  share every frame; `render(s)` vs `render(1)` at opacity `s` is within 4
  alpha levels (T2740's probe), so fade the visual's opacity (declarable, like
  the bell fade) instead of re-rendering.
