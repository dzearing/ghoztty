# T2261 - Toggling the banner of an unfocused pane on Windows may show the glide's frozen copy undimmed for ~200ms: BannerFreeze sits directly behind the card, which can put it above the unfocused-pane dim overlay (T2252 follow-up)

## In plain terms

When a pane is not the one you are typing in, Ghoztty dims it. Clicking that
pane's banner to fold or unfold it showed the pane at full brightness for a
fifth of a second while the banner moved, then dimmed it again. Now the pane
stays dimmed the whole time, as it does on the Mac.

## Goals

- [x] The frozen copy that stands in for the terminal during a banner glide
      sits under the pane's dim, whatever order the dim and the card are in.
- [x] An acceptance check that drives the real toggle on an unfocused pane
      and goes red without the fix.

## Summary

T2252's frozen copy (`BannerFreeze`) was seated with
`SetWindowPos(copy, card)`, directly beneath the banner card. The card, the
dim and the copy are popups with one owner, and their order is whatever last
moved them. Measured (the negative control below): the dim sits BELOW the card, so the copy landed above
the dim, and an unfocused pane showed undimmed for the length of the glide.
Mac stacks the unfocused-split dim over everything in the pane, the frozen
terminal included (`SurfaceView`'s ZStack: `FrozenTerminalView`, then the
banner, then the dim `Rectangle`).

Fix: the copy is seated beneath whichever of the card and a shown dim is
lower (`banner_glide.copySeat`, unit tested; the walk is
`BannerFreeze.seatAfter`/`isBelow`), on show and on every glide tick.
Negative control `BannerFreeze.T2261_NEUTERED`, pinned in both files.

Not in scope, noted for the record: on Windows the dim covers only the
terminal, not the banner band, while Mac's dim covers the banner too. The
copy's band rows during an expand therefore read like the rest of the
Windows band (undimmed), which is the Windows steady state.
