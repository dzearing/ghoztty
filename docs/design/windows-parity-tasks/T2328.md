# T2328 - No on-box acceptance proves a Windows strip holds still while another pane collapses onto the stack (T2312 landed with unit tests only; the stacked-pane harnesses cannot form a stack until T2295)

## Summary

T2312 (Mac 760272563) holds a strip that stays a strip at its old length, by its outer edge, while another pane collapses between it and the run, and gives it its 12 DIP reach (`blade_tuck`) only once the glide is over (`pane_stack_slide.Slide.shownStrip`, `Window.shownStripTab`). It is proved by a unit test in `pane_stack_slide.zig` only: nothing on the box watches the strip during a real collapse.

Mac's proof is `aStripStaysPutWhenAnotherCollapsesOntoIt` (both sides): tuck one pane, drag the next divider to collapse a second onto it, sample the first strip every 4 ms during the glide, and assert its outer edge never moves and no more of it shows than before.

Port it as a section of `test\win32\pane-stack-strip.ps1` (or a sibling) with `GHOZTTY_STACK_SLIDE_MS` stretching the glide, reading the strip band's pixels from the desk at a few points mid-glide on each side: the band must not appear in the gutter between the old strip and the arriving card. Needs T2295 first - the stacked-pane harnesses no longer form a stack at the 225 DIP minimum.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] Leading and trailing: the held strip's outer edge stays put and none of its band shows in the gutter ahead of the arriving card, sampled mid-glide on the test desktop
- [ ] Once the glide settles the strip reaches 12 DIP under the newcomer (the T2283 layout)
- [ ] `-NegativeControl` (no hold) fails the mid-glide check
