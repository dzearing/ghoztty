# T2493 - Composition step 3d: the banner becomes a child visual of the pane visual (T2467's host) instead of a WS_EX_LAYERED popup; its links and buttons keep working

## Summary

T2360 step 3, fourth overlay (after T2467 bell border, T2491 dim; T2492 is
the scrollbar). The banner (`BannerOverlay`, T35/T91) is a `WS_EX_LAYERED`
popup moved by hand on every layout, fold and drag. The goal is that it lives
in the pane's own visual tree, so it cannot lag its pane, while its links,
chevron and buttons keep working.

**Split 2026-10-31 (T2558, T2559)** - it does not fit one step, because the
banner is not inside the pane window at all. The pane's DComp tree is the
renderer's target on the surface HWND and is clipped to it; the banner band
(T101) sits ABOVE that HWND - the layout moves the owner down by the inset and
`BannerOverlay.updatePosition` puts the popup at `rect.top - inset`. T2491 hit
the same wall and kept the dim a popup on banner panes for exactly this
reason. So:

- **T2558 (geometry)**: on the composition path the surface spans its whole
  slot and carves the band off its top (the way `applyResize` already carves
  the scrollbar off its right): grid, renderer size, content-visual offset,
  mouse y, IME/caret, in-pane overlays. Knob off keeps today's geometry.
- **T2559 (pixels + input)**: the banner's existing frame uploads into a
  `comp_overlay.Layer` at the top of the pane tree, and the pane window's
  mouse handlers route the band to the banner's hit-test and click code.
  The dim exception from T2491 goes with it.

## In plain terms

The status card at the top of a terminal pane is a separate floating window
that gets dragged along whenever the pane moves, which is how it can trail
behind for a frame. Moving it into the pane's own picture first needs the pane
to own the strip of screen the card sits in - today that strip is outside it.
That geometry change and the card move are now two tasks.

## Design (technical, mine per go.md 5b)

Considered and rejected: the banner as a WS_CHILD sibling of the pane (child
windows do move with the top-level window for free, and in the same
DeferWindowPos batch as the pane), and the banner as a visual on the window's
chrome host (T2508). Both leave the card and the terminal in two different
compositor trees, so a fold or a glide still commits them separately, and
T2555's banner glide/collapse - the card and the terminal moving as one, Mac's
layer model - needs them in ONE tree. Carving the band out of the pane window
is the only shape that gives that.
