# T2774 - Every window-edge drag step repaints the desk over what the panes vacated (clearVacatedPaneStrips -> paintAlphaCorrect(paintMovedBands)) for 0.6-1.0 ms in ReleaseFast on every path: paint only the strips that moved, or reuse the band paint

## In plain terms

On every step of a window-edge drag, the app repaints the background around the panes
that just moved. That repaint is now one of the slowest parts of each step. When this
lands it repaints only what actually changed, so each step gets cheaper.

## Summary

T2624's stage table (ReleaseFast, `edge-drag-rate.ps1 -ContentArms -Trace
-ReleaseSandbox`) puts `clearVacatedPaneStrips`' desk paint (`vacated-dc` ->
`vacated-painted`, `paintAlphaCorrect(dc, paintMovedBands)`) at 0.6-1.0 ms
median per step in every scene and arm. That includes the `empty` arm and the
T2610 presenter path, where it is the largest single stage: row3 held 0.73 ms
of a 3.4-4.2 ms step (run 20261106-191029). The paint clips to the union box of
the panes' old rects, which on an edge drag is nearly the whole desk. Then the
band paint (`gutters-end` -> `paint-end`, 0.3-0.4 ms) paints the desk again.
Candidates: clip to the strips that actually changed (the old rects minus the
new ones), or make one of the two paints the only one on a live step. Keep the
T2516 rule that the in-between frame already shows desk pixels and never flat
background.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The vacated paint's median per step measurably lower in `edge-drag-rate.ps1 -ContentArms -Trace -ReleaseSandbox` (row3 and viewer).
- [ ] No flat-background frame round the cards on a window-edge drag (the T2516 film, run only when the user is away).
