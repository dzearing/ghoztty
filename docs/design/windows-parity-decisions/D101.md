# D101 - When you're signed in on a PC someone else shares under their account, should ticking Share this machine take sharing over for your account, or explain why it can't?

## Why this needs a call

On a Mac there is no sharing checkbox: a machine shares itself with whichever account added it, and a different person signed in just stops it serving. Windows has a checkbox, so a guest can click it, and we have to pick what that click does. Before this change it did nothing visible.
