# D100 - When you untick 'Share this machine', should this PC disappear from your other devices' machine list, or stay listed as offline?

## Why this needs a call

Mac no longer has an 'enrolled but not serving' state: since main 6dfb31232 a registered Mac is reachable, and the way to stop is signing out, which removes it. Windows has a 'Share this machine' checkbox whose untick keeps the PC registered but dark, so your other computers list it as offline and cannot open it. Which way the untick goes is something you would see on every other device.

## Resolution

Chosen: Untick removes this PC from your account (Recommended)
