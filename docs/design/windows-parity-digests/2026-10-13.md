## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **The light finishes its lap** — *High.* *New angle on finish notices:* when a
  busy pane stops, its border light runs one bright lap and then leaves a faint
  steady edge until you look at that pane. You can tell "just finished" from
  "idle all along" at a glance, with no toast.
- **Busy tabs glow too** — *Medium.* A tab with a busy pane gets a thin moving
  light under its title. Work in a background tab is visible without switching
  to it.
- **Hover a link, see where it goes** — *Medium.* Hovering a web address or a
  file path in the output shows a small card: the page's title, the file's first
  lines, or "file not found". You know what a click will do before you click.
- **Copy a pane as a picture** — *Medium.* *New angle on copy-as-image:* right-
  click a pane and copy the whole card, rounded corners and gradient included,
  ready to paste into a chat or a bug report.
- **How long it has been working** — *Medium.* A busy pane's sidebar row shows
  how long it has been running, like "4m". A Claude session that has been working
  far longer than usual stands out without opening it.

### Stability and performance

- **A busy light that costs nothing** — *Medium.* The circling light is drawn by
  Windows' compositor, not by redrawing the terminal. Ten busy panes do not slow
  your typing or drain a laptop battery.
