## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Flip through a folder of pictures** - *High.* In an image pane, the
  arrow keys step to the previous or next picture in the same folder. A
  folder of screenshots becomes something you can scan without leaving the
  terminal.
- **See what changed in a picture** - *High.* When a branch diff includes a
  changed image, the diff pane shows the old and new side by side with a
  slider. Today a changed PNG is just "binary file differs".
- **Phone photos stand the right way up** - *Medium.* An image pane honours
  the rotation a phone camera records, and one key turns a picture a
  quarter turn.
- **The palette flags commands that interrupt you** - *Medium.* Restart,
  close-all and sign-out carry a small warning mark and sort below safe
  matches. A stray Enter can't restart your sessions.
- **Uninstall asks what to keep** - *Medium.* Removing Ghoztty asks whether
  to keep your settings and saved sessions. Otherwise it leaves nothing
  behind.

### Stability and performance

- **A huge picture opens at once** - *Medium.* A 50-megapixel TIFF or HEIC
  shows a screen-sized version straight away. Full detail fills in as you
  zoom, so a big file never makes the pane sit blank.
