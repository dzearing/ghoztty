## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **See the size while you drag** - *High.* While a divider or a window
  edge moves, each pane shows its size in columns and rows in a small
  badge. It catches gently at 80 and 120 columns. Laying out panes for a
  tool that needs a set width stops being guesswork.
- **Hold my place while output keeps coming** - *High.* Scroll up in a
  busy pane and the view stays still while new lines arrive below. A
  small "312 new lines" chip at the bottom jumps you back. Today the
  only choices are reading a moving page or losing your spot.
- **Dividers line up on their own** - *Medium.* (New angle on snapping
  dividers: mouse drags, not keyboard nudges.) Drag a divider close to
  where a neighbouring split's divider sits, and it snaps into line. A
  window of four or six panes then forms a clean grid without careful
  pixel work.

### Stability and performance

- **What Ghoztty itself is using** - *Medium.* (New angle on the
  per-pane CPU and memory readout: the app's own cost, not the
  programs inside.) The Activity Monitor shows how much memory each
  window and pane costs Ghoztty. A window holding far more than its
  panes explain is marked, so a leak is seen on day one, not found by
  an audit.
- **Closing things makes the app lighter, and you can see it** -
  *Medium.* After you close a window or split, its memory comes back
  within a few seconds and the total in About drops. A long day of
  opening and closing panes leaves Ghoztty about as light as when it
  started.
