## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Drag a strip to unfold it** - *High.* In a stacked row, drag a folded
  strip toward the middle and the pane opens at the width you drop it. You
  no longer click it in and then resize it.
- **A restart keeps your reading place** - *High.* (New angle on resizing
  keeping your line.) After an update or a reboot, each pane comes back
  scrolled to where you were reading, not snapped to the bottom.
- **Step through folded panes from the keyboard** - *Medium.* One key slides
  the next folded pane in a row on show, and again for the one after. It
  works like Alt+Tab for a stacked row.
- **Rename a pane from its strip** - *Medium.* Double-click a folded strip's
  title to rename the pane in place, without unfolding it first.

### Stability and performance

- **Folded panes cost nothing while idle** - *Medium.* A pane folded into a
  strip stops drawing and stops rewrapping its text, so ten folded panes cost
  what one does.
- **A folded pane slides back in instantly** - *Medium.* The pane keeps its
  last picture while folded, so bringing it back never waits on a redraw,
  even with a huge scrollback.
