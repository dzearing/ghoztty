## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Bring a whole group to the front** - *High.* Click a sidebar group's
  header and every window in it comes forward together, in the places you
  left them. Windows has no per-project desktops like Mac's Spaces, and
  this gives you one click to switch projects.
- **A count of waiting agents on the taskbar** - *High.* *(New angle on
  pane notifications.)* The Ghoztty taskbar button shows how many panes are
  waiting on a question. Clicking it goes to the one that has waited
  longest, so you can tell from another app whether any agent is stuck.
- **A sidebar group's colour carries onto its windows** - *Medium.* *(New
  angle on per-project identity.)* The colour you give a group tints those
  windows' tab strip. The colour you chose means the same thing in the
  sidebar, on screen and in Alt+Tab.
- **A resize shows the size it will land on** - *Medium.* While you drag a
  divider or a window edge, a small label shows each pane's columns by rows,
  with a soft snap at 80 and 120 columns. Laying out a pane for a tool that
  wants a set width stops being trial and error.

### Stability and performance

- **One stuck window cannot take the others with it** - *High.* If one
  window stops answering, the sidebar and every other window keep working.
  The stuck one is marked "not responding" with a "Restart this window"
  action that brings its sessions back.
- **Opens before you finish the keystroke** - *Medium.* A start-up budget:
  the first window shows a prompt within a third of a second, and About
  shows how long the last launch took, so a slow start gets noticed.
