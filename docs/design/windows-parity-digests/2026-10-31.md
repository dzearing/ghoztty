## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Resize to a size you name** - *High.* Right-click a window's edge
  and type "120x40", or pick from your last few sizes. The window snaps to
  exactly that many columns and rows. No more dragging back and forth to
  fit a tool that wants a set width.
- **Keep this pane's width when the window resizes** - *High.* Lock a
  pane, say a narrow log or a sidebar-like viewer, at its current width.
  Resize the window and only the other panes grow or shrink. Today every
  pane scales together, so a carefully sized pane drifts every time.
- **How long since this pane printed anything** - *Medium.* Each pane's
  title strip, and each folded blade, shows "quiet 12m" once nothing has
  printed for a while. A stuck build or a finished job is obvious at a
  glance, without reading the output.
- **Drag a pane's name to swap two panes** - *Medium.* Grab a pane by its
  title strip and drop it on another pane; the two trade places, keeping
  their sizes. Rearranging stops needing a close and reopen.

### Stability and performance

- **Smooth on a busy machine** - *Medium.* When the computer is under
  heavy load (a build, a game in the background), Ghoztty lowers its own
  animation work first. Folds and slides finish quickly instead of
  stuttering, and your typing never waits on them.
- **The window resizes at the speed of your screen** - *High.* (New angle
  on smooth resizes: a visible promise, not just a fix.) A window-edge
  drag keeps up with every refresh of a 120 Hz screen. If a step ever
  takes too long, the About box says how many frames were late in your
  last resize.
