## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **The sidebar sorts by who needs you** — *High.* One toggle orders the
  sidebar by panes waiting on a question, then busy ones, then idle ones, across
  every window. When you come back to the desk, the top row is the one to answer.
- **Double-click a divider to even it out** — *Medium.* Double-clicking a
  divider makes the panes on each side equal, and a second double-click puts
  your sizes back. A lopsided layout is one gesture from tidy.
- **Nudge a divider from the keyboard, with snap points** — *Medium.* With a
  pane focused, a key moves its divider in small steps. It pauses briefly at
  halves and thirds, with a small tick, so you can resize without the mouse.
- **A gradient you can keep** — *Medium.* *New angle on the window backdrop:*
  right-click the backdrop to roll a new random gradient, or pin the one you
  have. That window then keeps a look you chose, across restarts.
- **Drag a picture out of an image pane** — *Medium.* *New angle on dragging
  out of a pane:* drag the picture from an image pane straight into a chat,
  an email or Explorer, the way you would from a browser.

### Stability and performance

- **A divider that never trails the mouse** — *Medium.* During a drag, panes
  show their last picture clipped to the new size, and the text rewraps once
  when you let go. The divider stays under your pointer even with ten panes
  open.
