## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **A banner's history on hover** - *High.* Hover a pane's banner and see
  its last ten statuses with times. You can see what an agent went through
  while you were away ("building", "tests red", "fixed", "done") instead
  of only where it ended.
- **Fold a banner down to a dot** - *Medium.* One click shrinks a banner to
  a small coloured dot on the pane's strip, which gives back the rows. The
  dot changes colour when the status does, and hovering it opens the
  banner again.
- **Dividers that work with a finger or a pen** - *Medium.* On a
  touchscreen or with a pen, dividers and card edges get a wider grab
  area. A long press on a pane opens its menu. Resizing a split on a
  tablet stops being a game of hitting a 4-pixel line.
- **Choose a light resize** - *Medium.* A setting for slow or remote
  machines: while you drag, only an outline moves, and the panes resize
  once when you let go. You trade the live preview for a drag that never
  stutters.

### Stability and performance

- **Keeps going through a graphics driver update or a dock change** -
  *High.* When Windows updates the graphics driver, or you dock or undock
  a laptop, every window carries on without a blank flash or a restart.
  Today that is the moment most GPU apps flicker or die.
- **Sharp the moment a window crosses monitors** - *Medium.* Drag a window
  from a laptop screen to a 4K monitor and its text re-renders at the new
  scale before the window lands. It never shows a blurry or wrongly sized
  frame on the way.
