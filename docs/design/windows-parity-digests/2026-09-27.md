## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Paste a screenshot, get a file** — *High.* Pasting an image into a pane
  saves it to a file and types its path. That is how you hand a picture to
  Claude Code, and today it takes a trip through Explorer.
- **Resizing keeps you on the line you were reading** — *High.* Scroll up,
  then split the pane, drag a divider or move to another monitor. The view
  stays on the same line instead of jumping. Reading back through a long log
  survives any layout change.
- **Text that stays readable over a see-through window** — *Medium.* With a
  translucent background, the text gets a faint edge when the wallpaper behind
  it is busy. Translucency stops trading readability for looks.
- **Peek through the window** — *Medium.* Hold a chord and the window fades
  almost clear; let go and it comes back. You can read what is underneath
  without moving anything.
- **"Did you mean +split?"** — *Medium.* A mistyped `ghoztty` verb or flag
  answers with the closest real one. Scripts and agents fix themselves on the
  first try instead of reading the help.

### Stability and performance

- **Restore big scrollbacks without the wait** — *Medium.* A restored pane shows
  its last screen at once and fills in older history behind it. Windows with
  long logs come back as fast as empty ones.
