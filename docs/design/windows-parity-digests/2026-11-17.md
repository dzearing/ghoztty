## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **See every workspace at once** - *High.* One key shows every
  workspace as a live tile across the window. Click one to switch. In
  one-window mode most of your work is hidden, and this gives you the
  overview back.
- **Flip back to the workspace you just left** - *High.* One chord goes
  back to the previous workspace, and pressing it again comes back. This
  is how you move between two jobs dozens of times an hour.
- **Turn on single-window mode without restarting** - *High.* Changing
  the setting folds your open windows into workspaces right away, and
  turning it off spreads them back out. Today you have to restart, which
  makes trying it out feel risky.
- **Send a pane to another workspace from the keyboard** - *Medium.*
  "Move pane to workspace..." picks a workspace by name, and the pane
  goes there without a drag. This is a new angle on the drag ideas, made
  for one-window mode, where the other workspace is never on screen to
  drop onto.

### Under the hood

- **Switching stays instant with many workspaces, and each release
  proves it** - *Medium.* A release measures a switch with twenty
  workspaces open and will not ship if it gets slower. Instant switching
  is the whole promise of the mode.
- **Undocking never scrambles a hidden workspace** - *Medium.* When the
  screen size or scale changes, hidden workspaces are laid out again for
  the new screen before you switch to them. The first look is never a
  re-flow.
