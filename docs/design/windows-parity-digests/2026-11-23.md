## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **A diff takes you back to where you were in each file** - *High.* When
  you move to another file in a diff pane and come back, you land on the
  change you were last reading, not the top. Long reviews stop losing
  your place.
- **The focused pane turns solid on a see-through window** - *Medium.* New
  angle on readable glass: the pane you are typing in becomes fully
  opaque while the others stay see-through, so the text you work on is
  always easy to read.
- **Press and hold to see a picture's last save** - *Medium.* In an image
  pane that reloads when its file is saved, holding a key shows the
  previous version in the same place, so you can see what your last edit
  changed.
- **A `ghoztty://` link says which pane it opens** - *Medium.* New angle on
  hover cards: hovering a `ghoztty://` link names the pane and window it
  will focus, or says the pane is gone and offers to open a fresh one.
- **Updated hook scripts say what changed** - *Medium.* When Ghoztty
  updates your agent hook scripts on start, a one-line note names which
  ones changed and offers to show the difference.
- **Carry Ghoztty on a USB stick** - *Medium.* A portable copy keeps its
  settings and saved sessions in its own folder, so plugging it into
  another PC brings your setup with it and leaves that PC untouched.

### Under the hood

- **Signing in never opens a window twice** - *Medium.* When signing back
  in brings back your remote windows, any that are already open are
  focused rather than opened again, however many times you sign in.
