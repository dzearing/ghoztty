### Reflection

**Restart has been the theme of the week, and it keeps finding new causes.**
A wrong window size, a missing PATH, a container that kills everything:
three separate bugs, each found because you restarted and something was off.
None of the tests caught them, because the tests start the app fresh. That
suggests one standing test: restart a busy, multi-window setup the way an
update does, and compare the before and after window by window. T1970 and
T1978 are close to that, and should end with it as a permanent check.

**Making the loop faster moved the bottleneck, as expected.** Now that lanes
and sweeps are quick, the on-screen checks are the slow part. The second loop
gets more done at once rather than making each turn quicker, which is the
right next lever. It will need watching: two loops on one box share the CPU,
and the timing tests already take turns because of it (T1982).

**Duplicate cards came from two places at once.** The same `main` change was
filed twice by two different checks (T1977 and T1988), and then a third time
by this morning's intake. All three are now one card, T2001. The `SIMILAR:`
warning caught it, but only on the third filing. Running `similar` before any
test-failure card is filed would catch it the first time.

**Toward the cutover:** the branch is now 16 commits behind `main` (T1902).

## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Where new output began** — *High.* When you come back to a pane that kept
  working, a thin line in the scrollback marks where the output you have not
  seen starts. One key jumps to it, so you read from there, not from the end.
- **Not really waiting? One click clears it** — *High.* If a pane shows the
  question ring and nothing is waiting, its sidebar row offers "not waiting".
  The pane goes back to idle, and the false alarm is logged so it gets fixed.
- **A restart you can trust before you click it** — *High.* Before "Restart to
  update", Ghoztty checks every session and says "all 12 sessions will come
  back", or names the ones that will not. You decide with the answer in front
  of you.
- **Which panes came back** — *Medium.* *New angle on marking panes that will
  not come back:* after a restart, each pane briefly shows "reconnected" or
  "new shell". You know at a glance which work survived.
- **Busy but silent** — *Medium.* A pane that says it is busy but has printed
  nothing for ten minutes gets a small "stuck?" mark in the sidebar. A hung
  build or a stalled agent shows itself before you go looking.

### Stability and performance

- **Restart in place** — *Medium.* An update restarts only the window process.
  Every pane keeps its live session and scroll position, and the windows
  redraw where they were in under a second, with nothing rebuilt from saved
  layout.
