## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Answer a question from the sidebar** — *High.* When a pane is waiting on
  a yes/no or a numbered choice, its hover card shows the question and the
  choices. Press 1, 2 or 3 to answer without leaving the pane you are in.
- **Light mode you can see and flip** — *High.* When Ghoztty turns effects off
  for Remote Desktop or a weak GPU, a small chip in the title bar says so.
  Click it to bring the glass back for this window, or to keep it off
  everywhere.
- **Pause a runaway pane** — *Medium.* One click freezes a pane's program in
  place while it eats your CPU, and the pane shows "paused". Resume when you
  are ready, and the program picks up where it was.
- **Twenty idle panes, one line** — *Medium.* With many panes open, the
  sidebar folds the idle ones into a single "14 idle" row. The busy and
  waiting panes stay in view, and the idle row opens when you hover it.
- **Your machines in the new-tab menu** — *Medium.* *New angle on choosing a
  shell from the new-tab button:* the same menu lists your signed-in machines
  that are online now. One click opens a shell on that machine.

### Stability and performance

- **Send only what changed over Remote Desktop** — *High.* *New angle on a
  usable window over RDP:* when you type, Ghoztty redraws only the rows that
  changed, so the remote link carries a line, not the whole window. Typing
  over a slow connection should feel close to local.
