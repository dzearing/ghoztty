## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **See the fold coming** - *High.* While you drag a divider, the pane that
  is about to fold shows its strip outline before you let go. Stop short and
  nothing folds, so a fold is never a surprise.
- **Animations follow your Windows setting** - *High.* With "Animation
  effects" turned off in Windows, folds, slides and the busy light snap into
  place instead of moving. People who get motion-sick can use stacked panes.
- **Unfold the whole row back as it was** - *Medium.* One key brings every
  folded pane in a row back to the widths it had before it folded. Undoing a
  run of folds is one press, not one per strip.
- **A cancelled update picks up where it stopped** - *Medium.* After Cancel
  or a dropped connection, the next Install resumes the download instead of
  starting over. On a slow link the second try takes seconds.
- **Turn a portable copy into a real install** - *Medium.* A portable
  copy's menu offers "Install Ghoztty". It runs the installer and brings
  your settings and saved sessions with it, so trying it out never means
  setting it up twice.

### Stability and performance

- **Slides keep up on a big screen** - *Medium.* A fold or unfold paints at
  the monitor's own refresh rate, even with several panes on a 4K display,
  so the slide never steps or stutters.
