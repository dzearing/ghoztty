## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Show me which of my shortcuts Windows takes first** *(new angle on the
  keybinding cheat sheet)* — *High.* A default or configured shortcut that
  Windows reserves gets a small mark in the cheat sheet and the command
  palette, with a one-click alternative. Today such a shortcut just does
  something else, and nothing says why.
- **Repair from inside the app** — *High.* A Help > Repair Ghoztty item that
  runs the installer's repair and brings every window back after it. Repair
  now exists (T1300), but only in Settings > Apps, where nobody looks while
  their terminal is misbehaving.
- **Know which machine a window is on in Alt+Tab** — *Medium.* A remote window's
  title in Alt+Tab and on the taskbar starts with the machine's name. With three
  windows open onto three machines, the switcher currently shows three
  identical titles.

### Stability and performance

- **Repair tells you what it fixed** — *Medium.* When a repair finishes, the
  reopened window says in one line what was put back (a missing session
  manager, a damaged shortcut) or that nothing needed fixing. A repair that
  reports nothing leaves you guessing whether it was the fix.

- **A frame-rate readout in the Activity Monitor** — *Medium.* Each pane shows
  how smoothly it is drawing, so "this pane feels sluggish" becomes a number
  you can point at. It also makes a stutter like the hero-thumbnail one visible
  the day it appears.
