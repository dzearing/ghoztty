## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **A parked pane that comes back when it needs you** — *High.* Park a Claude
  session or a long build out of the grid. It slides back into its old spot by
  itself when it asks a question or finishes, with a soft glow so you notice.
  Parking stops meaning "out of sight, out of mind".
- **The last thing each pane said, in the sidebar** — *High.* Under each row, the
  pane's last line of output in small grey text, or the question Claude is waiting
  on. With five agents running, you see who needs you without opening any of them.
- **Your project's color is its backdrop** — *Medium.* *New angle on per-project
  window identity:* now that windows have their own gradient, the hue comes from
  the repository. Every window on one project shares a color, and a new one opens
  already in it.
- **Park a whole task at once** — *Medium.* Pick three panes that belong together
  (a server, its logs, the Claude working on it) and park them as one named group.
  One click brings the set back exactly as it was laid out.

### Stability and performance

- **Parked panes cost nothing** — *Medium.* A parked pane stops drawing and checks
  its output less often, so ten of them cost the same as none. It comes back up to
  date the instant you restore it.
- **Readable glass, automatically** — *Medium.* *New angle on text that stays
  readable over a see-through window:* glass panes turn more solid while
  their text is dense, such as a build log scrolling past, and clear again when
  things are quiet. You keep the look without squinting at busy output.
