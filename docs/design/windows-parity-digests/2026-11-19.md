### Reflection

**An update path that never finishes is invisible by design.** The
service said "replacing itself" on every launch for two days and four
releases, and nothing counted how long that had been true. A status that
can stay "in progress" forever needs a clock beside it, the same way the
loop's own `turn_age` did. Worth a standing check: any "in progress"
state the app reports should have a bound and a log line when it is
exceeded.

**Fixes can be correct and still not reach you.** Three of last night's
wins live in the session service. They are tested, merged and released,
and on your PC they are not running. The release checks prove the
installed files are right. Nothing checks which service build is actually
running a day later. That gap belongs in the morning audit.

**Parallel-sensitive harnesses are now the biggest cluster in the

fifteen markers.

the same run, as pane-ingest-ab now does, is the only comparison worth
alarming on.

## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **A pane that cannot start shows the way out** - *High.* Instead of a
  raw error code, the pane says plainly that it could not reach your
  sessions and offers Retry and "Open a fresh shell here". New angle on
  "restore names what it could not bring back": the help sits in the
  pane that failed.
- **Copy a pane as a command** - *Medium.* Right-click a pane and get the
  one `ghoztty` line that recreates it: its folder, shell, name and place.
  Paste it into a script and your setup is reproducible.
- **Keys you type while a pane starts are kept** - *Medium.* Start typing
  the moment a new pane or restored session appears. What you typed is
  held and delivered once the shell is ready, so the first command is
  never half eaten.

### Under the hood

- **The session service never falls behind the app** - *High.* After an
  update, the app checks which service build is actually running. If a
  hand-over has not finished in a minute, it finishes it or tells you why.
  New angle on "an update never drops a live session": the update also
  has to land.
- **Relaunch is as quick as a new window, and each release proves it** -
  *Medium.* Quitting and reopening with kept sessions is timed on every
  release, and a slower relaunch holds the release back. New angle on
  "rehearse the restart": speed, not just correctness.
- **Every "in progress" has a clock** - *Medium.* Any update, hand-over or
  restore the app reports as underway gets a time limit and a plain note
  when it runs over. Nothing can say "working on it" for two days again.
