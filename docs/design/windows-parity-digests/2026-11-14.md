**The smoothness work has hit a wall, and the wall is the rules, not the
code.** Two weeks of P0 fold and divider work now ends at the same place
on every card. The next fact needs a capture on your real screen, or an
admin trace, and the loop may not take either. Four decisions (D104, D107,
D108 and the new trace ask) all come down to one question: when may the
loop use your screen? One answer would unblock most of the P0 band. So
would a single 20-minute sitting where you start the parked runs.

**When the P0s stall, the loop works on itself.** Yesterday's last three
closes and today's first pick are all loop plumbing. Each one is real. But
with P0s parked, the queue's P2 band leans toward tooling, because tooling
cards are the ones the loop can always validate alone. If the parked P0s
stay parked, M1's user-facing P2s should be promoted ahead of the loop's
own cards.

**A trace you can start in one click would pay for itself.** The admin
trace T2952/T2953 needs is the same shape the earlier freeze hunts needed.
A script that starts it at the right moment and stops it after one drag
would turn "parked for an admin trace" into a two-minute job for you.

## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Windows Magnifier follows your typing** - *High.* With Magnifier on,
  the zoomed view tracks the terminal's cursor as you type, the way it
  does in Notepad. Today a low-vision user has to pan to find where the
  text is going.
- **A secret you copy stays off your other PCs** - *High.* Copying
  something that looks like a key, token or password from a pane keeps it
  out of Windows clipboard history and cloud clipboard sync. It pastes
  normally here and does not show up in Win+V tomorrow.
- **The touch keyboard comes up on a tablet** - *Medium.* Tap a pane on a
  touch screen and the Windows touch keyboard opens, docked so it does not
  cover the prompt. Today a tablet user can see the terminal but not type
  into it without a keyboard.
- **The terminal warms with Night light** - *Medium.* When Windows Night
  light turns on in the evening, the terminal's white text and bright
  viewer pages ease warmer with the rest of the screen, instead of staying
  the one blue-white rectangle.
- **A private pane that opens with Windows Hello** - *Medium.* Mark a pane
  private. When the PC locks or you step away, it blurs, and your face or
  fingerprint brings it back. Someone at your unlocked desk cannot read it.

### Under the hood

- **A font you just installed shows up at once** - *Medium.* Install a new
  font while Ghoztty is open and it is in the font list straight away,
  with no restart that would cost you your panes.
