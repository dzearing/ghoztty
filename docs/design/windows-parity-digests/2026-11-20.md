## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **A moved shortcut tells you where it went** - *High.* When an update
  moves a shortcut, pressing the old one shows a small note for a few
  weeks: "Previous pane is now Win+Ctrl+[". Nobody has to find out from
  a release note that their muscle memory stopped working.
- **Take a session back with one click** - *Medium.* New angle on "this
  shell is open in another window": the window that lost its session
  shows a Take it back button beside the notice, instead of only saying
  what happened.
- **Quitting says what keeps running** - *Medium.* Closing the last
  window says "3 sessions keep running, 1 will end", so you know before
  you quit what will still be there when you reopen.
- **Settings say when a change takes effect, and offer the restart** -
  *Medium.* A setting that only applies on the next launch is marked as
  you change it, with a Restart now button that brings your panes back.

### Under the hood

- **A busy PC never makes relaunch fail** - *High.* Waiting for your
  sessions stretches when the PC is loaded, and tries once more before
  any pane shows an error. New angle on "a pane that cannot start shows
  the way out": most of the time it should not fail at all.
- **Many panes restore visible-first** - *Medium.* With twenty kept
  panes, the ones on screen are brought back first and the rest follow,
  so the window is usable in the time one pane takes.
