### Reflection

**A test that needs the real screen is a test that needs you to be absent, and
nothing checked that.** The harness work of August moved almost every GUI test
onto a hidden desktop. The handful that cannot move were declared "exceptions"
and then trusted. Being declared never meant being safe to run. T1794 turns
"is the user here?" into a check those scripts must pass before they start.

**One incident, three layers.** The 09-27 problem was not one bug. It was a test
harness that does not look before it draws (T1794), a CLI that grabs focus by
design (T1797), and a soak daemon that treats "nobody is working" as "nobody is
here" (T1795). Mac fixed the CLI layer on its own the same fortnight. Filing all
three, and putting the product fix at P0, is the right shape.

**A stopped loop is now visible in the right places.** Health read
`digest=missing` and `publish=stale-14d` the moment it was asked. What nothing
did was tell you that the stop was still in force while the backlog aged. That
was your choice, not a fault. A one-line "the loop has been parked for N days"
in the dashboard would make the pause a fact on screen rather than something to
remember.

**Toward the cutover:** the branch is now 33 commits behind `main` again (T1796).
That merge is the gate for four of today's eight Mac cards, so it is the next big
lift after the focus work. T87 (the Mac regression build) and the install
walkthrough (T1179) still stand in front of T1074.

## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Stay out of the way while a game is fullscreen** — *High.* *New angle on
  "a quiet hour":* it switches on by itself. When a game or a presentation owns
  the screen, Ghoztty holds its toasts, taskbar flashes and new windows. When
  you come back, one line tells you what it held.
- **Take me to what just opened** — *High.* Now that scripts open windows and
  panes behind you, one chord jumps to the newest one, and the tab that holds
  it keeps a soft mark until you look. Background-open stops meaning "lost".
- **A note on a session saying why you kept it** — *Medium.* Give a running
  session one line ("waiting on the nightly build"). It shows on its chooser card
  and in its tab tooltip. A week later, the chooser tells you which sessions
  still matter.
- **Copy just Claude's answer** — *Medium.* One click on a Claude Code reply
  copies that block alone, without the margins, the prompt or the status line
  around it. It builds on the clean-copy work Mac just shipped.
- **New windows open on the monitor you are using** — *Medium.* A window a
  script opens appears on the monitor where you are working, not on the one where
  Ghoztty last had a window.

### Stability and performance

- **Stop drawing a window nobody can see** — *Medium.* A window that is fully
  covered, or sits on another virtual desktop, stops redrawing until it is
  visible again. You get the CPU and GPU back during a game or a long build, and
  nothing visible changes.
