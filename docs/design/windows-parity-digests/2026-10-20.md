## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Peek at a folded pane** - *High.* Hover a strip in a stacked row and a
  live preview of that pane appears beside it. You can check on it without
  sliding it in and rearranging the row.
- **A folded pane tells you when it finishes** - *High.* (New angle on busy
  glow.) A strip carries the pane's busy light and progress. When the hidden
  job ends or asks a question, the strip lights up, so folding a pane never
  means forgetting it.
- **Middle-click a strip to close it** - *Medium.* Middle-click closes a
  folded pane the way it closes a browser tab, with the same busy-pane
  confirmation.
- **The focus glow in your Windows accent colour** - *Medium.* The focused
  pane's glow and the busy light follow the accent colour you picked in
  Windows Settings, and change when you change it.
- **Respect Windows Focus sessions** - *Medium.* (New angle on a quiet hour.)
  While a Focus session is on, pane alerts wait as badges instead of popping
  up. They show when it ends.

### Stability and performance

- **Warn before a sign-in runs out** - *Medium.* A day before your Ghoztty
  account sign-in expires, the account menu says so and offers to renew it.
  Your other machines then do not quietly drop off the list.
