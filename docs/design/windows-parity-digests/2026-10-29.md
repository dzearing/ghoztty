reports is set to ship as soon as it is fixed, and yesterday you sent
many. So the updater offered you something new about every ninety
minutes. Each one carried real fixes. But an update prompt that often

deciding: keep shipping each fix at once, or bundle them into a few
releases a day. A small bug turned up here too. The publisher counts

## Feature ideas

### What you see and press

- **Know when your report is fixed** - *High.* (New angle on release
  notes.) When an update carries a fix for something you reported, the
  window says so in one line, "the fold flicker you reported is fixed in
  this version". With many releases a day, you know which one to take.
- **Show me that glitch** - *High.* One key saves the last two seconds of
  the window as a short clip with its frame timings. Press it right after
  something looks wrong, and the report carries what you actually saw.
- **Choose how often updates are offered** - *Medium.* A setting: as they
  ship, once a day, or once a week. Fixes still arrive, but the prompt
  does not interrupt you every hour on a busy day.

### Stability and performance

- **An update that runs slower says so** - *Medium.* After an update, if
  folds and drags draw slower than they did on the last version, a
  one-line note says so and offers the previous release.
- **Smooth on both monitors** - *Medium.* Move a window from a 144 Hz
  screen to a 60 Hz one and its glides switch to that screen's refresh
  at once, with no stutter while it crosses.
- **Windows you are not watching skip the animation** - *Medium.* A
  window that is covered or minimized finishes its folds and slides in
  one step. The window you are using keeps every frame.
